Advertiser terms

Draft · last updated Nov 2, 2026

These terms apply to software vendors ("you") who create listings on Vettlo through the vendor portal. By creating an account you agree to them.

1. Listings and editorial review

  • Listings go live only after editorial approval. We may edit listing copy for accuracy and clarity, request changes, or decline or remove a listing at our discretion.
  • You must provide accurate information about your product, pricing and landing page, and keep it up to date. Changes to listing copy are reviewed again before they go live.
  • Your listing doesn't change your product's Vettlo score, our reviews or what we write about other products.

2. Positions

  • Each category shortlist has a limited number of paid positions (currently 1st, 2nd and 3rd), each held by one vendor at a time for a paid month.
  • A reservation holds a position for 7 days while the invoice is paid. Unpaid reservations lapse automatically.
  • When a position becomes free, the first vendor on its waitlist may claim it within 72 hours before it opens to others.
  • Your product appears in its position only while the month is paid and the listing is approved and active.
  • Prices are shown in the portal and may change for future months with notice there; a paid month keeps the price you paid.
  • Every page that may contain partner placements discloses that partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear.

3. Billing

  • Positions are billed in advance, one month at a time. A position goes live, or is extended by one month, when its invoice is paid.
  • There's no contract. If you don't renew, the position ends at the end of the paid month.
  • If we remove your listing or end your position early for reasons other than a breach of these terms, we refund the unused part of the month.
  • Impression and visit figures in the portal are for your information and don't affect the price.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Your product, listing and landing page must be lawful, must not be misleading, and must not infringe anyone's rights.
  • You're responsible for your own landing pages, tracking and compliance with privacy laws for visitors you receive.

5. No guarantees

We don't guarantee any number of impressions, clicks or conversions. The portal and reports are provided as is.

6. Changes and termination

You can pause listings, let positions lapse or close your account at any time. We may suspend accounts that breach these terms. We may update these terms; material changes will be announced in the portal.

7. Contact

Questions about these terms: contact us.
