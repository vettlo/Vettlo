Advertiser terms

Draft · last updated Nov 17, 2026

These terms apply to software vendors ("you") who create listings on Vettlo through the vendor portal. By creating an account you agree to them.

1. Listings and editorial review

  • Listings go live only after editorial approval. We may edit listing copy for accuracy and clarity, request changes, or decline or remove a listing at our discretion.
  • You must provide accurate information about your product, pricing and landing page, and keep it up to date. Changes to listing copy are reviewed again before they go live.
  • Your listing doesn't change your product's Vettlo score, our reviews or what we write about other products.

2. Plans

  • Plans are sold per category. A position plan places your product at the position you buy, from #1 to #10, on the page you choose (a category shortlist or an alternatives page): its ranked list, review cards and comparison table. Each position on each page is sold to one vendor at a time, and a product can't buy a position on its own alternatives page. If a list has fewer entries than your position, your product appears at its end. Basic lists your product under Other tools to consider.
  • A reservation holds a seat for 7 days while the invoice is paid. Unpaid reservations lapse automatically.
  • When a position becomes free, the first vendor on its waitlist may claim it within 72 hours before it opens to others.
  • Your product appears only while the plan is paid and the listing is approved and active.
  • Prices are shown in the portal and may change for future billing periods; a paid period keeps the price you paid. A running monthly subscription keeps its price until you stop it.
  • Every page that may contain partner placements discloses that partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear.

3. Billing

  • Plans are billed in advance, monthly or yearly. A plan goes live, or is extended by its billing period, when payment is confirmed.
  • Monthly plans paid by card renew automatically each month until you stop the renewal in the portal; the plan then ends at the end of the paid month. Yearly plans cost 12 months minus 10% and don't renew automatically.
  • If we remove your listing or end your plan early for reasons other than a breach of these terms, we refund the unused part of the paid period.
  • Impression and visit figures in the portal are for your information and don't affect the price.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Your product, listing and landing page must be lawful, must not be misleading, and must not infringe anyone's rights.
  • You're responsible for your own landing pages, tracking and compliance with privacy laws for visitors you receive.

5. No guarantees

We don't guarantee any number of impressions, clicks or conversions. The portal and reports are provided as is.

6. Changes and termination

You can pause listings, let plans lapse or close your account at any time. We may suspend accounts that breach these terms. We may update these terms; material changes will be announced in the portal.

7. Contact

Questions about these terms: contact us.
