Standards

Editorial standards

The rules every Vettlo writer and editor follows.

Independence

Editorial decisions are made by the editorial team. People who sell advertising do not assign, edit or approve reviews.

Accuracy

Prices, plan limits and feature claims are checked against the vendor's current public pages or our own test account. Every review shows its last verified date.

Use of AI

We may use AI tools to help with research summaries, transcription and copy editing. Testing, scoring, conclusions and final wording are done and approved by the editorial team.

Corrections

When we get something wrong, we fix it and note the change in the review's change log. Report an error through our contact page.

Editorial team

Reviews are published by the Vettlo Editorial Team. Questions about a review go through our contact page.
