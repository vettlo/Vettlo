Short answer

Gusto is our top pick among the 7 payroll tools we ranked, scoring 88/100. Paid plans start at $49/mo + $6/person.

  • Gusto (88/100): best for small businesses that want payroll, benefits and core people tools in one place.
  • OnPay (87/100): best value for multi-state payroll.
  • Rippling (82/100): best for growing companies that want payroll, benefits and employee records in one system.
  • RUN Powered by ADP (80/100): best for small businesses that want a large provider's compliance and help desk services.
  • Square Payroll (79/100): best for hourly, shift-based teams.

Payroll software calculates pay, withholds and files federal, state and local taxes, pays employees by direct deposit and produces W-2s and 1099s at year end. Almost every small-business tool now prices the same way, a monthly base fee plus a fee for each person paid, so the real comparison is what the base plan leaves out: multi-state payroll, next-day direct deposit, time tracking and access to HR help.

We scored seven payroll services on six weighted criteria. For a 10-person team on the entry full-service plan, published list prices run from $87 a month (Patriot Full Service, one state) to $120 (QuickBooks Workforce Payroll); Rippling and ADP quote by company, and ADP publishes no prices.

How to choose payroll software

  • Price your headcount, not the base fee. At 10 people, OnPay and Gusto Simple both cost $109 a month, Square Payroll $95 and QuickBooks Workforce Payroll $120. Per-person fees decide the bill as you grow.
  • Check multi-state before you sign. OnPay, Square Payroll and ADP RUN include multi-state payroll on their entry plan. Gusto needs Plus, and Patriot charges $12 a month for each additional state.
  • Decide how fast you need to pay people. QuickBooks includes next-day direct deposit from Workforce Payroll and same-day from Premium; Gusto puts next-day on Plus or sells it as a Simple add-on.
  • Count the add-ons. OnPay's HR tools ($15 + $2 per worker) and time tracking ($15 + $4 per worker) can add $90 a month at 10 people. Price the bundle you will run.
  • Look past introductory offers. Patriot, QuickBooks and ADP all advertise discounts or free months for new customers. Compare the regular price you'll pay in month four.

Payroll FAQ

What is the best payroll software for small businesses?

Gusto scores highest in our ranking (88/100) for small businesses that want payroll, benefits and HR in one place. OnPay (87/100) is the best value if you pay people in more than one state, since all 50 states are included for $49 a month plus $6 per person.

How much does payroll software cost?

Most small-business payroll costs a monthly base fee plus a per-person fee. At published prices, full-service payroll for 10 employees costs $87 a month on Patriot Full Service, $95 on Square Payroll, $109 on Gusto Simple or OnPay and $120 on QuickBooks Workforce Payroll.

What is the cheapest payroll software?

Patriot Basic is $17 a month plus $4 per worker, but you file payroll taxes yourself. For full-service payroll that files taxes, Patriot ($37 + $5 per worker) and Square Payroll ($35 + $6 per person) are the cheapest here.

Does ADP publish RUN prices?

No. Each RUN Powered by ADP package (Essential, Enhanced, Complete and HR Pro) shows a Get pricing button, and ADP was advertising free months for new customers when we checked on Oct 28, 2026.
