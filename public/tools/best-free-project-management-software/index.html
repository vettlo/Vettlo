1

Asana

Best for cross-functional teams that need structure
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan for up to 2 users.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $10.99/user/mo (Starter, billed annually ($13.49 monthly)).

Asana organizes work into projects, tasks and goals. Starter adds timeline and Gantt views, dashboards and unlimited automations; Advanced ($24.99) adds portfolios, goals, resource management, approvals and time tracking.

Strengths

  • Unlimited automations from the Starter plan
  • Timeline and Gantt views on Starter
  • Portfolios and resource management on Advanced

Watch-outs

  • Free plan is limited to 2 users
  • Portfolios and time tracking need the $24.99 Advanced plan

ClickUp

Best all-in-one workspace on a budget
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free Forever plan.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $9/user/mo (Core, billed yearly (shown against a $12 regular price)).

ClickUp combines tasks, docs, whiteboards, goals and time tracking in one workspace. Its paid plans are Core ($9) and Business ($17) per user billed yearly, plus Enterprise by quote. Gantt charts and native time tracking start on Core, and every plan includes a monthly allowance of ClickUp Brain AI credits.

Strengths

  • Gantt charts and time tracking on the first paid plan
  • Docs, whiteboards and goals in the same tool
  • Free Forever plan with unlimited tasks

Watch-outs

  • So many features that setup takes real effort
  • Core and Business prices are shown as discounts from higher regular prices; confirm your price at checkout
  • Extra AI credits are a paid add-on

Wrike

Best for complex projects and resource planning
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan; 14-day trial.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $10/user/mo (Team, billed annually, 2 to 15 users).

Wrike is a work management platform for larger and more complex projects. Team ($10) includes interactive Gantt charts and dashboards; Business ($25, 5 to 200 users) adds workspace templates and standard integrations; resource and capacity planning and budgeting are on Pinnacle.

Strengths

  • Interactive Gantt charts from the Team plan
  • Strong resource and capacity planning on higher tiers
  • Free plan and 14-day trial with no card

Watch-outs

  • Business and higher plans are annual-only
  • Seats are sold in groups of 5 or more
  • Resource planning needs a sales-priced plan

Best for simple boards and small teams

Free plan: Free plan (10 collaborators per Workspace).

FAQ

What are the best free project management tools?

Asana (Free plan for up to 2 users) ranks highest among the project management tools we rank that offer a free plan. ClickUp, monday.com, Wrike also have free plans.

Is there a completely free option?

Yes, each tool on this page lists a free plan on its pricing page. Free plans have limits, shown in the table above, and paid plans add more users, usage and features.

What do free plans usually leave out?

Free plans typically cap users, records or monthly usage and leave out advanced automation, reporting and admin controls. Check each tool's limits against your team size before you commit.

When should I move to a paid plan?

When you hit a limit in the free plan or need a feature it leaves out. The table shows where each tool's paid plans start; our full project management tools ranking compares the paid tiers.
