Short answer

Brex is our top pick among the 7 expense management tools we ranked, scoring 94/100. Paid plans start at $12/user/mo.

  • Brex (94/100): best overall for card-led spend control.
  • Ramp (92/100): best for automated receipt matching on a free plan.

Expense management software replaces the spreadsheet-and-shoebox routine: employees snap receipts or pay with company cards, the software matches receipts to transactions and checks them against policy, managers approve, and coded expenses sync to your accounting system. The tools differ most in whether they are built around their own corporate card, how they bill (free, per user, per active member or per report), and which plan first adds reimbursements, multi-level approvals and ERP integrations.

We scored seven expense management tools on six weighted criteria. For each one we list the free plan, the entry price and its billing basis, and the plan where reimbursements, advanced approvals and ERP sync start.

What are expense management tools?

Expense management software collects receipts, card transactions and mileage, checks them against company policy, routes them for approval and reimburses employees, then syncs coded expenses to the accounting system. Many tools now issue their own corporate cards with spend limits, so expenses are matched to receipts as they happen instead of being filed in monthly reports.

Key features to look for

  • Receipt capture. Scan or forward receipts and extract amount, date and merchant automatically.
  • Corporate cards and limits. Virtual and physical cards with per-person or per-team spend limits.
  • Policies and approvals. Flag out-of-policy spend and route expenses through one or more approvers.
  • Reimbursements. Pay back out-of-pocket expenses and mileage by ACH or bank transfer.
  • Accounting sync. Code expenses and sync them to QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite or Sage Intacct.

How to choose expense management software

  • Decide whether you want a new card. Brex, Ramp and BILL are built around their own corporate cards, subject to credit approval. Expensify, Zoho Expense and Concur also work with the cards you already have.
  • Check where reimbursements start. Brex Essentials and BILL include reimbursements at $0; Ramp's pricing page lists reimbursement controls from Plus ($15 per user plus a platform fee).
  • Match the billing unit to your team. Per-user plans suit teams where everyone spends. If only a few people file reports, Concur's per-report price or Expensify's pay-per-use Collect plan can cost less.
  • Price the plan with ERP sync. NetSuite and Sage Intacct need Ramp Plus, Expensify Control or Brex Premium; QuickBooks Online and Xero work on the free or entry plans.
  • Trial with a real month. Load last month's receipts and card transactions and see how many each tool matches and codes without manual edits.

Expense management FAQ

What is the best expense management software?

Brex scores highest in our ranking (94/100), with reimbursements and multi-level approvals on its free plan. Ramp (92/100) does the most automatic receipt matching for free, and Expensify (89/100) is the strongest for out-of-pocket receipts and reimbursements.

Is there free expense management software?

Yes. Brex Essentials, Ramp Free and BILL Spend & Expense cost $0 per user. Zoho Expense is free for up to 3 users with 20 receipt scans per user a month, and Navan Expense is free for the first 5 expensing users a month.

How much does Expensify cost?

Collect is $5 per member a month with no annual commitment. Control is $18 per member a month on an annual subscription ($36 pay-per-use), and can drop to as low as $9 when most spend runs on the Expensify Card.

How much does SAP Concur cost for a small business?

SAP Concur lists Base from $7 per expense report and Plus from $11 per report, with unlimited users. Premium, which adds Concur Travel, is custom-priced.
