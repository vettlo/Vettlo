What is Xero?

Xero is cloud accounting with no per-user license fees. Early caps you at 20 invoices and 5 bills a month; Growing ($59) removes those caps and adds auto-reconcile; Established ($97) adds multi-currency, projects and expense claims.

Who it's best for

Small businesses with several people in the books, and businesses outside the US, where Xero is widely used.

Why we rank it

Unlimited users on every plan make it the cheapest way to give your whole team and your accountant access.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • No per-user fees on any plan
  • Unlimited invoices and bills from Growing
  • Payroll via Gusto as an add-on

Watch-outs

  • Early's 20-invoice and 5-bill caps are tight
  • Multi-currency and projects need Established ($97)

Skip it if: You send more than 20 invoices a month but only want to pay entry-level prices.
