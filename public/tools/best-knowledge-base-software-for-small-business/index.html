Slab

Best for a clean, low-cost team wiki

Free trial / planFree plan (up to 10 users); 30-day money-back guarantee

Slab is a knowledge base built only for team documentation, with posts organized into topics and one search across connected tools. Free covers up to 10 users with verification, 90-day version history and 10 integrations. Startup ($6.67 per user billed annually) adds unlimited users, private topics, 365-day history and AI Autofix; Business ($12.50) adds AI Ask, SAML SSO, SCIM, unlimited history and an uptime SLA. Enterprise starts at 100 users and adds audit logs.

Who it's best for

Small and mid-size teams that want a focused wiki and SSO without paying $20 a seat.

Strengths

  • Free for up to 10 users, with content verification
  • SAML SSO and SCIM on Business ($12.50)
  • Unlimited version history on Business

Watch-outs

  • AI Ask needs Business
  • Audit logs need Enterprise, which starts at 100 users
  • No databases or project features

Skip it if: You want your wiki and project tracking in the same tool.

Notion

Best overall for a flexible wiki with docs and databases
Visit website ↗
Starting price$10/seat/moPlus; Business $20 with Notion AI and SAML SSO; yearly billing saves up to 20%
Free trial / planFree plan (unlimited blocks for individuals, limited for 2+ members)
Main score87/100
G2 user rating4.6/513,972 reviews

Notion is a workspace for docs, wikis and databases that many teams use as their company knowledge base. Free gives individuals unlimited blocks, 7 days of page history and 5 MB file uploads, with a block limit for workspaces of 2 or more members. Plus ($10 a seat) adds 30-day page history and 5 GB uploads; Business ($20) adds Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes, Enterprise Search, page verification, private teamspaces, SAML SSO and 90-day history. SCIM and the audit log are on Enterprise.

Who it's best for

Teams that want their wiki, project docs and lightweight databases in one flexible tool.

Strengths

  • Docs, wikis and databases in one workspace
  • AI search and page verification on Business ($20)
  • Rated 4.6/5 on G2 across 13,972 reviews, the most here

Watch-outs

  • Free plan limits blocks for workspaces with 2+ members
  • Notion AI is only a trial below Business
  • SCIM and the audit log need Enterprise

Skip it if: You want a simple wiki with fixed structure and nothing to design.

Slite

Best for AI answers from your docs on the entry plan

Free trial / plan14-day free trial, no card (500 credits)

Slite is a team knowledge base built around AI search.   • No free plan
  • SSO is OpenID on Pro; SCIM needs Enterprise
  • Reader-only seats need Enterprise

Skip it if: You need a free plan or SAML SSO below an enterprise contract.

Outline is a team wiki available as a cloud service or as software you host yourself. Confluence

Best for large teams and companies that use Jira
Visit website ↗
Starting priceFree up to 10 usersStandard and Premium are priced per user with volume tiers; Atlassian's pricing page didn't display rates when we checked. Teamwork Collection (Jira, Confluence, Loom) Standard is $14.02/user/mo
Free trial / planFree plan (up to 10 users, 2 GB storage); free Standard and Premium trials
Main score81/100
G2 user rating4.1/54,373 reviews

Confluence is Atlassian's team wiki, organized into spaces and pages and linked closely with Jira. Free covers up to 10 users with 2 GB of storage, but permissions can't be customized. Standard adds global, space and page permissions, Rovo AI, audit logs, archiving and 250 GB of storage; Premium adds analytics, automation, permission inspection, unlimited storage and a 99.9% uptime SLA. SSO and SCIM come with Enterprise or the Atlassian Guard add-on.

Who it's best for

Larger organizations, and engineering and product teams that already run Jira.

Strengths

  • Free for up to 10 users
  • Space and page permissions, audit logs and Rovo AI from Standard
  • Tight links between pages and Jira work

Watch-outs

  • Analytics and automation need Premium
  • SSO needs Enterprise or the Atlassian Guard add-on
  • Lowest G2 rating here (4.1/5)

Skip it if: You're a small team that wants a wiki people can learn in an afternoon.

FAQ

What are the best knowledge base tools for small businesses?

Slab scores highest (86/100) once price and ease of use carry more weight, followed by Notion, Nuclino.

How is this different from your main ranking?

Same tools and the same 0 to 5 ratings, but price and ease of use count for more, because small teams rarely have an admin or a big budget. The weights are in the table on this page. Nuclino move into the top three as a result.

Which knowledge base tools are cheapest for a small team?

See the starting prices in the comparison table; free plans and trials are listed next to them. Price the plan that covers your team size, not only the entry plan.

What should a small business look for?

A free plan or low entry price, quick setup without an admin, and the two or three features you need on day one. Avoid paying for enterprise controls you won't use yet.
