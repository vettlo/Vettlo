What is Attio?

Attio is a newer CRM with a flexible, spreadsheet-like data model that syncs email and calendar data to build records automatically.

Who it's best for

Startups and product-led teams that want to model their own objects without hiring an admin.

Why we rank it

Its flexible objects let a small team shape the CRM around a non-standard motion, such as partnerships or product-led sales.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Flexible objects and views without an admin
  • Automatic enrichment from email and calendar
  • Modern, fast interface

Watch-outs

  • Younger product with a smaller ecosystem
  • Fewer mature sales-specific features than incumbents

Skip it if: Teams that need established forecasting and CPQ should look at incumbents first.

Attio pricing

Paid plans start at $35/user/mo (Plus, billed annually ($44 monthly)). Free plan or trial: Free plan (3 seats); 14-day Pro trial.
