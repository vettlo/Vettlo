Short answer

BambooHR is our top pick among the 6 HRIS tools we ranked, scoring 85/100. Paid plans start at $250/mo.

  • BambooHR (85/100): best for small and midsize companies that want a complete, easy HRIS.
  • Factorial (82/100): best for small teams that want time off, time tracking and documents in one tool.
  • Zoho People (81/100): best low-cost HRIS, with a free plan for up to 5 users.
  • Rippling (80/100): best for growing companies that want HR, payroll and IT on one employee record.
  • HiBob (78/100): best for mid-size and international companies focused on culture and engagement.

An HRIS (human resources information system) is the system of record for your people: employee profiles and documents, org charts, time off, onboarding checklists and, on higher plans, performance reviews. Most HRIS vendors price per employee per month, but the plan that first includes performance reviews, time tracking or payroll varies a lot, and several vendors only publish a starting price.

We scored six HRIS platforms on six weighted criteria. Published entry prices run from free (Zoho People for up to 5 users, Connecteam's Small Business plan for up to 10) to $250 a month for up to 25 employees on BambooHR Core. Rippling and Factorial publish only a starting price per employee, and HiBob quotes every customer.

What is HRIS software?

An HRIS (human resources information system) is the system of record for employee data: personal details, job and compensation history, time off, documents and onboarding. Many HRIS platforms also run payroll and benefits, or connect to a payroll provider.

Key features to look for

  • Employee records. One profile per person with job, pay and document history.
  • Onboarding and offboarding. Checklists, e-signatures and equipment tasks.
  • Time off. Policies, requests, approvals and balances.
  • Reporting. Headcount, turnover and compensation reports.
  • Integrations. Payroll, benefits, applicant tracking and identity systems.

How to choose an HRIS

  • Price your headcount, not the entry price. At 20 employees, BambooHR Core costs $250 a month, Factorial Business about $160, Rippling's HRIS module from $160 before other modules, and Connecteam HR Basic $29 billed yearly.
  • Decide when you need performance reviews. BambooHR adds review cycles on Pro ($425 a month up to 25 employees), Zoho People on Premium, and HiBob and Rippling sell performance as a separate module.
  • Check where time tracking lives. Factorial includes time tracking and shift scheduling; Zoho People adds timesheets on Professional; BambooHR sells Time & Attendance as an add-on.
  • Plan the payroll connection. BambooHR and Rippling sell payroll in the same platform (BambooHR for US employees only). Zoho People pairs with Zoho Payroll; Connecteam and Factorial mostly connect to an outside payroll provider.
  • Ask for the full quote in writing. Rippling, HiBob and Factorial's Starter and Enterprise plans are quoted. Ask for the per-employee price, every module and any implementation fee before you compare.

HRIS FAQ

What is the best HRIS for small businesses?

BambooHR scores highest in our ranking (85/100). It publishes its prices, starting at $250 a month for up to 25 employees on Core, and covers records, time off and applicant tracking on that plan. Factorial (82/100) is a cheaper option at $8 per employee on its Business plan.

How much does HRIS software cost?

Most HRIS tools charge per employee per month. At published prices, 20 employees cost $250 a month on BambooHR Core, about $160 on Factorial Business and $29 on Connecteam HR Basic billed yearly. Rippling's HRIS starts at $8 per employee before other modules, and HiBob quotes every customer.

Is there a free HRIS?

Yes. Zoho People is free for up to 5 users, Connecteam's Small Business plan is free for up to 10 users with every hub, and Connecteam's limited free HR plan covers up to 30 users with fewer features.

What's the difference between an HRIS and payroll software?

An HRIS stores employee records, time off, onboarding and reviews; payroll software calculates pay and files taxes. BambooHR and Rippling sell both in one platform. See our payroll software ranking if you need full-service payroll.
