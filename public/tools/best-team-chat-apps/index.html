7 Best Team Chat Apps in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 7, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 7, 2026

Short answer

Slack is our top pick among the 7 team chat apps we ranked, scoring 90/100. Paid plans start at $7.25/user/mo.

  • Slack (90/100): best overall for channels, apps and external chat.
  • Microsoft Teams (88/100): best value, and best for Microsoft 365 companies.
  • Google Chat (79/100): best for Google Workspace teams.
  • Zoom Workplace (78/100): best for teams that meet more than they chat.
  • Pumble (77/100): best low-cost Slack-style chat.

Every app here does channels and direct messages. The differences are in the limits and in what you already pay for: Slack's free plan keeps 90 days of messages, Zulip's 10,000, and Pumble's and Chanty's unlimited history. Teams comes with Microsoft 365, Google Chat with Google Workspace and Team Chat with Zoom Workplace, so for many companies the real question is whether a standalone chat app is worth a second bill.

We scored seven team chat apps on six weighted criteria. For each one we list the free-plan limits, the entry paid price per user, and the plan where group calls, SSO and compliance controls start.

What are team chat apps?

A team chat app is a messaging tool for work, where conversations happen in channels organized by team, project or topic instead of long email threads. It adds direct and group messages, file sharing, search across past conversations, voice and video calls, and integrations that post updates from other tools into the right channel.

Key features to look for

  • Channels and threads. Public and private conversations by team or project, with replies kept together.
  • Search and message history. Find past messages and files; free plans often limit how far back you can search.
  • Calls and huddles. Quick voice or video calls and screen sharing without scheduling a meeting.
  • Integrations and bots. Alerts and updates from other apps posted into channels, plus workflows that act on them.
  • Guests and external channels. Work with clients, contractors and partners in shared channels, with admin controls.

How to choose a team chat app

  • Check what you already pay for. Microsoft 365 includes Teams, Google Workspace includes Google Chat and Zoom Workplace includes Team Chat. A standalone app like Slack or Pumble is a second bill, so be clear about what it adds.
  • Look at free-plan history limits. Slack's free plan keeps 90 days of messages and Zulip Cloud's free plan searches the last 10,000; Pumble and Chanty keep unlimited history for free.
  • Price the plan with SSO. SAML SSO starts on Slack Business+ ($15), Zulip Cloud Plus ($10), Pumble Enterprise ($6.99) and Chanty Business ($3).
  • Count outside collaborators. Slack Pro supports shared channels with up to 250 organizations; Pumble Business includes 5 single-channel guests per paid seat; Google Chat lets admins allow external spaces and guests.
  • Trial with a real project. Move one active project's conversation into the trial for a week and check search, notifications and threads on phones as well as laptops.

Team chat FAQ

What is the best team chat app?

Slack scores highest in our ranking (90/100) for its messaging, integrations and shared channels with outside companies. Microsoft Teams (88/100) is the best value at $4 per user per month with 300-person meetings included.

What is the best free team chat app?

Pumble's free plan has unlimited users and message history, with 1:1 calls and 10 GB of storage per workspace. Chanty's free plan keeps unlimited history for up to 5 members. Slack's free plan keeps 90 days of messages and allows 10 integrations.

How much does Slack cost?

Slack Pro is $7.25 per user per month billed annually or $8.75 monthly. Business+, which adds SAML SSO, SCIM and AI search, is $15 billed annually or $18 monthly. Enterprise+ is sold through sales.

Is Microsoft Teams cheaper than Slack?

Yes. Teams Essentials is $4 per user per month paid yearly, against $7.25 for Slack Pro billed annually. For 25 users that's $1,200 a year on Teams against $2,175 on Slack.
