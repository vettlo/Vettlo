What is Listar?

Listar is a French enrichment tool that looks up work emails and direct mobile numbers across around thirty data sources plus its own database, starting from a name, a LinkedIn profile, a company name or a SIRET number. It enriches records in HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive and Attio, and works through Zapier, Make, n8n, an API and AI assistants.

Who it's best for

Sales teams and agencies prospecting in France and Europe that want to pay only for contacts found, without a subscription.

Why we rank it

You're charged only when data is found (1 credit = €1: €0.10 per email, €0.50 per phone), credits never expire, and mobiles are checked on WhatsApp to confirm the line is active.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Pay only for results: €0.10 per email and €0.50 per phone found
  • Mobile lines checked on WhatsApp, not just format-validated
  • Credits don't expire; volume discounts up to 60% based on cumulative spend

Watch-outs

  • Only one G2 review so far, so the 85%+ enrichment rate is mostly the vendor's own figure
  • No intent data or large searchable database for account-based prospecting

Skip it if: You need a large searchable database with intent data for account-based selling.

Listar pricing

Paid plans start at €0.10/email found (Pay as you go, €0.50 per phone found, excl. VAT; no subscription). Free plan or trial: 5 free credits on sign-up in eligible countries; no card needed.
