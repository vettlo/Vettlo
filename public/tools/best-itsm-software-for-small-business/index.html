The reviews

1

Jira Service Management

Best overall, and for IT teams that work alongside Jira
Visit website ↗
Starting price$21.50/agent/moStandard, Service Collection list price; Premium $55.28 adds change and problem management
Free trial / planFree plan (3 agents); 14-day trial of Standard or Premium
Main score84/100
G2 user rating4.3/51,004 reviews

Jira Service Management is Atlassian's service desk, now sold in its Service Collection together with Customer Service Management, Assets and Rovo AI. The Free plan covers 3 agents with request queues, workflows, alerts and on-call schedules. Standard ($21.50 per agent per month) adds asset and configuration management with 5,000 objects and Rovo agents; Premium ($55.28) adds change management, advanced incident and problem management, a virtual service agent and 50,000 asset objects.

Who it's best for

IT and engineering teams that already use Jira and want service requests, incidents and changes linked to development work.

Strengths

  • Free plan for 3 agents, with alerts and on-call schedules
  • Assets and configuration management from Standard, with 5,000 objects included
  • Change management, problem management and a virtual service agent on Premium at $55.28

Watch-outs

  • Change and problem management need Premium
  • Asset objects beyond the included amount cost $0.02 each per month
  • Virtual agent conversations beyond 1,000 a month cost $0.30 each

Skip it if: Nobody in your company uses Jira and you want problem and change management on a single published plan; SysAid includes both at $59 per agent.

InvGate Service Management

Best for small IT teams that want ITIL and assets at a flat price
Visit website ↗
Starting price$1,499/yrStarter, fixed yearly price for 5 agents; Pro adds change management at $500 per agent a year, from $2,500
Free trial / planFree trial (length not stated)

InvGate Service Management is an ITIL-aligned service desk; InvGate also sells a separate Asset Management product. Starter ($1,499 a year, fixed price for 5 agents) includes incident and problem management, a service catalog, SLAs, IT asset management and service configuration management. Pro ($500 per agent per year, from $2,500, for 5 to 100 agents) adds change management and a virtual service agent; Enterprise starts at $12,000 a year.

Who it's best for

Small and mid-size IT teams that want incident, problem, asset and configuration management without paying for a top-tier plan.

Strengths

  • Problem management, IT asset management and configuration management on Starter
  • Change management and a virtual service agent on Pro at $500 per agent a year
  • Fixed $1,499 yearly price for Starter

Watch-outs

  • Only 20 reviews on G2, so its 4.6 rating rests on a small sample
  • Change management needs Pro
  • Annual prices only, and the trial length isn't stated

Skip it if: You have more than 100 agents; Pro covers 5 to 100 agents and Enterprise starts at $12,000 a year.

Freshservice

Best for IT teams that want an easy, complete ITIL service desk
Visit website ↗
Starting price$19/agent/moStarter, billed annually; Growth $49 adds SLAs and a service catalog; Pro $99
Free trial / plan14-day trial with all features
Main score83/100
G2 user rating4.6/51,387 reviews

Freshservice is the IT service management tool from Freshworks. Starter ($19 per agent per month, billed annually) covers incident, knowledge and task management with a support portal; Growth ($49) adds a service catalog, SLA management, on-call management and cloud management; Pro ($99) adds problem, change, release and major incident management. Freddy AI Copilot is a $29 per agent add-on, and Enterprise, priced on request, includes Freddy AI Agent and AI Insights.

Who it's best for

IT teams that want every ITIL process, from tickets to change and release management, in one product with a short setup.

Strengths

  • Rated 4.6/5 on G2 across 1,387 reviews
  • Service catalog, SLAs and on-call management from Growth ($49)
  • Problem, change, release and major incident management on Pro

Watch-outs

  • Problem and change management need Pro at $99 per agent
  • Freddy AI Copilot costs $29 per agent extra
  • No free plan; asset management is sold in packs of 500 asset units

Skip it if: You only need ticketing for a few agents; Jira Service Management is free for 3 agents and ServiceDesk Plus Standard for 5 technicians.

SysAid is an ITSM platform built around AI. Professional ($59 per agent per month, billed annually) includes request, incident, change and problem management, asset management, SLA management, dashboards and BI analytics, plus its Copilot and pre-built AI agents with 100,000 Workato credits.   • No free plan
  • A sandbox needs Enterprise, which starts at 20 agents

Skip it if: You have a handful of agents and simple ticketing needs; the free plans from Jira Service Management or ServiceDesk Plus cover that.

SolarWinds Service Desk is a cloud ITSM tool priced per technician, with unlimited requesters. Essentials ($39 per technician per month, billed annually) includes incident and change management, a service catalog, asset management, a CMDB, SLAs and a service portal. Advanced ($99) adds a virtual agent, advanced automations, runbooks and network discovery; Premier ($124) adds AI incident summaries, a visual CMDB with dependency mapping and a sandbox.

Who it's best for

IT teams that track a lot of hardware and software and want asset management and a CMDB tied to change management from the first plan.

Strengths

  • Change management, asset management and a CMDB on Essentials
  • Unlimited requesters on every plan
  • 30-day trial with Premier features

Watch-outs

  • Problem and release management aren't itemized on any plan
  • The virtual agent and AI features need Advanced ($99) or Premier ($124)
  • Asset pricing is on request

Skip it if: AI features are a priority from day one; they start on Advanced at $99 per technician.

FAQ

What are the best ITSM tools for small businesses?

Jira Service Management scores highest (83/100) once price and ease of use carry more weight, followed by InvGate Service Management, Freshservice.

How is this different from your main ranking?

Same tools and the same 0 to 5 ratings, but price and ease of use count for more, because small teams rarely have an admin or a big budget. The weights are in the table on this page.

Which ITSM tools are cheapest for a small team?

See the starting prices in the comparison table; free plans and trials are listed next to them. Price the plan that covers your team size, not only the entry plan.

What should a small business look for?

A free plan or low entry price, quick setup without an admin, and the two or three features you need on day one. Avoid paying for enterprise controls you won't use yet.
