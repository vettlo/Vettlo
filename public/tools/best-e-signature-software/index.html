Short answer

E-signature software lets you upload a contract, place signature and form fields, send it to one or more signers and get back a signed PDF with an audit trail. Every tool here does that, so the differences are in how sending is billed (per user, per sender or per document), how many envelopes each seat may send before overage fees, and which plan first adds bulk send, web forms, branding and payments.

We scored six e-signature tools on six weighted criteria. For each one we list the free plan or trial, the entry price, the sending limits, and the plan you need for bulk send.

What is e-signature software?

E-signature software lets you send a document for signature online: you upload it or build it from a template, place signature, date and form fields, and signers sign from any browser or phone without an account. The tool tracks who has signed, sends reminders, and returns a completed PDF with an audit trail that records each signer's actions and timestamps.

Key features to look for

  • Templates and fields. Reusable documents with signature, initial, date, text and drop-down fields placed once.
  • Signing order and reminders. Sequential or parallel signers, automatic reminders and expiry dates.
  • Bulk send and web forms. Send one document to many recipients, or publish a signable form on a link.
  • Audit trail and signer verification. Timestamped history of every action, plus options such as SMS codes or ID checks.
  • Integrations and API. Send from a CRM, cloud storage or your own app, and file signed copies automatically.

How to choose e-signature software

  • Count envelopes per sender. Docusign Standard and Business Pro and all signNow plans allow 100 envelopes or invites per user per year; signNow charges $0.96 to $3.60 for each extra invite. PandaDoc Starter, SignWell Light and Dropbox Sign's paid plans list unlimited documents or requests.
  • Find where bulk send starts. Bulk send is on Docusign Business Pro ($45), signNow Business Premium ($15), Acrobat Pro for teams ($23.99), SignWell Light ($10) and Dropbox Sign Standard ($25 per user, 2 users minimum). PandaDoc charges usage credits for it.
  • Price the number of senders, not signers. These tools charge for the people who send documents, not the people who sign them. SignWell's Business covers 3 senders for $30 a month, against $90 for 3 Docusign Standard users.
  • Check what the cheapest plan leaves out. PandaDoc Starter has 5 templates and no CRM integrations; Docusign Personal is one user and 5 envelopes a month; Dropbox Sign Essentials has 5 templates.
  • Send real documents during the trial. Rebuild your three most-used contracts as templates and send them to colleagues on phone and desktop before you buy.

E-signature FAQ

What is the best e-signature software?

Docusign scores highest in our ranking (88/100) for its signing options, integrations and admin controls. PandaDoc (85) is best for proposals and quotes, and signNow (84) is the cheapest per user at $8 billed annually.

What is the best free e-signature software?

SignWell's free plan sends 3 documents a month, Dropbox Sign's 3 signature requests a month with unlimited self-signing, and PandaDoc's 5 documents a month. BoldSign's free Essential plan allows 25 envelopes a month.

How much does Docusign cost?

Docusign Personal is $11 a month billed annually for one user and 5 envelopes a month. Standard is $30 per user a month and Business Pro $45, both billed annually with 100 envelopes per user per year. Enhanced is priced by quote.

Do e-signature tools limit how many documents you can send?

Some do. Docusign Standard and Business Pro and signNow's paid plans allow 100 envelopes or invites per user per year, and signNow charges $0.96 per extra invite on Business. PandaDoc Starter, SignWell Light and Dropbox Sign's paid plans list unlimited documents or requests.
