What is Outreach?

Outreach is an enterprise sales execution platform sold in four tiers (Amplify Essentials, Core, Plus and Pro) that include between 10,000 and 100,000 AI credits. Prices are only available from sales.

Who it's best for

Mid-market and enterprise sales orgs with a RevOps team that need standardized sequences, deal management and pipeline analytics across many reps.

Why we rank it

It's built for running large outbound teams consistently: governed sequences, deep CRM sync and manager-level analytics.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Deep sequencing and governance for large teams
  • Strong analytics for managers and RevOps
  • Mature CRM integration

Watch-outs

  • No public pricing or free trial
  • No built-in contact database; pair it with a data provider
  • Needs admin time to configure well

Skip it if: You're a team under about 15 reps without RevOps support.
