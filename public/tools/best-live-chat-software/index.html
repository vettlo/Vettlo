Short answer

LiveChat is our top pick among the 7 live chat tools we ranked, scoring 85/100. Paid plans start at $19/user/mo.

  • LiveChat (85/100): best overall for dedicated chat teams.
  • Intercom (84/100): best for AI-first chat and in-app messaging.
  • Freshchat (83/100): best for chat plus messaging apps on a budget.
  • Tidio (82/100): best for small online stores.
  • Crisp (81/100): best flat price for small teams.

Live chat software adds a chat widget to your website or app and gives your team an inbox to answer visitors in real time. Every tool here does that, so the differences are in how it's billed (per agent, per workspace or per conversation), how much AI is included before you pay per resolution, and which plan first adds routing, unlimited chat history and reports.

We scored seven live chat tools on six weighted criteria. For each one we list the free plan or trial, the entry price and billing basis, and what the AI costs once the included allowance runs out.

What is live chat software?

Live chat software puts a chat widget on your website or app so visitors can message your team in real time, and gives agents a shared inbox to answer, route and follow up on those conversations. Most tools now add chatbots or AI agents that answer common questions on their own and hand harder ones to a person.

Key features to look for

  • Chat widget. A customizable chat window for your website, app or online store.
  • Shared inbox and routing. Conversations assigned to the right agent or team, with transfers and supervision.
  • AI agents and chatbots. Automated answers from your help content, with handoff to a human.
  • Proactive messages. Triggered greetings and campaigns based on page, time or visitor data.
  • Reports. Chat volume, response times, ratings and agent performance.

How to choose live chat software

  • Price the plan with routing. LiveChat's routing rules and supervision start on Growth ($79), Intercom's round-robin on Advanced ($85) and Crisp's routing on Essentials ($95 for 10 seats). Compare the plan you'd really use.
  • Model AI at your volume. Intercom charges $0.99 per Fin outcome, Freshchat $49 per 100 AI sessions and LiveChat $49.50 per 50 extra resolutions. Olark charges a flat $400 a month.
  • Check the billing unit. Per-agent pricing suits small teams with steady chat; Crisp's per-workspace and Tidio's per-conversation pricing can be cheaper for larger or part-time teams.
  • Watch chat history limits. LiveChat Essential keeps 60 days of history; Crisp includes unlimited retention from Mini.
  • Test on your real site. Install the widget on a live page during the trial and answer a week of real chats before you commit.

What is the best live chat software?

LiveChat scores highest in our ranking (85/100), followed by Intercom (84) for AI-first chat and Freshchat (83) for chat plus messaging apps. tawk.to is the best free option.

What is the best free live chat software?
