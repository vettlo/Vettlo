What is Pendo?

Pendo is a product experience platform that combines product analytics with in-app guides, surveys and roadmaps. Free covers up to 500 monthly active users with product analytics, in-app guides and Pendo-branded NPS surveys and roadmaps; above 500 MAUs you can't create new guides, surveys or segments until you upgrade. Base includes analytics and guides, Core adds session replay, and Ultimate adds sentiment surveys (NPS, PMF, CSAT), Orchestrate, Listen and Data Sync. Every plan includes the Leo AI assistant and retroactive analytics, and paid plans are priced by MAUs.

Who it's best for

SaaS product teams that want to measure feature adoption and act on it with in-app guides in the same tool.

Why we rank it

It's the only tool here that pairs analytics with in-app guides and surveys on the free plan.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Free plan includes in-app guides and NPS surveys
  • Retroactive analytics and the Leo AI assistant on every plan
  • Paid plans aren't interrupted if you go over your MAU limit

Watch-outs

  • No published prices for paid plans
  • Free plan stops at 500 monthly active users
  • Session replay starts on Core, not Base

Skip it if: You have thousands of users and want to start free, or you only need analytics.
