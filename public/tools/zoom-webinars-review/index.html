What is Zoom Webinars?

Zoom Webinars is Zoom's webinar product, sold separately from Zoom Workplace meetings. The Webinars plan ($66.67 a month billed annually for 500 attendees) includes Q&A, chat, polls, live transcription, cloud recording, AI webinar summaries and up to 1,000 video panelists, with sessions up to 30 hours.

Who it's best for

Marketing and communications teams that run large live webinars and want registrations to land in HubSpot, Marketo or Salesforce.

Why we rank it

It's the only tool here whose entry webinar plan lists HubSpot, Marketo, Salesforce, Eloqua and Pardot integrations, and it scales to 100,000 attendees on larger capacities.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Eloqua, HubSpot, Marketo, Pardot and Salesforce integrations on every webinar plan
  • Up to 1,000 video panelists and 30-hour sessions
  • AI webinar summary included

Watch-outs

  • Basic landing pages and 720p video on the Webinars plan; advanced pages and 1080p on higher tiers
  • Backstage and breakout rooms aren't in the entry Webinars column
  • No free trial listed

Skip it if: You mostly run evergreen, pre-recorded webinars; tools built around automated replays fit better.

Zoom Webinars pricing

Paid plans start at $66.67/mo (Webinars, 500 attendees, billed annually ($89 monthly); Webinars Plus $82.50). Free plan or trial: No free trial listed.
