ITSM (IT service management) software runs the IT service desk: employees raise requests and report incidents through a portal, and IT tracks them against SLAs, manages problems and changes, and keeps a record of the hardware and software it supports. Most tools price per agent (the IT staff who work tickets), and requesters are free. The real price difference is where problem management, change management and asset tracking start: on the entry plan, on a top tier, or as paid add-ons.

We scored six ITSM tools on six weighted criteria. Two have free plans: Jira Service Management for 3 agents and ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus Standard for 5 technicians. Change management starts at $39 per technician on SolarWinds Service Desk Essentials, $55.28 per agent on Jira Service Management Premium, $59 on SysAid Professional and $99 on Freshservice Pro.

What is ITSM software?

ITSM (IT service management) software is the system an IT team uses to deliver and support technology for the rest of the company. Employees submit requests and report incidents through a portal or email, and IT works them against SLAs, investigates recurring problems, approves changes before they go live and tracks the devices, software and configuration items it supports. Most tools follow the ITIL framework and price per agent, with requesters free.

Key features to look for

  • Incident and request management. Tickets from a portal, email or chat, routed to the right group and tracked against SLAs.
  • Service catalog. A menu of standard requests, such as new laptops or software access, with approval steps.
  • Problem and change management. Root-cause tracking for recurring incidents and approval workflows for changes.
  • Asset management and CMDB. An inventory of hardware and software, and how configuration items depend on each other.
  • Self-service and AI. A knowledge base and virtual agents that answer common requests before they become tickets.

How to choose ITSM software

  • Price the plan with change and problem management. Change management starts on SolarWinds Essentials ($39), Jira Service Management Premium ($55.28), SysAid Professional ($59), InvGate Pro ($500 a year) and Freshservice Pro ($99). ServiceDesk Plus includes it on Enterprise or sells it as a $3,195-a-year add-on.
  • Count agents, not employees. Every tool here prices by the IT staff who work tickets; requesters are free. A 10-agent team on Freshservice Growth pays $5,880 a year, whatever the headcount.
  • Check how assets are billed. Jira Service Management includes 5,000 asset objects on Standard, ServiceDesk Plus prices Professional and Enterprise by technicians plus assets, and Freshservice sells asset units in packs of 500.
  • Start free if you're small. Jira Service Management is free for 3 agents and ServiceDesk Plus Standard for 5 technicians; both let you test real requests before you pay.
  • Trial with your own requests. Import a month of real tickets and run one change through approval in the trial to see how much setup each tool needs.

ITSM FAQ

What is the best ITSM software?

Jira Service Management scores highest in our ranking (84/100), with a free plan for 3 agents and change management on Premium at $55.28 per agent. Freshservice (83) is close behind and has the higher G2 rating, 4.6 against 4.3.

Is there free ITSM software?

Yes. Jira Service Management is free for up to 3 agents, and ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus Standard is free for up to 5 technicians, on one service desk instance.

How much does Freshservice cost?

Freshservice is $19 per agent per month for Starter, $49 for Growth and $99 for Pro, billed annually. Enterprise is priced on request, and Freddy AI Copilot is a $29 per agent add-on.

What's the difference between ITSM software and a help desk?

A help desk handles tickets. ITSM software adds the ITIL processes IT teams use around them: problem management to fix root causes, change management to approve and schedule changes, and asset and configuration management to track what IT supports.
