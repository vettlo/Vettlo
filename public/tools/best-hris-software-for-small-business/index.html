The reviews

1

Connecteam

Best for deskless and frontline teams that run HR from their phones
Visit website ↗
Starting price$29/mo for 30 usersHR Basic, billed yearly ($35 monthly); $0.50 per extra user
Free trial / planFree plan for up to 10 users; 14-day trial
Main score77/100
G2 user rating4.6/53,506 reviews

Connecteam is a mobile-first app for frontline teams; its HR & Skills hub covers employee documents, time off, onboarding packs, courses and recognition. Basic costs $29 a month for the first 30 users billed yearly ($35 monthly) plus $0.50 per extra user; Advanced ($49) adds e-sign documents and unlimited documents; Expert ($99) adds unlimited time-off policies and API access. A free Small Business plan covers all hubs for up to 10 users.

Who it's best for

Restaurants, retail, construction and field-service businesses whose employees don't sit at a computer.

Strengths

  • $29 a month covers the first 30 users on Basic
  • Free Small Business plan with every hub for up to 10 users
  • Rated 4.6/5 on G2 across more than 3,500 reviews

Watch-outs

  • Basic allows 6 time-off policies; unlimited needs Expert ($99)
  • No performance review cycles, only per-employee KPIs
  • Thinner employee records and reporting than a dedicated HRIS

Skip it if: You need performance reviews, compensation planning or detailed HR reporting.

2

BambooHR

Best for small and midsize companies that want a complete, easy HRIS
Visit website ↗
Starting price$250/moCore, up to 25 employees; above 25, from $10/employee/mo. Pro $425/mo, Elite $650/mo
Free trial / planNo trial listed; free price quote
Main score85/100
G2 user rating4.4/58,337 reviews

BambooHR is an HRIS sold in three plans. Core ($250 a month for up to 25 employees, or $10 per employee above 25) covers employee records, time off with unlimited custom policies, reporting, eNPS and applicant tracking for 5 job openings. Pro ($425, or $17 per employee) adds 360° reviews, 1:1s, recognition and 25 openings; Elite ($650, or $25) adds compensation planning, salary benchmarking and 50 openings. US payroll and benefits administration are add-ons.

Who it's best for

Companies with roughly 10 to 500 employees that want employee records, time off and hiring in one easy tool, and performance reviews when they're ready.

Strengths

  • Published prices on all three plans, month-to-month terms available
  • Time off with unlimited custom policies and applicant tracking on Core
  • Rated 4.4/5 on G2 across more than 8,000 reviews

Watch-outs

  • Flat $250 a month minimum, even for a 5-person company
  • Performance reviews need Pro ($425 a month or $17 per employee)
  • Payroll is an add-on for US employees only, with no published price

Skip it if: You have fewer than 10 employees; Zoho People's free plan or Connecteam's free Small Business plan covers basic records and time off.

3

Zoho People

Best low-cost HRIS, with a free plan for up to 5 users
Visit website ↗
Starting priceFrom $3/user/moPaid plans from 5 users; yearly billing saves more than 20%
Free trial / planFree plan for 5 users; 30-day trial
Main score81/100
G2 user rating4.5/5432 reviews

Zoho People has a free plan for up to 5 users and four paid plans with a 5-user minimum. Free includes custom time-off policies; Essential HR adds onboarding and offboarding, the employee database, document management and HR reports; Professional adds attendance, timesheets and rostering; Premium adds performance, compensation and engagement; Enterprise adds an HR help desk and learning management.

Who it's best for

Small businesses on a tight budget, especially those already using Zoho apps such as Zoho Payroll or Zoho Books.

Strengths

  • Free for up to 5 users
  • Plans start at $3 per user per month
  • 30-day free trial on all plans

Watch-outs

  • Performance management needs Premium; time tracking needs Professional
  • Plan prices vary by region and weren't shown to us
  • More configuration than BambooHR or Connecteam

Skip it if: You want an HRIS a non-HR manager can set up in an afternoon.

FAQ

What are the best HRIS tools for small businesses?

Connecteam scores highest (85/100) once price and ease of use carry more weight, followed by BambooHR, Zoho People.

How is this different from your main ranking?

Same tools and the same 0 to 5 ratings, but price and ease of use count for more, because small teams rarely have an admin or a big budget. The weights are in the table on this page. Connecteam move into the top three as a result.

Which HRIS tools are cheapest for a small team?

See the starting prices in the comparison table; free plans and trials are listed next to them. Price the plan that covers your team size, not only the entry plan.

What should a small business look for?

A free plan or low entry price, quick setup without an admin, and the two or three features you need on day one. Avoid paying for enterprise controls you won't use yet.
