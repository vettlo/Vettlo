What is Nuclino?

Nuclino is a lightweight wiki with docs, boards and visual canvases in one workspace. Free covers up to 50 items, 3 canvases and 2 GB of storage. Starter, from $6 per user a month, adds unlimited items and canvases, publishing, 30-day version history and 10 GB per user; Business adds Sidekick AI, SAML SSO, an audit log, team insights, unlimited version history and 20 GB per user. Nuclino counts guests and read-only members as users.

Who it's best for

Small teams that want a fast, simple wiki with almost no setup.

Why we rank it

It's the easiest tool here to learn, with a free plan and the lowest listed entry price.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Lowest listed entry price here, from $6 a user
  • Free plan with 2 GB of storage
  • Rated 4.7/5 on G2, the highest here (30 reviews)

Watch-outs

  • Free plan stops at 50 items
  • AI, SAML SSO and the audit log need Business
  • Guests and read-only members count as paid users

Skip it if: You have more than a few dozen pages and want to stay free.
