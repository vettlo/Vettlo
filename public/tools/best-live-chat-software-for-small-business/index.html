Tidio

Free trial / planFree plan (50 conversations, 10 seats); 7-day trial

Tidio combines live chat, a Lyro AI agent and Flows chatbots, priced by billable conversations rather than seats. A billable conversation counts only when a human agent replies or starts it. The free plan includes 50 billable conversations, 10 seats and 50 lifetime Lyro conversations; Starter ($24.17 a month billed annually) raises that to 100 conversations, and Growth (from $49.17) starts at 250. Lyro is a separate add-on from $32.50 a month for 50 AI conversations.

Who it's best for

Small ecommerce and service businesses that want chat and an AI agent without paying per seat.

Strengths

  • Rated 4.6/5 on G2 across 2,099 reviews
  • 10 seats on every self-serve plan, including Free
  • Only human-handled conversations are billable

Watch-outs

  • Automatic chat assignment, operating hours and macros aren't on Free
  • Lyro AI is billed separately from chat plans
  • Costs rise with conversation volume

Skip it if: You run a large support team that needs SLAs, skill-based routing and detailed agent reports.

Freshchat is the messaging and live chat tool from Freshworks. The free plan covers up to 10 agents with a web and mobile chat widget and a shared inbox. Growth ($19 an agent billed annually) adds social messengers, text messaging and reports; Pro ($49) adds intelligent routing, multiple SLA policies and multilingual conversations; Enterprise ($79) adds skill-based routing and audit logs. Who it's best for

Teams that answer website chat, social messengers and text messages from one inbox and want SLAs at a mid-range price.

  • Free plan for up to 10 agents
  • Social messengers and text messaging from Growth
  • Intelligent routing and multiple SLA policies on Pro

Watch-outs

  • Freddy AI Agent costs $49 per 100 sessions on top
  • Freddy AI Copilot is a $29 per agent add-on
  • Annual billing is needed for the listed prices (it saves 20%)

Skip it if: You only need a simple website widget for a few agents.

Best overall for dedicated chat teams

Free trial / plan14-day trial, no card needed
Main score85/100
G2 user rating4.5/5821 reviews

LiveChat is a live chat and AI support tool billed per user. Essential ($19 a user a month billed annually, $25 monthly) includes 1 AI agent with 10 AI resolutions a month, 60 days of chat history and 50 tracked live visitors. Growth ($79 billed annually, $99 monthly) adds unlimited history, chat supervision and takeover, shared canned responses, team performance reports, 10 AI agents and 200 AI resolutions a month. Sales and support teams that staff website chat all day and need supervision, routing and per-agent reports.

Strengths

  • 200 AI resolutions a month included on Growth
  • Chat supervision, takeover and team reports on Growth
  • 14-day trial with no card needed

Watch-outs

  • Essential keeps only 60 days of history and has no routing rules
  • Big jump from $19 to $79 a user
  • No free plan

Skip it if: You're a small team that wants free or flat-priced chat for a few agents.

FAQ

What are the best live chat tools for small businesses?

Tidio scores highest (86/100) once price and ease of use carry more weight, followed by Freshchat, LiveChat.

How is this different from your main ranking?

Same tools and the same 0 to 5 ratings, but price and ease of use count for more, because small teams rarely have an admin or a big budget. The weights are in the table on this page. Tidio move into the top three as a result.

Which live chat tools are cheapest for a small team?

See the starting prices in the comparison table; free plans and trials are listed next to them. Price the plan that covers your team size, not only the entry plan.

What should a small business look for?

A free plan or low entry price, quick setup without an admin, and the two or three features you need on day one. Avoid paying for enterprise controls you won't use yet.
