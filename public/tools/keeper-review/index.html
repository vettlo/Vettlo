What is Keeper?

Keeper has three business plans billed annually. Business Starter ($2 per user per month) is for teams of 5 to 10 users with an encrypted vault for each user, an admin console and credential sharing. Business (from $4, minimum 5 users) adds shared team folders, delegated administration, enforcement policies, security audit and activity reports, and a free Family Plan for every user. Enterprise (from $6 in Keeper's plan guide; quoted on its pricing page) adds SAML 2.0 SSO, SCIM and AD/LDAP sync, advanced 2FA and developer APIs. Advanced Reporting & Alerts and Compliance Reports are paid add-ons.

Who it's best for

Small and midsize businesses that want strong admin controls at a low per-user price and don't need SSO yet.

Why we rank it

Business costs from $4 a user and includes shared folders, enforcement policies, audit reports and a free family plan for staff, the lowest price here for that set.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Business Starter at $2 a user for 5 to 10 people
  • Free Family Plan for every user on Business
  • Rated 4.6/5 on G2

Watch-outs

  • SSO, SCIM and directory sync need Enterprise, which is quoted
  • Advanced reporting and compliance reports are add-ons
  • Prices weren't shown on its pricing page when we checked

Skip it if: You need SSO or SCIM provisioning with a published price; Bitwarden and Proton Pass list both.
