Short answer

ActiveCampaign is our top pick among the 5 marketing automation tools we ranked, scoring 86/100. Paid plans start at $15/mo.

  • ActiveCampaign (86/100): best for small and mid-sized teams that want deep automation at a low price.
  • HubSpot Marketing Hub (85/100): best all-in-one platform for teams that already use HubSpot CRM.
  • Brevo (82/100): best for budget automation priced by emails sent.
  • Salesforce Marketing Cloud Account Engagement (76/100): best for B2B teams that run sales on Salesforce.
  • Customer.io (74/100): best for product-led companies messaging users on behavior.

Marketing automation software runs the journeys that turn a sign-up into a customer: multi-step workflows triggered by what a contact does, lead scoring that tells sales who is ready, and reports that tie campaigns to pipeline. Email marketing tools cover the basics; the platforms here are judged on how far automation, scoring and attribution go, and on which plan first includes them.

We scored six platforms on six weighted criteria. Entry paid plans run from $15 a month for 1,000 contacts (ActiveCampaign Starter, billed yearly) to $1,250 a month for 10,000 contacts (Salesforce Account Engagement Growth+, billed annually). Built-in lead scoring starts at $499 a month on Brevo Professional, $800 a month plus a $3,000 onboarding fee on HubSpot Marketing Hub Professional, and $1,250 on Account Engagement; ActiveCampaign sells it in its Pipelines add-on from the $49 Plus plan.

Small and mid-sized B2B and B2C teams that want branching, multi-step journeys without paying enterprise prices.

How to choose marketing automation software

  • Find the plan where your must-have feature starts. Unlimited automation actions start at $49 a month on ActiveCampaign Plus and $18 on Brevo Standard; HubSpot puts full workflows on Professional at $800 a month.
  • Check where lead scoring lives. It's included from Growth+ on Account Engagement ($1,250), on HubSpot Professional ($800) and Brevo Professional ($499), and in a paid add-on on ActiveCampaign.
  • Add onboarding and seats to year one. HubSpot charges $3,000 one-time on Professional and $7,000 on Enterprise; Keap requires implementation. ActiveCampaign Plus includes 1 user and Pro 3.
  • Match the data model to your business. Account Engagement and HubSpot tie scoring to a CRM for B2B sales handoffs; Customer.io triggers journeys from product events for app and SaaS companies.
  • Build one real journey in the trial. Recreate your welcome or nurture sequence, with one branch and one goal, before you migrate contacts.

Marketing Automation FAQ

What is the best marketing automation software?

ActiveCampaign scores highest in our ranking (86/100) for most small and mid-sized teams, just ahead of HubSpot Marketing Hub (85/100), which is the better pick if you want lead scoring, custom reports and CRM on one plan and can pay $800 a month plus onboarding.

How much does marketing automation software cost?

On Oct 31, 2026: ActiveCampaign Starter $15 a month and Plus $49 for 1,000 contacts billed yearly, Brevo Standard $18 a month for 5,000 emails, Customer.io Essentials $100 a month, HubSpot Marketing Hub Professional $800 a month billed annually plus $3,000 onboarding, and Salesforce Account Engagement Growth+ $1,250 a month on an annual contract.

Which marketing automation tool includes lead scoring for the lowest price?

Brevo Professional includes contact scoring from $499 a month. HubSpot includes lead scoring on Professional ($800 a month plus $3,000 onboarding) and Salesforce on Account Engagement Growth+ ($1,250 a month). ActiveCampaign offers it through its Pipelines add-on from Plus.

What is the difference between email marketing and marketing automation software?

Email marketing tools focus on campaigns and simple automations. Marketing automation platforms add multi-step journeys across channels, lead scoring and attribution; for example, HubSpot Professional includes up to 300 workflows and 5 lead scores, and Account Engagement Plus+ adds B2B Marketing Analytics.
