What is Freshdesk?

Freshdesk is the help desk from Freshworks. Growth includes ticketing, a shared inbox, a customer portal and knowledge base, plus 500 Freddy AI Agent sessions; Pro adds multiple SLA policies, custom reporting and intelligent routing.

Who it's best for

Growing support teams that want SLAs, routing and an AI agent without Zendesk Suite pricing.

Why we rank it

It includes an AI agent allowance and a knowledge base on its entry plan, and multiple SLA policies on Pro.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • 500 AI agent sessions included
  • Knowledge base and customer portal from Growth
  • Multiple SLA policies on Pro

Watch-outs

  • Freddy AI Copilot is a $29 per agent add-on
  • No free plan listed

Skip it if: You need deep telephony in the same tool.

Freshdesk pricing

Paid plans start at $19/agent/mo (Growth, billed annually; Pro $55). Free plan or trial: 14-day trial of Enterprise.
