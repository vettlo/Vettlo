Short answer

MailerLite is our top pick among the 6 email marketing tools we ranked, scoring 85/100. Paid plans start at $10.80/mo.

  • MailerLite (85/100): best for small businesses that want automation at a low price.
  • Klaviyo (84/100): best for ecommerce brands.
  • ActiveCampaign (84/100): best for advanced automation.
  • Brevo (83/100): best for high-volume senders on a budget.
  • Mailchimp (79/100): best for beginners who want a familiar all-rounder.

Email marketing software sends your newsletters and campaigns, and runs the automations that welcome new subscribers, recover abandoned carts and nurture leads. Every tool here has a free plan or trial; the differences are in how far automation goes on the cheaper plans and how quickly the price climbs as your list grows.

We scored six tools on six weighted criteria and priced each at the same list size. At 1,000 contacts, entry plans run from $15 a month (ActiveCampaign Starter, billed yearly) to $33 (Kit Creator, billed yearly), and Brevo prices by emails sent rather than contacts.

How to choose email marketing software

  • Price at your list size, not the entry price. At 1,000 contacts MailerLite Comfort is $17.10 a month billed yearly, Klaviyo Email $30, Mailchimp Essentials $26.50 and ActiveCampaign Starter $15. Brevo prices by emails sent instead.
  • Check automation limits on the cheaper plans. Mailchimp Essentials caps flows at 4 steps, ActiveCampaign Starter at 5 actions per automation, and Brevo Starter has no automation.
  • Match the tool to your business model. Klaviyo is built around store data, ActiveCampaign around journeys and lead scoring, Kit around creators' newsletters.
  • Count the people who need access. Seats range from 1 on Brevo Starter to unlimited on MailerLite Power and Mailchimp Premium.
  • Send real campaigns during the trial. Import a segment of your list and send a campaign and one automation before you commit.

Email marketing FAQ

What is the best email marketing software?

MailerLite scores highest in our ranking for most small and mid-sized businesses. Klaviyo is the best pick for ecommerce brands and ActiveCampaign for advanced automation.

What is the best free email marketing software?

Kit's free plan covers up to 10,000 subscribers, Brevo's free plan sends 300 emails a day, and MailerLite's free plan includes 250 subscribers, 2,500 emails a month and 3 automations.

How much does email marketing software cost for 1,000 contacts?

On Oct 26, 2026: ActiveCampaign Starter $15 a month and MailerLite Comfort $17.10 (both billed yearly), Mailchimp Essentials $26.50, Klaviyo Email $30, and Kit Creator $33 billed yearly.

Is Mailchimp still worth it?

It's easy to use and integrates with almost everything, but automation is limited on Essentials and it costs more than MailerLite or Brevo at the same list size.
