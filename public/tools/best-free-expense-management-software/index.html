The free plans

1

Brex

Best overall for card-led spend control
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (Essentials).

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $12/user/mo (Premium; Essentials is $0 per user, Enterprise by quote).

Brex combines corporate cards with expense management, bill pay and travel booking. The free Essentials plan includes global card acceptance with one local card program, reimbursements, AI-powered custom rules, multi-level approval chains, accounting integrations and travel booking. Premium ($12 per user a month) adds multiple expense policies, live budgets, dynamic review chains, customizable ERP and HRIS integrations, VAT documentation and multi-entity support.

Strengths

  • Reimbursements and multi-level approvals on the free Essentials plan
  • Premium at $12 per user adds multiple policies, live budgets and multi-entity support
  • Rated 4.8/5 on G2

Watch-outs

  • Local card issuance in other countries needs the custom-priced Enterprise plan
  • Customizable ERP and HRIS integrations start on Premium, not Essentials

2

Ramp

Best for automated receipt matching on a free plan
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan; 30-day trial of Plus.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $15/user/mo (Plus, plus a platform fee based on team size; 20% off annual billing; Free plan $0).

Ramp is a corporate card and spend management platform. The free plan includes unlimited virtual and physical cards, automatic receipt collection and matching, expenses completed by SMS or Slack, configurable approval workflows, QuickBooks Online and Xero sync, bill pay and travel booking. Plus ($15 per user a month plus a platform fee) adds reimbursement limits and weekly batched reimbursements, AI expense reviews, automated approvals, NetSuite and Sage Intacct sync and multi-entity support.

Strengths

  • Unlimited cards, receipt matching and approval workflows on the free plan
  • Receipts and expense details by SMS or Slack
  • 30-day trial of Plus

Watch-outs

  • Plus adds a platform fee based on team size that isn't published
  • The pricing page lists reimbursement controls from Plus, not Free
  • NetSuite and Sage Intacct sync need Plus

Read our full Ramp review →

3

Zoho Expense

Best value for small teams
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (3 users); 14-day trial.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $3/user/mo (Standard, billed annually ($4 monthly); Premium $5 ($6 monthly)).

Zoho Expense is expense reporting software from the Zoho suite. The free plan covers up to 3 users with 20 receipt scans per user a month, GPS mileage and accounting integrations. Standard ($3 per user a month billed annually) raises scans to 200 per user and adds corporate card management with Visa and Mastercard feeds, multiple expense policies, cash advances, ACH reimbursements and SSO. Premium ($5) adds travel booking, automated per diems, advanced approvals, budgets and multi-entity management.

Strengths

  • Free plan for 3 users with accounting integrations
  • Standard at $3 per user billed annually
  • Travel booking, per diems and multi-entity on Premium ($5)

Watch-outs

  • Free plan allows only 20 receipt scans per user a month
  • ERP and HRMS integrations are a paid add-on on Standard
  • Travel add-on billed at $9.99 per trip

Read our full Zoho Expense review →

4

BILL Spend & Expense

Best free option with budgets and cards
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at Free ($0 per user a month; standard card and payment fees apply where applicable).

BILL Spend & Expense (formerly Divvy) pairs the BILL Divvy Card with expense software at $0 per user a month. It includes as many physical and virtual cards as you need, budgets with spend controls, AI receipt capture and transaction coding, reimbursements, real-time transaction tracking, a mobile app, rewards and API access. Credit lines are advertised from $1,000 to $5 million, subject to approval.

Strengths

  • $0 per user, with unlimited physical and virtual cards
  • Budgets with spend controls for teams and projects
  • Sits next to BILL's AP plans ($49 to $89 per user) if you also need bill pay

Watch-outs

  • Credit line and card access depend on approval
  • The pricing page doesn't detail approval workflows or which accounting integrations are included

Read our full BILL Spend & Expense review →

5

Navan

Best for companies that travel a lot
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (travel, plus 5 expensing users).

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $15/user/mo (Expense, after the first 5 expensing users a month (free); companies up to 300 employees).

Navan combines corporate travel booking with expense management. On Navan Business (up to 300 employees) travel booking is free, and Expense is free for the first 5 expensing users a month, then $15 per user. Expense includes reimbursements, receipt scanning, Navan corporate cards or existing Visa, Mastercard and Amex cards through Navan Connect, out-of-policy flagging, per diems, budgets and integrations with NetSuite, QuickBooks Online, Xero, Sage and Datev.

Strengths

  • Free travel booking for companies up to 300 employees
  • Free Expense for the first 5 expensing users each month
  • Works with existing corporate cards through Navan Connect

Watch-outs

  • $15 per expensing user after the first 5
  • Companies over 300 employees need Navan Enterprise, by quote

FAQ

What are the best free expense management tools?

Brex (Free plan (Essentials)) ranks highest among the expense management tools we rank that offer a free plan. Ramp, Zoho Expense, BILL Spend & Expense also have free plans.

Is there a completely free option?

Yes, each tool on this page lists a free plan on its pricing page. Free plans have limits, shown in the table above, and paid plans add more users, usage and features.

What do free plans usually leave out?

Free plans typically cap users, records or monthly usage and leave out advanced automation, reporting and admin controls. Check each tool's limits against your team size before you commit.

When should I move to a paid plan?

When you hit a limit in the free plan or need a feature it leaves out. The table shows where each tool's paid plans start; our full expense management tools ranking compares the paid tiers.
