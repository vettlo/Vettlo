What is Jira?

Jira is Atlassian's issue and project tracker for software teams. The free plan covers 10 users and 2 GB of storage; Standard ($9.70 per user monthly) adds 250 GB and 1,700 automation rule runs a month; Premium ($19.60) adds advanced planning, a sandbox, unlimited storage, a 99.9% uptime SLA and 24/7 support.

Who it's best for

Engineering organizations with several teams that need configurable workflows, permissions and cross-team planning.

Why we rank it

It has the deepest workflow, permission and planning options here, and its free plan covers a full team of 10.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Free plan for 10 users
  • Advanced planning, sandbox and 99.9% uptime SLA on Premium
  • Annual pricing drops to $950 a year for 10 users on Standard

Watch-outs

  • List prices rose on Oct 13, 2026 (Standard $9.05 to $9.70 per user monthly)
  • Premium costs about twice Standard
  • More configuration than lighter tools

Skip it if: You're a small team that wants a fast, opinionated tracker with little setup.
