What is HubSpot Sales Hub?

HubSpot's CRM pairs a free contact and deal database with paid Sales Hub tiers that add sequences, forecasting and custom reporting.

Who it's best for

Inbound-led teams of roughly 5 to 100 reps who already use, or plan to use, HubSpot for marketing.

Why we rank it

It removes the hand-off gap between marketing and sales: lifecycle stages, attribution and deal records live in the same objects, so RevOps does not maintain a sync.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Shared database with marketing removes most sync work
  • Free CRM tier makes a low-risk start
  • Large integration marketplace

Watch-outs

  • Costs rise steeply between tiers and with seat count
  • Advanced reporting and custom objects sit in upper tiers

Skip it if: Teams with complex territory rules or heavy CPQ needs may outgrow the mid tiers.
