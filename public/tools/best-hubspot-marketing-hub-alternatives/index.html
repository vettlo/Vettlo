Short answer

The best HubSpot Marketing Hub alternative for most teams is ActiveCampaign (86/100). Unlimited automation actions from $49 a month at 1,000 contacts; attribution on Pro at $79 a month, with no onboarding fee.

  • ActiveCampaign (86/100): best overall alternative.
  • Brevo (82/100): best low-cost alternative.
  • Salesforce Marketing Cloud Account Engagement (76/100): best for Salesforce CRM users.
  • Customer.io (74/100): best for product-led companies.

HubSpot Marketing Hub is the platform many B2B teams start with, but the jump from Starter to Professional ($800 a month billed annually plus a $3,000 onboarding fee) sends many of them looking. These are the alternatives we'd shortlist, depending on whether you want lower prices, Salesforce-native scoring or event-driven journeys.

Why teams look for HubSpot Marketing Hub alternatives

  • The Professional price jump. Starter is priced per seat, but full workflows, lead scoring and custom reports start on Professional at $800 a month billed annually ($890 monthly).
  • Onboarding fees. Professional requires a $3,000 one-time onboarding fee and Enterprise $7,000.
  • Contacts and seats add up. Professional includes 2,000 marketing contacts and 3 seats; extra seats cost $45 a month.
  • Attribution needs Enterprise. Multi-touch revenue attribution and A/B testing are listed on Enterprise, from $3,600 a month.

ActiveCampaign is a marketing automation platform built around a visual automation builder with branching and multiple triggers. Starter ($15 a month at 1,000 contacts, billed yearly) caps automations at 5 actions each; Plus ($49) removes the cap and adds landing pages and sales automation; Pro ($79) adds split testing inside automations, predictive sending and attribution, with 3 users; Enterprise ($145) adds custom objects, custom reporting and 5 users.

What you gain over HubSpot Marketing Hub

  • Unlimited automation actions from $49 a month at 1,000 contacts
  • Attribution on Pro at $79 a month, with no onboarding fee

What you give up

  • Lead scoring is in the Pipelines add-on
  • Custom reporting is an add-on below Enterprise

Skip it if: You need built-in lead scoring and custom reports on one plan without add-ons.

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) combines email, SMS and WhatsApp marketing and prices by emails sent rather than contacts. Standard (from $18 a month for 5,000 emails) adds unlimited multi-step workflows, A/B testing, AI send-time optimization and a landing page, with up to 3 users. Professional (from $499 a month for 150,000 emails) adds contact scoring and 10 users. Yearly billing saves 10%.

What you gain over HubSpot Marketing Hub

  • Unlimited multi-step workflows and A/B testing from $18 a month
  • Priced by emails sent, not contacts

What you give up

  • Contact scoring only on Professional ($499 a month)
  • No CRM-connected attribution

Skip it if: You need lead scoring and B2B attribution on a mid-priced plan.

Account Engagement (formerly Pardot) is Salesforce's B2B marketing automation product, priced per org per month on an annual contract. Growth+ ($1,250, 10,000 contacts) includes lead nurturing and scoring, 50 forms and landing pages and 25,000 API calls a day. Plus+ ($2,750) adds AI-powered scoring, B2B Marketing Analytics with 5 licenses and unlimited forms and landing pages. Advanced+ ($4,400) adds 2 business units and a dedicated IP; Premium+ ($15,000) includes 75,000 contacts.

What you gain over HubSpot Marketing Hub

  • Lead scoring included from Growth+
  • B2B Marketing Analytics on Plus+

What you give up

  • $1,250 a month on an annual contract
  • No free trial or free plan

Skip it if: You don't use Salesforce, or your budget is under $1,000 a month.

HubSpot Marketing Hub alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to HubSpot Marketing Hub?

ActiveCampaign for most small and mid-sized teams: unlimited automation actions start at $49 a month and attribution at $79 for 1,000 contacts billed yearly, with no onboarding fee, against $800 a month plus $3,000 onboarding for HubSpot Professional.

What is cheaper than HubSpot Marketing Hub Professional?

ActiveCampaign Plus ($49 a month) and Pro ($79) at 1,000 contacts billed yearly, Brevo Standard ($18 a month for 5,000 emails) and Customer.io Essentials ($100 a month). Brevo Professional, with contact scoring, starts at $499 a month.

Which HubSpot alternative is best for Salesforce users?

Salesforce Marketing Cloud Account Engagement, which includes lead scoring from its $1,250-a-month Growth+ edition, billed annually.

Which HubSpot alternative has a free plan?

Brevo, whose free plan sends 300 emails a day. ActiveCampaign has a 14-day trial with no credit card.
