We scored seven tools on six weighted criteria. Pay attention to the plan each feature sits on: Gantt and timeline views, portfolios and automations are often one or two tiers above the entry price.

How to choose project management software

  • Find the plan with the views you need. Asana and ClickUp include timeline or Gantt views on their first paid plan; monday.com needs Standard and Trello needs Premium. Compare that plan's price, not the entry price.
  • Count who needs a paid seat. monday.com has free viewers, Smartsheet free Contributors, and Basecamp no per-user fees at all. That changes the cost for wide teams.
  • Check automation limits. Asana's automations are unlimited on Starter; monday.com caps automation actions per month by plan.
  • Pilot with a real project. Run one live project for two weeks with the people who'll use it daily, including someone who dislikes new tools.
  • Plan for portfolios later. If leadership will want a cross-project view, check which plan adds portfolios before you commit.

Project management FAQ

What is the best project management software?

Asana scores highest in our ranking for most teams, balancing structure, automation and ease of use. ClickUp is the best value if you want docs and whiteboards in the same tool, and monday.com is the easiest to roll out company-wide.

What is the best free project management software?

ClickUp's Free Forever plan has unlimited tasks, Trello's free plan covers 10 collaborators per Workspace, and Asana and monday.com have free plans for up to 2 users.

Which project management tools include Gantt charts?

Asana (Starter), ClickUp (first paid plan), Wrike (Team) and Smartsheet (Pro) include Gantt charts on their entry paid plans. monday.com includes them from Standard.

How much does project management software cost?

Paid plans on this list start between $5 (Trello) and $10.99 (Asana) per user per month billed annually. Features like portfolios and resource planning usually cost $19 to $25 per user or more.
