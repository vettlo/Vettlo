Short answer

The best Gong alternative for most teams is Avoma (82/100). Call scoring, deal risks and forecasting at published add-on prices; 14-day trial with every add-on enabled.

  • Avoma (82/100): best overall alternative with published pricing.
  • Sybill (80/100): best for automating CRM updates.
  • Fireflies.ai (77/100): best on a tight budget.

Gong sets the bar for conversation intelligence, but it's sold by quote with a platform fee on top of per-user licenses. Teams look for alternatives when they want published pricing, a smaller commitment, or mainly need call notes and CRM updates rather than a full revenue platform.

Why teams look for Gong alternatives

  • No public pricing. Gong prices per user plus a platform fee and only shares numbers through sales.
  • Platform fee on top of seats. For small teams, a fixed fee can make the cost per rep high.
  • More than the team needs. Many teams mostly want recordings, summaries and CRM notes, not forecasting and enablement.
  • No free trial listed. Teams that want to test on their own calls first look for tools with trials or free plans.

Gong alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Gong?

For most mid-market teams, Avoma: its add-ons cover call scoring, deal risks and forecasting at published prices. Teams that mainly want CRM updates should also try Sybill.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Gong?

Avoma, Sybill and Fireflies.ai all publish prices between $19 and $90 per seat per month. Gong doesn't publish prices, so compare against a written quote.

Does Gong have a free trial?

Gong's pricing page doesn't mention a free trial; it asks you to request a custom proposal.
