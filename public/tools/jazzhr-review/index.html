What is JazzHR?

JazzHR is an ATS for small businesses, sold on yearly plans. Hero ($1,000 a year) covers job postings, applicant tracking, email templates, premium job listings and a LinkedIn Recruiter integration. Plus ($3,480) adds up to 200 active jobs, AI candidate matching, structured interviews and assessments, task automation and standard reporting; Pro ($5,508) adds offers and e-signatures, advanced visual reporting and a custom report builder.

Who it's best for

Small businesses that fill a few roles a year and want the lowest yearly cost for an ATS.

Why we rank it

Hero costs about $83 a month billed yearly, the lowest flat price here.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • $1,000 a year on Hero
  • AI matching and structured interviews from Plus
  • 21-day trial

Watch-outs

  • Hero lists job postings starting at 3 a month
  • Offers and e-signatures need Pro ($5,508 a year)
  • Monthly prices aren't shown on the pricing page

Skip it if: You need offers, e-signatures and advanced reports without paying for the top plan.
