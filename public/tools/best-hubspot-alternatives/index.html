6 Best HubSpot Alternatives for Sales Teams in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamPublished Oct 14, 2026 · Prices verified Oct 2, 2026

We may earn a commission when you click some links on this page. It never changes which tools we include or how we rank them. See how we make money.

HubSpot Sales Hub is a strong CRM, especially when marketing already runs on HubSpot. Teams usually look elsewhere when they need sales automation without the jump to Professional pricing, want something simpler for reps, or need a phone-first or enterprise-grade tool. These are the alternatives we'd shortlist.

Why teams look for HubSpot alternatives

  • The jump to Professional. HubSpot lists email sequences and forecasting on Sales Hub Professional at $90 per seat per month plus a $1,500 one-time onboarding fee, against $7 for Starter.
  • Costs that grow with seats. Per-seat pricing across tiers adds up quickly as a sales team grows.
  • More CRM than the team needs. Small pipeline-first teams sometimes find HubSpot's breadth slows reps down.
  • Very complex processes. Large organizations with territories and multi-team rules may need deeper customization.

HubSpot alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to HubSpot Sales Hub?

For most small and mid-sized sales teams, Pipedrive: it lists sequences, automation and forecast reports on its $39 Growth plan, while HubSpot lists sequences and forecasting from Professional at $90 per seat plus onboarding. Enterprises with complex processes should look at Salesforce.

Is there a free alternative to HubSpot?

HubSpot itself has a free plan for up to 2 users. Zoho CRM's free edition covers up to 3 users and Attio's free plan covers up to 3 seats.

What is cheaper than HubSpot Sales Hub Professional?

Pipedrive Growth ($39/seat/mo), Freshsales Pro ($39/user/mo) and Zoho CRM Professional are all listed well below HubSpot Professional's $90 per seat, and none lists an onboarding fee. Check that each plan covers the workflows you need.

Should I leave HubSpot if my marketing runs on it?

Usually not. The shared database between marketing and sales is HubSpot's biggest advantage. Leaving means maintaining a sync between two systems.
