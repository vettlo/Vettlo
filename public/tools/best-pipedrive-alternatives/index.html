6 Best Pipedrive Alternatives for Sales Teams in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamPublished Oct 14, 2026 · Prices verified Oct 2, 2026

We may earn a commission when you click some links on this page. It never changes which tools we include or how we rank them. See how we make money.

Pipedrive is one of the easiest CRMs for a small, pipeline-first sales team to adopt. Teams usually start looking elsewhere when they need marketing and sales on one database, deeper reporting across several teams, or calling built into the CRM. These are the alternatives we'd shortlist for each of those reasons.

Why teams look for Pipedrive alternatives

  • You need marketing and sales on one database. Pipedrive's marketing features are add-ons rather than core, so inbound-led teams often end up syncing two systems.
  • Reporting needs outgrow the pipeline view. Pipedrive lists custom-data reports from its Premium plan, and multi-team forecasting is lighter than enterprise CRMs.
  • Your process gets complex. Deep account hierarchies, territories and granular permissions are where Pipedrive is weakest.
  • You want calling inside the CRM. Pipedrive's pricing page doesn't list built-in calling, so phone-heavy teams often add a separate dialer.

Pipedrive alternatives FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Pipedrive?

Pipedrive has no free plan. HubSpot's free plan covers up to 2 users, Zoho CRM's free edition covers up to 3 users, and Attio's free plan covers up to 3 seats.

What is cheaper than Pipedrive?

On entry list prices billed annually, Freshsales Growth ($9/user/mo) and HubSpot Sales Hub Starter ($7/seat/mo) start below Pipedrive Lite ($14). Zoho CRM Standard matches it at $14. Compare plan features too: entry plans differ in what they include.

Is HubSpot better than Pipedrive?

HubSpot is better if your marketing runs on HubSpot or you need deep custom reporting. Pipedrive is better value if you need sequences, automation and forecast reports without HubSpot Professional pricing. See our full HubSpot vs Pipedrive comparison.

Which Pipedrive alternative is best for enterprise teams?

Salesforce Sales Cloud, which scores highest in our CRM rankings for pipeline management, automation and reporting, though it needs admin resources and costs more in total.
