5 Best BambooHR Alternatives in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 1, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 1, 2026

Short answer

The best BambooHR alternative for most teams is Factorial (82/100). $8 per employee on the Business plan, about $160 a month for 20 people; time tracking and shift scheduling included.

  • Factorial (82/100): best overall alternative.
  • Zoho People (81/100): best for the lowest price.
  • Rippling (80/100): best for growing companies.
  • HiBob (78/100): best for mid-size international teams.
  • Connecteam (77/100): best for frontline teams.

BambooHR is our top-ranked HRIS, but its flat $250 a month minimum is steep for very small teams, performance reviews need Pro, and payroll is a US-only add-on. Teams look elsewhere when they want a lower price per employee, time tracking included, or HR, payroll and IT in one platform.

Why teams look for BambooHR alternatives

  • A $250 monthly minimum. Core costs a flat $250 a month for companies with up to 25 employees, even if you have 5.
  • Performance reviews cost more. 360° reviews and 1:1s start on Pro at $425 a month, or $17 per employee above 25.
  • Payroll is US-only. BambooHR Payroll is an add-on for US employees only, with no published price.
  • Time tracking is extra. Time & Attendance is sold separately from the HRIS plans.

Factorial is an all-in-one HR platform covering employee records, document management, time off with a shared leave calendar, time tracking, shift scheduling, onboarding, recruiting, performance management and payroll reporting. Its Business plan is listed at $8 per employee per month for companies up to 100 employees; a Starter plan for up to 10 employees and an Enterprise plan are priced by quote.

  • Far fewer G2 reviews (109)

Skip it if: You want every plan price published before you talk to sales.

Zoho People has a free plan for up to 5 users and four paid plans with a 5-user minimum. Free includes custom time-off policies; Essential HR adds onboarding and offboarding, the employee database, document management and HR reports; Professional adds attendance, timesheets and rostering; Premium adds performance, compensation and engagement; Enterprise adds an HR help desk and learning management.

What you gain over BambooHR

  • Free for up to 5 users; paid plans from $3 per user
  • 30-day free trial on every plan

What you give up

  • Performance needs Premium
  • More setup work

Skip it if: You want an HRIS a non-HR manager can set up in an afternoon.

BambooHR alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to BambooHR?

Factorial for most small and midsize teams: it lists $8 per employee on its Business plan and includes time tracking and shift scheduling, which BambooHR sells separately.

What is cheaper than BambooHR?

For 20 employees, BambooHR Core costs $250 a month. Factorial Business is about $160, Connecteam HR Basic is $29 billed yearly, and Zoho People is free for up to 5 users.

Which BambooHR alternative includes payroll?

Rippling sells payroll as a module on the same employee record, and HiBob offers US and UK payroll modules. Zoho People pairs with the separate Zoho Payroll.

Which BambooHR alternative is free?

Zoho People is free for up to 5 users, and Connecteam's Small Business plan is free for up to 10 users.
