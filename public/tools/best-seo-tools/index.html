Short answer

Semrush is our top pick among the 5 SEO tools we ranked, scoring 87/100. Paid plans start at $117.33/mo.

  • Semrush (87/100): best overall for keyword and competitor research.
  • SE Ranking (85/100): best value for daily rank tracking.
  • Ahrefs (81/100): best for backlink research.
  • Serpstat (75/100): best budget all-in-one for small teams.
  • Ubersuggest (72/100): best for beginners on a tight budget.

SEO tools show what a website can't see on its own: what people search for, where your pages rank, who links to your competitors and what stops your pages from being indexed. The all-in-one platforms here cover keyword research, rank tracking, site audits and backlinks, and each now offers AI search visibility tracking on at least some plans. The real differences are in the limits: how many keywords you can track and how often, how many pages an audit crawls, and how many users the price includes.

We scored five SEO platforms and one crawler on six weighted criteria and read each plan's limits from the vendor's pricing page. Entry plans run from $29 a month (the low end of Ubersuggest's listed range) and $50 (Serpstat Individual, billed annually) to $129 a month (Ahrefs Lite). Rank tracking varies the most: Semrush's SEO plan tracks 500 keywords daily, SE Ranking Core 2,000 daily and Ahrefs Lite 750 with weekly updates.

What are SEO tools?

SEO tools collect search data a website can't see on its own: how many people search for a keyword, which pages rank for it, which sites link to a competitor and what technical problems stop pages from being indexed. All-in-one platforms combine keyword research, rank tracking, site audits and backlink data, and most now also track how often a brand is named in AI answers from ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews.

Key features to look for

  • Keyword research. Search volume, difficulty and related questions for the terms buyers type.
  • Rank tracking. Daily or weekly positions for a set of tracked keywords, by device and location.
  • Site audit. A crawler that flags broken links, redirect chains, missing tags and slow pages.
  • Backlink and competitor data. Which sites link to a domain and which keywords send competitors traffic.
  • AI search visibility. Tracked prompts that show whether a brand is cited in AI assistants' answers.

How to choose SEO tools

  • Count the keywords you'll track, and how often. Semrush's SEO plan tracks 500 keywords daily, SE Ranking Core 2,000 daily, Ahrefs Lite 750 with weekly updates, and Serpstat Individual allows 10,000 position checks a month.
  • Check what the entry plan leaves out. Semrush's SEO plan has only basic keyword research, Ahrefs Lite caps usage at 1,000 credits per user, and Serpstat Individual has no API access or AI visibility.
  • Price the seats you need. Ahrefs Lite includes 1 user and up to 2 more at $40 a month each, Semrush charges from $45 per extra user, SE Ranking from $16 per extra manager seat, and Serpstat Team includes 3 users.
  • Decide how much AI search tracking you need now. Semrush tracks 50 prompts a day from Starter, SE Ranking 25 a day on Core, Ahrefs 5 prompts on Lite through Brand Radar, and Serpstat 1,500 checks a month from Team.
  • Audit your own site during the trial. Semrush and Serpstat offer 7-day trials and SE Ranking 14 days with no card. Ahrefs has no trial, but Ahrefs Free gives site owners limited Site Explorer and Site Audit access.

SEO tools FAQ

What is the best SEO tool?

Semrush scores highest in our ranking (87/100) for its keyword and competitor research, daily rank tracking and AI visibility tracking. SE Ranking (85/100) is the best value, tracking 2,000 keywords daily for $103.20 a month billed annually.

How much do SEO tools cost?

On Nov 26, 2026, entry plans with published prices ran from $50 a month (Serpstat Individual, billed annually) to $129 a month (Ahrefs Lite). Semrush's SEO plan is $117.33 a month billed annually or $139 monthly, SE Ranking Core is $103.20 billed annually, and Ubersuggest lists a range of $29 to $99 a month.

Is there a free SEO tool?

Semrush's free plan allows 10 reports a day and 1 demo project, Ahrefs Free gives site owners limited access to Site Explorer and Site Audit, and Screaming Frog's free version crawls up to 500 URLs per crawl.

Which SEO tools track AI search visibility?

All five ranked tools do. Semrush includes AI visibility reports on its SEO plan and 50 tracked prompts a day from Starter, SE Ranking tracks 25 prompts a day across 5 AI engines on Core, Ahrefs tracks 5 prompts on Lite through Brand Radar, Serpstat includes 1,500 checks a month from Team, and Ubersuggest covers ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews on every paid tier.
