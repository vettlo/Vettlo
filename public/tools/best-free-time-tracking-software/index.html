The free plans

1

Clockify

Best overall and best value for most teams
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (up to 5 users); 7-day trial, no card.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $3.99/user/mo (Basic, billed annually ($4.99 monthly); approvals and invoicing on Standard ($5.49)).

Clockify is a time tracker from CAKE.com with timers, timesheets, a kiosk and reports. The free plan covers up to 5 users with unlimited tracking and projects; Basic ($3.99 per seat billed annually) adds billable rates; Standard ($5.49) adds approvals, invoicing, time off and locked timesheets; Pro ($7.99) adds budgets, GPS, scheduling, expenses and labor cost; Enterprise ($11.99) adds SSO, SCIM and an audit log.

Strengths

  • Approvals, invoicing and locked timesheets from $5.49 a seat billed annually
  • Free plan for up to 5 users with unlimited projects
  • SSO, SCIM and audit log on Enterprise ($11.99)

Watch-outs

  • Budgets, GPS and activity monitoring need Pro ($7.99)
  • Every invited user counts as a paid seat
  • Free trial lasts 7 days, the shortest here

Toggl Track

Best for easy, timer-based tracking
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (up to 3 users); 30-day trial.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $9/user/mo (Starter, billed annually ($12 monthly); Premium $16 ($24 monthly)).

Toggl Track is a timer-first time tracker with manual and real-time timers, calendar integrations, tasks and reports; Toggl now sells it as one subscription with planning features. Free covers up to 3 users; Starter ($9 per user billed annually) adds billable rates, project estimates, team reports and time off; Premium ($16) adds timesheet approvals, labor costs, profitability reports, fixed-fee projects, Jira and Asana integrations and SSO.

Strengths

  • Timers and calendar integrations on the free plan
  • 30-day trial of paid plans
  • Rated 4.5/5 on G2 across 1,684 reviews

Watch-outs

  • Free plan stops at 3 users
  • Approvals and SSO need Premium ($16 annually, $24 monthly)
  • Time off management and capacity planning are add-ons ($2 and $5 a user)

Everhour

Best for tracking time inside Asana, Jira and other PM tools
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (up to 5 users, no integrations); 14-day trial, no card.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $8.50/user/mo (Team, billed yearly, 5-seat minimum ($10 monthly)).

Everhour is a time tracker that adds timers to project management tools such as Asana and Jira. The free plan covers up to 5 users with time tracking, projects and reports but no integrations or financial metrics; the Team plan ($8.50 per seat billed yearly, minimum 5 seats) adds native integrations, billing, budgets, invoicing, scheduling, time off, time approval and SSO.

Strengths

  • Every feature, including SSO, on the single Team plan
  • Rated 4.7/5 on G2
  • Asana and Jira integrations on the paid plan

Watch-outs

  • Team plan bills at least 5 seats ($510 a year)
  • Free plan has no integrations
  • Smallest G2 review base here (183 reviews)

Hubstaff

Best for remote teams that need activity tracking and payroll
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (1 seat); 14-day trial, no card.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $4.99/user/mo (Starter, billed annually ($7 monthly); approvals and payroll on Team ($10)).

Hubstaff is a time tracker for distributed teams with activity levels, screenshots, app and URL tracking, and payroll. Free covers 1 seat; Starter ($4.99 per seat billed annually) includes 500 screenshots per seat a month and client invoices; Grow ($7.50) adds project budgets, expenses and 1 integration; Team ($10) adds timesheet approvals, full payroll, scheduling, time off and unlimited screenshots and integrations; Enterprise adds SSO, SCIM and HIPAA and SOC 2 compliance.

Strengths

  • Activity levels and screenshots on every plan
  • Timesheet approvals, payroll and scheduling on Team ($10)
  • Largest G2 review base here (2,338 reviews)

Watch-outs

  • Free plan is limited to the owner's seat
  • Screenshots are capped at 500 or 1,500 per seat on Starter and Grow
  • SSO needs Enterprise

FAQ

What are the best free time tracking tools?

Clockify (Free plan (up to 5 users); 7-day trial, no card) ranks highest among the time tracking tools we rank that offer a free plan. Toggl Track, Everhour, Hubstaff also have free plans.

Is there a completely free option?

Yes, each tool on this page lists a free plan on its pricing page. Free plans have limits, shown in the table above, and paid plans add more users, usage and features.

What do free plans usually leave out?

Free plans typically cap users, records or monthly usage and leave out advanced automation, reporting and admin controls. Check each tool's limits against your team size before you commit.

When should I move to a paid plan?

When you hit a limit in the free plan or need a feature it leaves out. The table shows where each tool's paid plans start; our full time tracking tools ranking compares the paid tiers.
