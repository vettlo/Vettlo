Short answer

The best QuickBooks alternative for most teams is Xero: best alternative for unlimited users.

  • Xero (86/100): best alternative for unlimited users.
  • Zoho Books (86/100): best value alternative.
  • FreshBooks (79/100): best for freelancers and agencies.
  • Wave (75/100): best free alternative.
  • Sage Intacct (81/100): best for growing finance teams.

QuickBooks Online is our top-ranked accounting tool for US small businesses, but its regular prices are high and its entry plan covers a single user. Teams look elsewhere for unlimited users, a free plan, simpler invoicing, or software built for a bigger finance team.

Why teams look for QuickBooks alternatives

  • Price after the promotion. Regular prices run from $38 to $340 a month.
  • User limits. Simple Start includes 1 user, Essentials 3 and Plus 5.
  • More than a freelancer needs. Service businesses often want simpler invoicing and time billing.
  • Outgrowing it. Multi-entity companies often move to mid-market financial software.

The alternatives

1

Xero

Best alternative for unlimited users

Xero is cloud accounting with no per-user license fees. Early caps you at 20 invoices and 5 bills a month; Growing ($59) removes those caps and adds auto-reconcile; Established ($97) adds multi-currency, projects and expense claims.

What you gain over QuickBooks

  • No per-user fees on any plan
  • Unlimited invoices and bills from Growing ($59)

What you give up

  • Early caps invoices and bills
  • Projects and multi-currency on Established

Skip it if: You send more than 20 invoices a month but only want to pay entry-level prices.

Zoho Books is accounting software priced per organization. The free plan covers businesses under $50K in annual revenue; Standard adds bank feeds and sales tax tracking, and Professional ($40 billed annually) adds inventory, multi-currency and project profitability.

What you gain over QuickBooks

  • Free plan under $50K revenue
  • Inventory and multi-currency at $40 a month billed annually

What you give up

  • Fewer accountants know it
  • Steeper learning curve

Skip it if: Your accountant insists on QuickBooks.

QuickBooks alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to QuickBooks?

Xero if several people need access, and Zoho Books if you want the most features for the price.

Is there a free alternative to QuickBooks?

Wave's Starter plan is free, and Zoho Books is free for businesses under $50K in annual revenue.

What should I use when I outgrow QuickBooks?

Mid-sized companies with multiple entities often move to Sage Intacct, which is priced by module and sold by quote.
