What is Tableau?

Tableau, part of Salesforce, is a visual analytics platform sold per user by role. On the Standard edition, Creator ($75 a user a month, billed annually) builds workbooks and data flows with Tableau Desktop and Prep Builder, Explorer ($42) edits existing workbooks, and Viewer ($15) views and interacts with dashboards. Enterprise ($115, $70 and $35) adds Advanced Management, Data Management and up to 10 sites. Every deployment needs at least one Creator, and all plans require an annual contract.

Who it's best for

Analyst and data teams that build many custom visual dashboards for a large audience of viewers.

Why we rank it

It rates highest here on visualization, with Tableau Pulse metrics and the largest G2 review base in the category.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Rated 4.4/5 on G2 across 4,235 reviews, the most here
  • Creator includes Tableau Desktop, Prep Builder and Tableau Pulse
  • Free Desktop edition for analysis of local files and databases

Watch-outs

  • Creators cost $75 a user a month, over five times Power BI Pro
  • Annual contract only; no monthly plans
  • Data Management and Advanced Management need Enterprise ($115 per Creator)

Skip it if: You need a low-cost tool for a small team or want to pay month to month.
