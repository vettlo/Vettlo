monday work management runs projects on colorful, customizable boards. Timeline and Gantt views and 250 automation actions a month start on Standard ($12); Pro ($19) raises that to 25,000 and adds time tracking and private boards.

Who it's best for

Teams with many non-technical members who need a tool people will adopt without training.

  • Unlimited free viewers on paid plans

Watch-outs

  • Paid plans start from 3 seats
  • Automation actions are capped per month below Enterprise

Skip it if: You're a team of one or two who only needs a simple task board.

Trello is a kanban board tool from Atlassian. The free plan covers 10 boards and 10 collaborators per Workspace; Premium ($10) adds calendar, timeline, table, dashboard and map views.

Who it's best for

Small teams and individuals who want a visual task board with almost no learning curve.

  • Unlimited Power-Ups per board, even on Free

Watch-outs

  • Timeline and calendar views need Premium
  • Light on reporting and portfolio management

Skip it if: You manage dependencies, budgets or many projects at once.

ClickUp

Best all-in-one workspace on a budget
Visit website ↗
Starting price$9/user/moCore, billed yearly (shown against a $12 regular price)
Free trial / planFree Forever plan
Main score86/100
G2 user rating4.6/515,284 reviews

ClickUp combines tasks, docs, whiteboards, goals and time tracking in one workspace. Its paid plans are Core ($9) and Business ($17) per user billed yearly, plus Enterprise by quote. Gantt charts and native time tracking start on Core, and every plan includes a monthly allowance of ClickUp Brain AI credits.

Who it's best for

Teams that want one tool for projects, docs and lightweight wikis, and are willing to spend time setting it up.

Strengths

  • Gantt charts and time tracking on the first paid plan
  • Docs, whiteboards and goals in the same tool
  • Free Forever plan with unlimited tasks

Watch-outs

  • So many features that setup takes real effort
  • Core and Business prices are shown as discounts from higher regular prices; confirm your price at checkout
  • Extra AI credits are a paid add-on

Skip it if: Your team wants something simple that works on day one; try Trello or Asana.

      FAQ

      What are the best project management tools for small businesses?

      monday.com scores highest (86/100) once price and ease of use carry more weight, followed by Trello, ClickUp.

      How is this different from your main ranking?

      Same tools and the same 0 to 5 ratings, but price and ease of use count for more, because small teams rarely have an admin or a big budget. The weights are in the table on this page. Trello move into the top three as a result.

      Which project management tools are cheapest for a small team?

      See the starting prices in the comparison table; free plans and trials are listed next to them. Price the plan that covers your team size, not only the entry plan.

      What should a small business look for?

      A free plan or low entry price, quick setup without an admin, and the two or three features you need on day one. Avoid paying for enterprise controls you won't use yet.
