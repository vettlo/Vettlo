What is Shortcut?

Shortcut (formerly Clubhouse) is an issue tracker with stories, epics, iterations and docs. Free covers 10 users and 1 team; Team ($8.50 per user) allows up to 5 teams and adds automations; Business ($12) adds objectives and key results, the metrics dashboard and advanced custom fields, with SAML SSO as an add-on.

Who it's best for

Small and mid-size product teams that want iterations, epics and docs in one simple tool.

Why we rank it

Its free plan covers 10 users with iterations, docs and reports, and paid plans stay below Linear's.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Free plan for 10 users with iterations and docs
  • Free read-only Observer seats on paid plans
  • 14-day trial without a card

Watch-outs

  • Automations need Team; metrics dashboard needs Business
  • SAML SSO is an add-on on Business
  • No pro-rated refunds on cancellation

Skip it if: You need deep cross-team portfolio planning.

Shortcut pricing

Paid plans start at $8.50/user/mo (Team; Business $12; yearly billing saves up to 25%). Free plan or trial: Free plan (10 users); 14-day trial, no card.
