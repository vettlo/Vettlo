What is Jira Service Management?

Jira Service Management is Atlassian's service desk, now sold in its Service Collection together with Customer Service Management, Assets and Rovo AI. The Free plan covers 3 agents with request queues, workflows, alerts and on-call schedules. Standard ($21.50 per agent per month) adds asset and configuration management with 5,000 objects and Rovo agents; Premium ($55.28) adds change management, advanced incident and problem management, a virtual service agent and 50,000 asset objects.

Who it's best for

IT and engineering teams that already use Jira and want service requests, incidents and changes linked to development work.

Why we rank it

It has a free plan for 3 agents, includes asset and configuration management from Standard, and adds change and problem management on Premium at $55.28 per agent, against $99 for Freshservice Pro.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Free plan for 3 agents, with alerts and on-call schedules
  • Assets and configuration management from Standard, with 5,000 objects included
  • Change management, problem management and a virtual service agent on Premium at $55.28

Watch-outs

  • Change and problem management need Premium
  • Asset objects beyond the included amount cost $0.02 each per month
  • Virtual agent conversations beyond 1,000 a month cost $0.30 each

Skip it if: Nobody in your company uses Jira and you want problem and change management on a single published plan; SysAid includes both at $59 per agent.
