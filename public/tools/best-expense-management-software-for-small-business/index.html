Brex

Best overall for card-led spend control
Visit website ↗
Starting price$12/user/moPremium; Essentials is $0 per user, Enterprise by quote
Free trial / planFree plan (Essentials)
Main score94/100
G2 user rating4.8/51,657 reviews

Brex combines corporate cards with expense management, bill pay and travel booking. The free Essentials plan includes global card acceptance with one local card program, reimbursements, AI-powered custom rules, multi-level approval chains, accounting integrations and travel booking. Premium ($12 per user a month) adds multiple expense policies, live budgets, dynamic review chains, customizable ERP and HRIS integrations, VAT documentation and multi-entity support.

Who it's best for

Startups and mid-sized companies that want company cards, reimbursements and approvals in one system, including teams with entities abroad.

Strengths

  • Reimbursements and multi-level approvals on the free Essentials plan
  • Premium at $12 per user adds multiple policies, live budgets and multi-entity support
  • Rated 4.8/5 on G2

Watch-outs

  • Local card issuance in other countries needs the custom-priced Enterprise plan
  • Customizable ERP and HRIS integrations start on Premium, not Essentials

Skip it if: You only need employees to submit receipts for reimbursement and don't want a new corporate card.

Ramp

Best for automated receipt matching on a free plan
Visit website ↗
Starting price$15/user/moPlus, plus a platform fee based on team size; 20% off annual billing; Free plan $0
Free trial / planFree plan; 30-day trial of Plus
Main score92/100
G2 user rating4.8/52,572 reviews

Ramp is a corporate card and spend management platform. The free plan includes unlimited virtual and physical cards, automatic receipt collection and matching, expenses completed by SMS or Slack, configurable approval workflows, QuickBooks Online and Xero sync, bill pay and travel booking. Plus ($15 per user a month plus a platform fee) adds reimbursement limits and weekly batched reimbursements, AI expense reviews, automated approvals, NetSuite and Sage Intacct sync and multi-entity support.

Who it's best for

Companies that want employees on company cards with receipts collected automatically and little manual expense reporting.

Strengths

  • Unlimited cards, receipt matching and approval workflows on the free plan
  • Receipts and expense details by SMS or Slack
  • 30-day trial of Plus

Watch-outs

  • Plus adds a platform fee based on team size that isn't published
  • The pricing page lists reimbursement controls from Plus, not Free
  • NetSuite and Sage Intacct sync need Plus

Skip it if: Most of your spend is employees paying out of pocket and you want reimbursement tools without a paid plan.

3

Zoho Expense

Best value for small teams
Visit website ↗
Starting price$3/user/moStandard, billed annually ($4 monthly); Premium $5 ($6 monthly)
Free trial / planFree plan (3 users); 14-day trial
Main score86/100
G2 user rating4.5/51,598 reviews

Zoho Expense is expense reporting software from the Zoho suite. The free plan covers up to 3 users with 20 receipt scans per user a month, GPS mileage and accounting integrations. Standard ($3 per user a month billed annually) raises scans to 200 per user and adds corporate card management with Visa and Mastercard feeds, multiple expense policies, cash advances, ACH reimbursements and SSO. Premium ($5) adds travel booking, automated per diems, advanced approvals, budgets and multi-entity management.

Who it's best for

Small and mid-sized businesses that want receipt capture, policies and reimbursements at the lowest per-user price, especially if they use Zoho Books.

Strengths

  • Free plan for 3 users with accounting integrations
  • Standard at $3 per user billed annually
  • Travel booking, per diems and multi-entity on Premium ($5)

Watch-outs

  • Free plan allows only 20 receipt scans per user a month
  • ERP and HRMS integrations are a paid add-on on Standard
  • Travel add-on billed at $9.99 per trip

Skip it if: You want a corporate card program with spend limits as the center of your process.

BILL Spend & Expense

Best free option with budgets and cards
Visit website ↗
Starting priceFree$0 per user a month; standard card and payment fees apply where applicable
Free trial / planFree plan
Main score85/100
G2 user rating4.5/52,243 reviews

BILL Spend & Expense (formerly Divvy) pairs the BILL Divvy Card with expense software at $0 per user a month. It includes as many physical and virtual cards as you need, budgets with spend controls, AI receipt capture and transaction coding, reimbursements, real-time transaction tracking, a mobile app, rewards and API access. Credit lines are advertised from $1,000 to $5 million, subject to approval.

Who it's best for

Small and mid-sized businesses that want budgets and cards for each team or project without a software fee, especially those already using BILL for payables.

Strengths

  • $0 per user, with unlimited physical and virtual cards
  • Budgets with spend controls for teams and projects
  • Sits next to BILL's AP plans ($49 to $89 per user) if you also need bill pay

Watch-outs

  • Credit line and card access depend on approval
  • The pricing page doesn't detail approval workflows or which accounting integrations are included

Skip it if: You need multi-level approval chains and ERP sync spelled out before you buy.

Expensify

Best for receipt scanning and reimbursements
Visit website ↗
Starting price$5/member/moCollect, pay-per-use with no annual commitment; Control $18 billed annually ($36 pay-per-use)
Free trial / planFree Submit workspace for individuals
Main score89/100
G2 user rating4.5/55,690 reviews

Expensify is expense reporting software with its own Expensify Card. Collect ($5 per member a month, no annual commitment) includes unlimited receipt scanning and mileage tracking, expense approvals, third-party card feeds, next-day ACH and global reimbursement, QuickBooks Online and Xero sync, bill pay and Expensify Travel. Control ($18 per member a month on an annual subscription, or $36 pay-per-use) adds multi-level approvals, NetSuite and Sage Intacct, Workday and BambooHR, custom expense rules and SAML SSO.

Who it's best for

Companies where employees pay out of pocket and need receipts scanned and reimbursed quickly, from small teams to firms on NetSuite or Sage Intacct.

Strengths

  • Unlimited receipt scanning, mileage and global reimbursement from $5 a member
  • No annual commitment on Collect
  • Control drops to as low as $9 a member when most spend is on the Expensify Card

Watch-outs

  • Multi-level approvals and ERP integrations need Control at $18 to $36 a member
  • Control's annual plan charges $36 for each active member above your subscription size

Skip it if: You want unlimited free corporate cards with approvals built in; Brex and Ramp include them at $0.

Navan combines corporate travel booking with expense management. On Navan Business (up to 300 employees) travel booking is free, and Expense is free for the first 5 expensing users a month, then $15 per user. Expense includes reimbursements, receipt scanning, Navan corporate cards or existing Visa, Mastercard and Amex cards through Navan Connect, out-of-policy flagging, per diems, budgets and integrations with NetSuite, QuickBooks Online, Xero, Sage and Datev.

Who it's best for

Companies with frequent business travel that want trips and expenses in one place.

Strengths

  • Free travel booking for companies up to 300 employees
  • Free Expense for the first 5 expensing users each month
  • Works with existing corporate cards through Navan Connect

Watch-outs

  • $15 per expensing user after the first 5
  • Companies over 300 employees need Navan Enterprise, by quote

Skip it if: Your team rarely travels and you mainly need card controls.

FAQ

What are the best expense management tools for small businesses?

Brex scores highest (92/100) once price and ease of use carry more weight, followed by Ramp, Zoho Expense.

How is this different from your main ranking?

Same tools and the same 0 to 5 ratings, but price and ease of use count for more, because small teams rarely have an admin or a big budget. The weights are in the table on this page. Zoho Expense move into the top three as a result.

Which expense management tools are cheapest for a small team?

See the starting prices in the comparison table; free plans and trials are listed next to them. Price the plan that covers your team size, not only the entry plan.

What should a small business look for?

A free plan or low entry price, quick setup without an admin, and the two or three features you need on day one. Avoid paying for enterprise controls you won't use yet.
