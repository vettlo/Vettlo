What is Kaspr?

Kaspr offers unlimited B2B emails on paid plans with a yearly allowance of phone numbers and direct emails: 1,200 phone credits on Starter and 2,400 on Business ($79/user/mo annually).

Who it's best for

Small sales teams and recruiters who need a steady supply of phone numbers at a predictable price.

Why we rank it

Unlimited B2B emails on paid plans, published per-user prices and a free plan make costs easy to forecast.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Unlimited B2B emails on paid plans
  • Published per-user pricing in USD, EUR and GBP
  • Free plan with monthly email and phone credits

Watch-outs

  • Phone and direct email credits are capped per year
  • Enrichment and integrations are lighter than enterprise vendors

Skip it if: You need large-scale CRM enrichment or intent data.

Kaspr pricing

Paid plans start at $49/user/mo (Starter, billed annually ($65 monthly)). Free plan or trial: Free plan; no card needed.
