PandaDoc

Best for sales-led contracts and proposals

PandaDoc is a document and e-signature platform that now includes contract lifecycle management on its Business and Enterprise plans, rolling out gradually to accounts. Starter ($19 per seat billed annually) includes a contract repository and expiration alerts. Business ($49 per seat) adds AI data extraction, approval workflows, automatic reminders and expirations, CRM integrations and payments. AI renewal alerts are included on Enterprise, which is priced by quote.

Who it's best for

Sales and revenue teams whose contracts start as proposals or quotes and need to be tracked after signature.

Strengths

  • Repository from Starter; AI extraction and approvals on Business
  • Free plan and a 14-day trial
  • Unlimited documents and e-signatures from Starter

Watch-outs

  • CLM is rolling out gradually, so it may not be in your workspace yet
  • AI renewal alerts need Enterprise
  • Fewer legal-review tools than dedicated CLMs

Skip it if: Legal runs complex, multi-party negotiations with heavy redlining.

2

Zoho Contracts

Best free and low-cost option
Visit website ↗
Starting price$25/user/moStarting price billed annually, as stated by Zoho; lower-priced Lite users for approvers
Free trial / planFree plan (3 users, 10 contracts); 15-day trial
Main score77/100
G2 user rating4.6/514 reviews

Zoho Contracts covers authoring, approvals, online negotiation, e-signature through Zoho Sign, renewals and amendments. The free plan allows 3 users, 10 contracts, 5 counterparties and 1 approval workflow. Standard, which Zoho lists from $25 per user a month billed annually, has unlimited templates and approval workflows, 25 contracts per user a month and 500 counterparties. Professional removes those limits and adds obligations management, intake forms, audit logs and Zoho CRM and Docusign integrations. Lite user licenses, for people who only approve, collaborate or sign, cost less than full users.

Who it's best for

Small businesses, and companies already on Zoho, that want approvals and renewals without an enterprise contract.

Strengths

  • Free plan for 3 users
  • Lowest published price here, from $25 per user
  • Cheaper Lite licenses for approvers and signers

Watch-outs

  • Standard caps contracts at 25 per user a month and 500 counterparties
  • Obligations, intake forms and CRM integration need Professional
  • Only 14 G2 reviews

Skip it if: You handle large volumes of complex negotiated contracts.

ContractSafe

Best for a searchable repository with unlimited users

Free trial / planFree trial (length not listed)
Main score76/100

ContractSafe is contract management priced by the number of contracts stored, with unlimited users on every plan. Organize includes secure storage and search, unlimited reminders and tagging, AI data extraction, AI contract chat, custom fields, dashboards and user roles. Finalize adds redlining, approval workflows, e-signature, Word and Docusign integrations, Zapier and SSO. Maximize adds APIs, Salesforce, intake forms, templates, lifecycle tracking and AI contract review. Prices start at $450 a month billed annually and rise through volume tiers from 100 or fewer contracts to over 10,000; archived documents and attachments don't count.

Who it's best for

Finance, operations and legal teams that mainly need every signed contract in one place, with dates extracted and reminders set.

Strengths

  • Unlimited users on all plans
  • AI extraction and contract chat from Organize
  • Monthly billing available

Watch-outs

  • Redlining, approvals and e-signature need Finalize
  • Templates and intake forms need Maximize
  • Only the starting price is published; each tier's price depends on volume

Skip it if: You want to draft and negotiate contracts on the entry plan.

4

Docusign IAM

Best for teams already signing with Docusign
Visit website ↗
Starting price$45/user/moIAM Starter, annual plan billed monthly; Standard $50 and Professional $80, 3 users minimum
Free trial / planNo trial listed; 30-day refund on annual plans
Main score81/100
G2 user rating4.3/5503 reviews

Docusign IAM (Intelligent Agreement Management) adds AI agreement management to Docusign eSignature. Starter ($45 per user a month on an annual plan) includes eSignature Standard features, 1 workflow, AI search and Agreement Manager, with 100 sends per user per year. Standard ($50, 3 users minimum) allows 3 workflows and unlimited sending from the web app; Professional ($80) adds 10 workflows plus Business Pro features such as web forms, bulk send, conditional logic and SSO. Agreement Manager processes unlimited new documents plus a one-time 5,000 historical documents per user. AI-Assisted Review is on the quote-only Enterprise plan.

Who it's best for

Companies that already send contracts through Docusign and want AI search, extraction and workflows on top of it.

Strengths

  • Published prices from $45 per user
  • Unlimited web-app sending on Standard and Professional
  • 5,000 historical documents per user imported and analyzed (one-time)

Watch-outs

  • Starter caps sending at 100 per user per year
  • Redlining and Agreement Desk are sold through sales; AI-Assisted Review needs Enterprise
  • No trial listed

Skip it if: You negotiate most contracts in redlines and want drafting tools on the base plan.

Concord is contract management for routine agreements, priced per platform with 5 users included. Essentials ($499 a month paid annually) includes unlimited documents and e-signatures, AI Copilot and extraction, collaboration and negotiation, deadline reminders, amendment management and unlimited free viewers and guests. Business ($899) adds intake forms, approval workflows, custom roles and reports and integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Drive, Box, Dropbox and Zapier. FAQ

What are the best contract management tools for small businesses?

PandaDoc scores highest (80/100) once price and ease of use carry more weight, followed by Zoho Contracts, ContractSafe.

How is this different from your main ranking?

Same tools and the same 0 to 5 ratings, but price and ease of use count for more, because small teams rarely have an admin or a big budget. The weights are in the table on this page. PandaDoc, Zoho Contracts, ContractSafe move into the top three as a result.

Which contract management tools are cheapest for a small team?

See the starting prices in the comparison table; free plans and trials are listed next to them. Price the plan that covers your team size, not only the entry plan.

What should a small business look for?

A free plan or low entry price, quick setup without an admin, and the two or three features you need on day one. Avoid paying for enterprise controls you won't use yet.
