What is Salesloft?

Salesloft groups Cadence (sequences), Conversation Intelligence, Deals and Rhythm for account teams, plus Clari Forecast, Inspect and analytics for leaders. Pricing is only available from sales.

Who it's best for

Enterprise and mid-market sales orgs that want outreach, call recording and forecasting from one vendor.

Why we rank it

Its product line covers the rep's daily workflow and the leader's forecast in one place, which reduces the number of tools RevOps has to connect.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Sequences, call intelligence and forecasting from one vendor
  • Highly rated by users (4.5/5 on G2 across almost 10,000 reviews)
  • Built for manager coaching and inspection

Watch-outs

  • No public pricing
  • No built-in contact database
  • More platform than a small team needs

Skip it if: You only need email sequences and a lightweight dialer.

Add-ons (Forecasting, Account Agent, Coaching Agent, Dialer)AvailableAvailable
  • Salesloft's pricing page (salesloft.com/pricing) shows no plans or prices; it books a call with sales, and the help center points customers to their account representative for pricing.
  • Add-ons available with any package: Forecasting (forecast roll-up and submission with AI next-step recommendations for deals), Account Agent (buyer identification, account and person research agents), Coaching Agent (Sales Strategist Agent) and Dialer, priced in a separate Dialer Pricing Guide.
  • Calling from the Salesloft mobile app needs the Dialer add-on.
  • Legacy packages sold from February 2020 to August 2025 (Premier, Prospect, Sell, Engage, Enterprise, Essentials) are no longer sold; the legacy Essentials Conversations trial allowed 20 recordings per user over 6 months.
  • No free trial or minimum seat count is stated on Salesloft's pricing page or packages article.
