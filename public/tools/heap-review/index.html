What is Heap?

Heap, part of Contentsquare, is a product analytics tool that captures user interactions automatically, so you can define events after the fact instead of tagging every click in code. Free covers up to 10,000 sessions a month, 6 months of data history, 1 project and SSO. Growth adds the Sense AI assistant, unlimited users and reports, CSV exports and 12 months of history. Pro adds account analytics, an engagement matrix and report alerts, with session replay, heatmaps and the Heap Connect warehouse sync as add-ons. Premier adds a warehouse integration, unlimited projects, custom permissions, audit logs and SCIM.

Who it's best for

Teams that want answers from existing user behavior without engineers instrumenting events first.

Why we rank it

Automatic capture means you can analyze past behavior for events nobody planned, and SSO is included on every plan, even Free.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Captures interactions automatically, so events can be defined later
  • SSO on every plan, including Free
  • Unlimited users and reports from Growth

Watch-outs

  • No published prices for any paid plan
  • Session replay and the warehouse sync are add-ons below Premier
  • Free plan keeps 6 months of data

Skip it if: You need to see a price before talking to sales, or want replay included.
