5 Best Toggl Alternatives in 2026

Short answer

The best Toggl alternative for most teams is Clockify (89/100). Approvals, invoicing and time off from $5.49 a seat billed annually; free for up to 5 users.

  • Clockify (89/100): best overall alternative.
  • Everhour (85/100): best for Asana and Jira users.
  • Hubstaff (84/100): best for remote teams.
  • Harvest (83/100): best for invoicing clients.
  • Timely (78/100): best for automatic tracking.

Toggl Track has one of the easiest timers in the category, but teams often look elsewhere when they grow. Common reasons are the 3-user free plan, approvals and SSO that need Premium at $16 a user billed annually, and add-ons for time off and capacity planning.

Why teams look for Toggl alternatives

  • Small free plan. Toggl's free plan covers up to 3 users; Clockify and Everhour are free for 5, and TimeCamp has no user limit.
  • Approvals on Premium. Timesheet approvals, labor costs and profitability reports need Premium, at $16 a user billed annually or $24 monthly.
  • Paid add-ons. Time off management ($2 a user) and capacity management ($5 a user billed annually) are sold separately.
  • No automatic capture. Time is recorded with timers or calendar entries; tools like Timely draft timesheets from background activity.

Toggl alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Toggl Track?

Clockify for most teams: it's free for 5 users and includes approvals and invoicing on Standard at $5.49 a seat billed annually, against $16 for Toggl Premium. Timely is the pick if you want time captured automatically.

Is there a free alternative to Toggl?

Yes. TimeCamp's free plan has unlimited users and projects, and Clockify and Everhour are free for up to 5 users. Toggl's own free plan covers up to 3 users.

Which Toggl alternative is cheapest?

Clockify Basic and TimeCamp Starter both start at $3.99 a user a month billed annually. For approvals, Clockify Standard at $5.49 is the cheapest.
