OnPay

Best value for multi-state payroll
Visit website ↗
Starting price$49/mo + $6/personOne plan; HR add-on $15 + $2/worker, time tracking $15 + $4/worker
Free trial / planFirst month free

OnPay is full-service payroll with one base plan: $49 a month plus $6 per person paid. It includes unlimited pay runs, tax filings and payments in all 50 states, W-2s and 1099s, and integrations with no extra fee. HR tools, time tracking, compliance resources and live HR support are paid add-ons.

Who it's best for

Small businesses with employees or contractors in more than one state that want one simple price.

Strengths

  • Tax filing in all 50 states included at $49 + $6 per person
  • No per-pay-run or integration fees
  • Rated 4.8/5 on G2

Watch-outs

  • HR tools cost $15 + $2 per worker a month extra
  • Time tracking is a $15 + $4 per worker add-on
  • Mailed year-end forms cost $10 each

Skip it if: You want HR, time tracking and payroll bundled on one plan without add-ons.

Gusto

Best for small businesses that want payroll, benefits and core people tools in one place
Visit website ↗
Starting price$49/mo + $6/personSimple, single-state; Plus $80 + $12/person; Premium $180 + $22/person
Free trial / planFree to set up; you pay once you run payroll
Main score88/100
G2 user rating4.6/513,677 reviews

Gusto is full-service payroll with built-in HR and benefits administration. Simple ($49 a month plus $6 per person) covers single-state payroll, tax filing and core HR; Plus ($80 plus $12 per person) adds multi-state payroll, next-day direct deposit and time tracking; Premium ($180 plus $22 per person) adds access to HR experts. A Contractor plan costs $35 a month plus $6 per contractor.

Who it's best for

Small businesses that want payroll, health benefits administration and core HR handled by one tool.

Strengths

  • Payroll, core HR and health benefits administration in one product
  • Contractor-only plan at $35 a month plus $6 per contractor
  • Rated 4.6/5 on G2 across more than 13,000 reviews

Watch-outs

  • Simple is single-state; multi-state payroll needs Plus ($80 + $12 per person)
  • Next-day direct deposit is a Plus feature or a Simple add-on
  • Per-person fees double from Simple to Plus

Skip it if: You have employees in several states and want that on the cheapest plan; OnPay includes all 50 states for $49 + $6.

Patriot Payroll

Best for very small businesses on a tight budget
Visit website ↗
Starting price$37/mo + $5/workerFull Service Payroll; Basic (you file taxes) $17 + $4/worker
Free trial / plan30 days free, then 50% off for 3 months
Main score78/100
G2 user rating4.8/5630 reviews

Patriot Payroll comes in two plans. Basic ($17 a month plus $4 per worker) runs payroll and direct deposit while you file taxes yourself; Full Service ($37 plus $5 per worker) adds federal, state and local tax filings and deposits. Each additional state costs $12 a month, and time and attendance and HR software are add-ons at $6 a month plus $2 per employee each.

Who it's best for

Businesses with a few employees in one state that want the lowest per-worker price and don't need HR tools.

Strengths

  • $5 per worker on Full Service, $4 on Basic
  • 30-day free trial, then 50% off for 3 months
  • Rated 4.8/5 on G2

Watch-outs

  • Each additional state costs $12 a month
  • Basic doesn't file taxes for you
  • Time tracking and HR are separate add-ons

Skip it if: You have employees in several states or want benefits and HR in the same tool.

Square Payroll

Best for hourly, shift-based teams

  • Thin HR tools compared with Gusto or Rippling

FAQ

What are the best payroll tools for small businesses?

OnPay scores highest (88/100) once price and ease of use carry more weight, followed by Gusto, Patriot Payroll.

How is this different from your main ranking?

Same tools and the same 0 to 5 ratings, but price and ease of use count for more, because small teams rarely have an admin or a big budget. The weights are in the table on this page. Patriot Payroll move into the top three as a result.

Which payroll tools are cheapest for a small team?

See the starting prices in the comparison table; free plans and trials are listed next to them. Price the plan that covers your team size, not only the entry plan.

What should a small business look for?

A free plan or low entry price, quick setup without an admin, and the two or three features you need on day one. Avoid paying for enterprise controls you won't use yet.
