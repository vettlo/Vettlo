5 Best Tableau Alternatives in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 3, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 3, 2026

Short answer

The best Tableau alternative for most teams is Microsoft Power BI (92/100). Pro at $14 a user a month, against $75 for a Tableau Creator; Free Power BI Desktop for modeling and building reports.

  • Microsoft Power BI (92/100): best overall alternative.
  • Qlik Cloud Analytics (82/100): best for large audiences.
  • Metabase (81/100): best open-source alternative.
  • Zoho Analytics (80/100): best for small businesses.
  • Data Studio (78/100): best free alternative.

Tableau rates highest on visualization in our business intelligence ranking, but it's also among the most expensive. Teams usually look elsewhere because of the $75 Creator license, the annual-only contract, or the need to give a license to every viewer. These five tools cost less, price differently or are free to start.

Why teams look for Tableau alternatives

  • High Creator price. A Tableau Creator costs $75 a user a month on Standard and $115 on Enterprise, billed annually.
  • Annual contract only. All Tableau products require an annual contract, billed annually; there are no monthly plans.
  • A license for every viewer. Viewers cost $15 a month each on Standard, so large audiences add up; Qlik Standard and Domo don't charge per user.
  • Governance on Enterprise. Data Management and Advanced Management are on Tableau Enterprise, at $115 per Creator, $70 per Explorer and $35 per Viewer.

Tableau alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Tableau?

Microsoft Power BI for most teams: Pro costs $14 a user a month against $75 for a Tableau Creator, and it scores 92/100 in our ranking against 83/100 for Tableau. Qlik Cloud Analytics is the pick if you have many viewers.

Is there a free alternative to Tableau?

Yes. Data Studio (formerly Looker Studio) is free for creators and viewers, and Metabase's open-source edition is free to self-host with unlimited users. Tableau's own free Desktop edition can't connect to Tableau Cloud or Server.

Which Tableau alternative is cheapest for 10 people?

Data Studio's standard version is free. Among paid plans, Metabase Starter costs $1,405 a year for 10 users billed annually and Power BI Pro $1,680, against $3,240 for Tableau Standard with 2 Creators and 8 Viewers.
