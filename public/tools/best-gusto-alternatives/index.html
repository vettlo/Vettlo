Short answer

The best Gusto alternative for most teams is OnPay (87/100). Multi-state payroll in all 50 states for $49 a month plus $6 per person; rated 4.8/5 on G2.

  • OnPay (87/100): best overall alternative.
  • Square Payroll (79/100): best for hourly and shift-based teams.
  • Patriot Payroll (78/100): best for the lowest per-worker price.
  • Rippling (82/100): best for growing companies.
  • QuickBooks Payroll (77/100): best for QuickBooks users.

Gusto is our top-ranked payroll software, but its entry plan is single-state, and the per-person fee doubles when you move to Plus for multi-state payroll and next-day direct deposit. Teams look elsewhere when they pay people in several states, want a lower bill per employee, or need payroll, HR and benefits on one platform.

Why teams look for Gusto alternatives

  • Multi-state costs more. Simple covers one state; multi-state payroll needs Plus at $80 a month plus $12 per person.
  • Per-person fees climb by plan. Gusto charges $6 per person on Simple, $12 on Plus and $22 on Premium.
  • Next-day pay isn't on Simple. Next-day direct deposit is a Plus feature or a paid add-on on Simple.
  • Growing companies outgrow it. Teams adding global payroll, time tracking or deeper HR often look at an all-in-one platform.

Patriot Payroll comes in two plans. Basic ($17 a month plus $4 per worker) runs payroll and direct deposit while you file taxes yourself; Full Service ($37 plus $5 per worker) adds federal, state and local tax filings and deposits. Each additional state costs $12 a month, and time and attendance and HR software are add-ons at $6 a month plus $2 per employee each.

What you gain over Gusto

  • 30-day free trial

What you give up

  • Each additional state costs $12 a month
  • No built-in HR without the add-on

Skip it if: You have employees in several states or want benefits and HR in the same tool.

Gusto alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Gusto?

OnPay for most small businesses: it costs the same $49 a month plus $6 per person as Gusto Simple but includes payroll and tax filing in all 50 states.

What is cheaper than Gusto?

For 10 employees, Square Payroll costs $95 a month and Patriot Full Service $87, against $109 for Gusto Simple and $200 for Gusto Plus at published prices.

Which Gusto alternative is best for multi-state payroll?

OnPay and Square Payroll include multi-state payroll on their only full-service plan; Gusto needs Plus.

Which Gusto alternative is best for a growing company?

Rippling, which adds HRIS, benefits, time tracking and global payroll on the same platform, priced by quote from $8 per employee per month plus a base fee.
