What is Rootly?

Rootly sells incident response and on-call as separate products you can combine. Incident Response ($20 per user a month) runs incidents in Slack, Teams or Google Chat with the @Rootly AI agent, AI Scribe, AI Retrospectives, playbooks, action items and one external plus one internal status page. On-Call ($20 per user) adds paging by SMS, voice and push, alert routing, up to 20 schedules and escalation policies, and one live call routing number. AI SRE, which suggests probable root causes and drafts fixes, is priced on request.

Who it's best for

Teams that want AI to take notes and draft retrospectives, and that may buy response and on-call at different times.

Why we rank it

AI Scribe and AI Retrospectives are included on its entry Incident Response product, and you can buy on-call later without changing tools.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • AI Scribe and AI Retrospectives on the $20 Incident Response product
  • Works in Slack, Teams or Google Chat
  • Shadow rotations, PTO handling and an on-call pay calculator in On-Call

Watch-outs

  • No free plan
  • Response and on-call together cost $40 per user a month
  • Private incidents and custom incident types need Enterprise

Skip it if: You want a free start; PagerDuty, ilert and incident.io all have free plans with on-call.
