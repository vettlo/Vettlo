An applicant tracking system (ATS) is where hiring happens: you post a job to your careers page and job boards, applications land in a pipeline, and recruiters and hiring managers screen, schedule interviews, score candidates and send offers in one place. Vendors price in very different ways, which makes them hard to compare: per user (Manatal), a flat monthly fee with unlimited users (Breezy HR), a fee set by your company's employee count (Workable and Ashby), a yearly plan (JazzHR) or a custom quote (Greenhouse and Teamtailor).

We scored seven applicant tracking systems on six weighted criteria. Published entry prices run from $15 per user a month on Manatal Professional and $157 a month on Breezy HR Startup, both billed annually, to $299 a month for companies with 1 to 20 employees on Workable Standard and $400 a month on Ashby Foundations. None of the seven has a free plan; Workable, Breezy HR and Manatal list free trials of 14 to 15 days with no card needed.

What are applicant tracking systems?

An applicant tracking system (ATS) manages hiring from job post to offer. It publishes openings to a careers page and job boards, collects every application in a pipeline, and lets recruiters and hiring managers screen candidates, schedule interviews, share feedback and send offers, keeping a record of each step for reporting and compliance.

Key features to look for

  • Job posting and careers page. Publish openings to a branded careers site and job boards from one place.
  • Candidate pipeline. Stages for every role, with resume parsing, bulk actions and automations that move candidates on.
  • Interview scheduling. Calendar sync and self-scheduling links so candidates pick a time without email back-and-forth.
  • Scorecards and collaboration. Structured interview kits, scorecards and comments so hiring managers rate candidates the same way.
  • Offers and reporting. Offer letters and e-signatures, plus reports on time to hire, sources and EEO data.

How to choose an applicant tracking system

  • Work out what you'll pay at your size. Workable and Ashby set prices by employee count, Manatal by user, Breezy HR at a flat fee with unlimited users, and JazzHR by the year. Price the same team on each one.
  • Check job and candidate limits. Manatal Professional allows 15 jobs and 10,000 candidates, and JazzHR Plus lists up to 200 active jobs. Workable and Breezy HR list unlimited jobs or positions.
  • See where offers and e-signatures start. Workable includes offer letter templates and e-signatures on every plan; Breezy HR adds e-signatures on Growth and offer management on Business; JazzHR puts both on Pro.
  • Count AI as a line item. Breezy HR sells AI from $30 per 100,000 credits, Workable includes 3,000 credits and sells packs from $600, and Teamtailor lists unlimited AI usage in its plan.
  • Run one real role through the trial. Post a live job, invite a hiring manager and schedule two interviews. You'll see quickly how easy it is for people outside HR.

Applicant tracking FAQ

What is the best applicant tracking system?

Workable scores highest in our ranking (88/100). Its Standard plan, $299 a month for companies with 1 to 20 employees, includes posting to 200+ job boards, self-scheduling, e-signatures and a custom report builder. Ashby (86/100) is the pick for recruiting analytics, and Greenhouse (82/100) for structured hiring at larger companies.

How much does an applicant tracking system cost?

At published prices, Manatal starts at $15 per user a month billed annually, Breezy HR at $157 a month with unlimited users, JazzHR at $1,000 a year, Workable at $299 a month for 1 to 20 employees and Ashby at $400 a month for up to 100 employees. Greenhouse and Teamtailor quote every customer.

Is there a free applicant tracking system?

None of the seven ATS tools we ranked has a free plan. Zoho Recruit has a free plan with 1 active job. Workable offers a 15-day trial, and Breezy HR and Manatal 14-day trials, all with no credit card needed.

What's the difference between an ATS and an HRIS?

An ATS manages hiring, from job posts to offers; an HRIS manages people after they're hired, with records, time off and onboarding. Workable includes a basic HRIS on every plan, and BambooHR includes applicant tracking. See our HRIS software ranking for the employee side.
