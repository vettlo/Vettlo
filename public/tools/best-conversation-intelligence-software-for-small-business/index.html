Small teams that want every call recorded, transcribed and searchable for little money.

      FAQ

      What are the best conversation intelligence tools for small businesses?

      Fireflies.ai scores highest (83/100) once price and ease of use carry more weight, followed by Avoma, Sybill.

      How is this different from your main ranking?

      Same tools and the same 0 to 5 ratings, but price and ease of use count for more, because small teams rarely have an admin or a big budget. The weights are in the table on this page. Fireflies.ai move into the top three as a result.

      Which conversation intelligence tools are cheapest for a small team?

      See the starting prices in the comparison table; free plans and trials are listed next to them. Price the plan that covers your team size, not only the entry plan.

      What should a small business look for?

      A free plan or low entry price, quick setup without an admin, and the two or three features you need on day one. Avoid paying for enterprise controls you won't use yet.
