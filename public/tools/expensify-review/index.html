What is Expensify?

Expensify is expense reporting software with its own Expensify Card. Collect ($5 per member a month, no annual commitment) includes unlimited receipt scanning and mileage tracking, expense approvals, third-party card feeds, next-day ACH and global reimbursement, QuickBooks Online and Xero sync, bill pay and Expensify Travel. Control ($18 per member a month on an annual subscription, or $36 pay-per-use) adds multi-level approvals, NetSuite and Sage Intacct, Workday and BambooHR, custom expense rules and SAML SSO.

Who it's best for

Companies where employees pay out of pocket and need receipts scanned and reimbursed quickly, from small teams to firms on NetSuite or Sage Intacct.

Why we rank it

It covers receipt scanning, mileage and fast reimbursement on a $5 plan without a corporate card, rated 4.5/5 on G2 from 5,690 reviews.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Unlimited receipt scanning, mileage and global reimbursement from $5 a member
  • No annual commitment on Collect
  • Control drops to as low as $9 a member when most spend is on the Expensify Card

Watch-outs

  • Multi-level approvals and ERP integrations need Control at $18 to $36 a member
  • Control's annual plan charges $36 for each active member above your subscription size

Skip it if: You want unlimited free corporate cards with approvals built in; Brex and Ramp include them at $0.
