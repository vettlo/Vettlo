What is Nooks?

Nooks is an AI sales platform built around a parallel dialer. Its pricing page describes packages for buyer signals, AI sequencing, parallel dialing and coaching, plus an optional data package, all sold by custom quote.

Who it's best for

Outbound SDR teams that spend most of the day on cold calls and want dialing, coaching and signals in one tool.

Why we rank it

It pairs parallel dialing with coaching and buyer signals, and it holds the highest G2 rating on this list from a large review base.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Parallel dialing for high-volume cold calling
  • Coaching and buyer signals in the same platform
  • Rated 4.8/5 on G2 from 1,700+ reviews

Watch-outs

  • No public pricing
  • No free trial listed
  • More than an inbound or mixed-use team needs

Skip it if: Your reps make a few dozen calls a day and also take inbound calls.
