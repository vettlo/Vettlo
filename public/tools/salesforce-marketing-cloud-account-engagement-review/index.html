What is Salesforce Marketing Cloud Account Engagement?

Account Engagement (formerly Pardot) is Salesforce's B2B marketing automation product, priced per org per month on an annual contract. Growth+ ($1,250, 10,000 contacts) includes lead nurturing and scoring, 50 forms and landing pages and 25,000 API calls a day. Plus+ ($2,750) adds AI-powered scoring, B2B Marketing Analytics with 5 licenses and unlimited forms and landing pages. Advanced+ ($4,400) adds 2 business units and a dedicated IP; Premium+ ($15,000) includes 75,000 contacts.

Who it's best for

B2B companies with a Salesforce admin that need scoring and analytics tied to Salesforce opportunities.

Why we rank it

Lead scoring is included from the first edition, and Plus+ adds AI-powered scoring and B2B Marketing Analytics for pipeline reporting.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Lead nurturing and scoring included from Growth+
  • AI-powered scoring and B2B Marketing Analytics on Plus+
  • 10,000 contacts included on every edition below Premium+

Watch-outs

  • Starts at $15,000 a year on an annual contract
  • No free trial listed
  • Needs admin time to set up and run

Skip it if: You don't use Salesforce, or your budget is under $1,000 a month.
