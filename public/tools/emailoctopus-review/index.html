What is EmailOctopus?

EmailOctopus is an email marketing tool for newsletters, automated sequences, landing pages and signup forms, priced by subscribers with a monthly email allowance. The free Starter plan covers 2,500 subscribers and 10,000 emails a month; Pro removes EmailOctopus branding and adds unlimited users, landing pages and forms, smart send timing, gradual sending and reports kept forever. EmailOctopus Connect is a separate version that sends through your own Amazon SES account.

Who it's best for

Small businesses, nonprofits and newsletter senders that want a clean editor and simple automations at a low monthly price.

Why we rank it

It's the cheapest paid plan on this list at 1,000 subscribers, the free plan sends 10,000 emails a month to up to 2,500 subscribers, and Pro automations allow up to 650 steps with up to 3 triggers each.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Pro is $12 a month at 1,000 subscribers, billed yearly
  • Free plan covers 2,500 subscribers and 10,000 emails a month
  • Unlimited users with roles on Pro

Watch-outs

  • Up to 50 custom fields and 50 tags, even on Pro
  • No ecommerce flows or built-in CRM
  • A/B testing isn't listed among plan features on the pricing page

Skip it if: You need store-driven flows, lead scoring or a built-in sales CRM.
