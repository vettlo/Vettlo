What is Asana?

Asana organizes work into projects, tasks and goals. Starter adds timeline and Gantt views, dashboards and unlimited automations; Advanced ($24.99) adds portfolios, goals, resource management, approvals and time tracking.

Who it's best for

Marketing, operations and product teams that run many projects at once and need clear owners, dates and status.

Why we rank it

It balances structure and ease of use better than any tool here, and unlimited automations start on its first paid plan.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Unlimited automations from the Starter plan
  • Timeline and Gantt views on Starter
  • Portfolios and resource management on Advanced

Watch-outs

  • Free plan is limited to 2 users
  • Portfolios and time tracking need the $24.99 Advanced plan

Skip it if: You want a flexible all-in-one workspace with docs, whiteboards and chat; look at ClickUp.

Asana pricing

Paid plans start at $10.99/user/mo (Starter, billed annually ($13.49 monthly)). Free plan or trial: Free plan for up to 2 users.
