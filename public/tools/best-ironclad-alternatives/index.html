5 Best Ironclad Alternatives in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 5, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 5, 2026

Short answer

The best Ironclad alternative for most teams is Juro (82/100). Unlimited users, workflows and templates on every plan; business teams draft, negotiate and sign in one browser editor.

  • Juro (82/100): best for self-serve contracting.
  • Docusign IAM (81/100): best published price for e-signature-heavy teams.
  • PandaDoc (80/100): best for sales-led contracts.
  • Concord (79/100): best flat price for unlimited contracts.
  • ContractSafe (76/100): best for a company-wide repository.

Ironclad is our top-ranked contract management platform, but it publishes no prices, sells API access and obligation management as add-ons, and G2 reviewers report about 4 months to implement it. Teams look elsewhere when they want published prices, a faster setup, unlimited users or a tool closer to how their sales team already works.

Why teams look for Ironclad alternatives

  • No published prices. Every Ironclad plan is quoted; its own cost guide puts most mid-market CLM spend at $15,000 to $150,000 a year.
  • Add-ons on the order form. API access, obligation management, clickwrap and SAP or NetSuite integrations are sold as extras.
  • Implementation time. G2 reviewers report about 4 months to implement Ironclad, against 3 months for Juro.
  • More than a small team needs. Teams that mainly store contracts and track renewals can do that from $25 a user or $450 a month.

Juro is an AI contract automation platform where sales, HR and other business teams create, negotiate, approve and sign contracts in the browser while legal controls the templates. Every plan includes unlimited users, workflows and templates. Juro prices each plan on contract volume and integrations: standard integrations are included, while Salesforce, HubSpot and Workday integrations cost extra. AI Extract, AI Draft, AI Review and an MCP connection are options you select when requesting a plan, and quotes are available in USD, GBP or EUR.

  • Salesforce, HubSpot and Workday integrations cost extra

Docusign IAM

Best published price for e-signature-heavy teams
Visit website ↗
Starting price$45/user/moIAM Starter, annual plan billed monthly; Standard $50 and Professional $80, 3 users minimum
Free trial / planNo trial listed; 30-day refund on annual plans

Docusign IAM (Intelligent Agreement Management) adds AI agreement management to Docusign eSignature. Starter ($45 per user a month on an annual plan) includes eSignature Standard features, 1 workflow, AI search and Agreement Manager, with 100 sends per user per year. Standard ($50, 3 users minimum) allows 3 workflows and unlimited sending from the web app; Professional ($80) adds 10 workflows plus Business Pro features such as web forms, bulk send, conditional logic and SSO. Agreement Manager processes unlimited new documents plus a one-time 5,000 historical documents per user. AI-Assisted Review is on the quote-only Enterprise plan.

What you gain over Ironclad

  • Published prices from $45 per user a month
  • One-time import of 5,000 historical documents per user

What you give up

  • Redlining sold through sales; AI-Assisted Review needs Enterprise
  • Starter caps sending at 100 per user per year

Skip it if: You negotiate most contracts in redlines and want drafting tools on the base plan.

Concord is contract management for routine agreements, priced per platform with 5 users included. Essentials ($499 a month paid annually) includes unlimited documents and e-signatures, AI Copilot and extraction, collaboration and negotiation, deadline reminders, amendment management and unlimited free viewers and guests. Business ($899) adds intake forms, approval workflows, custom roles and reports and integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Drive, Box, Dropbox and Zapier. Enterprise ($1,299) adds a clause library, bulk send, an open API and custom branding. Extra users cost $49, $69 or $89 a month by plan.

What you give up

Ironclad alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Ironclad?

Juro (82/100 in our ranking) if business teams send most contracts themselves, and Docusign IAM (81/100) if you want published prices and already sign with Docusign.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Ironclad?

Zoho Contracts starts at $25 per user a month and has a free plan for 3 users. PandaDoc Business is $49 per seat, ContractSafe starts at $450 a month with unlimited users, and Concord at $499 a month for 5 users.

Which Ironclad alternative has unlimited users?

Juro and ContractSafe include unlimited users on every plan. Concord includes 5 users plus unlimited free viewers and guests.
