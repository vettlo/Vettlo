What is Avoma?

Avoma is an AI meeting assistant with optional Conversation Intelligence and Revenue Intelligence add-ons ($29 per seat per month each, billed annually). You only pay for users who record meetings; viewers are free.

Who it's best for

Growing sales and customer success teams that want call scoring and deal insights without an enterprise contract.

Why we rank it

Its add-ons cover AI call scoring with SPICED, Sandler and MEDDICC scorecards, deal risks and forecasting, all at published prices, and notes save to CRM records on every plan.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Published prices, including add-ons
  • Free viewer and collaborator seats
  • 14-day trial with every add-on enabled, no card needed

Watch-outs

  • Coaching and deal features cost extra on top of the base plan
  • Startup plan caps at 25 paid seats

Skip it if: You need a single vendor for engagement, forecasting and enablement at enterprise scale.

Avoma pricing
