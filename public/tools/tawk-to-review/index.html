What is tawk.to?

tawk.to is free live chat software with no limit on agents, chat volume or websites, plus a free knowledge base and ticketing. It makes money from optional add-ons: removing the "Powered by tawk.to" branding costs $29 a month, the AI Assist bot starts at $29 a month (with a free tier for low chat volume), and trained chat agents can be hired for $1 an hour.

Who it's best for

Small businesses, nonprofits and side projects that want website chat for any number of agents at no cost.

Why we rank it

It's the only tool here with no seat, chat or website limits on the free plan.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Free for unlimited agents, chats and sites
  • Free knowledge base and ticketing
  • Hired chat agents available for $1 an hour

Watch-outs

  • Removing tawk.to branding costs $29 a month
  • AI Assist is a paid add-on above a low-volume free tier
  • Fewer automation and reporting tools than paid platforms

Skip it if: You want AI and advanced routing built into one plan.
