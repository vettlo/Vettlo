What is Trello?

Trello is a kanban board tool from Atlassian. The free plan covers 10 boards and 10 collaborators per Workspace; Premium ($10) adds calendar, timeline, table, dashboard and map views.

Who it's best for

Small teams and individuals who want a visual task board with almost no learning curve.

Why we rank it

It's the simplest and cheapest way to get a team organized, with unlimited Power-Ups on every board.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Learn it in minutes
  • Cheapest paid plan on this list
  • Unlimited Power-Ups per board, even on Free

Watch-outs

  • Timeline and calendar views need Premium
  • Light on reporting and portfolio management

Skip it if: You manage dependencies, budgets or many projects at once.
