What is Reply.io?

Reply.io runs sequences across email and other channels. Its Multichannel plan is $89 per user per month billed annually ($99 monthly), and prices rise with active contact volume.

Who it's best for

SMB and mid-market outbound teams that want multichannel sequences without enterprise pricing.

Why we rank it

It covers multichannel sequencing at roughly half the price point teams usually expect from enterprise tools, with a free trial to test it.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Multichannel sequences on a published price
  • 14-day free trial
  • Monthly billing available

Watch-outs

  • Prices rise with active contacts
  • Less depth in coaching analytics than Outreach or Salesloft

Skip it if: You need enterprise governance across hundreds of reps.
