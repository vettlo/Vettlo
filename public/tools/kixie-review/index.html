What is Kixie?

Kixie is a calling and texting platform with bi-directional CRM integration on every plan (HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Pipedrive and Zapier). Its Multi-Line PowerDialer plan dials up to 4 lines at once, with unlimited US and Canada minutes.

Who it's best for

SMB sales teams that call and text from inside their CRM.

Why we rank it

It offers multi-line dialing, texting and two-way CRM sync on one plan, and you can try it for 7 days without a card.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Multi-line PowerDialer on up to 4 lines
  • Two-way CRM sync and texting on every plan
  • Unlimited US and Canada minutes

Watch-outs

  • Pricing page doesn't show prices
  • ConnectionBoost and Conversation Intelligence are add-ons

Skip it if: Your team calls mostly outside the US and Canada.
