What is Semrush?

Semrush is an SEO and marketing platform sold as separate toolkits. The SEO plan ($117.33 a month billed annually) covers 5 websites and 500 keywords tracked daily, with Site Audit, Position Tracking and AI visibility reports. Starter ($165.17) adds the full keyword research and competitor tools and 50 AI prompts tracked a day; Pro+ ($248.17) raises limits to 15 websites and 1,500 keywords and adds historical data, content optimization and cannibalization reports.

Who it's best for

In-house marketing teams and agencies that want keyword, competitor and AI search data in one subscription.

Why we rank it

It combines keyword and competitor research, daily rank tracking on every plan and AI visibility tracking across Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini in one subscription, with a free plan and a 7-day trial to test it.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • 500 keywords tracked daily on the SEO plan
  • 50 AI prompts tracked a day from Starter
  • Free plan with 10 reports a day

Watch-outs

  • The SEO plan has only basic keyword research; full tools start on Starter
  • Extra users from $45 a month each
  • Historical data needs Pro+ ($248.17 billed annually)

Skip it if: You only need a rank tracker or a crawler for one small site.
