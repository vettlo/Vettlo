What is Ironclad?

Ironclad is a contract lifecycle management platform built around a Workflow Designer for intake, roles and approvals, and a Repository with dashboards and Insights reporting. Its AI features include Ironclad Assistant, AI Playbooks, AI Clauses, conversational search and Smart Import for existing contracts, and Ironclad Signature handles e-signatures. API access, obligation management, clickwrap and SAP or NetSuite integrations are add-ons on the order form, and Jurist, a generative AI legal assistant, is sold per seat.

Who it's best for

In-house legal teams at mid-size and large companies that run many contract types through custom approval workflows.

Why we rank it

It has the most complete workflow builder and AI tooling here, from playbook-based review to bulk import of legacy contracts, with SAML SSO and e-signature included in the platform.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Workflow Designer for custom intake, roles and approval routing
  • AI Playbooks, AI Clauses and Smart Import for existing contracts
  • Built-in e-signature and SAML SSO

Watch-outs

  • No published prices; every plan is quoted
  • API access, obligation management and ERP integrations cost extra
  • G2 reviewers report about 4 months to implement

Skip it if: You're a small team that mainly needs a place to store signed contracts and get renewal reminders.
