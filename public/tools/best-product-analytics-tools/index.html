Short answer

Mixpanel is our top pick among the 7 product analytics tools we ranked, scoring 89/100. Paid plans start at $140/mo at 1.5M events.

  • Mixpanel (89/100): best overall for self-serve product analytics.
  • PostHog (88/100): best value, and best for engineering-led teams.
  • Amplitude (85/100): best for larger teams with deep analysis and governance needs.
  • Statsig (84/100): best for experimentation-first teams.
  • Heap (74/100): best for automatic event capture without tracking plans.

Product analytics tools record what people do inside your app or website, such as sign-ups, clicks, feature use and purchases, and turn those events into funnels, retention curves, paths and user cohorts. Every tool below covers those basics. They differ most in how they charge (by events, sessions or monthly active users), how much the free plan includes, whether session replay, feature flags and A/B tests come in the same product, and which plan first adds SAML single sign-on.

We scored seven product analytics tools on six weighted criteria. For each one we list the free plan, the entry paid price and billing unit, and the plan where SSO, data warehouse sync and experiment results start, using only what each vendor's own pricing page showed on Nov 9, 2026.

What are product analytics tools?

Product analytics tools record what users do inside a website or app as events, such as sign-ups, clicks, feature use and purchases, and turn them into funnels, retention curves, user paths and cohorts. Product, growth and engineering teams use them to see where users drop off, which features drive retention and how releases change behavior. Many now bundle session replay, feature flags and A/B testing on the same event data.

Key features to look for

  • Funnels and conversion. See how many users complete each step of a flow and where they drop off.
  • Retention and cohorts. Track whether users come back over days or weeks, and group them by behavior or properties.
  • Event tracking or autocapture. SDKs that send custom events, or automatic capture of clicks and page views to define events later.
  • Session replay, flags and experiments. Watch individual sessions, roll out features gradually and measure A/B tests on the same data.
  • Data governance and warehouse sync. Tracking plans, permissions, SSO, and connectors that send data to or from a data warehouse.

How to choose a product analytics tool

  • Price it at your real event volume. At 10M events a month, PostHog's product analytics costs about $324 and Mixpanel Growth $1,176 billed monthly ($840 billed yearly). Statsig Pro is $150 for 5M events plus $0.05 per 1,000 above that. Count events from your current tracking before you compare.
  • Know the billing unit. Mixpanel, Amplitude, PostHog and Statsig charge by events, LogRocket by sessions captured, Heap by sessions and Pendo by monthly active users. A busy app with few users is cheaper on MAU pricing; a large audience with light use is cheaper on events.
  • Find the plan with SSO. Heap includes SSO on every plan and LogRocket from Core ($83 a month). Mixpanel has SAML SSO and SCIM on Growth. PostHog sells SAML in its $750-a-month Scale add-on, and Amplitude (Growth) and Statsig (Enterprise) need a quote.
  • Decide whether you want replay, flags and A/B tests in the same tool. PostHog, Statsig, Amplitude and Mixpanel bundle all three. LogRocket focuses on replay, Pendo on in-app guides, and Heap sells replay as an add-on.
  • Check how long data is kept. Free plans keep 6 months (Heap), 1 year (PostHog, Statsig, Amplitude) or 2 years (Mixpanel). Statsig Pro and Amplitude Growth keep data without a time limit.

Product Analytics Tools FAQ

What is the best product analytics tool?

Mixpanel scores highest in our ranking at 89/100, for teams that want self-serve funnels, retention and cohorts with SAML SSO on its Growth plan. PostHog (88/100) is the best value and the best all-in-one for engineering-led teams, and Amplitude (85/100) suits larger organizations with deep governance needs.

Is there a free product analytics tool?

Yes, all seven tools here have a free plan. Amplitude and Statsig include 2M events a month, Mixpanel and PostHog 1M, Heap 10,000 sessions, LogRocket 1,000 sessions and Pendo 500 monthly active users. Statsig's free plan also includes 50,000 session replays a month.

How much does product analytics software cost?

It depends on volume. At 10M events a month, PostHog's product analytics costs about $324 and Mixpanel Growth $1,176 billed monthly or $840 billed yearly. Statsig Pro is $150 a month for 5M events, and LogRocket Core is $83 a month for 10,000 sessions billed annually. Heap and Pendo quote paid plans.

What's the difference between product analytics and web analytics?

Web analytics tools such as Google Analytics focus on traffic: where visitors come from, which pages they view and which campaigns convert. Product analytics tools track what signed-in users do inside your product, event by event, to measure activation, feature adoption and retention over weeks and months.
