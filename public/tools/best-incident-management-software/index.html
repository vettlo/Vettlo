Incident management software pages the right engineer when monitoring detects a problem, opens a channel in Slack or Microsoft Teams to coordinate the fix, keeps customers updated on a status page and runs the postmortem afterwards. Most tools price per user or responder a month, and the catch is that on-call and incident response are often separate line items: incident.io and Rootly sell on-call as an add-on, while PagerDuty sells status pages and AI as add-ons to its Professional plan.

We scored six incident management tools on six weighted criteria, using plan details from each vendor's pricing page on Nov 19, 2026. Four have a free plan: PagerDuty for 5 users, ilert for 5 users, FireHydrant for 10 responders without on-call, and incident.io Basic with single-team on-call. For on-call plus incident response, paid prices run from $21 per user on PagerDuty Professional (billed annually) to $40 per user for Rootly's Incident Response and On-Call products together. Atlassian stopped selling Opsgenie on June 4, 2025, and will shut it off on April 5, 2027.

What are incident management tools?

Incident management software helps engineering and operations teams respond when a service breaks. It receives alerts from monitoring tools, pages whoever is on call through push, SMS or phone, escalates if nobody acknowledges, and gives responders a shared place, usually a Slack or Teams channel, to coordinate the fix and update stakeholders. Afterwards it records a timeline for the postmortem. Most tools price per user or responder a month, sometimes with on-call, status pages or AI sold separately.

Key features to look for

  • On-call scheduling and escalation. Rotations, overrides and escalation policies that page the next person if an alert isn't acknowledged.
  • Alert routing and grouping. Alerts from monitoring tools routed to the right team, with duplicates grouped to cut noise.
  • Incident response in chat. Declaring an incident, assigning roles and posting updates from Slack or Microsoft Teams.
  • Status pages. Public or internal pages that tell customers and colleagues what is down and when it is fixed.
  • Postmortems. Timelines, action items and retrospective templates, increasingly drafted with AI.

How to choose incident management software

  • Add on-call and response together. Some tools sell them separately. A 10-person on-call team pays about $3,000 a year on incident.io Team with on-call (annual), $3,000 on FireHydrant Pro, $3,480 on Better Stack and $4,800 on Rootly with both products, before alert or SMS charges.
  • Count who needs a paid seat. Better Stack charges only responders and gives other team members free access; ilert Pro includes free viewer users; PagerDuty and incident.io price every user on the plan.
  • Check SMS and phone limits. PagerDuty Free and ilert Free cap SMS and voice at 100 notifications a month; Better Stack and ilert Pro include unlimited calls and texts; FireHydrant sells SMS and voice separately.
  • Price the status page. FireHydrant Pro includes unlimited public status pages, incident.io one on every plan below Enterprise, Better Stack with every responder license, and PagerDuty Professional sells Status Pages as an add-on.
  • Plan your Opsgenie exit. Opsgenie shuts off on April 5, 2027. Import your schedules and escalation policies into a trial of two tools and run one real page through each before you pick.

Incident Management FAQ

What is the best incident management software?

incident.io scores highest in our ranking (90/100): its free Basic plan includes single-team on-call, and Team with multi-team on-call costs $25 per user a month on annual billing. Rootly (85) is second, with AI Scribe and AI Retrospectives on its $20 Incident Response product.

Is there free incident management software?

Yes. PagerDuty Free covers 5 users with one schedule and 100 SMS or phone notifications a month, ilert Free covers 5 users, incident.io Basic includes single-team on-call, and FireHydrant's free plan covers 10 responders but without on-call.

How much does incident management software cost for a 10-person team?

For on-call plus incident response, about $2,520 a year on PagerDuty Professional (annual, before add-ons), $3,000 on incident.io Team with on-call or FireHydrant Pro, $3,480 on Better Stack and $4,800 on Rootly with both products, at Nov 19, 2026 list prices.

What replaces Opsgenie?

Atlassian stopped selling Opsgenie on June 4, 2025, and will shut it off on April 5, 2027, deleting data that hasn't been moved. Atlassian's path is Jira Service Management, whose free plan covers 3 agents with alerts and on-call; teams that want a standalone tool often move to PagerDuty or incident.io.
