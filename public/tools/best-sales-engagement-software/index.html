A sales engagement platform runs your outreach: multi-step sequences across email, phone and LinkedIn, the sending infrastructure behind them, and the reporting that tells managers what's working. Pick the wrong one and you'll see it in bounced domains, reps working outside the CRM and sequences nobody maintains.

We scored seven platforms on six weighted criteria. Two of the best-known enterprise options, Outreach and Salesloft, don't publish prices; we say so in the table rather than guess.

Apollo.io combines a B2B contact database with email sequencing, a dialer and deal management. Its pricing page lists 240 million contacts and 30 million companies.

Who it's best for

Startups and mid-market outbound teams that want prospect data and outreach in one place instead of buying them separately.

Why it made the shortlist

Few tools pair a large contact database with full sequencing at this price. Basic includes unlimited sequences, a deliverability suite with email warm-up, CRM integrations and a US dialer (credits apply).

Strengths

  • Contact data and sequences in one product
  • Free plan to start (2 sequences, 900 credits a year per seat)
  • Deliverability suite and warm-up on Basic

Watch-outs

  • Credits limit how many contacts you can reveal and enrich
  • Analytics and coaching are lighter than Outreach or Salesloft

Skip it if: Your team already pays for a separate data provider and needs enterprise governance more than prospecting.

How to choose a sales engagement platform

  • Decide where your contact data comes from. If you don't already pay for a data provider, a tool with a built-in database such as Apollo.io removes a second contract.
  • Protect your domain first. For cold outreach, check warm-up, inbox rotation and sending limits before features. A burned domain costs more than any license.
  • Match the platform to team size. Outreach and Salesloft pay off with many reps and a RevOps owner; smaller teams usually move faster with Apollo.io, Reply.io or lemlist.
  • Ask for the real price. Outreach and Salesloft don't publish prices. Get a written quote for your seat count and contract length before comparing.
  • Check CRM sync both ways. Activities, replies and meeting outcomes should land in the CRM automatically, or reps will stop logging them.

Sales engagement FAQ

What is the best sales engagement platform?

For most startups and mid-market teams, Apollo.io: it scores highest in our ranking because it pairs a large contact database with unlimited sequences on its $49 Basic plan. Enterprises that need governance and analytics across many reps usually shortlist Outreach and Salesloft.

How much do Outreach and Salesloft cost?

Neither publishes prices. Outreach lists four Amplify tiers with a 'Request pricing' button, and Salesloft directs buyers to talk to sales. Expect annual contracts quoted per seat.

What is the difference between sales engagement and cold email software?

Sales engagement platforms run multichannel sequences (email, calls, LinkedIn) tied to the CRM, with analytics for managers. Cold email tools such as Instantly focus on sending volume and deliverability across many inboxes.

Is there a free sales engagement platform?

Apollo.io has a free plan with 2 sequences and 900 credits per seat per year, and Mixmax has a free plan for 50 recipients a month. Most others offer 14-day trials.

Do I need a sales engagement platform if my CRM has sequences?

Not always. CRMs such as HubSpot Sales Hub Professional and Pipedrive Growth include email sequences. Dedicated platforms add multichannel steps, deliverability controls and deeper analytics.
