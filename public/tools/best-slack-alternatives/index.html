5 Best Slack Alternatives in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 7, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 7, 2026

Short answer

The best Slack alternative for most teams is Microsoft Teams (88/100). Chat plus 300-participant, 30-hour meetings from $4 per user; email and Office web apps from $7 with Business Basic.

  • Microsoft Teams (88/100): best value alternative.
  • Google Chat (79/100): best for Google Workspace teams.
  • Pumble (77/100): Cheapest Slack-style alternative.
  • Zulip (75/100): best for threaded, async teams.
  • Chanty (73/100): best for small teams on a budget.

Slack is our top-ranked team chat app, but it's often a second bill on top of Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, which already include chat. Teams also leave because the free plan keeps only 90 days of messages, or because SSO needs Business+ at $15 per user.

Why teams look for Slack alternatives

  • You may already pay for chat. Microsoft 365 includes Teams and Google Workspace includes Google Chat, so a Slack license can duplicate what you have.
  • 90-day free history. Slack's free plan keeps 90 days of messages; Pumble and Chanty keep unlimited history for free.
  • SSO costs $15 per user. SAML SSO and SCIM start on Business+, at $15 per user billed annually.
  • Price per user. Pumble Pro is $2.49 per seat and Chanty Business $3 per user, both billed annually, against $7.25 for Slack Pro.

Slack alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Slack?

Microsoft Teams for value: Teams Essentials is $4 per user paid yearly and includes 300-person meetings. If your company uses Gmail, Google Chat is included in every Google Workspace plan.

Is there a free Slack alternative with unlimited history?

Yes. Pumble's free plan keeps unlimited message history for unlimited users, and Chanty's free plan keeps unlimited history for up to 5 members.

Which Slack alternative is cheapest?

Pumble Pro at $2.49 per seat billed annually, followed by Chanty Business at $3 per user. Both are less than half of Slack Pro's $7.25.
