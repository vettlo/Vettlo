Short answer

Zendesk is our top pick among the 7 help desk tools we ranked, scoring 87/100. Paid plans start at $19/agent/mo.

  • Zendesk (87/100): best for growing teams that need every channel.
  • Zoho Desk (85/100): best value for multichannel support.
  • Freshdesk (83/100): best for teams that want AI and SLAs at a mid-range price.
  • Intercom (82/100): best for AI-first support and in-app messaging.
  • HubSpot Service Hub (78/100): best for teams already on HubSpot CRM.

A help desk turns customer emails, chats and calls into tickets your team can assign, track and answer on time. Most tools now sell AI agents that resolve tickets on their own, and that's where pricing gets harder to compare: the seat price is only part of the bill once AI resolutions are charged per outcome.

We scored seven help desks on six weighted criteria. For each one we list the entry price, the plan where live chat, SLAs and automation start, and how AI is billed.

Zendesk is a customer service platform. Support Team ($19 per agent billed annually) covers email ticketing, routing and automations; Suite Team ($55) adds AI agents, a knowledge base, live chat, messaging and telephony.

Who it's best for

Support teams that handle email, chat, social and phone at volume and expect to grow.

Why it made the shortlist

It's the most complete help desk here, with omnichannel routing, a large app marketplace and AI agents included from Suite Team.

Strengths

  • Every channel, from email to voice, in the Suite plans
  • AI agents included from Suite Team, with 5 to 10 automated resolutions per agent per month
  • Large integration marketplace

Watch-outs

  • Live chat and knowledge base need Suite Team ($55), not Support Team
  • Extra AI resolutions cost $1.50 to $2.00 each
  • More setup than lighter tools

Skip it if: You're a small team answering mostly email; a simpler shared inbox will do.

How to choose help desk software

  • Price the plan with your channels. Zendesk's live chat and knowledge base start on Suite Team, Zoho Desk's on Standard. Compare the plan that covers the channels you use.
  • Model AI costs at your volume. Intercom bills AI per outcome, Zendesk charges for resolutions above the included amount, and Help Scout charges $0.75 per resolution. Estimate monthly volume before comparing.
  • Check where SLAs start. Freshdesk includes multiple SLA policies on Pro; Intercom's SLAs are on Expert.
  • Watch for onboarding fees. HubSpot Service Hub Professional requires a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee.
  • Trial with real tickets. Forward a week of real customer email into the trial and see how fast agents find, assign and answer it.

Help desk FAQ

What is the best help desk software?

Zendesk scores highest in our ranking for teams that need every channel. Zoho Desk is the best value, and Freshdesk is a strong middle ground with AI and SLAs at a lower price.

What is the best free help desk software?

Zoho Desk has a free plan for 3 agents, Help Scout for 5 users with 1 inbox, and HubSpot offers free service tools for up to 2 users.

How much does Zendesk cost?

Zendesk Support Team is $19 per agent per month billed annually ($25 monthly). Suite Team, which adds AI agents, live chat and a knowledge base, is $55 billed annually ($69 monthly).

How do AI agents change help desk pricing?

Many vendors now charge per AI resolution on top of seats: Intercom from $0.99 per outcome, Help Scout $0.75 per resolution, and Zendesk $1.50 to $2.00 per resolution above the included amount.
