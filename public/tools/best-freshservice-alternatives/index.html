Short answer

The best Freshservice alternative for most teams is Jira Service Management (84/100). Free plan for 3 agents; change and problem management on Premium at $55.28, against $99 for Freshservice Pro.

  • Jira Service Management (84/100): best overall alternative.
  • SysAid (81/100): best for AI and ITIL on one plan.
  • InvGate Service Management (82/100): best flat-price alternative.
  • ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus (79/100): best free or on-premises alternative.
  • SolarWinds Service Desk (78/100): best for change management and a CMDB from the entry plan.

Freshservice is one of the most popular ITSM tools and ranks second in our shortlist, but problem and change management sit on its $99 Pro plan, and there's no free plan. Teams look elsewhere when they want change management for less, a free start for a small IT team, or AI included instead of a $29 add-on.

Why teams look for Freshservice alternatives

  • Change and problem management need Pro. Freshservice puts problem, change and release management on Pro at $99 per agent per month, billed annually.
  • No free plan. Freshservice offers a 14-day trial; Jira Service Management and ServiceDesk Plus have free plans for small teams.
  • AI Copilot costs extra. Freddy AI Copilot is a $29 per agent add-on, and Freddy AI Agent is included on Enterprise.
  • Assets are sold separately. IT asset management is bought in packs of 500 asset units.

Freshservice alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Freshservice?

Jira Service Management for most teams: it's free for 3 agents and puts change and problem management on Premium at $55.28 per agent, against $99 for Freshservice Pro.

Is there a free alternative to Freshservice?

Jira Service Management is free for up to 3 agents and ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus Standard for up to 5 technicians. Freshservice itself has a 14-day trial but no free plan.

Which Freshservice alternative is cheapest for change management?

SolarWinds Service Desk includes change management on Essentials at $39 per technician per month, billed annually. InvGate Pro costs $500 per agent per year, from $2,500.

Which Freshservice alternative includes AI?

SysAid Professional ($59 per agent) includes its Copilot and pre-built AI agents, and ServiceDesk Plus includes Zia AI in every edition.
