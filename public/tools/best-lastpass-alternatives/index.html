5 Best LastPass Alternatives in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Oct 30, 2026 · Prices verified Oct 30, 2026

Short answer

The best LastPass alternative for most teams is Bitwarden (90/100). SCIM and Directory Connector from $4 a user on Teams; sSO, policies and self-hosting for $6 on Enterprise.

  • Bitwarden (90/100): best overall alternative.
  • 1Password (88/100): best for ease of use.
  • Keeper (85/100): best low-cost alternative.
  • Proton Pass (82/100): best for cheap SSO.
  • Dashlane (83/100): best for phishing protection.

LastPass is still widely used, but the security incidents it disclosed in 2022 led many IT teams to review it, and its Business plan limits SSO to 3 apps unless you pay $11 a user for Business Max. Teams look elsewhere for a stronger track record, cheaper SSO or provisioning on a lower plan.

Why teams look for LastPass alternatives

  • Track record. LastPass disclosed security incidents in 2022, and many security reviews now ask about vendor history.
  • SSO is capped on Business. Business ($7 a user) includes SSO for up to 3 apps; unlimited apps need Business Max at $11.
  • Teams leaves out directory sync. Directory integrations and federated login start on Business, not on the $4 Teams plan.
  • Teams stops at 50 users. The Teams plan is for companies with fewer than 50 employees.

The alternatives

1

Bitwarden

Best overall alternative

Bitwarden is an open-source password manager with two business plans. Teams ($4 per user per month, billed annually) includes shared collections, event and audit logs, vault health reports, Directory Connector and SCIM provisioning. Enterprise ($6) adds passwordless SSO, enterprise policies, custom roles, account recovery, self-hosting and a free Families plan for every user. A free organization lets two people share.

What you gain over LastPass

  • SCIM and Directory Connector from $4 a user on Teams
  • SSO, policies and self-hosting for $6 on Enterprise

What you give up

  • SSO isn't on Teams
  • Staff Families plans need Enterprise

Skip it if: Your team is under 10 people and only needs shared vaults; 1Password's Teams Starter Pack costs $299.40 a year for up to 10 users.

1Password sells two self-serve business plans. Teams Starter Pack ($24.95 a month, billed annually, for up to 10 users) covers shared vaults, role-based permissions and 5 guest accounts. Business ($8.99 per user per month, billed annually) adds identity-provider integrations with Okta, Entra ID, OneLogin and Duo, unlock with SSO, automated provisioning, custom groups, reports, an audit trail, Watchtower alerts, 20 guests and a free 1Password Families membership for everyone in the company.

What you gain over LastPass

  • SSO unlock, provisioning and free Families accounts on Business
  • Teams Starter Pack: $299.40 a year for up to 10 users

What you give up

  • $8.99 a user on Business
  • Enterprise is quoted

Skip it if: Price is the deciding factor at 10+ users; Bitwarden Enterprise includes SSO for $6 a user.

Keeper

Best low-cost alternative

Keeper has three business plans billed annually. Business Starter ($2 per user per month) is for teams of 5 to 10 users with an encrypted vault for each user, an admin console and credential sharing. Business ($3.75) adds shared team folders, delegated administration, enforcement policies, security audit and activity reports, and a free Family Plan for every user. Enterprise, now quoted, adds SAML 2.0 SSO, SCIM and AD/LDAP sync, advanced 2FA and developer APIs. Advanced Reporting & Alerts and Compliance Reports are paid add-ons.

What you gain over LastPass

  • Business at $3.75 a user with shared folders and policies
  • Free Family Plan for every Business user

What you give up

  • SSO and SCIM need quoted Enterprise
  • Reporting add-ons cost extra

Skip it if: You need SSO or SCIM provisioning with a published price; Bitwarden and Proton Pass list both.

Pass Essentials ($1.99 per user per month, for 3 to 30 users) includes vault and item sharing, a built-in 2FA authenticator, Dark Web Monitoring, unlimited hide-my-email aliases and enforced 2FA. What you give up

  • 3-user minimum
  • Essentials has no activity log or team policies

Skip it if: You need deep reporting, delegated admin roles or a long enterprise track record.

Dashlane sells one self-serve password plan, Omnix Password Management (formerly Dashlane Business), at $8 per user per month billed annually. Omnix Credential Protection ($4) is a separate risk-detection product without a vault; Omnix Enterprise is custom-priced for 50 or more employees.

LastPass alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to LastPass?

Bitwarden for most businesses: SCIM provisioning costs $4 a user on Teams, and SSO with enterprise policies $6 on Enterprise, against $7 for LastPass Business with SSO for only 3 apps.

What is cheaper than LastPass?

For 10 users billed annually, Proton Pass Essentials costs $238.80 a year, Keeper Business Starter $240 and Keeper Business $450, against $480 for LastPass Teams and $840 for LastPass Business.

Which LastPass alternative is easiest to roll out?

1Password, which rates 5/5 on sharing and ease of use in our ranking and includes a free Families membership for every Business user.

Which LastPass alternative has the cheapest SSO?

Proton Pass Professional includes SSO and SCIM for $4.99 per user per month billed annually, and Bitwarden Enterprise for $6. LastPass Business costs $7 and limits SSO to 3 apps.
