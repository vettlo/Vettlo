What is Wrike?

Wrike is a work management platform for larger and more complex projects. Team ($10) includes interactive Gantt charts and dashboards; Business ($25, 5 to 200 users) adds workspace templates and standard integrations; resource and capacity planning and budgeting are on Pinnacle.

Who it's best for

Agencies and project management offices that juggle many projects, approvals and shared resources.

Why we rank it

It handles complex, multi-team projects well, with Gantt charts on its first paid plan and deep resource planning higher up.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Interactive Gantt charts from the Team plan
  • Strong resource and capacity planning on higher tiers
  • Free plan and 14-day trial with no card

Watch-outs

  • Business and higher plans are annual-only
  • Seats are sold in groups of 5 or more
  • Resource planning needs a sales-priced plan

Skip it if: You're a small team that wants a light, quick tool.

Wrike pricing

Paid plans start at $10/user/mo (Team, billed annually, 2 to 15 users). Free plan or trial: Free plan; 14-day trial.
