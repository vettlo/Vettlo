What is RUN Powered by ADP?

RUN Powered by ADP is ADP's payroll service for businesses with 1 to 49 employees. Essential covers payroll, direct deposit, tax filing, multi-state payroll and onboarding; Enhanced adds state unemployment management, job costing and labor law posters; Complete adds an HR HelpDesk and handbook wizard; HR Pro adds applicant tracking, training and legal assistance.

Who it's best for

Small businesses that expect to need HR help desk support, compliance services and add-ons such as workers' compensation from one large provider.

Why we rank it

It includes multi-state payroll on its entry package and offers the deepest HR and compliance services of any tool here.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Multi-state payroll and tax filing on the entry package
  • HR HelpDesk, handbook and legal assistance on higher packages
  • Rated 4.7/5 on G2 across more than 6,000 reviews

Watch-outs

  • No published prices; every package shows Get pricing
  • Time tracking, retirement and health insurance are add-ons
  • Promotional free months make the year-one cost hard to compare

Skip it if: You want to see the full price before you talk to sales.
