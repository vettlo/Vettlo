What is Zoho CRM?

Zoho CRM offers workflow automation, territory management and analytics at lower price points, inside the wider Zoho suite.

Who it's best for

SMB and mid-market teams that need more customization than entry-level CRMs but have a tight budget.

Why we rank it

It covers features usually reserved for upper tiers elsewhere, which makes it a strong value pick if your team can handle a denser interface.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Broad feature set for the price
  • Fits well with other Zoho apps
  • Customizable modules and workflows

Watch-outs

  • Interface feels dense to new users
  • Third-party integrations are thinner than HubSpot or Salesforce

Skip it if: Teams that prize a minimal interface may struggle with adoption.

Zoho CRM pricing
