What is Salesforce Sales Cloud?

Sales Cloud is the most customizable CRM on this list, with a deep ecosystem of apps, consultants and admins.

Who it's best for

Mid-market and enterprise organizations with multiple sales motions, territories and a dedicated admin team.

Why we rank it

When the process is complex, configurability matters more than out-of-the-box simplicity, and nothing on this list matches its depth.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Highly configurable objects, rules and permissions
  • Largest app and partner ecosystem
  • Scales to very large orgs

Watch-outs

  • Needs admin resources to configure and maintain
  • Total cost goes well beyond the license

Skip it if: Overkill for teams under about 20 reps without an admin.

Salesforce Sales Cloud pricing

Paid plans start at $25/user/mo (Starter Suite, monthly or annual). Free plan or trial: Free Suite + free trial.
