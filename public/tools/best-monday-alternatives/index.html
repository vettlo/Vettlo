Short answer

The best monday.com alternative for most teams is Asana (87/100). Unlimited automations and timeline view on the $10.99 Starter plan; portfolios, goals and workload at a published price on Advanced ($24.99).

  • Asana (87/100): best overall alternative.
  • ClickUp (86/100): best value for Gantt and time tracking.
  • Smartsheet (81/100): best for unlimited automations.
  • Wrike (79/100): best for small teams that need Gantt.
  • Trello (77/100): best cheap, simple alternative.

monday.com is the easiest project management tool on our list to roll out across a company, but its pricing is built around seats and tiers. Teams look elsewhere when the 3-seat minimum or the 3-board free plan doesn't fit, when 250 automation actions a month on Standard run out, or when they need portfolios and resource planning without an Enterprise quote.

Why teams look for monday.com alternatives

  • 3-seat minimum. Paid plans start from 3 seats, so a team of one or two pays for seats it doesn't use; the free plan stops at 2 seats and 3 boards.
  • Basic has no timeline or automations. Timeline and Gantt views and automations start on Standard at $12 per seat billed annually, not on the $9 Basic plan.
  • Automation caps. Standard includes 250 automation actions a month and Pro 25,000; Enterprise raises the limit to 250,000.
  • Time tracking and private boards cost more. Both start on Pro at $19 per seat billed annually.
  • Portfolios are Enterprise-only. Portfolio and resource management are listed only on Enterprise, which is priced by quote.

monday.com alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to monday.com?

Asana scores highest in our ranking (87/100 vs 85 for monday.com). Its $10.99 Starter plan includes timeline view and unlimited automations, which on monday.com need Standard ($12) with a 250-action monthly cap.

Is there a cheaper alternative to monday.com?

Trello Standard is $5 per user billed annually and ClickUp Core is $9, which includes Gantt charts and time tracking. On monday.com, Basic is $9 per seat with a 3-seat minimum, and time tracking needs Pro at $19.

Which monday.com alternative has the best free plan?

Trello's free plan covers 10 boards and 10 collaborators per Workspace, and ClickUp has a Free Forever plan. monday.com's free plan is limited to 2 seats and 3 boards.

Which alternative has portfolios without an Enterprise plan?

Asana Advanced ($24.99 per user billed annually) includes portfolios and goals. monday.com and Smartsheet list portfolios only on Enterprise.
