5 Best Apollo.io Alternatives in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamPublished Oct 14, 2026 · Prices verified Oct 14, 2026

We may earn a commission when you click some links on this page. It never changes which tools we include or how we rank them. See how we make money.

Apollo.io is popular because it bundles contact data with sequencing at a low price. Teams look elsewhere when credits run short, when deliverability for cold email becomes the bottleneck, or when the outbound team grows enough to need enterprise governance. These are the alternatives we'd shortlist.

Why teams look for Apollo.io alternatives

  • Credits run out. Apollo plans grant a fixed number of credits per seat per year, and revealing or enriching contacts uses them.
  • Cold email deliverability. High-volume senders often want inbox rotation across many accounts, which dedicated cold email tools price differently.
  • Enterprise governance. Large SDR teams may need stricter sequence governance, coaching and forecasting than Apollo focuses on.
  • LinkedIn and personalization. Some teams want LinkedIn steps and heavier personalization built into the sequence.

Instantly is a cold email platform built around sending volume. The Growth plan includes 5,000 emails and 1,000 uploaded contacts a month with unlimited connected email accounts; Hypergrowth raises that to 125,000 emails.

Apollo.io alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Apollo.io?

For high-volume cold email, Instantly. For personalized multichannel outreach, lemlist. For enterprise teams, Salesloft or Outreach. None of them includes a contact database as large as Apollo's, so budget for data separately.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Apollo.io?

Instantly's Growth plan is $37.60 per month billed annually as a flat plan rather than per seat, though it's email-focused and doesn't include Apollo's contact database.

Which Apollo alternative has a free plan?

Mixmax has a free plan for 50 recipients a month. Instantly lets you start free without a card; lemlist and Reply.io offer 14-day trials.
