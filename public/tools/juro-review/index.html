What is Juro?

Juro is an AI contract automation platform where sales, HR and other business teams create, negotiate, approve and sign contracts in the browser while legal controls the templates. Every plan includes unlimited users, workflows and templates. Juro prices each plan on contract volume and integrations: standard integrations are included, while Salesforce, HubSpot and Workday integrations cost extra. AI Extract, AI Draft, AI Review and an MCP connection are options you select when requesting a plan, and quotes are available in USD, GBP or EUR.

Who it's best for

Scaling companies where non-lawyers send routine contracts such as NDAs, sales agreements and offer letters from approved templates.

Why we rank it

It's built for self-serve contracting, with browser-based drafting and negotiation, unlimited users and templates on every plan, and a 4.5/5 G2 rating from 248 reviews.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Unlimited users, workflows and templates on every plan
  • Drafting, negotiation, approvals and signing in one browser editor
  • AI Extract, Draft and Review available

Watch-outs

  • No published prices
  • Salesforce, HubSpot and Workday integrations cost extra
  • No free trial listed
