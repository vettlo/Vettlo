Short answer

Apollo.io is our top pick among the 6 B2B contact data providers we ranked, scoring 86/100. Paid plans start at $49/seat/mo.

  • Apollo.io (86/100): best for data plus outreach on a budget.
  • ZoomInfo (83/100): best for enterprise go-to-market teams.
  • Lusha (81/100): best for simple self-serve contact lookups.
  • Cognism (77/100): best for verified mobile numbers.
  • Kaspr (75/100): best for small teams that need phone numbers.

Contact data is what your outbound team runs on: verified emails, direct dials and mobile numbers, plus company data to build lists and enrich the CRM. The hard part of comparing providers is pricing. Most sell credits, a phone number often costs several times more than an email, and the biggest vendors don't publish prices at all.

We scored six providers on six weighted criteria and spell out how each one's credits work, so you can compare the real cost of the contacts you need.

How to choose a B2B data provider

  • Count the contacts you actually need. Estimate monthly emails and phone numbers per rep, then compare plans on that basis, not on the headline price.
  • Check what a phone number costs. Lusha charges 5 credits for a phone versus 1 for an email; Kaspr caps phone credits per year. Phone-heavy teams should model this first.
  • Test with your own ICP. Ask for a free data sample or use the free plan on 50 accounts you know well, and check how many emails bounce and phones connect.
  • Ask the big vendors for a written quote. ZoomInfo and Cognism don't publish prices. Get quotes for the same seat count and credit volume before comparing.
  • Plan the CRM sync. Decide whether data enriches the CRM automatically or only when reps export, and who owns duplicates.

B2B data providers FAQ

What is the best B2B data provider?

For most startups and mid-market teams, Apollo.io: it scores highest in our ranking because it pairs a large database with outreach, a free plan and a $49 Basic plan. Enterprises running account-based programs usually shortlist ZoomInfo.

How much does ZoomInfo cost?

ZoomInfo doesn't publish prices. Its pricing page says cost depends on features, number of licenses and credit usage, and offers a free trial of its sales editions.

Which B2B data provider has a free plan?

Apollo.io, Lusha (40 credits a month) and Kaspr all have free plans. UpLead offers a 7-day trial with 5 credits.

Which provider is best for mobile numbers?

Cognism focuses on phone-verified mobile numbers and offers on-demand verification on its Pro plan. For self-serve options, compare Lusha's 5-credit phone reveals with Kaspr's yearly phone allowance.

Do unused credits roll over?

It depends on the vendor and billing cycle. Lusha's monthly plans roll unused credits over up to twice the limit, while its annual plans reset unused credits at the end of the year.
