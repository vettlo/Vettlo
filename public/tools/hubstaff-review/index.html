What is Hubstaff?

Hubstaff is a time tracker for distributed teams with activity levels, screenshots, app and URL tracking, and payroll. Free covers 1 seat; Starter ($4.99 per seat billed annually) includes 500 screenshots per seat a month and client invoices; Grow ($7.50) adds project budgets, expenses and 1 integration; Team ($10) adds timesheet approvals, full payroll, scheduling, time off and unlimited screenshots and integrations; Enterprise adds SSO, SCIM and HIPAA and SOC 2 compliance.

Who it's best for

Remote and outsourced teams paid by the hour that need proof of work, scheduling and payroll in one tool.

Why we rank it

It goes furthest on activity tracking and payroll, with approvals, scheduling and payments on the $10 Team plan.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Activity levels and screenshots on every plan
  • Timesheet approvals, payroll and scheduling on Team ($10)
  • Largest G2 review base here (2,338 reviews)

Watch-outs

  • Free plan is limited to the owner's seat
  • Screenshots are capped at 500 or 1,500 per seat on Starter and Grow
  • SSO needs Enterprise

Skip it if: Your team would see screenshots and activity tracking as surveillance.

Hubstaff pricing

Paid plans start at $4.99/user/mo (Starter, billed annually ($7 monthly); approvals and payroll on Team ($10)). Free plan or trial: Free plan (1 seat); 14-day trial, no card.
