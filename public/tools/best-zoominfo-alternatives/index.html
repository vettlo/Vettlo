5 Best ZoomInfo Alternatives in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamPublished Oct 14, 2026 · Prices verified Oct 14, 2026

We may earn a commission when you click some links on this page. It never changes which tools we include or how we rank them. See how we make money.

ZoomInfo is the broadest B2B data platform, but it's sold by quote and built for large go-to-market teams. Teams look elsewhere when they want published pricing, a smaller commitment, or better mobile numbers for a specific motion. These are the alternatives we'd shortlist.

Why teams look for ZoomInfo alternatives

  • No public pricing. ZoomInfo prices by features, licenses and credits, and only shares numbers through sales.
  • More platform than needed. Small and mid-sized teams often use a fraction of the sales, marketing and talent products.
  • Mobile number quality for phone-heavy teams. Teams that live on the phone often compare providers specifically on verified mobiles.
  • Self-serve buying. Some teams want to start on a free plan and upgrade without a sales cycle.

ZoomInfo alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to ZoomInfo?

For most teams, Apollo.io: it publishes prices, has a free plan and includes outreach. Phone-heavy teams should also trial Cognism for verified mobile numbers.

Is there a cheaper alternative to ZoomInfo?

Lusha, Kaspr, UpLead and Apollo.io all publish prices starting between about $37 and $74 a month. ZoomInfo doesn't publish prices, so compare against a written quote.

Which ZoomInfo alternative has a free plan?

Apollo.io, Lusha and Kaspr have free plans. UpLead offers a 7-day trial and Cognism offers a free data sample.
