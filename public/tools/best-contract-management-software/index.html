Short answer

Ironclad is our top pick among the 7 contract management tools we ranked, scoring 84/100.

  • Ironclad (84/100): best overall for in-house legal teams.
  • Juro (82/100): best for business teams that self-serve contracts.
  • Docusign IAM (81/100): best for teams already signing with Docusign.
  • PandaDoc (80/100): best for sales-led contracts and proposals.
  • Concord (79/100): best for unlimited contracts at a flat team price.

Contract management software, often called CLM (contract lifecycle management), takes a contract from request to renewal: intake, drafting from templates, redlining with the other side, internal approvals, signature, and then a searchable repository that reminds you before a renewal or notice date. The tools here all store and track contracts; they differ in how much of the drafting and approval work they handle, how much of it AI does, and how they charge.

Pricing is the hardest part to compare. Some vendors charge per user, some charge a platform fee that includes a set number of users, some price by how many contracts you store, and the two enterprise-focused tools here publish no prices at all. We scored seven tools on six weighted criteria and list, for each, the entry price, what it is billed on, and the plan where approval workflows start.

What is contract management software?

Contract management software, also called contract lifecycle management (CLM), handles a contract from the first request to renewal. Teams request and draft contracts from approved templates, negotiate redlines, route them for internal approval and signature, then store them in a searchable repository that tracks key dates, obligations and renewals.

Key features to look for

  • Intake and templates. Request forms and approved templates so routine contracts start the same way every time.
  • Redlining and negotiation. Edit, comment and track changes with the other side, with version history.
  • Approval workflows. Route contracts to legal, finance or managers based on value, type or clause changes.
  • Repository and AI extraction. Searchable storage that pulls out parties, dates, values and terms automatically.
  • Renewal and obligation alerts. Reminders before renewal, notice and expiry dates, and tracking of what each party owes.

How to choose contract management software

  • Count your contracts as well as your users. ContractSafe prices by contracts stored, from 100 or fewer to over 10,000; Zoho Contracts Standard allows 25 contracts per user a month; Concord and PandaDoc list unlimited documents.
  • Decide who needs a full seat. Juro and ContractSafe include unlimited users, Concord includes unlimited free viewers and guests, and Zoho sells cheaper Lite licenses for people who only approve or sign. Docusign IAM and PandaDoc charge for every user.
  • Find the plan where approvals start. Approval workflows are on Zoho Contracts Standard, PandaDoc Business ($49 a seat), Concord Business ($899 a month) and ContractSafe Finalize.
  • Plan the import of existing contracts. Docusign IAM includes a one-time 5,000 historical documents per user, Ironclad has Smart Import, and ContractSafe extracts dates and terms with AI on its entry plan. Ask each vendor whether migration is included.
  • Ask how long setup takes. G2 reviewers report about 4 months to implement Ironclad and 3 months for Juro. Ask for a project plan before you sign, and test with three real contract types.

Contract management FAQ

What is the best contract management software?

Ironclad scores highest in our ranking (84/100) for its workflow designer, AI review and import tools, but it's quote-only. Juro (82) is best for self-serve contracting by business teams, and Docusign IAM (81) has the best e-signature tools with published prices from $45 per user.

How much does contract management software cost?

Published prices start at $25 per user a month for Zoho Contracts, $49 per seat for PandaDoc Business and $45 to $80 per user for Docusign IAM. Concord starts at $499 a month for 5 users ($5,988 a year) and ContractSafe at $450 a month with unlimited users. Ironclad's own cost guide says most mid-market teams spend $15,000 to $150,000 a year, plus one-time implementation fees of $5,000 to $100,000 or more.

Is there free contract management software?

Zoho Contracts has a free plan for 3 users, 10 contracts, 5 counterparties and 1 approval workflow, and a 15-day trial of paid features. PandaDoc's free plan sends 5 documents a month, but its CLM features start on Business at $49 a seat.

What's the difference between contract management and e-signature software?

E-signature software gets a finished document signed. Contract management also covers intake, drafting from templates, redlining, approvals and a repository that tracks renewal and notice dates. Docusign IAM Starter, for example, costs $45 per user a month against $30 for Docusign eSignature Standard.
