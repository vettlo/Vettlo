What is Lusha?

Lusha is a self-serve contact data tool with a Chrome extension and CRM integrations. A verified email costs 1 credit and a phone number costs 5 credits.

Who it's best for

SMB and mid-market reps who want to look up contacts quickly without a sales-led contract.

Why we rank it

Transparent, self-serve plans and a permanent free plan make it easy to start, and its credit costs are spelled out on the pricing page.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Permanent free plan with 40 credits a month
  • Clear credit costs: 1 per email, 5 per phone
  • Premium plan includes 5 free seats and team credit allocation

Watch-outs

  • Phone numbers use credits five times faster than emails
  • Unused annual credits reset at the end of the year

Skip it if: You need millions of records for bulk enrichment; look at the Scale plan or enterprise vendors.

Lusha pricing

Paid plans start at $37.45/mo (Starter, billed yearly, 4,800 credits per year, 1 seat). Free plan or trial: Free plan (40 credits a month).

  • Credit costs are the same on every plan: a verified email is 1 credit, a phone number 5, company information 1, a CRM export 1 per record written, and an API request 1 credit per up to 25 results plus the data revealed. Data Waterfall matches cost 2 credits per email and 8 per phone, charged only on a confirmed match.
  • Monthly plans roll unused credits over up to twice the plan's monthly limit; annual plans issue all credits up front and reset unused credits at the end of the year. Extra credits can be bought at any time.
  • Plans auto-renew at the price in effect at renewal; cancel at least 14 days before renewal. Seats on Pro and Premium can be added but not reduced, and monthly plans can be frozen for one or two months up to twice a year.
  • Lusha's pricing page renders Starter, Pro and Premium prices in an interactive slider, so they don't appear in its page text; checking and lookups with no match cost no credits.
