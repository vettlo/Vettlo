5 Best Mixpanel Alternatives in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 9, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 9, 2026

Short answer

The best Mixpanel alternative for most teams is PostHog (88/100). About $324 a month at 10M analytics events, against $1,176 on Mixpanel Growth billed monthly; no seat fees, and replay, flags and experiments each have their own free allowance.

  • PostHog (88/100): best overall alternative and lowest price.
  • Amplitude (85/100): best for more free events and deep governance.
  • Statsig (84/100): best for experiments on the same data.
  • Heap (74/100): best for automatic capture.
  • LogRocket (73/100): best for replay tied to errors.

Mixpanel has a free plan with 1M events a month and puts SAML SSO on its Growth plan, but its price climbs quickly with event volume and some popular features are paid add-ons. Teams usually look elsewhere when they want lower usage prices, more free events, replay and A/B testing on the same meter, or a tool that captures events without a tracking plan.

Why teams look for Mixpanel alternatives

  • Cost rises with events. Growth is $0 for the first 1M events, then $140 a month at 1.5M, $613 at 5M and $1,176 at 10M, billed monthly. Above 20M events a month it needs a sales quote.
  • Add-ons for common needs. Group Analytics (account-level analysis for B2B products) and Data Pipelines cost extra on Growth.
  • Small free report allowance. Free allows 5 saved reports per seat; unlimited saved reports, cohorts and metrics need Growth.
  • Enterprise-only features. Anomaly detection, root cause analysis, HIPAA tools and the audit log are on Enterprise.

Mixpanel alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Mixpanel?

PostHog for most teams: it scores 88/100 in our ranking against 89 for Mixpanel and costs much less at volume, about $324 a month for 10M analytics events against $1,176 on Mixpanel Growth billed monthly. Amplitude (85/100) is the closest like-for-like alternative, with 2M free events a month.

Is there a free alternative to Mixpanel?

Yes. Amplitude and Statsig include 2M events a month on their free plans, PostHog 1M events plus 5,000 session recordings, and Heap 10,000 sessions. Amplitude and Statsig list unlimited team members on Free, and PostHog has no per-seat charges.

Is PostHog cheaper than Mixpanel?

At list prices, yes. At 2M events a month PostHog's product analytics costs $50 against $259 on Mixpanel Growth billed monthly ($185 billed yearly); at 10M it's about $324 against $1,176 ($840 yearly). Mixpanel includes SAML SSO on Growth, while PostHog charges $750 a month for SAML in its Scale add-on.
