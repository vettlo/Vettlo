MailerLite

MailerLite is an email marketing tool with an automation builder, landing pages, websites and signup forms. Comfort includes 50 automations and 3 seats; Power ($22.50 billed yearly at 500 subscribers) adds unlimited emails, automations and seats, multiple automation triggers and 24/7 live chat support.

Who it's best for

Small businesses, creators and lean marketing teams that want newsletters and automations without a steep learning curve.

Strengths

  • Free plan with 3 automations and a landing page
  • Low prices that scale gently with list size
  • Easy to learn

Watch-outs

  • Multiple automation triggers need Power
  • Free plan locks campaigns above 250 subscribers

Skip it if: You need deep ecommerce data or a built-in sales CRM.

Brevo

Best for high-volume senders on a budget
Visit website ↗
Starting price$8.08/moStarter, 5,000 emails a month, billed yearly ($9 monthly); Standard $16.17
Free trial / planFree plan (300 emails a day)
Main score83/100

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) combines email, SMS and transactional email, priced by monthly email volume rather than contacts. Standard adds unlimited multi-step automations, A/B testing, send-time optimization and a landing page, and removes the Brevo footer; Professional ($449.08 a month billed yearly) adds WhatsApp, 10 seats and phone support.

Who it's best for

Businesses with large lists that send a few campaigns a month and want marketing and transactional email in one place.

Strengths

  • Priced by emails sent, not contacts
  • Unlimited automations and A/B testing on Standard
  • Free plan sends 300 emails a day

Watch-outs

  • Starter has no automation or A/B testing
  • Extra seats are a paid add-on on Standard; 10 included on Professional
  • SMS credits sold separately

Skip it if: You need advanced ecommerce flows or a large team on a mid-priced plan.

FAQ

What are the best email marketing tools for small businesses?

MailerLite scores highest (89/100) once price and ease of use carry more weight, followed by Brevo, Kit.

How is this different from your main ranking?

Same tools and the same 0 to 5 ratings, but price and ease of use count for more, because small teams rarely have an admin or a big budget. The weights are in the table on this page. Brevo, Kit move into the top three as a result.

Which email marketing tools are cheapest for a small team?

See the starting prices in the comparison table; free plans and trials are listed next to them. Price the plan that covers your team size, not only the entry plan.

What should a small business look for?

A free plan or low entry price, quick setup without an admin, and the two or three features you need on day one. Avoid paying for enterprise controls you won't use yet.
