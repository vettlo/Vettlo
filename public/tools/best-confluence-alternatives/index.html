5 Best Confluence Alternatives in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 9, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 9, 2026

Short answer

The best Confluence alternative for most teams is Notion (87/100). AI search, page verification and SAML SSO on Business ($20 a seat); docs, wikis and databases in one workspace.

  • Notion (87/100): best overall alternative.
  • Slab (84/100): best focused wiki for less.
  • Slite (80/100): best for AI answers on the entry plan.
  • Outline (78/100): best flat price or self-hosted.
  • Nuclino (76/100): best for small teams.

Confluence is free for up to 10 users and scales to very large companies, but its useful controls sit on paid tiers and many teams find it heavy to use day to day. Teams usually look elsewhere when they want a wiki people enjoy writing in, AI answers or SSO without an add-on, or pricing they can see up front.

Why teams look for Confluence alternatives

  • Features spread across tiers. Custom permissions, audit logs and Rovo AI start on Standard; analytics and automation need Premium.
  • SSO costs extra. SSO and SCIM come with Enterprise or the Atlassian Guard add-on, not with Standard or Premium alone.
  • Learning curve. Confluence rates 3/5 on ease of use in our ranking, the lowest of the seven tools we scored.
  • Small free plan. Free stops at 10 users and 2 GB of storage, and its permissions can't be customized.

Confluence alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Confluence?

Notion for most teams: it scores 87/100 in our ranking against 81 for Confluence, and Business ($20 a seat) includes AI search, page verification and SAML SSO. Slab (84/100) is the best focused wiki, with SAML SSO and SCIM at $12.50 a user.

Is there a free alternative to Confluence?

Yes. Slab is free for up to 10 users with content verification, Nuclino for up to 50 items, and Notion has a free plan with a block limit for teams. Outline's self-hosted Community Edition is free if you run it on your own servers.

Can I move my Confluence pages to another wiki?

Usually, but check before you commit. Outline lists a Confluence importer on its self-hosted Business plan. For the other tools, confirm the vendor's import options for your page count, attachments and permissions, and run a test import during the free plan or trial.
