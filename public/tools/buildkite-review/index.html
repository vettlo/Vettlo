What is Buildkite?

Buildkite runs pipelines on agents you host or on its hosted machines, with a control plane in the cloud. Free covers 5 users, 2,000 hosted Linux minutes a month and 10 concurrent self-hosted jobs; Pro ($30 per active user) adds SSO, signed pipelines, 4,000 hosted Linux vCPU minutes and 10 self-hosted agents, then $3.50 per agent a month. Enterprise adds SCIM, audit log exports and a 99.95% uptime commitment.

Who it's best for

Engineering organizations with big monorepos or strict security needs that want to keep code and builds on their own machines.

Why we rank it

Self-hosted agents scale to hundreds of concurrent jobs, and dynamic pipelines let a build generate its own steps.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Dynamic and signed pipelines
  • Up to 250 concurrent self-hosted agents on Pro
  • 30-day trial with no card

Watch-outs

  • $30 per active user is the highest seat price here
  • Free plan stops at 5 users
  • Smallest G2 review base here (32 reviews)

Skip it if: You're a small team that wants hosted CI with no infrastructure to run.
