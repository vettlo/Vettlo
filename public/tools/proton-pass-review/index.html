What is Proton Pass?

Proton Pass for Business comes in two plans billed annually, both with a 3-user minimum. Pass Essentials ($1.99 per user per month, for 3 to 30 users) includes vault and item sharing, a built-in 2FA authenticator, Dark Web Monitoring, unlimited hide-my-email aliases and enforced 2FA. Pass Professional ($4.99) adds SSO and SCIM, an activity log, customizable team policies, group sharing, file attachments, a CLI and Proton Sentinel.

Who it's best for

Small teams that want the lowest price here, or SSO and SCIM for under $5 a user.

Why we rank it

Pass Professional has the lowest published price here for SSO and SCIM ($4.99 a user), and Essentials is the cheapest plan here for 3 to 30 users.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • SSO and SCIM from $4.99 a user
  • Unlimited email aliases and Dark Web Monitoring on Essentials
  • Rated 4.7/5 on G2

Watch-outs

  • Essentials has no activity log, team policies or SSO
  • 3-user minimum on every plan
  • Only 127 G2 reviews to judge by

Skip it if: You need deep reporting, delegated admin roles or a long enterprise track record.
