The free plans

1

Notion

Best overall for a flexible wiki with docs and databases
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (unlimited blocks for individuals, limited for 2+ members).

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $10/seat/mo (Plus; Business $20 with Notion AI and SAML SSO; yearly billing saves up to 20%).

Notion is a workspace for docs, wikis and databases that many teams use as their company knowledge base. Free gives individuals unlimited blocks, 7 days of page history and 5 MB file uploads, with a block limit for workspaces of 2 or more members. Plus ($10 a seat) adds 30-day page history and 5 GB uploads; Business ($20) adds Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes, Enterprise Search, page verification, private teamspaces, SAML SSO and 90-day history. SCIM and the audit log are on Enterprise.

Strengths

  • Docs, wikis and databases in one workspace
  • AI search and page verification on Business ($20)
  • Rated 4.6/5 on G2 across 13,972 reviews, the most here

Watch-outs

  • Free plan limits blocks for workspaces with 2+ members
  • Notion AI is only a trial below Business
  • SCIM and the audit log need Enterprise

2

Slab

Best for a clean, low-cost team wiki
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (up to 10 users); 30-day money-back guarantee.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $6.67/user/mo (Startup, billed annually; Business $12.50 with AI Ask, SAML SSO and SCIM).

Slab is a knowledge base built only for team documentation, with posts organized into topics and one search across connected tools. Free covers up to 10 users with verification, 90-day version history and 10 integrations. Startup ($6.67 per user billed annually) adds unlimited users, private topics, 365-day history and AI Autofix; Business ($12.50) adds AI Ask, SAML SSO, SCIM, unlimited history and an uptime SLA. Enterprise starts at 100 users and adds audit logs.

Strengths

  • Free for up to 10 users, with content verification
  • SAML SSO and SCIM on Business ($12.50)
  • Unlimited version history on Business

Watch-outs

  • AI Ask needs Business
  • Audit logs need Enterprise, which starts at 100 users
  • No databases or project features

Read our full Slab review →

3

Confluence

Best for large teams and companies that use Jira
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (up to 10 users, 2 GB storage); free Standard and Premium trials.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at Free up to 10 users (Standard and Premium are priced per user with volume tiers; Atlassian's pricing page didn't display rates when we checked. Teamwork Collection (Jira, Confluence, Loom) Standard is $14.02/user/mo).

Confluence is Atlassian's team wiki, organized into spaces and pages and linked closely with Jira. Free covers up to 10 users with 2 GB of storage, but permissions can't be customized. Standard adds global, space and page permissions, Rovo AI, audit logs, archiving and 250 GB of storage; Premium adds analytics, automation, permission inspection, unlimited storage and a 99.9% uptime SLA. SSO and SCIM come with Enterprise or the Atlassian Guard add-on.

Strengths

  • Free for up to 10 users
  • Space and page permissions, audit logs and Rovo AI from Standard
  • Tight links between pages and Jira work

Watch-outs

  • Analytics and automation need Premium
  • SSO needs Enterprise or the Atlassian Guard add-on
  • Lowest G2 rating here (4.1/5)

4

Nuclino

Best for small teams that want the simplest wiki
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (50 items, 3 canvases, 2 GB); 14-day Starter trial.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $6/user/mo (Starter, per Nuclino's site; Business adds Sidekick AI, SAML SSO and an audit log; yearly billing saves up to 25%).

Nuclino is a lightweight wiki with docs, boards and visual canvases in one workspace. Free covers up to 50 items, 3 canvases and 2 GB of storage. Starter, from $6 per user a month, adds unlimited items and canvases, publishing, 30-day version history and 10 GB per user; Business adds Sidekick AI, SAML SSO, an audit log, team insights, unlimited version history and 20 GB per user. Nuclino counts guests and read-only members as users.

Strengths

  • Lowest listed entry price here, from $6 a user
  • Free plan with 2 GB of storage
  • Rated 4.7/5 on G2, the highest here (30 reviews)

Watch-outs

  • Free plan stops at 50 items
  • AI, SAML SSO and the audit log need Business
  • Guests and read-only members count as paid users

FAQ

What are the best free knowledge base tools?

Notion (Free plan (unlimited blocks for individuals, limited for 2+ members)) ranks highest among the knowledge base tools we rank that offer a free plan. Slab, Confluence, Nuclino also have free plans.

Is there a completely free option?

Yes, each tool on this page lists a free plan on its pricing page. Free plans have limits, shown in the table above, and paid plans add more users, usage and features.

What do free plans usually leave out?

Free plans typically cap users, records or monthly usage and leave out advanced automation, reporting and admin controls. Check each tool's limits against your team size before you commit.

When should I move to a paid plan?

When you hit a limit in the free plan or need a feature it leaves out. The table shows where each tool's paid plans start; our full knowledge base tools ranking compares the paid tiers.
