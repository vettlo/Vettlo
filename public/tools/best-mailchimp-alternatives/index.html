Short answer

The best Mailchimp alternative for most teams is MailerLite (85/100). Comfort is $17.10 a month at 1,000 subscribers billed yearly; free plan includes automations and a landing page.

  • MailerLite (85/100): best overall alternative.
  • Brevo (83/100): best for large lists.
  • ActiveCampaign (84/100): best for advanced automation.
  • Klaviyo (84/100): best for ecommerce.
  • Kit (77/100): best for creators.

Mailchimp is the email tool most teams start with, but they often outgrow it on price or automation. These are the alternatives we'd shortlist, depending on whether you want lower prices, deeper automation or ecommerce data.

Why teams look for Mailchimp alternatives

  • Automation limits on Essentials. Flows are capped at 4 steps; up to 200 flows need Standard.
  • Price as the list grows. Essentials is $13 a month up to 500 contacts and $26.50 up to 1,500; Standard is $20 and $45 at the same sizes.
  • A small free plan. The free plan covers 250 contacts and 500 sends a month, with no automation flows.
  • Premium is a big jump. Phone support and multivariate testing are on Premium, from $350 a month.

Skip it if: You need deep ecommerce data or a built-in sales CRM.

Mailchimp alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Mailchimp?

MailerLite for most small businesses: it's cheaper at the same list size and includes automations on its free plan.

What is cheaper than Mailchimp?

At 1,000 contacts, MailerLite Comfort is $17.10 a month and ActiveCampaign Starter $15 (both billed yearly), against $26.50 for Mailchimp Essentials. Brevo Starter is $8.08 a month billed yearly for 5,000 emails.

Which Mailchimp alternative has the best free plan?

Kit's free plan covers up to 10,000 subscribers; Brevo's sends 300 emails a day.

Which Mailchimp alternative is best for ecommerce?

Klaviyo, whose flows and segments use real-time store data.
