What is Factorial?

Factorial is an all-in-one HR platform covering employee records, document management, time off with a shared leave calendar, time tracking, shift scheduling, onboarding, recruiting, performance management and payroll reporting. Its Business plan is listed at $8 per employee per month for companies up to 100 employees; a Starter plan for up to 10 employees and an Enterprise plan are priced by quote.

Who it's best for

Small and midsize companies that want time tracking and shift scheduling in the same tool as their employee records.

Why we rank it

It includes time tracking and scheduling alongside core HR at a published $8 per employee, which BambooHR sells separately.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • $8 per employee on the Business plan, about $160 a month for 20 employees
  • Time off, time tracking and shift scheduling in one platform
  • Recruiting and onboarding built in

Watch-outs

  • Only the Business plan has a published price; Enterprise is quoted
  • Few G2 reviews (109) compared with BambooHR or Rippling
  • No published price for the Starter plan (up to 10 employees)

Skip it if: You want every plan price published before you talk to sales.
