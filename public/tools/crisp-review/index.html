What is Crisp?

Crisp is a shared inbox for website chat, email and messaging, priced per workspace instead of per agent. The free plan includes 2 seats and the chat widget. Mini ($45 a month) covers 4 seats, a shared email inbox and unlimited data retention; Essentials ($95) covers 10 seats and adds the omnichannel inbox, routing rules, a workflow builder, an AI chatbot and a knowledge base; Plus ($295) covers 20 seats, then $10 a seat, and adds ticketing and white labelling.

Who it's best for

Startups and small teams that want chat, email and a knowledge base for several people at one flat price.

Why we rank it

Ten seats with routing, AI and a knowledge base cost $95 a month on Essentials, less than two Growth seats on LiveChat.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Flat workspace price: 10 seats for $95 a month on Essentials
  • Free plan for 2 seats
  • AI credits included from Mini ($5 to $75 a month)

Watch-outs

  • Routing rules and the knowledge base need Essentials
  • Ticketing needs Plus ($295)
  • Advanced analytics only on Plus

Skip it if: You need detailed per-agent reports or plan to grow past 20 agents soon.
