What is lemlist?

lemlist is a cold outreach tool with personalized sequences. Its Multichannel plan adds LinkedIn automation and a built-in dialer, and its lemwarm email warm-up is included on all plans.

Who it's best for

Agencies, founders and small outbound teams that rely on personalized cold email and LinkedIn.

Why we rank it

Warm-up and deliverability protection on every plan, plus LinkedIn steps and calling on Multichannel, cover the core of cold outreach in one tool.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Email warm-up (lemwarm) on all plans
  • LinkedIn automation and dialer on Multichannel
  • No credit card needed for the trial

Watch-outs

  • Pricing page doesn't clearly state per-user versus per-workspace billing
  • Contact data and buying-intent signals are paid add-ons

Skip it if: Your outreach runs mostly through a CRM-centric enterprise process.

lemlist pricing
