What is LiveChat?

LiveChat is a live chat and AI support tool billed per user. Essential ($19 a user a month billed annually, $25 monthly) includes 1 AI agent with 10 AI resolutions a month, 60 days of chat history and 50 tracked live visitors. Growth ($79 billed annually, $99 monthly) adds unlimited history, chat supervision and takeover, shared canned responses, team performance reports, 10 AI agents and 200 AI resolutions a month. SSO and audit logs are on Enterprise, priced by quote.

Who it's best for

Sales and support teams that staff website chat all day and need supervision, routing and per-agent reports.

Why we rank it

It's the most complete chat-first tool here, and Growth includes 200 AI resolutions a month in the seat price.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • 200 AI resolutions a month included on Growth
  • Chat supervision, takeover and team reports on Growth
  • 14-day trial with no card needed

Watch-outs

  • Essential keeps only 60 days of history and has no routing rules
  • Big jump from $19 to $79 a user
  • No free plan

Skip it if: You're a small team that wants free or flat-priced chat for a few agents.
