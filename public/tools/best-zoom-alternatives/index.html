5 Best Zoom Alternatives in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 1, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 1, 2026

Short answer

The best Zoom alternative for most teams is Microsoft Teams (90/100). 300 participants and 30-hour meetings from $4 per user; business email and Office apps from $7 with Business Basic.

  • Microsoft Teams (90/100): best value alternative.
  • Google Meet (85/100): best for Google Workspace teams.
  • Webex (84/100): best for large enterprises.
  • Zoho Meeting (79/100): Cheapest paid alternative.
  • Whereby (71/100): Simplest for client calls.

Zoom is our top-ranked video conferencing tool, but many teams pay for it on top of Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, which already include meetings. Others leave because of the 40-minute free limit, the 100-participant cap on Pro, or because they want a lower per-user price.

Why teams look for Zoom alternatives

  • You may already pay for meetings. Microsoft 365 includes Teams and Google Workspace includes Meet, so a Zoom license can be a second bill for the same job.
  • Short free meetings. Zoom's free Basic plan ends meetings at 40 minutes; Teams, Google Meet and Zoho Meeting allow 60.
  • Participant caps by plan. Pro allows 100 participants; 300 needs Business at $18.33 per user billed annually.
  • Price per user. Teams Essentials allows 300-person, 30-hour meetings for $4 per user paid yearly.

Zoom alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Zoom?

Microsoft Teams for value: Teams Essentials allows 300-person, 30-hour meetings for $4 per user paid yearly. If your company uses Gmail, Google Meet is included in every Google Workspace plan.

Is there a free alternative to Zoom with longer meetings?

Yes. Microsoft Teams, Google Meet and Zoho Meeting all allow free 60-minute meetings for up to 100 participants, compared with Zoom's 40 minutes.

Which Zoom alternative is cheapest?

Zoho Meeting starts at $1 per host per month for its smallest participant tier. Teams Essentials, at $4 per user paid yearly, is the cheapest option for large meetings.
