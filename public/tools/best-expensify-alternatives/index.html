5 Best Expensify Alternatives in 2026

Short answer

The best Expensify alternative for most teams is Brex (94/100). Reimbursements and multi-level approvals on the free plan; premium at $12 per user adds multiple policies and multi-entity.

  • Brex (94/100): best overall alternative.
  • Ramp (92/100): best for automatic receipt matching.
  • Zoho Expense (86/100): Cheapest paid alternative.
  • BILL Spend & Expense (85/100): best free alternative with budgets.
  • Navan (84/100): best for travel-heavy teams.

Expensify is one of the best-known expense tools and ranks third in our shortlist (89/100), but multi-level approvals and ERP integrations need its Control plan at $18 to $36 a member. Teams look elsewhere when they want free corporate cards with approvals, a lower per-user price, or travel and expenses in one place.

Why teams look for Expensify alternatives

  • Advanced features need Control. Multi-level approvals, NetSuite and Sage Intacct sync, custom rules and SSO start on Control at $18 per member billed annually, or $36 pay-per-use.
  • Overage pricing on annual plans. Control's annual subscription charges $36 for each active member above your subscription size.
  • Free cards elsewhere. Brex, Ramp and BILL include unlimited corporate cards at $0 per user.
  • The free plan is for individuals. Expensify's free Submit workspace doesn't include approvals, payments, company cards or accounting integrations.

Expensify alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Expensify?

Brex (94/100 in our ranking) if you want corporate cards with reimbursements and approvals on a free plan, and Zoho Expense (86/100) for the lowest paid price at $3 per user billed annually.

Is there a free alternative to Expensify?

Brex Essentials, Ramp Free and BILL Spend & Expense are $0 per user. Zoho Expense is free for 3 users, and Navan Expense is free for the first 5 expensing users a month.

Which Expensify alternative is cheapest for a small team?

For 10 users, Zoho Expense Standard is $30 a month billed annually, against $50 a month on Expensify Collect; Brex, Ramp and BILL have $0 plans.
