What is ActiveCampaign?

ActiveCampaign is a marketing automation platform built around a visual automation builder with branching and multiple triggers. Starter ($15 a month at 1,000 contacts, billed yearly) caps automations at 5 actions each; Plus ($49) removes the cap and adds landing pages and sales automation; Pro ($79) adds split testing inside automations, predictive sending and attribution, with 3 users; Enterprise ($145) adds custom objects, custom reporting and 5 users.

Who it's best for

Small and mid-sized B2B and B2C teams that want branching, multi-step journeys without paying enterprise prices.

Why we rank it

It offers the most automation for the money here: unlimited automation actions start at $49 a month, and attribution and predictive sending at $79, against $800 a month plus onboarding for comparable HubSpot workflows.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Unlimited automation actions from $49 a month at 1,000 contacts
  • Attribution and predictive sending on the $79 Pro plan
  • 14-day trial with no credit card

Watch-outs

  • Lead scoring and pipelines are in a paid Pipelines add-on
  • Starter and Plus include 1 user; Pro 3
  • Custom reporting is an add-on below Enterprise

Skip it if: You need built-in lead scoring and custom reports on one plan without add-ons.

ActiveCampaign pricing

Paid plans start at $15/mo (Starter, 1,000 contacts, billed yearly; Plus $49, Pro $79, Enterprise $145). Free plan or trial: 14-day trial, no card needed.
