What is Mailchimp?

Mailchimp is an email marketing platform from Intuit with a drag-and-drop builder, 300+ integrations and plans priced by contacts. Essentials allows automation flows of up to 4 steps; Standard adds up to 200 flows, predictive segments and generative AI; Premium starts at $350 a month.

Who it's best for

Small businesses and first-time email marketers who want a well-known tool with many integrations.

Why we rank it

The editor and reporting are polished and easy to learn, and it connects to almost any tool through 300+ integrations.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Polished editor and reports
  • 300+ integrations
  • 24/7 email and chat support on paid plans

Watch-outs

  • Essentials limits automations to 4 steps
  • Free plan has no automation flows and 250 contacts
  • Costs more than MailerLite or Brevo at the same list size

Skip it if: Automation is central to your marketing and budget is tight.

Mailchimp pricing

Paid plans start at $13/mo (Essentials, up to 500 contacts; $26.50 up to 1,500; Standard from $20). Free plan or trial: Free plan (250 contacts); 14-day trial.
