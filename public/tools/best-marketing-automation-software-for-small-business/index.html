ActiveCampaign

ActiveCampaign is a marketing automation platform built around a visual automation builder with branching and multiple triggers. Starter ($15 a month at 1,000 contacts, billed yearly) caps automations at 5 actions each; Plus ($49) removes the cap and adds landing pages and sales automation; Pro ($79) adds split testing inside automations, predictive sending and attribution, with 3 users; Enterprise ($145) adds custom objects, custom reporting and 5 users.

Who it's best for

Small and mid-sized B2B and B2C teams that want branching, multi-step journeys without paying enterprise prices.

Strengths

  • Unlimited automation actions from $49 a month at 1,000 contacts
  • Attribution and predictive sending on the $79 Pro plan
  • 14-day trial with no credit card

Watch-outs

  • Lead scoring and pipelines are in a paid Pipelines add-on
  • Starter and Plus include 1 user; Pro 3
  • Custom reporting is an add-on below Enterprise

Skip it if: You need built-in lead scoring and custom reports on one plan without add-ons.

Brevo

Best for budget automation priced by emails sent

Main score82/100

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) combines email, SMS and WhatsApp marketing and prices by emails sent rather than contacts. Standard (from $18 a month for 5,000 emails) adds unlimited multi-step workflows, A/B testing, AI send-time optimization and a landing page, with up to 3 users. Professional (from $499 a month for 150,000 emails) adds contact scoring and 10 users. Yearly billing saves 10%.

Who it's best for

Small teams with large lists that want automation and A/B testing for under $20 a month.

Strengths

  • Unlimited multi-step workflows from $18 a month
  • Priced by emails sent, not contacts
  • Free plan sends 300 emails a day

Watch-outs

  • Starter has no marketing automation
  • Contact scoring starts on Professional at $499 a month
  • Standard allows up to 3 users

Skip it if: You need lead scoring and B2B attribution on a mid-priced plan.

HubSpot Marketing Hub

Best all-in-one platform for teams that already use HubSpot CRM
Visit website ↗
Starting price$7/seat/moStarter, 1,000 marketing contacts (also listed at $20/seat); Professional $800/mo billed annually
Free trial / planFree plan (up to 2 users)
Main score85/100
G2 user rating4.4/516,039 reviews

HubSpot Marketing Hub is the marketing suite on HubSpot's CRM. Starter includes 1,000 marketing contacts and limited automation (up to 10 automated actions for email). Professional ($800 a month billed annually, $890 monthly) includes 3 seats, 2,000 contacts, omni-channel workflows (up to 300), lead scoring (up to 5 scores) and up to 100 custom reports, with a $3,000 one-time onboarding fee. Enterprise ($3,600 a month, 5 seats, 10,000 contacts, $7,000 onboarding) adds A/B testing, multi-touch revenue attribution and up to 1,000 workflows.

Who it's best for

Mid-sized B2B teams that want marketing, CRM and reporting in one system and can budget for Professional.

Strengths

  • Workflows, lead scoring and custom reports together on Professional
  • Multi-touch revenue attribution on Enterprise
  • Free plan for up to 2 users

Watch-outs

  • Professional costs $800 a month plus a $3,000 onboarding fee
  • Starter automation is limited to 10 actions for email
  • Extra seats cost $45 a month on Professional and $75 on Enterprise

Skip it if: You need multi-step automation on a budget under a few hundred dollars a month.

Customer.io is a messaging automation platform that triggers journeys from product and behavioral data. Essentials ($100 a month) includes 5,000 profiles, 1 million emails, the visual workflow builder, journeys, segments and 2 object types; extra profiles cost $0.009 each and extra emails $0.12 per 1,000. Premium (from $1,000 a month, billed annually) adds 10 object types, premium support, 90-day onboarding and HIPAA compliance at extra cost.

Who it's best for

SaaS and app companies whose engineers send event data and whose marketers build lifecycle journeys from it.

Strengths

  • Journeys triggered by product events and objects
  • 1 million emails a month on Essentials
  • Startup program: 12 months free for qualifying startups

Watch-outs

  • No lead scoring or CRM listed
  • Needs developer help to send event data
  • Premium starts at $1,000 a month

Skip it if: You're a B2B team that needs lead scoring and a sales handoff.

FAQ

What are the best marketing automation tools for small businesses?

ActiveCampaign scores highest (88/100) once price and ease of use carry more weight, followed by Brevo, HubSpot Marketing Hub.

How is this different from your main ranking?

Same tools and the same 0 to 5 ratings, but price and ease of use count for more, because small teams rarely have an admin or a big budget. The weights are in the table on this page.

Which marketing automation tools are cheapest for a small team?

See the starting prices in the comparison table; free plans and trials are listed next to them. Price the plan that covers your team size, not only the entry plan.

What should a small business look for?

A free plan or low entry price, quick setup without an admin, and the two or three features you need on day one. Avoid paying for enterprise controls you won't use yet.
