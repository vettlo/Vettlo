What is Orum?

Orum is an AI parallel dialer for outbound teams. Pricing is by quote with a 9-seat minimum, paid plans have no dial limits, and AI Coaching and webhooks are add-ons.

Who it's best for

SDR teams of nine or more reps whose main job is live cold calling.

Why we rank it

It's built purely for calling volume, and unlike most quote-only dialers it lets you test it with a free trial of up to 500 dials.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • No dial limits on paid plans
  • Free trial of up to 500 dials
  • Focused purely on outbound calling

Watch-outs

  • 9-seat minimum rules out small teams
  • No public pricing
  • AI Coaching costs extra

Skip it if: You have fewer than nine reps who dial.
