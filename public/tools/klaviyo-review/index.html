What is Klaviyo?

Klaviyo is a B2C marketing platform built on customer profiles that combine store, email and SMS data. Paid email plans are priced by active profiles, with 10 email sends per profile per month, and SMS, reviews and other products are billed on top.

Who it's best for

Online stores on Shopify, WooCommerce and similar platforms that want flows driven by purchase and browsing data.

Why we rank it

Its segmentation and automated flows use real-time store data, and paid plans include predictive analytics and generative AI for segments, content and flows.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Segments and flows built on real-time ecommerce data
  • Predictive analytics on paid plans
  • 350+ integrations

Watch-outs

  • Price rises quickly with active profiles
  • Email support on the free plan only for the first 60 days
  • More than a B2B newsletter needs

Skip it if: You're a B2B company sending newsletters and lead nurture emails.

Klaviyo pricing

Paid plans start at $20/mo (Email, 500 active profiles, 5,000 emails a month; $30 at 1,000). Free plan or trial: Free plan (250 profiles, 500 emails a month).
