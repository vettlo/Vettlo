What is Qlik Cloud Analytics?

Qlik Cloud Analytics is the cloud version of Qlik Sense, priced by data capacity rather than per seat above its entry plan. Starter ($300 a month billed annually) covers 10 users and 10 GB of data with dashboards, sharing, report generation and no-code automations; Standard ($825) includes 25 GB with additional users at no cost, plus shared and managed spaces; Premium ($2,750) includes 50 GB and adds automated machine learning, anonymous public access and SAP data extraction.

Who it's best for

Mid-size and large companies that want many people viewing dashboards without buying a license for each one.

Why we rank it

From Standard up, extra users cost nothing, so per-person cost falls as more people use dashboards.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Additional users at no cost from Standard ($825 a month)
  • Governed shared and managed spaces from Standard
  • Automated machine learning and public access on Premium

Watch-outs

  • Starter is capped at 10 GB of data, and extra capacity can't be added
  • Standard costs $9,900 a year before any extra capacity
  • Trial length isn't stated on the pricing page

Skip it if: You're a team of a few people who only need a handful of dashboards.
