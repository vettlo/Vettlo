What is Livestorm?

Livestorm is a browser-based webinar platform priced by attendee credits: one credit per unique participant per session, counted live, on replay or on demand. Pro allows up to 3,000 live attendees and 4-hour sessions, with unlimited team members and up to 25 speakers at once.

Who it's best for

Teams with several hosts that run frequent small and mid-size webinars and don't want to pay per seat.

Why we rank it

Team members are free, there's a permanent free plan to start, and registrants who never show up don't use credits.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Unlimited team members and moderators on every plan
  • Registrants who don't attend use no credits
  • Free plan with no time limit on the account

Watch-outs

  • Billed yearly only; unused credits don't roll over
  • Salesforce, Marketo, Pardot and Dynamics integrations are Enterprise only
  • Free plan caps sessions at 30 minutes

Skip it if: You run very large webinars often; credits for thousands of unique attendees a year add up quickly.

Livestorm pricing

Paid plans start at $1,200/yr (Pro, 400 attendee credits, billed annually (€1,000); 1,000 credits $2,900). Free plan or trial: Free plan (30 attendee credits a year, 30-minute sessions).
