6 Best Video Conferencing Software in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 1, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 1, 2026

Short answer

Zoom Workplace is our top pick among the 6 video conferencing tools we ranked, scoring 93/100. Paid plans start at $14.16/user/mo.

  • Zoom Workplace (93/100): best overall for meetings, recording and AI notes.
  • Microsoft Teams (90/100): best value, and best for Microsoft 365 companies.
  • Google Meet (85/100): best for Google Workspace teams.
  • Webex (84/100): best for large enterprises with strict security needs.
  • Zoho Meeting (79/100): best budget pick for small teams.

Every tool here can run a video call. The differences are in the limits: how long a free meeting lasts (30 to 60 minutes), how many people can join (4 to 1,000), whether recordings go to the cloud, and whether AI notes cost extra. Many teams already pay for one of these inside Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, so the real question is often whether a standalone tool is worth a second bill.

We scored six video conferencing tools on six weighted criteria. For each one we list the free-plan limits, the entry paid price, and the plan where cloud recording, larger meetings and webinars start.

What is video conferencing software?

Video conferencing software lets people meet over video and audio from a browser, desktop app or phone, with screen sharing, chat and recording. Business plans add longer and larger meetings, cloud recordings, AI summaries and the admin and security controls IT teams need.

Key features to look for

  • Video meetings and screen sharing. HD video calls with screen, window or tab sharing.
  • Meeting limits. Caps on meeting length and participants, which set which plan you need.
  • Recording and transcripts. Local or cloud recordings, often with searchable transcripts.
  • AI notes and summaries. Automatic meeting summaries, action items and in-meeting questions.
  • Calendar and app integrations. Join links added from Google Calendar or Outlook, plus admin controls such as SSO.

How to choose video conferencing software

  • Check what you already pay for. Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace plans include Teams or Meet. A standalone tool only makes sense if it does something those don't.
  • Compare free-plan time limits. Free meetings end after 30 minutes on Whereby, 40 on Zoom and Webex, and 60 on Teams, Google Meet and Zoho Meeting.
  • Find the plan where recording starts. Google Workspace records meetings from Business Standard ($14), not Starter; Zoom includes 10 GB of cloud recording from Pro; Zoho Meeting from Standard.
  • Size your biggest meeting. Zoom Pro and Google Business Starter cap meetings at 100 participants, Teams Essentials at 300. Price the plan that covers your largest regular meeting.
  • Test with outside guests. Run a real client call on the trial and watch how long guests take to join from a phone and a locked-down work laptop.

Video conferencing FAQ

What is the best video conferencing software?

Zoom Workplace scores highest in our ranking (93/100) for its recording and included AI summaries. Microsoft Teams is the best value at $4 per user per month for 300-person, 30-hour meetings.

What is the best free video conferencing software?

Microsoft Teams, Google Meet and Zoho Meeting all allow free 60-minute meetings for up to 100 participants. Zoom's and Webex's free meetings end at 40 minutes, and Whereby's free plan allows 4 participants for 30 minutes.

How much does Zoom cost?

Zoom Workplace Pro is $14.16 per user per month billed annually ($16.99 monthly) for 100-participant meetings. Business is $18.33 billed annually ($21.99 monthly) for 300 participants. The Basic plan is free with a 40-minute limit.

Which video conferencing tool includes recording?

Zoom Pro includes 10 GB of cloud recording per license, Teams Essentials includes recordings, Zoho Meeting Standard includes 5 GB per host, and Whereby Pro includes unlimited recording. Google Meet records from Workspace Business Standard ($14).
