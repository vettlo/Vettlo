The free plans

1

Freshchat

Best for chat plus messaging apps on a budget
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (up to 10 agents); 14-day trial.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $19/agent/mo (Growth, billed annually; Pro $49; Freddy AI Agent $49 per 100 sessions).

Freshchat is the messaging and live chat tool from Freshworks. The free plan covers up to 10 agents with a web and mobile chat widget and a shared inbox. Growth ($19 an agent billed annually) adds social messengers, text messaging and reports; Pro ($49) adds intelligent routing, multiple SLA policies and multilingual conversations; Enterprise ($79) adds skill-based routing and audit logs. The Freddy AI Agent costs $49 per 100 sessions.

Strengths

  • Free plan for up to 10 agents
  • Social messengers and text messaging from Growth
  • Intelligent routing and multiple SLA policies on Pro

Watch-outs

  • Freddy AI Agent costs $49 per 100 sessions on top
  • Freddy AI Copilot is a $29 per agent add-on
  • Annual billing is needed for the listed prices (it saves 20%)

2

Tidio

Best for small online stores
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (50 conversations, 10 seats); 7-day trial.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $24.17/mo (Starter, billed annually, 100 billable conversations; Growth from $49.17; Lyro AI from $32.50).

Tidio combines live chat, a Lyro AI agent and Flows chatbots, priced by billable conversations rather than seats. A billable conversation counts only when a human agent replies or starts it. The free plan includes 50 billable conversations, 10 seats and 50 lifetime Lyro conversations; Starter ($24.17 a month billed annually) raises that to 100 conversations, and Growth (from $49.17) starts at 250. Lyro is a separate add-on from $32.50 a month for 50 AI conversations.

Strengths

  • Rated 4.6/5 on G2 across 2,099 reviews
  • 10 seats on every self-serve plan, including Free
  • Only human-handled conversations are billable

Watch-outs

  • Automatic chat assignment, operating hours and macros aren't on Free
  • Lyro AI is billed separately from chat plans
  • Costs rise with conversation volume

3

Crisp

Best flat price for small teams
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (2 seats); 14-day trial, no card needed.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $45/mo (Mini, per workspace with 4 seats; Essentials $95 with 10 seats; Plus $295 with 20).

Crisp is a shared inbox for website chat, email and messaging, priced per workspace instead of per agent. The free plan includes 2 seats and the chat widget. Mini ($45 a month) covers 4 seats, a shared email inbox and unlimited data retention; Essentials ($95) covers 10 seats and adds the omnichannel inbox, routing rules, a workflow builder, an AI chatbot and a knowledge base; Plus ($295) covers 20 seats, then $10 a seat, and adds ticketing and white labelling.

Strengths

  • Flat workspace price: 10 seats for $95 a month on Essentials
  • Free plan for 2 seats
  • AI credits included from Mini ($5 to $75 a month)

Watch-outs

  • Routing rules and the knowledge base need Essentials
  • Ticketing needs Plus ($295)
  • Advanced analytics only on Plus

tawk.to

Best free live chat

Free plan: Free plan (no limit on agents, chats or sites).

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at Free (Branding removal $29/mo; AI Assist from $29/mo; hired chat agents $1/hour).

tawk.to is free live chat software with no limit on agents, chat volume or websites, plus a free knowledge base and ticketing. It makes money from optional add-ons: removing the "Powered by tawk.to" branding costs $29 a month, the AI Assist bot starts at $29 a month (with a free tier for low chat volume), and trained chat agents can be hired for $1 an hour.

Watch-outs

  • AI Assist is a paid add-on above a low-volume free tier
  • Fewer automation and reporting tools than paid platforms

FAQ

What are the best free live chat tools?

Freshchat (Free plan (up to 10 agents); 14-day trial) ranks highest among the live chat tools we rank that offer a free plan. Tidio, Crisp, tawk.to also have free plans.

Is there a completely free option?

Yes, each tool on this page lists a free plan on its pricing page. Free plans have limits, shown in the table above, and paid plans add more users, usage and features.

What do free plans usually leave out?

Free plans typically cap users, records or monthly usage and leave out advanced automation, reporting and admin controls. Check each tool's limits against your team size before you commit.

When should I move to a paid plan?

When you hit a limit in the free plan or need a feature it leaves out. The table shows where each tool's paid plans start; our full live chat tools ranking compares the paid tiers.
