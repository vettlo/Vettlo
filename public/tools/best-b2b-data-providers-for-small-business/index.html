Who it's best for

Startups and mid-market teams that want prospect data and outreach in one subscription.

Strengths

  • Large database with outreach built in
  • Free plan to start

Watch-outs

  • Fewer compliance controls marketed than enterprise data vendors

Skip it if: You need the deepest firmographic and intent data for enterprise account-based selling.

Lusha

Best for simple self-serve contact lookups

Starting price$37.45/moStarter, billed yearly, 4,800 credits per year, 1 seat
Free trial / planFree plan (40 credits a month)
Main score81/100

Lusha is a self-serve contact data tool with a Chrome extension and CRM integrations. A verified email costs 1 credit and a phone number costs 5 credits.

Who it's best for

SMB and mid-market reps who want to look up contacts quickly without a sales-led contract.

Strengths

  • Permanent free plan with 40 credits a month
  • Clear credit costs: 1 per email, 5 per phone
  • Premium plan includes 5 free seats and team credit allocation

Watch-outs

  • Phone numbers use credits five times faster than emails
  • Unused annual credits reset at the end of the year

Skip it if: You need millions of records for bulk enrichment; look at the Scale plan or enterprise vendors.

FAQ

What are the best B2B contact data providers for small businesses?

Apollo.io scores highest (88/100) once price and ease of use carry more weight, followed by Lusha, Kaspr.

How is this different from your main ranking?

Same tools and the same 0 to 5 ratings, but price and ease of use count for more, because small teams rarely have an admin or a big budget. The weights are in the table on this page. Kaspr move into the top three as a result.

Which B2B contact data providers are cheapest for a small team?

See the starting prices in the comparison table; free plans and trials are listed next to them. Price the plan that covers your team size, not only the entry plan.

What should a small business look for?

A free plan or low entry price, quick setup without an admin, and the two or three features you need on day one. Avoid paying for enterprise controls you won't use yet.
