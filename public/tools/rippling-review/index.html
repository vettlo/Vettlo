What is Rippling?

Rippling is a modular workforce platform. You pick products, such as payroll, global payroll, contractor payments, HRIS, benefits administration and time and attendance, and Rippling quotes a price that starts at $8 per employee per month plus a monthly base fee.

Who it's best for

Companies that expect to add HR, benefits, time tracking or international hires to payroll.

Why we rank it

It has the widest product range here, so payroll, HR records, benefits and time tracking share one employee database as the company grows.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Payroll, HRIS, benefits and time tracking on one employee record
  • Global payroll and contractor payments in the same platform
  • Rated 4.7/5 on G2 across more than 14,000 reviews

Watch-outs

  • Only the $8 starting price is published; the base fee and module prices come from a quote
  • No free trial listed
  • More setup than a payroll-only tool

Skip it if: You only need to pay a handful of employees in one state.
