signNow

signNow, from airSlate, is an e-signature tool sold per user. Business ($8 per user billed annually, $20 monthly) covers core signing with custom branding, advanced fields and team document sharing. Business Premium ($15) adds bulk sending and reminders; Enterprise ($30) adds security, compliance and workflow controls. Paid plans include 100 signature invites per user per year.

Who it's best for

Small and midsize teams that send a steady but moderate number of agreements and want the lowest per-user price.

Strengths

  • Lowest per-user price here: $8 billed annually
  • Bulk sending on Business Premium at $15
  • Trial needs no credit card

Watch-outs

  • 100 invites per user per year; extra invites cost $0.96 to $3.60 each
  • Monthly billing is more than double the annual price on Business
  • Trial is only 7 days

Skip it if: One person sends hundreds of agreements a month; the invite caps and overage fees add up.

SignWell is an e-signature tool priced by sender, not by signer or document. The free plan covers 3 documents a month with 1 sender and 1 template. Light ($10 a month billed yearly) adds unlimited documents, 5 templates per sender, template links and bulk send; Business ($30 for 3 senders) adds unlimited templates, custom branding, in-person signing, attachments and 50 SMS credits a month.

Who it's best for

Freelancers and small teams with one to a few people sending documents.

Strengths

  • Unlimited documents and bulk send from Light ($10)
  • Free plan with API access (25 API documents a month)
  • Rated 4.8/5 on G2

Watch-outs

  • Free plan allows only 3 documents a month
  • Custom branding needs Business ($30)
  • Extra senders cost $10 to $15 a month each

Skip it if: You need enterprise controls such as SSO across a large organization.

PandaDoc

Best for sales proposals, quotes and contracts

Starting price$19/seat/moStarter, billed annually; Business $49
Free trial / planFree plan (5 documents a month); 14-day trial
Main score85/100

PandaDoc combines a document editor with e-signatures. Starter ($19 per seat billed annually) includes unlimited documents and e-signatures sent from the app, a drag-and-drop editor and up to 5 templates. Business ($49) adds unlimited templates, pricing tables, a product catalog, payment collection, CRM integrations, approval workflows and branding.

Who it's best for

Sales and account teams that build proposals and quotes and want them signed and paid in one document.

Strengths

  • Unlimited documents and e-signatures from Starter
  • Pricing tables, payments and CRM integrations on Business
  • Free plan with 5 documents a month and no card needed

Watch-outs

  • Starter allows only 5 templates and no CRM integrations
  • Bulk send, web forms and API documents use paid usage credits
  • Automations without credits can cost up to $5 per document

Skip it if: You only need to send finished PDFs for signature.

Dropbox Sign, formerly HelloSign, is Dropbox's e-signature service. The free plan sends 3 signature requests a month with unlimited self-signing. Essentials ($15 a month billed yearly) is for one user with 5 templates; Standard ($25 per user, from 2 users) raises templates to 15 and adds bulk send; Premium, for 5 or more users, is priced by quote.

Who it's best for

Small teams that want a clean, quick signing flow, and businesses that keep their files in Dropbox.

Strengths

  • Very simple sending and signing
  • Unlimited signature requests on paid plans
  • Free plan and a 30-day trial

Watch-outs

  • Bulk send needs Standard, at least 2 users ($600 a year)
  • Only 5 templates on Essentials and 15 on Standard
  • Premium is priced by quote

Skip it if: You need web forms, payments or deep CRM workflows.

Docusign

Best overall for growing and regulated businesses

Docusign eSignature is the best-known e-signature service. Personal ($11 a month billed annually) is for one user and 5 envelopes a month. Standard ($30 per user) adds shared templates, branding, delegated and scheduled sending and in-person signing; Business Pro ($45) adds web forms, payment collection, bulk send, PowerForms and signer attachments. Standard and Business Pro allow up to 50 users and 100 envelopes per user per year.

Who it's best for

Companies that send contracts across teams and want the widest set of integrations, signer options and admin controls.

Strengths

  • Web forms, payments and bulk send on Business Pro
  • 1,000+ integrations and an established API
  • Identity verification and SMS delivery available (one-time bonus of 5 each on Standard and Business Pro)

Watch-outs

  • Most expensive per user here: $30 to $45 billed annually
  • Standard and Business Pro cap sending at 100 envelopes per user per year
  • SSO and conditional routing need the custom-priced Enhanced plan

Skip it if: You send a few dozen agreements a month and want the lowest per-user price.

FAQ

What are the best e-signature tools for small businesses?

signNow scores highest (86/100) once price and ease of use carry more weight, followed by SignWell, PandaDoc.

How is this different from your main ranking?

Same tools and the same 0 to 5 ratings, but price and ease of use count for more, because small teams rarely have an admin or a big budget. The weights are in the table on this page. SignWell move into the top three as a result.

Which e-signature tools are cheapest for a small team?

See the starting prices in the comparison table; free plans and trials are listed next to them. Price the plan that covers your team size, not only the entry plan.

What should a small business look for?

A free plan or low entry price, quick setup without an admin, and the two or three features you need on day one. Avoid paying for enterprise controls you won't use yet.
