What is Outline?

Outline is a team wiki available as a cloud service or as software you host yourself. Cloud plans are flat monthly fees by team size: Starter $10 for 1 to 10 members, Team $79 for 11 to 100 and Business $249 for 101 to 200, each with AI question answering, SSO, groups and permissions, an audit log and API access. Self-hosted, the Community Edition is free with limited features, and Business is $4 per user a month billed annually with a 10-user minimum.

Who it's best for

Teams that want predictable pricing, or engineering teams that want to run the wiki on their own servers.

Why we rank it

Every cloud plan includes AI answers, SSO and an audit log, and 100 people cost $79 a month.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Flat pricing: $79 a month covers up to 100 members
  • AI answers, SSO and an audit log on every cloud plan
  • Self-hosted option, free in the Community Edition

Watch-outs

  • No cloud free plan; the workspace turns read-only after the 30-day trial until you pay
  • No verification workflow listed
  • Smallest G2 review base here (31 reviews)

Skip it if: You want content verification and stale-page reports built in.
