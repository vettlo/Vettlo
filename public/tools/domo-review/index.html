What is Domo?

Domo is a cloud data platform that combines connectors, data pipelines, dashboards, workflows and AI agents. It's sold on credits rather than seats: credits are used when you store data, update tables, run workflows or use ML inference, and users are unlimited. Domo publishes no prices; the 30-day trial is a full enterprise version with no credit cap, excluding Auto ML, Jupyter Workspaces and some governance features.

Who it's best for

Companies that want data integration, dashboards and apps in one platform and want everyone to have access.

Why we rank it

It covers pipelines and dashboards in one tool with unlimited users, and its trial has no credit cap.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Unlimited users with no per-seat fees
  • Data pipelines, workflows and dashboards in one platform
  • 30-day trial without a card

Watch-outs

  • No published prices; you need a demo for a quote
  • Costs depend on credit usage, so they're harder to predict
  • The old free plan is closed to new sign-ups

Skip it if: You want a price you can check before you talk to sales.
