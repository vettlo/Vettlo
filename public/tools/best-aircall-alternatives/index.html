5 Best Aircall Alternatives in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamPublished Oct 14, 2026 · Prices verified Oct 14, 2026

We may earn a commission when you click some links on this page. It never changes which tools we include or how we rank them. See how we make money.

Aircall is an easy phone system to roll out, but its Power Dialer starts on the $50 Professional plan and it isn't built for high-volume cold calling. Teams look elsewhere when they need parallel dialing, more dialing modes, or a lower price for outbound.

Why teams look for Aircall alternatives

  • No dialer on Essentials. The Power Dialer starts on Professional, so the entry price isn't what outbound teams pay.
  • No parallel dialing. SDR teams that cold call all day often want to dial several numbers at once.
  • AI features cost extra. AI Assist Pro is a $49 per seat add-on.
  • Seat minimum. Plans require at least 3 licenses.

JustCall is a cloud phone system for sales and support. Pro ($49) adds a power dialer, Pro Plus ($89) adds AI call scoring and real-time agent assist, and the custom-priced SalesPro plan adds a predictive dialer on up to 10 lines.

What you gain over Aircall

  • Power dialer on Pro and predictive dialing on SalesPro
  • AI call scoring on Pro Plus
  • 2-user minimum

What you give up

  • Predictive dialing needs a custom-priced plan
  • Lower G2 rating than Aircall (4.3 vs 4.4)

Skip it if: You only need inbound calling and basic outbound.

Aircall alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Aircall?

For teams that want more dialing modes at published prices, JustCall. For dedicated cold-calling SDR teams, Nooks or Orum.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Aircall?

JustCall Pro includes a power dialer at $49 per user billed annually, and CloudTalk Essential includes a smart dialer at €29. Aircall's Power Dialer starts at $50 on Professional.

Does Aircall have a power dialer?

Yes, on the Professional and Enterprise plans. The Essentials plan doesn't include it.
