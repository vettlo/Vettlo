What is HubSpot Service Hub?

HubSpot Service Hub adds ticketing, a help desk, knowledge base and customer feedback tools to HubSpot's CRM. The full help desk workspace and knowledge base are on Professional, which carries a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee.

Who it's best for

Companies whose sales and marketing teams already use HubSpot and want support on the same customer records.

Why we rank it

Support, sales and marketing share one customer database, so agents see the full history without a sync.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Same customer records as HubSpot sales and marketing
  • Free tools to start
  • Strong reporting dashboards

Watch-outs

  • Professional has a required $1,500 onboarding fee
  • Big price jump from Starter to Professional

Skip it if: You don't use HubSpot for anything else.

HubSpot Service Hub pricing

Paid plans start at $7/seat/mo (Starter, billed annually ($20 monthly); Professional $90 plus $1,500 onboarding). Free plan or trial: Free tools for up to 2 users.
