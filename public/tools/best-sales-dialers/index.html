Short answer

Nooks is our top pick among the 6 sales dialers we ranked, scoring 84/100.

  • Nooks (84/100): best for SDR teams that want parallel dialing plus coaching.
  • Orum (83/100): best for high-volume calling with a free trial.

A sales dialer decides how many conversations your reps have each day. The options split into two groups: cloud phone systems with a power dialer built in, sold at published per-seat prices, and parallel dialers that call several numbers at once, usually sold by quote to dedicated SDR teams.

We scored six dialers on six weighted criteria. Watch which plan the dialer actually sits on: several vendors leave it out of their entry plan or sell it as an add-on.

How to choose a sales dialer

  • Decide between power and parallel dialing. Power dialers call one number after another; parallel dialers call several at once. Parallel dialers suit dedicated cold-calling SDR teams, power dialers suit everyone else.
  • Check which plan includes the dialer. Aircall Essentials and JustCall Team have no dialer, and CloudTalk charges extra for its power dialer below Expert. Compare the plan you'd actually buy.
  • Mind the seat minimums. Orum requires 9 seats, Aircall and CloudTalk Expert 3, JustCall 2. Small teams can rule options out fast.
  • Trial it on your real list. Run a trial on your own contacts and compare conversations per hour, not dials.
  • Confirm calling rules for your markets. Do-not-call lists and consent rules vary by country. Check what compliance tools each vendor includes or sells as add-ons.

Sales dialers FAQ

What is the best sales dialer?

For dedicated SDR teams, Nooks tops our ranking, with Orum close behind; both are parallel dialers sold by quote. For teams that want published prices, JustCall scores highest of the cloud phone systems.

What is the difference between a power dialer and a parallel dialer?

A power dialer calls numbers one after another automatically. A parallel dialer calls several numbers at the same time and connects the rep to whoever answers, which raises conversations per hour for cold calling.

Which sales dialer has a free trial?

JustCall and CloudTalk offer 14-day trials without a card, Kixie a 7-day trial, and Orum a free trial capped at 500 dials.

How much does Aircall cost?

Aircall Essentials is $30 per license per month and Professional, which includes the Power Dialer, is $50, both billed annually with a 3-license minimum.
