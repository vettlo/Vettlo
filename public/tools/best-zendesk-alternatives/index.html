5 Best Zendesk Alternatives in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Oct 26, 2026 · Prices verified Oct 26, 2026

Short answer

The best Zendesk alternative for most teams is Zoho Desk (85/100). Live chat and a knowledge base from Standard (€14); free plan for 3 agents.

  • Zoho Desk (85/100): best value alternative.
  • Freshdesk (83/100): best like-for-like alternative.
  • Intercom (82/100): best for AI-first support.
  • Front (76/100): best for email-heavy teams.
  • Help Scout (75/100): best for small teams.

Zendesk is our top-ranked help desk, but its useful features sit in the Suite plans, and AI resolutions add to the bill. Teams look elsewhere when they want live chat and a knowledge base at a lower price, a simpler tool, or AI billed differently.

Why teams look for Zendesk alternatives

  • Key features need Suite. Live chat, a knowledge base and AI agents start on Suite Team at $55 per agent billed annually.
  • AI costs extra above the allowance. Additional AI resolutions cost $1.50 to $2.00 each.
  • Setup effort. Smaller teams often find Zendesk more than they need to configure.
  • No free plan. Teams that want to start free look at Zoho Desk, Help Scout or HubSpot.

Zendesk alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Zendesk?

Zoho Desk for value and Freshdesk for a like-for-like feature set at a lower price.

Is there a free alternative to Zendesk?

Zoho Desk has a free plan for 3 agents and Help Scout for 5 users; Zendesk itself offers a 14-day trial but no free plan.

Which Zendesk alternative is best for small teams?

Help Scout is the easiest to set up and has a free plan for 5 users.
