Short answer

The best Intercom alternative for most teams is LiveChat (85/100). 200 AI resolutions a month included on Growth; chat supervision and team reports on Growth ($79).

  • LiveChat (85/100): best like-for-like alternative.
  • Freshchat (83/100): best for messaging apps and SLAs.
  • Tidio (82/100): best for small online stores.
  • Crisp (81/100): best flat price for small teams.
  • tawk.to (76/100): best free alternative.

Intercom is one of the strongest live chat tools for AI and in-app messaging, but its AI is billed per outcome and routing starts on the $85 Advanced plan. Teams look elsewhere when they want predictable pricing, a free plan, or a simpler chat tool for a small team.

Why teams look for Intercom alternatives

  • AI costs grow with volume. Fin is billed at $0.99 per outcome on every plan, on top of seats.
  • Routing starts on Advanced. Round-robin assignment, workflows and multiple team inboxes need Advanced at $85 a seat billed annually.
  • The entry price is introductory. Essential's $19 is a new-customer price; the regular annual price is $29 a seat.
  • No free plan. Intercom offers a 14-day trial; tawk.to, Freshchat, Tidio and Crisp all have free plans.

Intercom alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Intercom?

LiveChat (85/100 in our ranking) for a like-for-like chat tool with 200 AI resolutions included on Growth, and Freshchat (83/100) for a free plan and SLAs at a lower price.

Is there a free alternative to Intercom?

tawk.to is free for unlimited agents and chats, Freshchat has a free plan for up to 10 agents, and Crisp's free plan covers 2 seats.

Which Intercom alternative is cheapest for a small team?

tawk.to costs nothing. Among paid plans, Crisp Mini is $45 a month for 4 seats and Essentials $95 for 10, against $19 to $29 a seat on Intercom Essential plus $0.99 per Fin outcome.
