What is Zoho Desk?

Zoho Desk is a help desk from the Zoho suite. Express covers email and social ticketing and workflows; Standard adds live chat, messaging apps, a knowledge base and custom reports; Professional adds telephony and Zia AI.

Who it's best for

Budget-conscious teams that want live chat, a knowledge base and automation without enterprise pricing.

Why we rank it

It offers more channels and automation per euro than anything else here, with a free plan for 3 agents.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Free plan for up to 3 agents
  • Live chat, WhatsApp and a knowledge base from Standard
  • Fits the wider Zoho suite

Watch-outs

  • Interface is busier than lighter tools
  • Telephony and Zia AI need Professional

Skip it if: You want the cleanest, simplest inbox for a small team.

Zoho Desk pricing

Paid plans start at €7/agent/mo (Express, billed annually; Standard €14 (euro prices last confirmed Oct 26, 2026)). Free plan or trial: Free plan (3 agents); 15-day trial.
