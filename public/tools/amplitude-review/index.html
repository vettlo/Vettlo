What is Amplitude?

Amplitude is a digital analytics platform covering product analytics, session replay, feature flags, experiments, and guides and surveys. Free includes 2M events a month, unlimited team members, 10,000 session replays, unlimited feature flags, 1 active experiment and 1 year of data. Plus adds behavioral cohorts (up to 20 per organization) and 2 years of data, priced by event volume up to 70M events. Growth adds SSO, project-level permissions, feature experiment results, unlimited cohorts and unlimited data access; Enterprise adds SCIM and advanced permissions. Growth and Enterprise are priced by quote.

Who it's best for

Mid-size and large product organizations that want governed, company-wide analytics with experiments in the same platform.

Why we rank it

Its free plan is the largest by events here (2M a month), and its analysis and data governance are as deep as any tool we scored.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Free plan includes 2M events a month and unlimited team members
  • Analytics, replay, flags, experiments and surveys in one platform
  • Most reviewed tool here: 4.4/5 across 3,102 G2 reviews

Watch-outs

  • SSO and experiment results need Growth, which is priced by quote
  • Plus prices above 2M events weren't shown in Amplitude's page text
  • Plus caps behavioral cohorts at 20 per organization

Skip it if: You want a published price for SSO or for more than 2M events before you talk to sales.
