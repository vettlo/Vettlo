What is ClickUp?

ClickUp combines tasks, docs, whiteboards, goals and time tracking in one workspace. Its paid plans are Core ($9) and Business ($17) per user billed yearly, plus Enterprise by quote. Gantt charts and native time tracking start on Core, and every plan includes a monthly allowance of ClickUp Brain AI credits.

Who it's best for

Teams that want one tool for projects, docs and lightweight wikis, and are willing to spend time setting it up.

Why we rank it

It packs more views and features per dollar than anything else here, and its Free Forever plan has unlimited tasks.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Gantt charts and time tracking on the first paid plan
  • Docs, whiteboards and goals in the same tool
  • Free Forever plan with unlimited tasks

Watch-outs

  • So many features that setup takes real effort
  • Core and Business prices are shown as discounts from higher regular prices; confirm your price at checkout
  • Extra AI credits are a paid add-on

Skip it if: Your team wants something simple that works on day one; try Trello or Asana.
