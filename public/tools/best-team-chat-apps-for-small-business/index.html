The reviews

1

Slack

Best overall for channels, apps and external chat
Visit website ↗
Starting price$7.25/user/moPro, billed annually ($8.75 monthly); Business+ $15
Free trial / planFree plan (90 days of message history, 10 integrations)
Main score90/100
G2 user rating4.5/540,414 reviews

Slack is a channel-based messaging app for work. Pro ($7.25 per user billed annually) adds unlimited message history and integrations, group huddles, external channels with up to 250 organizations, and AI summaries of threads, channels and huddles; Business+ ($15) adds SAML SSO, SCIM, AI search, translations and full data exports.

Who it's best for

Companies that live in chat and connect it to many other tools, or work in shared channels with clients and partners.

Strengths

  • Unlimited history and integrations from Pro
  • Shared channels with up to 250 outside organizations from Pro
  • AI thread, channel and huddle summaries on Pro

Watch-outs

  • Free plan keeps 90 days of messages and allows 1:1 huddles only
  • SAML SSO and SCIM need Business+ ($15)
  • Legal holds, DLP and HIPAA support are on Enterprise+

Skip it if: Your company already pays for Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace and mostly chats internally.

2

Microsoft Teams

Best value, and best for Microsoft 365 companies
Visit website ↗
Starting price$4/user/moTeams Essentials, paid yearly; Microsoft 365 Business Basic $7
Free trial / planFree plan (60-minute group meetings, 100 participants); 1-month trial
Main score88/100
G2 user rating4.4/519,422 reviews

Microsoft Teams combines chat, calling, meetings and file sharing. Teams Essentials ($4 per user paid yearly) includes chat and 300-participant, 30-hour meetings with 10 GB of cloud storage per user; Microsoft 365 Business Basic ($7) adds business email, 1 TB of storage per user and web versions of Word, Excel and PowerPoint.

Who it's best for

Companies that run on Microsoft 365, or teams that want chat and large meetings in one app for the lowest per-user price.

Strengths

  • Chat plus 300-participant, 30-hour meetings from $4 per user
  • Email, 1 TB storage and Office web apps from $7
  • Free version allows 60-minute group meetings for 100 people

Watch-outs

  • Business plans are annual, auto-renewing subscriptions
  • Copilot is an add-on on Essentials and Business Basic
  • Trial needs a credit card and converts to paid

Skip it if: You want the lightest, chat-first app and don't use Microsoft 365.

3

Pumble

Best low-cost Slack-style chat
Visit website ↗
Starting price$2.49/seat/moPro, billed annually ($2.99 monthly); Business $3.99, Enterprise $6.99
Free trial / planFree plan (unlimited users and history, 10 GB per workspace)
Main score77/100
G2 user rating4.5/540 reviews

Pumble is a team chat app from CAKE.com with channels, direct messages and calls. The free plan has unlimited users and message history with 1:1 calls and 10 GB of storage per workspace; Pro ($2.49 per seat billed annually) adds group meetings and screen sharing, Business ($3.99) adds meeting recordings and guests, and Enterprise ($6.99) adds SSO, data retention and an audit log.

Who it's best for

Small and mid-size teams that want Slack-style channels with full history at the lowest per-seat price.

Strengths

  • Free plan with unlimited users and message history
  • Group meetings from $2.49 per seat
  • SSO, data retention and audit log from $6.99

Watch-outs

  • Free plan allows 3 integrations and 1:1 calls only
  • Rated by 40 reviewers on G2, far fewer than the big suites
  • Smaller app ecosystem than Slack or Teams

Skip it if: You rely on dozens of third-party integrations or shared channels with other companies.

4

Google Chat

Best for Google Workspace teams
Visit website ↗
Starting price$7/user/moWorkspace Business Starter list price; Vault and eDiscovery from Business Plus ($22)
Free trial / plan14-day trial
Main score79/100
G2 user rating4.6/548,282 reviews

Google Chat is the messaging app in Google Workspace, with direct messages, group messages and spaces next to Gmail, Drive and Meet. Every Workspace plan includes it; Business Starter ($7 per user) adds 100-participant Meet calls and 30 GB of storage per user, and Business Plus ($22) adds Vault, eDiscovery and 500-participant meetings.

Who it's best for

Teams on Gmail and Google Calendar that want chat included with their email and documents.

Strengths

  • Included in every Google Workspace plan
  • Email, Drive, Docs and Meet in the same subscription
  • External chat and guest accounts can be turned on by admins

Watch-outs

  • Fewer chat-specific features and integrations than Slack
  • Vault and eDiscovery need Business Plus ($22)
  • Outside users can't be added to group messages, only to external spaces

Skip it if: You only need chat and don't want to pay for a full office suite.

  • Free meetings end at 40 minutes
  • Fewer chat integrations than Slack

Skip it if: Chat is your main need and meetings are occasional.

Chanty

Best for small teams that want chat and tasks together
Visit website ↗
Starting price$3/user/moBusiness, billed annually ($4 monthly)
Free trial / planFree plan (up to 5 members, unlimited history)
Main score73/100
G2 user rating4.4/557 reviews

Chanty is team chat with built-in task management. The free plan covers up to 5 members with unlimited searchable history, 1:1 calls and 20 GB of storage per team; Business ($3 per user billed annually) adds unlimited group chats, channels and group calls, task boards, SSO, retention policies, HIPAA compliance with a BAA and 20 GB of storage per user.

Who it's best for

Small teams that want chat, calls and simple task boards in one inexpensive app.

Strengths

  • SSO, 2FA and HIPAA BAA on the $3 Business plan
  • Task boards and to-dos built in
  • Unlimited searchable history on the free plan

  • Free plan is capped at 5 members and 10 integrations
  • Group calls need Business
  • No free trial of Business, only a 14-day money-back guarantee

Skip it if: Your team is larger than a few dozen people and needs a wide app marketplace.

7

Zulip

Best open-source option for threaded discussions
Visit website ↗
Starting price$6.67/user/moCloud Standard, billed annually ($8 monthly); self-hosted is free
Free trial / planFree plan (10,000 messages of search history, 5 GB storage)
Main score75/100
G2 user rating4.6/5117 reviews

Zulip is open-source team chat that organizes every channel into named topics, so separate discussions don't mix. Zulip Cloud Standard ($6.67 per user billed annually) adds unlimited search history, 5 GB of storage per user and retention policies; Plus ($10, 10-user minimum) adds SAML SSO and SCIM. The self-hosted version is free with all features.

Who it's best for

Engineering, research and distributed teams that run many parallel discussions and read them asynchronously, or want to self-host.

Strengths

  • Topics keep parallel conversations separate
  • Free self-hosted edition with all features
  • Guest accounts and advanced roles on the free cloud plan

Watch-outs

  • Calls run through a linked provider such as Jitsi Meet or Zoom
  • Free cloud plan searches only the last 10,000 messages
  • SAML SSO needs Plus, with a 10-user minimum

Skip it if: You want built-in group calls and the most familiar Slack-style interface.

FAQ

What are the best team chat apps for small businesses?

Slack scores highest (87/100) once price and ease of use carry more weight, followed by Microsoft Teams, Pumble.

How is this different from your main ranking?

Same tools and the same 0 to 5 ratings, but price and ease of use count for more, because small teams rarely have an admin or a big budget. The weights are in the table on this page. Pumble move into the top three as a result.

Which team chat apps are cheapest for a small team?

See the starting prices in the comparison table; free plans and trials are listed next to them. Price the plan that covers your team size, not only the entry plan.

What should a small business look for?

A free plan or low entry price, quick setup without an admin, and the two or three features you need on day one. Avoid paying for enterprise controls you won't use yet.
