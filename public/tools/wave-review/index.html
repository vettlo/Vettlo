What is Wave?

Wave offers free invoicing and bookkeeping on its Starter plan, with card processing fees when customers pay online. Pro ($19 a month) adds automatic bank imports, receipt capture and payment reminders.

Who it's best for

Sole proprietors and very small businesses that want basic books for free.

Why we rank it

Its free plan includes unlimited invoices, estimates, bills and bookkeeping records.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Free plan with unlimited invoices and bills
  • Pro is $190 a year
  • Simple to use

Watch-outs

  • Automatic bank imports need Pro
  • Limited reporting and no inventory

Skip it if: You have employees, inventory or more than one person doing the books.

Wave pricing

Paid plans start at $19/mo (Pro, billed monthly ($190 a year); Starter is free). Free plan or trial: Free Starter plan.
