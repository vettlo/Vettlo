What is LastPass?

LastPass has three business plans billed annually. Teams ($4 per user per month, for fewer than 50 users) includes an admin console, shared folders, 25 security policies and dark web monitoring. Business ($7) adds directory integrations, federated login, groups, SIEM integrations, 100+ policies, SSO for up to 3 apps and LastPass Families for employees. Business Max ($11) adds unlimited SSO apps, advanced MFA and SaaS, AI and web monitoring.

Who it's best for

Existing LastPass customers and companies that want 100+ preset policies and SSO for a few apps on one plan.

Why we rank it

Business includes more preset security policies (100+) than any plan here lists, plus directory integrations and family accounts for $7 a user.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • 100+ security policies on Business
  • Dark web monitoring on every plan
  • Rated 4.5/5 on G2 across more than 2,000 reviews

Watch-outs

  • Security incidents LastPass disclosed in 2022 lower its track-record score
  • Business SSO covers only 3 apps; unlimited needs Business Max at $11
  • Teams has no directory integration or SSO

Skip it if: Vendor track record weighs heavily in your security review.
