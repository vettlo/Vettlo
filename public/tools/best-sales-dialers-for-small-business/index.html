Aircall is a cloud phone system for sales and support teams. Its Power Dialer is included on Professional ($50 per license per month billed annually) and Enterprise, not on Essentials ($30).

Who it's best for

Teams that handle inbound and outbound calls and want a phone system that's easy to roll out.

Strengths

  • Published prices and a 25% annual discount
  • Easy setup for inbound and outbound teams
  • Power Dialer on Professional

Watch-outs

  • No dialer on the Essentials plan
  • AI Assist Pro is a $49 per seat add-on
  • 3-license minimum

Skip it if: Your SDRs need parallel dialing for high-volume cold calls.

  • Parallel dialer is a €39 add-on

Skip it if: You want a single quote-based platform for a large SDR floor.

FAQ

What are the best sales dialers for small businesses?

Aircall scores highest (83/100) once price and ease of use carry more weight, followed by CloudTalk, JustCall.

How is this different from your main ranking?

Same tools and the same 0 to 5 ratings, but price and ease of use count for more, because small teams rarely have an admin or a big budget. The weights are in the table on this page. Aircall, CloudTalk move into the top three as a result.

Which sales dialers are cheapest for a small team?

See the starting prices in the comparison table; free plans and trials are listed next to them. Price the plan that covers your team size, not only the entry plan.

What should a small business look for?

A free plan or low entry price, quick setup without an admin, and the two or three features you need on day one. Avoid paying for enterprise controls you won't use yet.
