The free plans

1

Mixpanel

Best overall for self-serve product analytics
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (1M events a month, unlimited seats, 10K session replays).

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $140/mo at 1.5M events (Growth billed monthly ($100/mo billed yearly); first 1M events a month free; $1,176/mo at 10M events).

Mixpanel is an event-based product analytics tool for funnels, retention, flows and cohorts, with session replay, feature flags and experiments added in recent years. Free covers 1M events a month with unlimited seats, 5 saved reports per seat, 10,000 session replays and 2 years of data. Growth includes the first 1M events in its base price and is priced by volume up to 20M events ($140 a month at 1.5M, $1,176 at 10M, billed monthly); it adds unlimited saved reports, 20,000 included replays, warehouse connectors, the Query API, SAML SSO and SCIM. Group Analytics and Data Pipelines are paid add-ons, and anomaly detection, HIPAA tools and the audit log are on Enterprise.

Strengths

  • Free plan with 1M events a month and unlimited seats
  • SAML SSO and SCIM on Growth, which starts at $0 for the first 1M events
  • Published price table up to 20M events, with yearly billing about 29% lower

Watch-outs

  • Free plan allows only 5 saved reports per seat
  • Group Analytics and Data Pipelines cost extra
  • Above 20M events a month you need a sales quote

2

PostHog

Best value, and best for engineering-led teams
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (1M events, 5,000 session recordings and 1M feature flag requests a month).

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $50/mo at 2M events (Product analytics, pay as you go: first 1M events free, then $0.00005 per event, falling to $0.0000343 from 2M; about $324/mo at 10M; no seat fees).

PostHog is a product analytics platform for developers that bundles analytics, session replay, feature flags and experiments, each billed separately by usage. Every month the first 1M analytics events, 5,000 recordings and 1M flag requests are free. The free plan has 1 project, 1 year of data retention and community support; adding a card moves you to pay-as-you-go with 6 projects, 2 years of retention and email support. Analytics events cost $0.00005 each from 1M to 2M and $0.0000343 from 2M to 15M, with lower rates above that. There are no per-seat charges; SSO enforcement is in the Boost add-on ($250 a month), SAML in Scale ($750) and role-based access in Enterprise ($2,000).

Strengths

  • Lowest published price per event here: about $324 a month at 10M events
  • Analytics, replay, flags and experiments in one platform, each with its own free allowance
  • No per-seat charges and billing limits you set yourself

Watch-outs

  • SAML SSO needs the Scale add-on at $750 a month
  • Free plan keeps data for 1 year and has 1 project
  • Built for technical users, so it rates 3.5/5 on ease of use

3

Amplitude

Best for larger teams with deep analysis and governance needs
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (2M events a month, unlimited team members, 10K session replays).

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at Free to 2M events (Plus starts at $0 with the first 2M events free and is priced by event volume up to 70M; Amplitude didn't list Plus amounts in its page text when we checked; Growth and Enterprise are custom).

Amplitude is a digital analytics platform covering product analytics, session replay, feature flags, experiments, and guides and surveys. Free includes 2M events a month, unlimited team members, 10,000 session replays, unlimited feature flags, 1 active experiment and 1 year of data. Plus adds behavioral cohorts (up to 20 per organization) and 2 years of data, priced by event volume up to 70M events. Growth adds SSO, project-level permissions, feature experiment results, unlimited cohorts and unlimited data access; Enterprise adds SCIM and advanced permissions. Growth and Enterprise are priced by quote.

Strengths

  • Free plan includes 2M events a month and unlimited team members
  • Analytics, replay, flags, experiments and surveys in one platform
  • Most reviewed tool here: 4.4/5 across 3,102 G2 reviews

Watch-outs

  • SSO and experiment results need Growth, which is priced by quote
  • Plus prices above 2M events weren't shown in Amplitude's page text
  • Plus caps behavioral cohorts at 20 per organization

4

Statsig

Best for experimentation-first teams
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (Developer: 2M events, 50K session replays, unlimited seats).

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $150/mo (Pro, 5M events included, then $0.05 per 1,000 events; Enterprise custom).

Statsig combines A/B testing, feature flags, product and web analytics and session replay in one platform. The free Developer plan includes 2M events a month, unlimited flag and config checks, 50,000 session replays, unlimited seats and 1 year of analytics retention. Pro ($150 a month) includes 5M events, then charges $0.05 per 1,000 events, and adds advanced experimentation and product analytics, 100,000 replays, unlimited retention, change reviews and API controls. SSO, role-based access, warehouse-native deployment and HIPAA eligibility are on Enterprise.

Strengths

  • Free plan with 2M events, 50,000 replays and unlimited seats
  • Pro at $150 a month includes 5M events and unlimited retention
  • Highest G2 rating here: 4.7/5 across 348 reviews

Watch-outs

  • SSO and role-based access need Enterprise
  • Above 5M events, Pro costs $0.05 per 1,000, more per event than PostHog
  • Analysis is less deep than Mixpanel or Amplitude for non-experiment questions

5

Heap

Best for automatic event capture without tracking plans
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (up to 10K monthly sessions, 6 months of data).

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at Pricing on request (Growth price is estimated after you install the snippet; Pro and Premier use custom session pricing).

Heap, part of Contentsquare, is a product analytics tool that captures user interactions automatically, so you can define events after the fact instead of tagging every click in code. Free covers up to 10,000 sessions a month, 6 months of data history, 1 project and SSO. Growth adds the Sense AI assistant, unlimited users and reports, CSV exports and 12 months of history. Pro adds account analytics, an engagement matrix and report alerts, with session replay, heatmaps and the Heap Connect warehouse sync as add-ons. Premier adds a warehouse integration, unlimited projects, custom permissions, audit logs and SCIM.

Strengths

  • Captures interactions automatically, so events can be defined later
  • SSO on every plan, including Free
  • Unlimited users and reports from Growth

Watch-outs

  • No published prices for any paid plan
  • Session replay and the warehouse sync are add-ons below Premier
  • Free plan keeps 6 months of data

6

LogRocket

Best for session replay with analytics and errors
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (1,000 sessions a month); 14-day free trial, no card.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $83/mo (Core at 10,000 sessions, billed annually ($990/yr); Pro from $292/mo with Galileo AI).

LogRocket records user sessions and pairs the replays with product analytics, error tracking and frontend performance monitoring. It's priced by sessions captured a month, and analytics, log and error events are unlimited. Free covers 1,000 sessions. Core ($83 a month for 10,000 sessions, billed annually) adds conversion funnels, path analysis, heatmaps, alerting, surveys and SSO. Pro (from $292 a month for 10,000 sessions) adds Galileo AI session summaries and issue detection, role-based access and audit logging. Enterprise adds unlimited seats and a self-hosted option.

Strengths

  • Session replay tied to errors, logs and performance data
  • Funnels, paths and heatmaps on Core, with SSO
  • Rated 4.6/5 on G2 across 2,404 reviews

Watch-outs

  • Free plan covers only 1,000 sessions a month
  • No feature flags or A/B testing
  • Cost grows with sessions captured, not with events

Read our full LogRocket review →

7

Pendo

Best for analytics with in-app guides
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (up to 500 MAUs); 30-day full-platform trial through sales.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at Pricing on request (Base, Core and Ultimate are priced by monthly active users (MAUs) and features).

Pendo is a product experience platform that combines product analytics with in-app guides, surveys and roadmaps. Free covers up to 500 monthly active users with product analytics, in-app guides and Pendo-branded NPS surveys and roadmaps; above 500 MAUs you can't create new guides, surveys or segments until you upgrade. Base includes analytics and guides, Core adds session replay, and Ultimate adds sentiment surveys (NPS, PMF, CSAT), Orchestrate, Listen and Data Sync. Every plan includes the Leo AI assistant and retroactive analytics, and paid plans are priced by MAUs.

Strengths

  • Free plan includes in-app guides and NPS surveys
  • Retroactive analytics and the Leo AI assistant on every plan
  • Paid plans aren't interrupted if you go over your MAU limit

Watch-outs

  • No published prices for paid plans
  • Free plan stops at 500 monthly active users
  • Session replay starts on Core, not Base

FAQ

What are the best free product analytics tools?

Mixpanel (Free plan (1M events a month, unlimited seats, 10K session replays)) ranks highest among the product analytics tools we rank that offer a free plan. PostHog, Amplitude, Statsig also have free plans.

Is there a completely free option?

Yes, each tool on this page lists a free plan on its pricing page. Free plans have limits, shown in the table above, and paid plans add more users, usage and features.

What do free plans usually leave out?

Free plans typically cap users, records or monthly usage and leave out advanced automation, reporting and admin controls. Check each tool's limits against your team size before you commit.

When should I move to a paid plan?

When you hit a limit in the free plan or need a feature it leaves out. The table shows where each tool's paid plans start; our full product analytics tools ranking compares the paid tiers.
