What is HubSpot Marketing Hub?

HubSpot Marketing Hub is the marketing suite on HubSpot's CRM. Starter includes 1,000 marketing contacts and limited automation (up to 10 automated actions for email). Professional ($800 a month billed annually, $890 monthly) includes 3 seats, 2,000 contacts, omni-channel workflows (up to 300), lead scoring (up to 5 scores) and up to 100 custom reports, with a $3,000 one-time onboarding fee. Enterprise ($3,600 a month, 5 seats, 10,000 contacts, $7,000 onboarding) adds A/B testing, multi-touch revenue attribution and up to 1,000 workflows.

Who it's best for

Mid-sized B2B teams that want marketing, CRM and reporting in one system and can budget for Professional.

Why we rank it

Professional puts workflows, lead scoring and custom reports on one plan connected to the CRM, and Enterprise adds multi-touch revenue attribution, which few tools here include.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Workflows, lead scoring and custom reports together on Professional
  • Multi-touch revenue attribution on Enterprise
  • Free plan for up to 2 users

Watch-outs

  • Professional costs $800 a month plus a $3,000 onboarding fee
  • Starter automation is limited to 10 actions for email
  • Extra seats cost $45 a month on Professional and $75 on Enterprise

Skip it if: You need multi-step automation on a budget under a few hundred dollars a month.
