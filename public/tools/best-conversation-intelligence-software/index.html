How to choose a conversation intelligence tool

  • Decide who the tool is for. Manager-led coaching across many reps points to Gong or Avoma's add-ons. Saving reps time on notes and CRM updates points to Sybill or Fireflies.ai.
  • Price the plan with the features you need. Avoma's coaching is a $29 add-on and Fireflies.ai's conversation intelligence starts on Business. Compare the plan that includes call scoring, not the cheapest one.
  • Count recorder seats, not users. Avoma charges only for people who record; viewers are free. That changes the math for teams where managers mostly listen.
  • Check consent and recording rules. Recording laws vary by country and US state. Confirm how each tool announces the recorder and handles consent before rollout.
  • Test CRM sync on real deals. Run a trial on live calls and check that summaries and fields land on the right CRM records.

Conversation intelligence FAQ

What is conversation intelligence software?

It records and transcribes sales calls and meetings, then analyzes them for coaching, deal risks and trends, and often writes notes and fields back to the CRM.

How much does Gong cost?

Gong doesn't publish prices. Its pricing page says licenses are priced per user, plus a platform fee based on the number of users. You need a quote.

What is the cheapest conversation intelligence tool?

Fireflies.ai includes conversation intelligence on its Business plan at $19 per seat per month billed annually. Avoma's Startup plan is $19 per recorder seat, but its Conversation Intelligence add-on costs $29 more.

Is there a free conversation intelligence tool?

Fireflies.ai and Sybill have free plans for recording, transcripts and summaries, with limits. Coaching and team analytics need a paid plan.
