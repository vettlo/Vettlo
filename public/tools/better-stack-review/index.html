What is Better Stack?

Better Stack combines uptime monitoring, logs and on-call in one product. The free plan includes 10 monitors and heartbeats, one status page and Slack and email alerts. A responder license ($29 a month billed annually, $34 monthly) unlocks incidents with on-call, monitoring and status pages, including unlimited phone call and SMS alerts. Slack and Teams channel-based incident workflows cost $9 more per responder, and team members who don't take on-call are free.

Who it's best for

Small engineering teams that don't have separate monitoring and want uptime checks, paging and a status page from one vendor.

Why we rank it

One license covers on-call, monitoring and status pages with unlimited phone and SMS alerts, and it shares the highest G2 rating here, 4.8/5 across 344 reviews.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Unlimited phone call and SMS alerts on the responder license
  • Monitoring and status pages included, so no separate uptime tool
  • Unlimited free team members

Watch-outs

  • Slack and Teams incident workflows cost $9 per responder extra
  • Postmortems and incident roles aren't listed on the pricing page
  • Logs and traces beyond the free tier are billed in separate bundles

Skip it if: You already use Datadog, Grafana or another monitoring stack and need deep incident coordination; incident.io and Rootly focus on that.
