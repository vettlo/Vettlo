What is Square Payroll?

Square Payroll is full-service payroll for $35 a month plus $6 per person paid. It includes federal and state tax filings, multistate payroll, next-day direct deposit, scheduling, overtime calculations and direct-tip tracking. Contractor-only payroll costs $6 per person with no base fee.

Who it's best for

Restaurants, retailers and service businesses with hourly staff, especially those already using Square for payments.

Why we rank it

It has the lowest full-service base fee here and includes multistate payroll, next-day direct deposit and tip tracking without add-ons.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Lowest full-service base fee here at $35 a month
  • Multistate payroll and next-day direct deposit included
  • Contractor-only payroll at $6 per person, no base fee

Watch-outs

  • Health insurance, 401(k) and workers' comp are add-ons
  • Thin HR tools compared with Gusto or Rippling
  • Few G2 reviews (31) to judge by

Skip it if: You need HR features such as an HR resource center or an HR advisor.
