Short answer

The best Greenhouse alternative for most teams is Workable (88/100). Published prices, from $299 a month for 1 to 20 employees; 15-day trial with no card needed.

  • Workable (88/100): best overall alternative.
  • Ashby (86/100): best for recruiting analytics.
  • Teamtailor (80/100): best for employer branding.
  • Breezy HR (79/100): best flat price for small teams.
  • Manatal (78/100): best for the lowest price.

Greenhouse is the best-known ATS for structured hiring, but it publishes no prices and lists no free trial, and smaller teams often don't need everything in it. Teams look elsewhere when they want a published price, a trial they can start today, or a tool that suits a smaller hiring volume.

Why teams look for Greenhouse alternatives

  • No published prices. Greenhouse prices every customer by hiring volume, organizational complexity and plan.
  • No self-serve trial. Its pricing page offers a demo but lists no free trial.
  • Key features sit on higher plans. Texting, sourcing automation and a BI connector start on Plus; an audit log and developer sandbox on Pro.
  • Built for recruiting teams. Small companies hiring a few roles a year may not use its structured hiring depth.

Greenhouse alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Greenhouse?

Workable for most small and midsize companies: it publishes its prices, includes 200+ job boards, self-scheduling, e-signatures and a custom report builder on Standard, and offers a 15-day trial. Ashby is the closest match for teams that care most about recruiting analytics.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Greenhouse?

Greenhouse doesn't publish prices, so a direct comparison needs its quote. Among published prices, Manatal starts at $15 per user a month, Breezy HR at $157 a month with unlimited users and JazzHR at $1,000 a year.

Which Greenhouse alternative has a free trial?

Workable offers 15 days and Breezy HR and Manatal 14 days, all without a credit card. JazzHR offers a 21-day trial.

Which Greenhouse alternative is best for startups?

Ashby, built for fast-growing companies; its Foundations plan is $400 a month for companies up to 100 employees and includes the ATS, scheduling, sourcing and core dashboards.
