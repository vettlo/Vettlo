Short answer

  • QuickBooks Online (87/100): best overall for US small businesses.

Accounting software keeps your books: bank feeds and reconciliation, invoices and bills, and the reports your accountant and tax return depend on. Most vendors advertise a steep introductory discount, so the number that matters is the regular price once the promotion ends, plus what each plan limits: users, invoices, bills or billable clients.

We scored six tools on six weighted criteria and list each one's regular price, what the entry plan caps, and where features like inventory, projects and multi-currency start.

Who it's best for

How to choose accounting software

  • Compare regular prices, not promotions. QuickBooks, Xero and FreshBooks advertise big introductory discounts. Budget for the price you'll pay after the first few months.
  • Check the limits on the entry plan. QuickBooks Simple Start is one user, Xero Early caps invoices and bills, and FreshBooks Lite caps billable clients.
  • Ask your accountant. If you work with an accountant or bookkeeper, use the tool they already know; it saves hours at month end and tax time.
  • Plan for inventory, projects and currencies. These usually start on mid-tier plans: QuickBooks Plus, Xero Established or Zoho Books Professional.
  • Make sure your bank connects. Bank feeds drive reconciliation. Check that your bank is supported before you migrate.

Accounting FAQ

What is the best accounting software for small businesses?

QuickBooks Online scores highest in our ranking for US small businesses. Xero is the best choice if several people need access, and Zoho Books is the best value.

What is the best free accounting software?

Wave's Starter plan is free with unlimited invoices and bills. Zoho Books is free for businesses under $50K in annual revenue.

How much does QuickBooks Online cost?

QuickBooks Online's regular prices are $38 a month for Simple Start, $85 for Essentials, $140 for Plus and $340 for Advanced, with introductory discounts for new customers.

Is Xero cheaper than QuickBooks?

At regular prices, Xero Early is $27 a month versus $38 for QuickBooks Simple Start, and Xero has no per-user fees. Xero Early caps you at 20 invoices and 5 bills a month.
