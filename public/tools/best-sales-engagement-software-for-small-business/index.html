The reviews

1

Apollo.io

Best for teams that want data and sequences in one tool
Visit website ↗
Starting price$49/seat/moBasic, billed annually
Free trial / planFree plan; 14-day trial of paid plans
Main score84/100
G2 user rating4.7/59,851 reviews

Apollo.io combines a B2B contact database with email sequencing, a dialer and deal management. Its pricing page lists 240 million contacts and 30 million companies.

Who it's best for

Startups and mid-market outbound teams that want prospect data and outreach in one place instead of buying them separately.

Strengths

  • Contact data and sequences in one product
  • Free plan to start (2 sequences, 900 credits a year per seat)
  • Deliverability suite and warm-up on Basic

Watch-outs

  • Credits limit how many contacts you can reveal and enrich
  • Analytics and coaching are lighter than Outreach or Salesloft

Skip it if: Your team already pays for a separate data provider and needs enterprise governance more than prospecting.

Reply.io runs sequences across email and other channels. Its Multichannel plan is $89 per user per month billed annually ($99 monthly), and prices rise with active contact volume.

  • 14-day free trial
  • Monthly billing available

Watch-outs

  • Prices rise with active contacts
  • Less depth in coaching analytics than Outreach or Salesloft

Skip it if: You need enterprise governance across hundreds of reps.

lemlist is a cold outreach tool with personalized sequences. Its Multichannel plan adds LinkedIn automation and a built-in dialer, and its lemwarm email warm-up is included on all plans.

Who it's best for

Agencies, founders and small outbound teams that rely on personalized cold email and LinkedIn.

Strengths

  • Email warm-up (lemwarm) on all plans
  • LinkedIn automation and dialer on Multichannel
  • No credit card needed for the trial

Watch-outs

  • Pricing page doesn't clearly state per-user versus per-workspace billing
  • Contact data and buying-intent signals are paid add-ons

Skip it if: Your outreach runs mostly through a CRM-centric enterprise process.

Instantly is a cold email platform built around sending volume. The Growth plan includes 5,000 emails and 1,000 uploaded contacts a month with unlimited connected email accounts; Hypergrowth raises that to 125,000 emails.

Who it's best for

Founders, agencies and lean outbound teams sending cold email at volume across many inboxes.

Strengths

  • Flat pricing instead of per seat
  • Highest G2 rating on this list (4.8/5)

Watch-outs

  • Email-first; not a full multichannel sales engagement suite
  • CRM integration is lighter than enterprise platforms

Skip it if: Your reps need calls, LinkedIn steps and CRM-governed sequences in one place.

Outreach is an enterprise sales execution platform sold in four tiers (Amplify Essentials, Core, Plus and Pro) that include between 10,000 and 100,000 AI credits. Prices are only available from sales.

Who it's best for

Mid-market and enterprise sales orgs with a RevOps team that need standardized sequences, deal management and pipeline analytics across many reps.

Strengths

Watch-outs

  • Needs admin time to configure well

Skip it if: You're a team under about 15 reps without RevOps support.

FAQ

What are the best sales engagement platforms for small businesses?

Apollo.io scores highest (84/100) once price and ease of use carry more weight, followed by Reply.io, lemlist.

How is this different from your main ranking?

Same tools and the same 0 to 5 ratings, but price and ease of use count for more, because small teams rarely have an admin or a big budget. The weights are in the table on this page. Reply.io, lemlist move into the top three as a result.

Which sales engagement platforms are cheapest for a small team?

See the starting prices in the comparison table; free plans and trials are listed next to them. Price the plan that covers your team size, not only the entry plan.

What should a small business look for?

A free plan or low entry price, quick setup without an admin, and the two or three features you need on day one. Avoid paying for enterprise controls you won't use yet.
