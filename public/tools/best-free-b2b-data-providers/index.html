The free plans

1

Apollo.io

Best for data plus outreach on a budget
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan; 14-day trial.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $49/seat/mo (Basic, billed annually).

Apollo.io pairs a contact database its pricing page sizes at 240 million contacts and 30 million companies with email sequencing and core CRM integrations.

Strengths

  • Large database with outreach built in
  • Free plan to start
  • Published pricing from a free plan up

Watch-outs

  • Phone data uses credits and varies by region
  • Fewer compliance controls marketed than enterprise data vendors

2

Lusha

Best for simple self-serve contact lookups
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan (40 credits a month).

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $37.45/mo (Starter, billed yearly, 4,800 credits per year, 1 seat).

Lusha is a self-serve contact data tool with a Chrome extension and CRM integrations. A verified email costs 1 credit and a phone number costs 5 credits.

Strengths

  • Permanent free plan with 40 credits a month
  • Clear credit costs: 1 per email, 5 per phone
  • Premium plan includes 5 free seats and team credit allocation

Watch-outs

  • Phone numbers use credits five times faster than emails
  • Unused annual credits reset at the end of the year

Kaspr

Best for small teams that need phone numbers
Visit website ↗

Free plan: Free plan; no card needed.

When you outgrow it: paid plans start at $49/user/mo (Starter, billed annually ($65 monthly)).

Kaspr offers unlimited B2B emails on paid plans with a yearly allowance of phone numbers and direct emails: 1,200 phone credits on Starter and 2,400 on Business ($79/user/mo annually).

Strengths

  • Unlimited B2B emails on paid plans
  • Published per-user pricing in USD, EUR and GBP
  • Free plan with monthly email and phone credits

Watch-outs

  • Phone and direct email credits are capped per year
  • Enrichment and integrations are lighter than enterprise vendors

FAQ

What are the best free B2B contact data providers?

Apollo.io (Free plan; 14-day trial) ranks highest among the B2B contact data providers we rank that offer a free plan. Lusha, Kaspr also have free plans.

Is there a completely free option?

Yes, each tool on this page lists a free plan on its pricing page. Free plans have limits, shown in the table above, and paid plans add more users, usage and features.

What do free plans usually leave out?

Free plans typically cap users, records or monthly usage and leave out advanced automation, reporting and admin controls. Check each tool's limits against your team size before you commit.

When should I move to a paid plan?

When you hit a limit in the free plan or need a feature it leaves out. The table shows where each tool's paid plans start; our full B2B contact data providers ranking compares the paid tiers.
