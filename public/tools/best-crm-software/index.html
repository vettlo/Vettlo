Short answer

Salesforce Sales Cloud is our top pick among the 7 CRMs we ranked, scoring 91/100. For smaller teams that want a free plan, HubSpot Sales Hub and Pipedrive are the strongest picks.

  • Salesforce Sales Cloud (91/100): best for enterprises with complex sales processes.
  • HubSpot Sales Hub (88/100): best for teams that want marketing and sales on one database.
  • Pipedrive (82/100): best for small, pipeline-first sales teams.
  • Zoho CRM (78/100): best for budget-conscious teams that want depth.
  • Freshsales (76/100): best for teams that want built-in AI scoring on a budget.

A CRM is the system your reps will open more than any other tool, so a bad pick shows up as missing activity data, unreliable forecasts and reps keeping deals in spreadsheets. We built this shortlist for sales and RevOps leaders choosing a CRM for a team of 5 to 500 reps.

Each tool below was scored against six weighted criteria, listed in How we scored. For each one we say who it's for and, just as important, who should skip it.

How to choose a CRM

  • Map your motion first. Inbound, outbound, partner-led and product-led sales need different objects and automation. Write down the stages a deal passes through before you open a trial.
  • Count every seat that will touch it. Managers, SDRs, CS and finance often need access. Per-seat pricing changes fast when you include them.
  • Test your three hardest workflows. Lead routing, multi-contact deals and forecast roll-ups expose limits quickly. Run them with your own data during the trial.
  • Check the data exit. Confirm you can export activity history and custom fields, not only contacts.
  • Price the whole first year. Add implementation, migration, integrations and admin time to the license.

CRM software FAQ

What is the best CRM for a small sales team?

For teams under about 10 reps, ease of adoption usually matters more than feature depth. Pipedrive and HubSpot's entry tiers are the most common starting points; Attio suits founder-led teams that want a flexible setup, and folk is a lighter option for relationship-led work. See our guide to the best CRM for small businesses. Start a trial with your real pipeline before deciding.

How much does CRM software cost?

Most CRMs price per seat per month, with free or entry tiers for small teams and steep jumps for automation, forecasting and custom objects. Budget for implementation, data migration and integrations too, which can exceed license cost at enterprise scale.

Should sales and marketing share one CRM?

Sharing one database removes sync work and makes attribution easier. It matters most for inbound-led motions. Outbound-heavy teams with separate marketing tools can run well with a sales-first CRM and a clean integration.

How long does a CRM migration take?

Small teams can move in days. Mid-market migrations with custom fields, historical activity and integrations commonly take several weeks. Plan field mapping and deduplication before the cutover.
