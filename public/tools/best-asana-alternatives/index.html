5 Best Asana Alternatives in 2026

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Oct 15, 2026 · Prices verified Oct 15, 2026

Short answer

The best Asana alternative for most teams is ClickUp (86/100). Docs, whiteboards and time tracking in one tool; Free Forever plan with unlimited tasks.

  • ClickUp (86/100): best all-in-one alternative.
  • monday.com (85/100): best for company-wide adoption.
  • Smartsheet (81/100): best for spreadsheet-minded teams.
  • Wrike (79/100): best for complex projects.
  • Trello (77/100): best for simple, cheap boards.

Asana is our top-ranked project management tool, but it isn't the right fit for every team. People look elsewhere when the 2-user free plan is too small, when they need portfolios without paying for Advanced, or when they want docs and whiteboards in the same place.

Why teams look for Asana alternatives

  • Free plan is tiny. Asana's free Personal plan covers only 2 users.
  • Key features are on Advanced. Portfolios, goals, resource management and time tracking need the $24.99 plan.
  • Separate tools for docs. Teams that want docs, wikis and whiteboards alongside tasks often look for an all-in-one.
  • Spreadsheet habits. Teams that plan in Excel may prefer a grid-based tool.

Asana alternatives FAQ

What is the best alternative to Asana?

ClickUp if you want docs and whiteboards in the same tool, and monday.com if ease of adoption matters most.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Asana?

Trello starts at $5 and ClickUp at $7 per user billed yearly, compared with $10.99 for Asana Starter.

Which Asana alternative has the best free plan?

ClickUp's Free Forever plan has unlimited tasks, and Trello's free plan covers 10 collaborators per Workspace, versus 2 users on Asana's free plan.
