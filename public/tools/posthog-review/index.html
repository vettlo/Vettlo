What is PostHog?

PostHog is a product analytics platform for developers that bundles analytics, session replay, feature flags and experiments, each billed separately by usage. Every month the first 1M analytics events, 5,000 recordings and 1M flag requests are free. The free plan has 1 project, 1 year of data retention and community support; adding a card moves you to pay-as-you-go with 6 projects, 2 years of retention and email support. Analytics events cost $0.00005 each from 1M to 2M and $0.0000343 from 2M to 15M, with lower rates above that. There are no per-seat charges; SSO enforcement is in the Boost add-on ($250 a month), SAML in Scale ($750) and role-based access in Enterprise ($2,000).

Who it's best for

Startups and engineering-led product teams that want analytics, replay, flags and A/B tests in one place at usage prices.

Why we rank it

It's the cheapest per event here and the most complete all-in-one product: 10M analytics events cost about $324 a month, with no seat fees.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • Lowest published price per event here: about $324 a month at 10M events
  • Analytics, replay, flags and experiments in one platform, each with its own free allowance
  • No per-seat charges and billing limits you set yourself

Watch-outs

  • SAML SSO needs the Scale add-on at $750 a month
  • Free plan keeps data for 1 year and has 1 project
  • Built for technical users, so it rates 3.5/5 on ease of use

Skip it if: You need SAML SSO on a small budget, or most of your users are non-technical.
