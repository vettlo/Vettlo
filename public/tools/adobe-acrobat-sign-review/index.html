What is Adobe Acrobat Sign?

Adobe sells e-signatures for small teams inside Acrobat for teams. Acrobat Standard for teams ($16.99 per license a month on an annual plan) lets you request signatures and track responses alongside PDF editing. Acrobat Pro for teams ($23.99) adds web forms, bulk sending and brand customization. Larger Acrobat Sign Solutions plans are priced by quote.

Who it's best for

Teams that already work in PDFs and want editing, conversion and signing under one license.

Why we rank it

Each license includes Adobe's full PDF editor as well as e-signatures, which the dedicated signing tools don't.

Pros and cons

Strengths

  • PDF editing and e-signatures in one license
  • Bulk sending, web forms and branding on Pro for teams
  • Trial for up to 10 licenses

Watch-outs

  • Annual plans billed monthly charge half the remaining commitment if you cancel after 14 days
  • No month-to-month price listed for business plans
  • Enterprise Acrobat Sign plans are quote-only

Skip it if: You don't need a PDF editor and want the simplest sending tool.
