Short answer

Zoom Webinars is our top pick among the 5 webinar tools we ranked, scoring 83/100. Paid plans start at $66.67/mo.

  • Zoom Webinars (83/100): best overall for large webinars with CRM sync.
  • Livestorm (82/100): best for teams that want a free plan and unlimited hosts.
  • Webex Webinars (80/100): best for live, simulive and on-demand events in one license.
  • WebinarJam (78/100): best for sales webinars with offers and automated replays.
  • Demio (75/100): best for B2B marketers who run event series.

Webinar software hosts one-to-many online events: people register on a landing page, get reminder emails, watch a live or pre-recorded session, ask questions and vote in polls, and you get a report of who attended. The plans differ less in features than in limits: how many attendees a session holds (50 to 5,000 on the entry and mid plans here), how long it can run (1 to 30 hours), and whether automated replays and CRM sync cost extra.

We scored five webinar platforms on six weighted criteria. Pricing models vary more than in most categories: Zoom, Demio and WebinarJam charge a monthly fee per license or host at a set room size, Webex sells a yearly license per 1,000-attendee tier, and Livestorm sells a yearly pack of attendee credits, so a team running one 100-person webinar a month needs about 1,200 credits a year.

What is webinar software?

Webinar software runs one-to-many online events: attendees register on a landing page, receive reminder emails, watch a live or pre-recorded presentation and take part through chat, Q&A and polls, while hosts get registration and attendance reports. Unlike meeting tools, it separates presenters from the audience and is built to turn viewers into leads.

Key features to look for

  • Registration pages and reminders. Branded sign-up forms, confirmation and reminder emails, and source tracking.
  • Audience engagement. Chat, moderated Q&A, polls, handouts and on-screen offers or calls to action.
  • Automated and on-demand webinars. Recorded sessions replayed on a schedule (simulive) or watched on demand.
  • Attendee limits and session length. Caps on live attendees and minutes per session, which decide the plan you need.
  • CRM and marketing integrations. Registrants and attendance synced to HubSpot, Salesforce, Marketo or email tools.

How to choose webinar software

  • Price your real attendance, not the room size. Livestorm counts one credit per unique person per session, so twelve 100-person webinars use about 1,200 credits a year ($4,125 at the 1,500-credit tier). Zoom, Demio and WebinarJam charge the same fee whether the room fills or not.
  • Check the session length cap. WebinarJam stops at 1 to 4 hours depending on plan, Livestorm Pro at 4 hours and Demio Starter at 3; Webex allows 24 hours and Zoom 30.
  • Find the plan where CRM sync starts. Zoom lists HubSpot, Marketo and Salesforce on its entry Webinars plan; Demio lists them on Premium and Livestorm puts Salesforce and Marketo on Enterprise.
  • Decide whether you need automated replays. WebinarJam includes EverWebinar from Basic, Demio adds automated webinars on Growth, and Webex includes simulive on its entry license.
  • Run a full test event. Use the trial or free plan to send a real registration page and reminder email to colleagues, then check what the attendance report and CRM record show afterward.

Webinar software FAQ

What is the best webinar software?

Zoom Webinars scores highest in our ranking (83/100), mainly for its CRM integrations on the entry plan and its scale of up to 1,000 panelists and 30-hour sessions. Livestorm (82/100) is the best choice for teams with many hosts, since team members are free.

How much does webinar software cost?

Entry plans run from $39 a month (WebinarJam Starter, 100 attendees, billed annually) to $66.67 a month (Zoom Webinars, 500 attendees, billed annually). Webex showed its 1,000-attendee license at $675 a year on Nov 23, 2026, and Livestorm Pro starts at $1,200 a year for 400 attendee credits.

Is there free webinar software?

Livestorm has a permanent free plan with 30 attendee credits a year, 30-minute sessions and up to 10 events. The other tools here offer trials instead: Demio for 14 days (20 attendees, 1-hour sessions) and WebinarJam for $1 for 14 days.

Which webinar software supports automated webinars?

WebinarJam includes its EverWebinar automated product from the Basic plan ($79 a month billed annually), Demio includes automated and on-demand webinars from Growth ($117 per host billed yearly), and Webex Webinars includes simulive and on-demand streaming on its 1,000-attendee license.
