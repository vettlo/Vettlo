About Vettlo

We help teams choose software they won't regret.

Vettlo is an independent publication that tests and scores the software businesses run on, from CRM and help desks to accounting and email marketing. Every shortlist shows its criteria, its weights and the prices we checked, so you can see exactly why a tool ranks where it does.

What we do

Shortlists built the way a careful buyer would build them

Most software decisions start with a vendor demo or a "best of" list that ranks whoever pays most. We do the slower work instead: we sign up for trials and free plans, test the workflows each team relies on, score every tool on weighted criteria built for its category, and check every price against the vendor's own pricing page on the date shown.

The result is a shortlist you can check line by line. If a tool hides its prices, puts a key feature on a higher plan or charges per AI resolution, we say so on the page.

Our mission

Make software buying transparent.

Buyers should be able to see why a tool is recommended, what it really costs for a team their size and which plan they'll actually need, without sitting through a sales call to find out.

Our vision

The first place teams check before they buy.

We want every team, from a two-person startup to a growing finance department, to start a software decision with a Vettlo shortlist, and to trust it because they can check it.

Our promise

Four things we will always do

01

Never sell a ranking

Inclusion, order and scores are editorial. Sponsored placements are labeled and sit outside the ranked list.

02

Show our working

Criteria, weights and every rating are published, so any score can be broken down and checked.

03

Check prices at the source

Prices come from each vendor's own pricing page, with the date we checked them on every review.

04

Keep reviews current

We re-check plans and prices at least every 90 days and update the page, and its date, when something changes.

How a review gets made

From a category to a ranked shortlist

Full methodology
  1. Define the jobWe set the criteria and weights that matter for the category, such as automation for email tools or AI resolution costs for help desks.
  2. Test hands-onWe use trials and free plans to work through the everyday tasks each team relies on.
  3. Score and verifyEach tool is rated on every criterion, prices are checked on the vendor's pricing page and user ratings are pulled from G2.
  4. Publish and re-checkTools scoring 70 or more make the shortlist. We revisit each review at least every 90 days.
