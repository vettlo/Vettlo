Write for Vettlo

We publish practitioners: people who have chosen, rolled out or run the software their team depends on.

What we're looking for

  • Hands-on tool reviews in a category you've worked in
  • Playbooks from real rollouts, migrations and stack consolidations
  • Opinionated takes on buying and running software well

What we don't accept

  • Pieces written on behalf of a vendor, or with links a vendor paid for
  • Generic or AI-generated drafts without first-hand experience

How to pitch

Send a short pitch, your LinkedIn profile and two writing samples through our contact page.
