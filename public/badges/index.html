Vettlo badges

Products ranked on a Vettlo shortlist can show it on their own website with a badge. Badges are free and never sold.

Two badges

  • Top pick goes to the product ranked first on a shortlist.
  • Shortlisted goes to every other product in the ranked list.

Each badge names the category and year, and links to the review it comes from.

How to get yours

If your product is ranked on one of our shortlists, contact us from your company email and we'll send the embed code for your product.

Rules

  • Use the badge only for the product, category and year it was issued for.
  • Keep the link to the review and don't change the design.
  • If a product drops off a shortlist at a re-check, its badge should come down.
  • Badges are never a condition of being reviewed, and showing one doesn't affect rankings.
