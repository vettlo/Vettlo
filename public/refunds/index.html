Refund policy

How refunds and cancellations work for positions and listings bought on Vettlo.

Last updated Nov 20, 2026

14-day refunds

You can get a full refund of any payment, whether it's your first payment or a renewal, if you ask within 14 days of that payment. No questions asked.

How to ask for a refund

Email vendors@vettlo.com or use our contact page with the email address you used to pay. We refund to the payment method you used. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

Products we don't accept

If we decline your product after you've paid, or you cancel before your product goes live, we refund the full payment, whenever that happens.

Cancelling a plan

Plans renew automatically every month or year until you cancel in the vendor portal. A cancelled plan doesn't renew and stays live until the end of the period you paid for. After the 14-day window, the current period isn't refunded.

If we end your plan early for a reason other than a breach of our advertiser terms, we refund the unused part of the period.
