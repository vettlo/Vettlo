How Much Does Help Desk Software Cost in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 1, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 1, 2026

Short answer

Entry plans for the help desks we rank start between $7 and $25 per agent per month in US dollars, and Zoho Desk lists euro prices. The entry plan is rarely the full bill: live chat, knowledge bases and SLAs often sit on higher tiers, and most vendors now charge for AI resolutions on top of seats.

What drives the cost

  • Agents. Almost every help desk charges per agent (or seat) per month, so cost scales with team size. Some offer free light or viewer seats.
  • Plan tier. Channels such as live chat and phone, a knowledge base, SLAs and custom reporting often start one or two tiers above the entry plan.
  • AI resolutions. Many vendors now charge per AI resolution on top of seats: Intercom from $0.99 per outcome, Help Scout $0.75 per resolution, and Zendesk $1.50 to $2.00 per resolution above the included amount.
  • Billing period. Annual billing is usually cheaper than paying month to month.
  • One-time fees. Some vendors charge onboarding fees on higher tiers, such as HubSpot Service Hub Professional.

How to keep the bill down

  1. Price the plan that covers the channels you use today, not every channel you might add.
  2. Estimate monthly AI resolutions before turning on an AI agent, and set a cap if the vendor offers one.
  3. Give occasional users free light or collaborator seats where available.
  4. Start on a free plan if your team is small; see the free options below.

FAQ

How much does help desk software cost per agent?

Entry plans for the help desks we rank start between $7 and $25 per agent per month in US dollars, with higher tiers for more channels, SLAs and AI.

Is there free help desk software?

Zoho Desk has a free plan for 3 agents, Help Scout for 5 users with 1 inbox, and HubSpot offers free service tools for up to 2 users.

Do AI agents cost extra?

Many vendors now charge per AI resolution on top of seats: Intercom from $0.99 per outcome, Help Scout $0.75 per resolution, and Zendesk $1.50 to $2.00 per resolution above the included amount.

Which help desk is best value?

Zendesk scores highest in our ranking for teams that need every channel. Zoho Desk is the best value, and Freshdesk is a strong middle ground with AI and SLAs at a lower price.
