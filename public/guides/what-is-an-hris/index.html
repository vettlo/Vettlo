What Is an HRIS? Features, Types and Cost

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 8, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 8, 2026

Short answer

An HRIS (human resources information system) is the system of record for your employees: profiles, job and pay history, documents, time off and onboarding, with self-service for staff and reports for HR. It doesn't calculate pay or file taxes on its own; that's payroll software, which many HRIS vendors sell as a module or connect to. At published prices, the HRIS platforms we rank cost $29 (Connecteam, billed yearly) to $250 (BambooHR) a month for 20 employees, while HiBob quotes every customer.

What an HRIS does

An HRIS replaces the spreadsheets, shared folders and email threads where employee information usually starts out. Every person gets one record, and the rest of the system works off it:

  • Employee records. One profile per person with job, pay and document history.
  • Onboarding and offboarding. Checklists, e-signatures and equipment tasks.
  • Time off. Policies, requests, approvals and balances.
  • Reporting. Headcount, turnover and compensation reports.
  • Integrations. Payroll, benefits, applicant tracking and identity systems.

Three groups use it. HR keeps records accurate, runs onboarding and pulls headcount and turnover reports. Managers approve time off and see their team's details. Employees use self-service to update their address or bank details, request leave, sign documents and find policies, which removes a lot of routine requests from HR's inbox.

Higher plans and add-on modules extend the same record into performance reviews, compensation planning, time tracking, applicant tracking and payroll. Which of those you get, and on which plan, is where HRIS products differ most.

HRIS vs HRMS, HCM, payroll and ATS

HR software terms overlap, and vendors use them loosely. In common usage:

  • HRIS means the core: employee records, documents, time off, onboarding and reporting.
  • HRMS (human resource management system) usually means an HRIS plus operational tools such as time tracking, attendance and payroll.
  • HCM (human capital management) is the broadest term, used mostly by enterprise suites. It adds talent management, such as recruiting, performance, learning, compensation and workforce planning, on top of core HR, as in Workday HCM.

Two neighboring categories are separate products, even when one vendor sells both:

  • Payroll software. An HRIS stores employee records, time off, onboarding and reviews; payroll software calculates pay and files taxes. BambooHR and Rippling sell both in one platform. See our payroll software ranking if you need full-service payroll.
  • Applicant tracking systems. An ATS manages hiring, from job posts to offers; an HRIS manages people after they're hired, with records, time off and onboarding. Workable includes a basic HRIS on every plan, and BambooHR includes applicant tracking. See our applicant tracking ranking for the hiring side.

What matters in practice is that payroll, hiring and the HRIS share one employee record, either inside one platform or through an integration, so nobody types the same new hire in three places.

The main types of HRIS

  • Core HRIS for small and midsize companies covers records, time off, onboarding and reporting in an easy package, with reviews and hiring on higher plans. BambooHR and Factorial sit here.
  • All-in-one HR, payroll and IT platforms sell modules on one employee database, so onboarding can set up pay, apps and devices together. Rippling is the best-known example; our BambooHR vs Rippling comparison covers the trade-off.
  • Mid-market and international HR platforms focus on culture, engagement and multi-country teams, with talent, compensation and payroll as add-on modules. HiBob works this way.
  • Low-cost HR suites publish low per-user prices, often with a free tier, and fit best alongside the same vendor's other apps. Zoho People is one.
  • Mobile-first apps for frontline teams put documents, time off and onboarding on employees' phones for staff who don't sit at a computer, at flat prices per user band. Connecteam sits here.

Other HR platforms in the category include Personio, Paycor, Workday HCM and Deel. Our HRIS shortlist scores the ranked platforms on core HR, time off and onboarding, performance, payroll and integrations, ease of use and price.

How to choose an HRIS

  1. Price your headcount, not the entry price. At 20 employees, BambooHR Core costs $250 a month, Factorial from about $160 at its $8 starting price, Rippling's HRIS module from $160 before other modules, and Connecteam HR Basic $29 billed yearly.
  2. Decide when you need performance reviews. BambooHR adds review cycles on Pro ($425 a month up to 25 employees), Zoho People on Premium, and HiBob and Rippling sell performance as a separate module.
  3. Check where time tracking lives. Factorial sells time tracking and shift scheduling as modules; Zoho People adds timesheets on Professional; BambooHR sells Time & Attendance as an add-on.
  4. Plan the payroll connection. BambooHR and Rippling sell payroll in the same platform (BambooHR for US employees only). Zoho People pairs with Zoho Payroll; Connecteam and Factorial mostly connect to an outside payroll provider.
  5. Ask for the full quote in writing. Rippling, HiBob and Factorial quote most of what you buy. Ask for the per-employee price, every module and any implementation fee before you compare.

FAQ

What does HRIS stand for?

Human resources information system: software that stores and manages employee data, such as personal details, job and pay history, documents and time off, and the HR processes built on it, such as onboarding.

What's the difference between an HRIS and payroll software?

An HRIS stores employee records, time off, onboarding and reviews; payroll software calculates pay and files taxes. BambooHR and Rippling sell both in one platform. See our payroll software ranking if you need full-service payroll.

How much does an HRIS cost?

At published prices, the HRIS platforms we rank cost $29 (Connecteam, billed yearly) to $250 (BambooHR) a month for 20 employees on their entry plans. HiBob doesn't publish prices. Factorial and Rippling's price covers only the base HRIS module.

Is there a free HRIS?

Yes. Zoho People is free for up to 5 users, Connecteam's Small Business plan is free for up to 10 users with every hub, and Connecteam's limited free HR plan covers up to 30 users with fewer features.
