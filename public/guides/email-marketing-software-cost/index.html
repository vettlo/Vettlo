How Much Does Email Marketing Software Cost in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 1, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 1, 2026

Short answer

Paid email marketing plans start from $8.08 a month (Brevo) among the tools we rank, and the price rises with your list size. On Oct 26, 2026: ActiveCampaign Starter $15 a month and MailerLite Comfort $17.10 (both billed yearly), Mailchimp Essentials $26.50, Klaviyo Email $30, and Kit Creator $33 billed yearly. Most tools also have a free plan for small lists.

Three ways vendors charge

  • By contacts or subscribers. The most common model: the price steps up as your list grows, usually with a monthly send limit tied to list size. Mailchimp, MailerLite, ActiveCampaign and Kit work this way.
  • By active profiles. Similar, but counted on profiles you can message. Klaviyo prices email this way.
  • By emails sent. You pay for monthly volume rather than list size, which suits big lists that send a few campaigns a month. Brevo prices this way.

Because the models differ, compare tools at your own list size and sending frequency, not at the headline price.

What the higher tiers add

Entry plans usually cover campaigns and basic forms. The next tier is where multi-step automation, A/B testing, landing pages and removal of the vendor's logo tend to appear, and the top tier adds things like phone support, more seats and advanced segmentation. Our shortlist notes which plan first includes each of these for every tool.

Free plans

Kit's free plan covers up to 10,000 subscribers, Brevo's free plan sends 300 emails a day, and MailerLite's free plan includes 250 subscribers, 2,500 emails a month and 3 automations.

Free plans cap contacts or sends and often lock automation, so they suit a new list or a simple newsletter.

Why do prices go up when my list grows?
