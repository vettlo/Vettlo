What Is Conversation Intelligence Software? Features and Cost

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 5, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 5, 2026

Short answer

Conversation intelligence software records and transcribes sales calls and video meetings, then analyzes them to coach reps, flag deal risks and write notes back to the CRM. Unlike a plain AI notetaker, it adds call scoring, coaching workflows and reporting across the whole team. Among the tools we rank, plans that include these features start at $19 per seat per month (Fireflies.ai), while Gong sells by quote.

What conversation intelligence software does

The tool joins your calls and video meetings as a recorder, or connects to your phone system, and works in three steps: it captures the conversation, transcribes it with speaker labels, then analyzes what was said. Around that, platforms add:

  • Recording and transcription. Automatic capture of calls and video meetings.
  • AI summaries and notes. Action items and key moments pushed to the CRM.
  • Call scoring and coaching. Scorecards against a sales methodology and talk-ratio analysis.
  • Deal and pipeline insights. Risk alerts based on what buyers say and how engaged they are.
  • Searchable call library. Find every mention of a competitor, objection or feature.

Reps use it to skip note-taking and follow-up admin. Sales managers use it to coach without sitting in on calls: they review scored calls, jump to key moments such as pricing or objections, and see patterns across the team, such as which reps talk too much or which competitors come up most.

Conversation intelligence vs AI notetakers

The line between the two has blurred, because many notetakers now sell a sales plan. The useful difference is who the output is for:

  • An AI notetaker records, transcribes and summarizes meetings for the people in them. It's a personal productivity tool, and it works the same for a sales call, a stand-up or an interview.
  • Conversation intelligence treats calls as team data. It adds call scoring and scorecards, coaching workflows for managers, analytics across reps, deal risk signals and structured CRM updates.

The practical effect is on price: the sales features usually sit on a higher plan or a paid add-on. Fireflies.ai's Pro plan starts at $10, but conversation intelligence starts on Business at $19 per seat per month. Avoma's Startup plan is $19 per recording seat, but AI call scoring and coaching come with its Conversation Intelligence add-on at $29 more, so a seat with coaching costs $48, or $77 with Revenue Intelligence for deal risk and forecasting. Otter.ai's Business plan syncs notes to Salesforce or HubSpot for up to 5 users; the Sales Notetaker and live coaching are on Enterprise. Sales dialers also record and transcribe calls; if your team mostly calls by phone, compare with our sales dialer shortlist.

The main types of conversation intelligence tool

  • Enterprise revenue platforms put coaching, deal inspection and forecasting on the same conversation data, sold by annual contract to sales orgs with a RevOps team. Gong is the best-known example.
  • Meeting assistants with conversation intelligence add-ons publish prices and let you buy only the modules you need, such as call scoring or deal risk. Avoma works this way.
  • AI sales assistants focus on the admin after a call: summaries, follow-up emails and CRM field updates for individual reps, with lighter team coaching. Sybill sits here.
  • Notetakers with a sales tier start as general meeting recorders and add conversation intelligence and team analytics on a business plan, at the lowest prices in the category. Fireflies.ai is one.

Other tools in the category include Chorus by ZoomInfo, Jiminny, Modjo, tl;dv, Fathom and Grain. Our shortlist scores the ranked tools on recording, coaching, deal insights, CRM integration, ease of use and price.

How to choose a conversation intelligence tool

  1. Decide who the tool is for. Manager-led coaching across many reps points to Gong or Avoma's add-ons. Saving reps time on notes and CRM updates points to Sybill or Fireflies.ai.
  2. Price the plan with the features you need. Avoma's coaching is a $29 add-on and Fireflies.ai's conversation intelligence starts on Business. Compare the plan that includes call scoring, not the cheapest one.
  3. Count recorder seats, not users. Avoma charges only for people who record; viewers are free. That changes the math for teams where managers mostly listen.
  4. Check consent and recording rules. Recording laws vary by country and US state. Confirm how each tool announces the recorder and handles consent before rollout.
  5. Test CRM sync on real deals. Run a trial on live calls and check that summaries and fields land on the right CRM records.

FAQ

What is conversation intelligence software?

It records and transcribes sales calls and meetings, then analyzes them for coaching, deal risks and trends, and often writes notes and fields back to the CRM.

What is the difference between conversation intelligence and an AI notetaker?

A notetaker records and summarizes meetings for the people in them. Conversation intelligence treats calls as team data: it scores calls, supports manager coaching, reports across reps and flags deal risks. Many notetakers sell these features on a higher plan.

How much does conversation intelligence software cost?

Among the tools we rank, plans that include conversation intelligence features cost $19 (Fireflies.ai) to $77 (Avoma) per seat per month at published prices, or $2,280 to $9,240 a year for 10 seats. Gong doesn't publish prices.

Is there a free conversation intelligence tool?

Fireflies.ai and Sybill have free plans for recording, transcripts and summaries, with limits. Coaching and team analytics need a paid plan.
