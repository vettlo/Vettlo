How Much Does Payroll Software Cost in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 4, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 4, 2026

Short answer

Full-service payroll software, which files your payroll taxes, costs a monthly base fee of $35 to $50 plus $5 to $7 per employee among the providers we rank that publish prices. For 10 employees that's $87 a month (Patriot Payroll) to $120 (QuickBooks Payroll), or $1,044 to $1,440 a year. Rippling and RUN Powered by ADP price by quote.

How payroll software is priced

Almost every small-business payroll service charges the same way: a monthly base fee for the account plus a fee for each person you pay that month. The base fee matters most for very small teams; past a handful of employees, the per-person fee decides the bill.

  • Full-service vs do-it-yourself. Full-service plans calculate, file and pay federal, state and local payroll taxes for you. Patriot also sells a Basic plan for $17 a month plus $4 per worker, but you file the taxes yourself.
  • Who counts as a person. The per-person fee can cover contractors as well as employees; OnPay, for example, counts both. If you pay many contractors, check how each vendor counts them, or look at a contractor-only plan.
  • Plan tiers. Higher tiers raise both the base and the per-person fee, and add things like multi-state payroll, faster direct deposit, time tracking and HR support.
  • Quotes. Platforms that bundle payroll with HR, benefits or IT often publish only a starting price or none at all.

Our Gusto vs QuickBooks Payroll comparison matches these tiers feature by feature. Other costs to price in:

  • Extra states. OnPay, Square Payroll and ADP RUN include multi-state payroll on their entry plan. Gusto needs Plus, and Patriot charges $12 a month for each additional state. At 10 employees in two states, Patriot Full Service comes to $99 a month.
  • OnPay add-ons. OnPay's single plan is $109 a month for 10 people. Its HR tools ($15 + $2 per worker) add $35 and time tracking ($15 + $4 per worker) adds $55, so the bundle comes to $199 a month.
  • Faster direct deposit. QuickBooks includes next-day direct deposit from Workforce Payroll and same-day from Premium; Gusto puts next-day on Plus or sells it as a Simple add-on.
  • Contractors. Square Payroll's contractor-only payroll costs $6 per person with no base fee; Gusto's Contractor plan costs $35 a month plus $6 per contractor.
  • Benefits and insurance. Health insurance, retirement plans and workers' compensation are usually separate products or add-ons, priced on top of payroll.

How to budget for payroll software

  1. Count everyone you pay, employees and contractors, and the states they work in.
  2. Find the cheapest plan on each provider that covers your must-haves, such as multi-state payroll, next-day deposit or time tracking, then multiply its per-person fee by your headcount and add the base fee.
  3. Add the add-ons you'll switch on, such as HR tools, time tracking or extra states, and multiply by 12.
  4. Look past introductory offers. Patriot, QuickBooks and ADP all advertise discounts or free months for new customers. Compare the regular price you'll pay in month four.
  5. Run a test payroll before your next pay date, and check how it posts to your accounting software.

FAQ

How much does payroll software cost per employee?

Among the providers we rank that publish prices, full-service payroll costs $5 to $7 per employee per month on the entry plan, plus a monthly base fee of $35 to $50.

How much does payroll software cost for 10 employees?

Most small-business payroll costs a monthly base fee plus a per-person fee. At published prices, full-service payroll for 10 employees costs $87 a month on Patriot Full Service, $95 on Square Payroll, $109 on Gusto Simple or OnPay and $120 on QuickBooks Workforce Payroll.

What is the cheapest payroll software?

Patriot Basic is $17 a month plus $4 per worker, but you file payroll taxes yourself. For full-service payroll that files taxes, Patriot ($37 + $5 per worker) and Square Payroll ($35 + $6 per person) are the cheapest here.

Does ADP publish payroll prices?

No. Each RUN Powered by ADP package (Essential, Enhanced, Complete and HR Pro) shows a Get pricing button, and ADP was advertising up to 6 months free for businesses that switch when we checked on Nov 7, 2026.
