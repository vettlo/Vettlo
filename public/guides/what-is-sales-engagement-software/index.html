What Is Sales Engagement Software? Features, Types and Cost

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 3, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 3, 2026

Short answer

Sales engagement software runs multi-step outreach sequences across email, phone and LinkedIn, so reps contact prospects on schedule and stop when someone replies. It sits next to the CRM: the CRM is the record of contacts and deals, while the engagement platform does the outreach and logs the activity back. Published entry prices among the platforms we rank start at $49 per user per month (Apollo.io and Reply.io), while Salesloft and Outreach sell by quote.

What sales engagement software does

A sequence is the core of every sales engagement platform: a timed series of steps, such as an email on day one, a call task on day three and a LinkedIn message on day five, that a prospect moves through until they reply, book a meeting or reach the end. Around that, platforms add:

  • Sequences. Timed series of emails, calls and tasks that stop when a prospect replies.
  • Email deliverability tools. Mailbox warm-up, sending limits and domain health checks.
  • Dialer and call tasks. Calling from the browser, often with recording.
  • CRM sync. Two-way sync of contacts, activities and outcomes.
  • Analytics and A/B testing. Reply and meeting rates by sequence, step and template.

The result is that follow-up no longer depends on each rep's memory or inbox. Managers can see which sequences, steps and templates get replies and copy what works across the team.

Sales engagement vs CRM vs cold email tools

The three overlap, so it helps to separate what each is for:

  • A CRM is the system of record: contacts, companies, deals and pipeline stages. CRMs such as HubSpot Sales Hub Professional and Pipedrive Growth include email sequences. Dedicated platforms add multichannel steps, deliverability controls and deeper analytics.
  • A sales engagement platform runs the outreach itself: multichannel sequences across email, calls and LinkedIn, sending controls, and reply and meeting analytics for managers, with the activity synced back to the CRM.
  • Cold email tools are narrower. Cold email tools such as Instantly focus on sending volume and deliverability across many inboxes.

Most outbound teams run a CRM plus one outreach tool. If your CRM's built-in sequences cover email-only follow-up for a handful of reps, you may not need a second tool yet; see our CRM shortlist for which plans include them.

The main types of sales engagement platform

  • Enterprise sales execution platforms add governed sequences, call recording, deal management and forecasting for large teams with a RevOps owner. Outreach and Salesloft sit here; our Outreach vs Salesloft comparison covers which package to ask each for.
  • Data plus engagement in one tool bundles a B2B contact database with sequencing, so you don't buy prospect data separately. Apollo.io is the best-known example; credits limit how many contacts you can reveal.
  • Multichannel outreach tools run email, LinkedIn and call steps at a published, self-serve price for SMB and mid-market teams, such as Reply.io and lemlist.
  • Cold email platforms focus on sending volume and deliverability across many connected inboxes, such as Instantly. They're email-first rather than full multichannel suites.

Inbox add-ons that put sequences inside Gmail, such as Mixmax, are a lighter fifth option. Our shortlist scores each platform on sequencing, deliverability, data, CRM sync, analytics and price.

How to choose a sales engagement platform

  1. Decide where your contact data comes from. If you don't already pay for a data provider, a tool with a built-in database such as Apollo.io removes a second contract.
  2. Protect your domain first. For cold outreach, check warm-up, inbox rotation and sending limits before features. A burned domain costs more than any license.
  3. Match the platform to team size. Outreach and Salesloft pay off with many reps and a RevOps owner; smaller teams usually move faster with Apollo.io, Reply.io or lemlist.
  4. Ask for the real price. Outreach and Salesloft don't publish prices. Get a written quote for your seat count and contract length before comparing.
  5. Check CRM sync both ways. Activities, replies and meeting outcomes should land in the CRM automatically, or reps will stop logging them.

FAQ

What is a sales sequence?

A sequence is a timed series of outreach steps, such as emails, call tasks and LinkedIn messages, that a prospect moves through automatically. It stops when the prospect replies or books a meeting, so reps don't follow up with someone who already answered.

Is sales engagement software the same as a CRM?

No. A CRM stores contacts, companies and deals and tracks the pipeline; a sales engagement platform runs the outreach and syncs the resulting activity back to the CRM. Some CRMs include basic sequences, and some engagement platforms include light deal tracking.

How much does sales engagement software cost?

Per-user entry plans for the platforms we rank start at $49 per user per month (Apollo.io and Reply.io). Salesloft and Outreach don't publish prices. lemlist and Instantly list plan prices without a per-user unit; see our shortlist for each plan's terms.

Is there free sales engagement software?

Apollo.io has a free plan with 2 sequences and 900 credits per seat per year, and Mixmax has a free plan for 50 recipients a month. Most others offer 14-day trials.
