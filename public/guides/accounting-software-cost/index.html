How Much Does Accounting Software Cost in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 7, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 7, 2026

Short answer

Small business accounting software costs $15 (Zoho Books) to $38 (QuickBooks Online) a month at regular prices for the entry plan among the tools we rank, or $180 to $456 a year. It's priced per business, not per user, but plans cap users, invoices or clients, so growing teams often pay for a mid-tier plan, and Zoho Books and Wave have free plans. Introductory discounts cut the first few months, not the price you'll pay after that.

How accounting software is priced

Small business accounting tools charge a flat monthly fee for your business (the company file or organization), not a fee per employee. What decides your price is which plan you need, and plans are gated in a few ways:

  • Users. Some plans include a fixed number of people who can log in. QuickBooks Online steps up by users, while Xero has no per-user fees on any plan.
  • Volume. Entry plans may cap invoices and bills (Xero Early) or billable clients (FreshBooks Lite), and Zoho Books caps invoices, bills and records per year on every plan.
  • Features. These usually start on mid-tier plans: QuickBooks Plus, Xero Established or Zoho Books Professional.
  • Introductory offers. QuickBooks, Xero and FreshBooks advertise big introductory discounts. Budget for the price you'll pay after the first few months.
  • Billing period. Zoho Books quotes its headline price billed annually and charges more month to month; QuickBooks and Xero list monthly prices.

Mid-market financial software such as Sage Intacct works differently: it's sold by module and priced by quote, usually with an implementation project.

Beyond the plan itself, budget for:

  • Payroll. Payroll is usually a separate subscription. Xero Payroll, powered by Gusto, costs $36 a month plus $6 per employee or contractor, or $96 a month for 10 people, on top of any Xero plan. QuickBooks offers payroll as an add-on at checkout but doesn't list its price on the accounting pricing page. Our payroll cost guide compares the providers.
  • Extra people. FreshBooks charges $11 a month for each team member you add. Zoho Books sells extra users for $2.50 a month billed annually ($3 monthly). Xero has no per-user fees on any plan.
  • Feature add-ons. Xero Early can add auto-reconcile for $5 a month, which brings it to $32; Growing includes it.
  • Payment and bill-pay fees. Wave's free plan charges card processing fees when customers pay invoices online. QuickBooks includes free standard ACH bill payments up to a monthly allotment, then charges $0.50 per transaction. Online card and bank payments from customers carry processing fees with most tools, so check the rates if you get paid through your invoices.
  • Your accountant or bookkeeper. Most plans include free access for your accountant (QuickBooks includes 2 or 3 accountant users depending on the plan), but their fees for bookkeeping, month-end close and tax returns are a separate cost, and often a larger one than the software.
  • Mid-market software. Sage Intacct is priced by modules and company profile, and it needs implementation, so expect a quote that covers both. See our Sage Intacct review.

How to budget for accounting software

  1. Count the people who need access, including a bookkeeper, and the invoices and bills you handle each month. Those set your plan.
  2. List the features you'll need within a year, such as inventory, projects, multi-currency or bill approvals, and find the first plan on each tool that includes them.
  3. Multiply the regular price by 12, then add payroll, extra users and any add-ons.
  4. Ask your accountant. If you work with an accountant or bookkeeper, use the tool they already know; it saves hours at month end and tax time.
  5. Make sure your bank connects. Bank feeds drive reconciliation. Check that your bank is supported before you migrate.

FAQ

How much does accounting software cost per month?

Entry plans for the accounting tools we rank cost $15 to $38 a month at regular prices. Plans with more users, inventory or projects cost more; for example, QuickBooks Advanced is $340, Xero Established is $97 and Zoho Books Ultimate is $240 a month.

How much does QuickBooks Online cost?

QuickBooks Online's regular prices are $38 a month for Simple Start, $85 for Essentials, $140 for Plus and $340 for Advanced, with introductory discounts for new customers.

Is Xero cheaper than QuickBooks?

At regular prices, Xero Early is $27 a month versus $38 for QuickBooks Simple Start, and Xero has no per-user fees. Xero Early caps you at 20 invoices and 5 bills a month.

Is there free accounting software?

Wave's Starter plan is free with unlimited invoices and bills. Zoho Books is free for businesses under $50K in annual revenue.
