How Much Does a Business Password Manager Cost in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 6, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 6, 2026

Short answer

Business password managers charge per user per month, billed annually. Among the tools we rank, entry plans cost $1.99 (Proton Pass) to $8.99 (1Password) per user, or $238.80 to $1,078.80 a year for 10 users. Single sign-on usually needs a higher plan: SSO starts at $4.99 a user on Proton Pass Professional, $6 on Bitwarden Enterprise, $7 on LastPass Business (3 apps), $8 on Dashlane and $8.99 on 1Password Business.

How business password managers are priced

Every password manager we rank uses the same basic model: a price per user per month, with the headline price assuming you pay for a year up front. What changes between vendors is which plan unlocks the features IT asks for.

  • Plan ladders. Most vendors sell two or three business plans. The entry plan covers vaults, sharing and an admin console; higher plans add single sign-on (SSO), automatic provisioning from your identity provider (SCIM), advanced policies and reporting.
  • User limits. Some plans only apply to a certain team size. Proton Pass Essentials is for 3–30 users and LastPass Teams is for under 50 users; outside those limits you move to another plan.
  • Flat small-team packs. 1Password also sells Teams Starter Pack for $24.95 a month for up to 10 users, a fixed fee rather than a per-user price.
  • Quotes at the top. 1Password and Dashlane price their enterprise plans by quote.
  • Free tiers. Bitwarden's free organization lets two people share passwords without paying. Beyond that, business features need a paid plan.

How to budget for a business password manager

  1. Count seats, not just employees. Include contractors and anyone who needs shared logins, and check the plan's user minimum or maximum.
  2. Decide whether you need SSO and SCIM now. If your company already uses an identity provider such as Okta or Microsoft Entra ID, price the plan that connects to it, since that is usually the one you end up on.
  3. Ask about add-ons before you sign. Keeper sells advanced reporting and compliance reports as add-ons, and Bitwarden sells Secrets Manager separately. Price the bundle you will run.
  4. Count the family accounts. 1Password Business, Bitwarden Enterprise, Keeper Business, Dashlane and LastPass Business include free family accounts, which keeps employees' personal logins out of the work vault.
  5. Multiply by 12. Headline prices assume annual billing, so the first invoice is a full year.

FAQ

How much does a business password manager cost?

At published annual prices, 10 users cost $238.80 a year on Proton Pass Essentials, $240 on Keeper Business Starter, $480 on Keeper Business, Bitwarden Teams or LastPass Teams, $960 on Dashlane and $1,078.80 on 1Password Business.

Which business password manager includes SSO for the lowest price?

Proton Pass Professional, at $4.99 per user per month billed annually, includes SSO and SCIM. Bitwarden Enterprise is next at $6 and adds self-hosting and free Families plans for staff.

What is the cheapest business password manager?

Proton Pass has the lowest entry price we found, $1.99 per user per month billed annually on Pass Essentials (3–30 users), or $238.80 a year for 10 users. It doesn't include SSO on that plan.

Is there a free password manager for business?

Bitwarden has a free organization for two people that can share passwords. It is the only free business option among the tools we rank; for more people or admin features you need a paid plan, and the others offer free trials.
