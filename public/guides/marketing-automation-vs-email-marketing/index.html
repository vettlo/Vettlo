Marketing Automation vs Email Marketing: What's the Difference?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 9, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 9, 2026

Short answer

Email marketing software sends newsletters and campaigns to a list and runs simple triggered emails, such as a welcome series. Marketing automation software runs multi-step journeys across email and other channels based on what each contact does, scores leads and syncs with a CRM so sales knows who is ready to buy. Among the tools we rank, email marketing entry plans cost $8.08 (Brevo) to $33 (Kit) a month at the smallest list size, while marketing automation entry plans run from $7 per seat (HubSpot Marketing Hub) to $1,250 (Salesforce Marketing Cloud Account Engagement) a month, and lead scoring usually sits several tiers above the entry plan.

What each one does

Email marketing software

Email marketing software sends newsletters and campaigns to your subscribers and runs automated emails, such as welcome series, abandoned-cart reminders and lead nurturing. It also manages sign-up forms, list segmentation and reporting on opens, clicks and revenue. The core features:

  • Email builder and templates. Drag-and-drop design for campaigns and newsletters.
  • Automation. Triggered sequences based on sign-ups, behavior or dates.
  • Segmentation. Send to subsets of your list based on data and engagement.
  • Forms and landing pages. Grow the list from your website.
  • Reporting and A/B testing. Opens, clicks, conversions and subject-line tests.

Marketing automation software

Marketing automation software runs multi-step campaigns across email and other channels based on what each lead does, and scores leads so sales knows who is ready to talk. It goes beyond email marketing with lead scoring, CRM sync and journeys that span the whole funnel. The core features:

  • Visual journey builder. Branching workflows triggered by behavior, data or dates.
  • Lead scoring. Points for engagement and fit, with handoff to sales at a threshold.
  • CRM integration. Two-way sync so sales and marketing see the same data.
  • Multichannel messaging. Email plus SMS, in-app or ads audiences, depending on the tool.
  • Attribution and reporting. Which campaigns create pipeline and revenue.

The short version: an email tool is organized around the list and the campaign, and asks "what do we send this week?" A marketing automation platform is organized around the contact and the journey, and asks "what should happen next for this person, and are they ready for sales?"

Where the two overlap

The categories blur in the middle. ActiveCampaign and Brevo appear in both of our rankings, scored against each category's own criteria:

Tools like these are a sensible middle step: you get campaigns and newsletters on the entry plan and can move up to deeper journeys without migrating your list. The catch is that the automation features usually start one or two plans up. Mailchimp Essentials caps flows at 4 steps, ActiveCampaign Starter at 5 actions per automation, and Brevo Starter has no automation.

At the other end, HubSpot Marketing Hub sells a Starter plan with limited automation and puts full workflows and lead scoring on Professional, which is why its alternatives page and our HubSpot vs ActiveCampaign comparison focus on that jump.

Which one do you need?

Email marketing software is enough if you mostly send newsletters, promotions and a handful of automated emails; you sell online and want abandoned-cart and post-purchase flows; or there's no sales team waiting for qualified leads. Start with our email marketing shortlist (MailerLite ranks first).

You need marketing automation if sales needs to know which leads are ready, you want journeys that branch on behavior across several channels, or you have to show which campaigns create pipeline. Start with our marketing automation shortlist (ActiveCampaign ranks first).

Whichever you pick, two checks from our rankings apply:

  1. Price the plan that includes your must-have feature. Unlimited automation actions start at $49 a month on ActiveCampaign Plus and $18 on Brevo Standard; HubSpot puts full workflows on Professional at $800 a month.
  2. Test one real workflow in the trial. Recreate your welcome or nurture sequence, with one branch and one goal, before you migrate contacts.

FAQ

What is the difference between email marketing and marketing automation?

Email marketing tools focus on campaigns and simple automations. Marketing automation platforms add multi-step journeys across channels, lead scoring and attribution; for example, HubSpot Professional includes up to 300 workflows and 5 lead scores, and Account Engagement Plus+ adds B2B Marketing Analytics.

Is marketing automation just automated email?

No. Automated emails are part of it, but marketing automation also scores leads, branches journeys on behavior, can use channels such as SMS, and syncs with a CRM so marketing and sales share the same view of each contact.

Which tools do both email marketing and marketing automation?

ActiveCampaign and Brevo are in both of our rankings: campaigns and newsletters on the entry plans, with deeper automation on higher plans.

Which marketing automation tool has lead scoring for the lowest price?

Brevo Professional includes contact scoring from $499 a month. HubSpot includes lead scoring on Professional ($800 a month plus $3,000 onboarding) and Salesforce on Account Engagement Growth+ ($1,250 a month). ActiveCampaign offers it through its Pipelines add-on from Plus.
