Klaviyo vs Mailchimp: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 8, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 8, 2026

We may earn money when you click links on this page. Partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our email marketing ranking, Klaviyo scores 84/100 and Mailchimp scores 79/100. Klaviyo rates higher on automation and segmentation because its flows run on store data, while Mailchimp costs less to start at 500 contacts, is easier to learn and lists seats and phone support plan by plan.

Pick Klaviyo if…

  • You run an online store and want segments and flows built on purchase and browsing data
  • You want flows, segmentation and A/B testing on the entry plan (Email, $20 a month at 500 profiles)
  • You want email, SMS, WhatsApp, reviews and a Customer Hub on one customer profile

Pick Mailchimp if…

  • You want the lower entry price ($13 vs $20 a month at 500 contacts)
  • Several people need access (3 seats on Essentials, 5 on Standard, unlimited on Premium)
  • You want phone support and 4 onboarding sessions with a dedicated specialist (Premium)

Where Klaviyo is stronger

  • Segments and flows on store data. Flows and segments use real-time purchase and browsing data from Shopify, WooCommerce and similar platforms, which is why it rates 5/5 on automation and 4.5/5 on segmentation in our ranking, against 3.5 and 4 for Mailchimp.
  • Automation from the free plan. Flows are listed from the free plan, and Email adds A/B testing at $20 a month for 500 profiles. On Mailchimp, the free plan has no flows and Essentials caps them at 4 steps.
  • More channels on one profile. SMS, MMS, WhatsApp and RCS, reviews, a Customer Hub and a helpdesk sit on the same customer profile as email, each billed separately.
  • Billing on active profiles. Unsubscribed and suppressed profiles don't count toward your tier.

Where Mailchimp is stronger

  • Lower entry price. Essentials is $13 a month at 500 contacts against $20 for Klaviyo Email, $84 less a year.
  • Easier to learn. Its editor and reports are polished and quick to pick up, which is why it rates 4.5/5 on ease of use in our ranking against 3.5 for Klaviyo.
  • Seats and support by plan. Essentials includes 3 seats, Standard 5 and Premium unlimited; Premium adds phone and priority support and 4 onboarding sessions.
  • Predictive segments and AI on Standard. Standard ($20 at 500 contacts) adds predictive segmentation, multivariate testing, send time optimization and generative AI at no additional cost.

How to decide

  1. Decide whether your email is driven by store data; if it is, start with Klaviyo.
  2. Count your contacts and price both at your real tier, not the 500-contact entry price.
  3. Count the people who need access: Mailchimp lists 3 seats on Essentials and 5 on Standard.
  4. Build one campaign and one automated flow on each free plan before you move your list.

Klaviyo vs Mailchimp FAQ

Is Klaviyo more expensive than Mailchimp?

At 500 contacts, on the entry plans, yes: Klaviyo Email is $20 a month against $13 for Mailchimp Essentials. Mailchimp Standard, which adds branching flows and predictive segments, is also $20. At 10,000 contacts Mailchimp lists $130 for Essentials and $200 for Standard for the first 12 months; check Klaviyo's slider at your list size.

Which is better for ecommerce?

Klaviyo. Its segments and flows use real-time store data from platforms such as Shopify and WooCommerce, and it rates 5/5 on automation in our ranking against 3.5/5 for Mailchimp.

Which has the better free plan?

Klaviyo's, for automation. Both cover 250 contacts and 500 sends a month, but Klaviyo's includes flows and segmentation and email support for 60 days. Mailchimp's has no automation flows, 1 seat and email support for 30 days.

Which scores higher in Vettlo's ranking?

Klaviyo, with 84/100 against 79/100 for Mailchimp. Klaviyo rates higher on automation and segmentation; Mailchimp rates higher on ease of use and price.
