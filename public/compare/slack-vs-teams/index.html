Slack vs Microsoft Teams: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 7, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 7, 2026

We may earn money when you click links on this page. Partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our team chat ranking, Slack scores 90/100 and Microsoft Teams scores 88/100. Slack is the stronger chat app, with unlimited integrations and shared channels on Pro, while Teams costs less and includes 300-person meetings from its $4 Essentials plan.

Pick Slack if…

  • Chat is the center of your day and you connect it to many other tools: Pro has unlimited integrations
  • You work in shared channels with clients or partners: Pro supports up to 250 outside organizations
  • You want AI summaries of threads, channels and huddles on the $7.25 plan
  • You'd like monthly billing: Pro is $8.75 a user month to month

Pick Microsoft Teams if…

  • You want the lowest price: Teams Essentials is $4 a user paid yearly
  • You need large meetings in the same app: 300 participants and 30-hour meetings from Essentials
  • Your company already uses Microsoft 365, or wants email and Office web apps for $7 a user
  • You need 1 TB of file storage per user, included from Business Basic

Where Slack is stronger

  • Better chat and integrations. Pro removes the free plan's 10-integration cap, and Slack scores 5/5 for messaging and integrations in our ranking against 4/5 for Teams.
  • Shared channels with outside companies. Pro supports external channels with up to 250 organizations.
  • AI on the entry plan. Thread, channel and huddle summaries are included on Pro; Teams needs a $21 Copilot add-on below Business Standard.
  • Monthly billing. Pro is $8.75 a user month to month; Microsoft's business plans are paid yearly.

Where Microsoft Teams is stronger

  • Lower price. Teams Essentials costs $1,200 a year for 25 users, against $2,175 for Slack Pro.
  • Meetings included. Every business plan includes 300-participant, 30-hour meetings.
  • Email and Office apps. Business Basic adds business email, 1 TB of storage per user and Word, Excel and PowerPoint on the web for $7.
  • Better free meetings. Free Teams allows 60-minute group meetings for up to 100 people; Slack's free plan allows 1:1 huddles only.

How to decide

  1. Check whether your company already pays for Microsoft 365; if so, Teams is already included in most plans.
  2. List the outside companies you chat with; Slack Pro's shared channels are the main reason teams pay for both.
  3. Price SSO: SAML SSO is on Slack Business+ at $15 a user, so compare that with your Microsoft identity setup.
  4. Run one active project in both for a week and compare search, notifications and calls on phones and laptops.

Slack vs Microsoft Teams FAQ

Is Slack or Microsoft Teams cheaper?

Teams. Teams Essentials is $4 per user per month paid yearly, and Slack Pro is $7.25 billed annually. For 25 users that's $1,200 a year on Teams and $2,175 on Slack.

Does either have a free plan?

Yes. Slack's free plan keeps 90 days of messages with 10 integrations and 1:1 huddles. Free Teams allows group meetings of up to 60 minutes for 100 people with 5 GB of storage per user.

Which is better for working with other companies?

Slack. Pro supports shared channels with up to 250 outside organizations, so clients and partners can work in the same channel.

Which scores higher in Vettlo's ranking?

Slack, with 90/100 against 88/100 for Microsoft Teams. Slack rates higher on messaging, integrations and ease of use; Teams rates higher on calls, admin and price.
