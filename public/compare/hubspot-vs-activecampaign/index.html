HubSpot Marketing Hub vs ActiveCampaign: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Oct 31, 2026 · Prices verified Oct 31, 2026

Short answer

In our marketing automation ranking, HubSpot Marketing Hub scores 85/100 and ActiveCampaign scores 86/100. ActiveCampaign offers comparable automation depth for a fraction of the price, while HubSpot includes lead scoring and custom reports on Professional, connected to its CRM, at $800 a month plus a $3,000 onboarding fee.

Pick HubSpot Marketing Hub if…

  • You want workflows, lead scoring and custom reports on one plan without add-ons
  • Your sales team already works in HubSpot CRM
  • You need multi-touch revenue attribution (Enterprise)

Pick ActiveCampaign if…

  • You want unlimited automation actions for $49 a month instead of $800
  • You'd rather not pay an onboarding fee
  • You need attribution and predictive sending for under $100 a month

Where HubSpot Marketing Hub is stronger

  • Lead scoring and reports on one plan. Professional includes up to 5 lead scores and 100 custom reports with no add-ons.
  • One system with the CRM. Marketing Hub runs on HubSpot's CRM, so sales sees scores and campaign history on the same records.
  • A free plan. The free plan covers up to 2 users and includes email automation and lead capture forms.

Where ActiveCampaign is stronger

  • Much lower price for full automation. Unlimited automation actions cost $588 a year on Plus at 1,000 contacts, against $12,600 in year one for HubSpot Professional with onboarding.
  • No onboarding fee. None is listed on any plan; HubSpot charges $3,000 on Professional and $7,000 on Enterprise.
  • Attribution on a mid-tier plan. Pro ($79 a month) includes attribution and predictive sending; HubSpot lists multi-touch attribution on Enterprise at $3,600 a month.

How to decide

  1. List the features you need on day one: lead scoring and custom reports point to HubSpot Professional or ActiveCampaign Pro with add-ons.
  2. Price year one at your contact count, adding HubSpot's onboarding fee and any ActiveCampaign add-ons.
  3. Count users: ActiveCampaign Plus includes 1, Pro 3; HubSpot Professional includes 3 seats.
  4. If your sales team already uses HubSpot CRM, weigh the value of one system against the price gap.

HubSpot Marketing Hub vs ActiveCampaign FAQ

Is ActiveCampaign cheaper than HubSpot?

Yes, at every tier we priced. At 1,000 contacts billed yearly, ActiveCampaign Plus costs $588 a year and Pro $948, against $12,600 in year one for HubSpot Marketing Hub Professional ($800 a month plus $3,000 onboarding).

Which has built-in lead scoring?

HubSpot includes lead scoring on Professional, with up to 5 scores. ActiveCampaign sells lead scoring in its Pipelines add-on, available from Plus.

Does HubSpot charge an onboarding fee for Marketing Hub?

Yes: $3,000 one-time on Professional and $7,000 on Enterprise. Starter has none, and ActiveCampaign lists none.

Which scores higher in Vettlo's ranking?

ActiveCampaign, with 86/100 against 85/100 for HubSpot Marketing Hub. ActiveCampaign rates higher on price; HubSpot rates higher on lead scoring and reporting.
