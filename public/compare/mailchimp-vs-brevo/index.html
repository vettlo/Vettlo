Mailchimp vs Brevo: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Oct 27, 2026 · Prices verified Oct 27, 2026

We may earn a commission when you click some links on this page. It never changes which tools we include or how we rank them. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our email marketing ranking, Mailchimp scores 79/100 and Brevo scores 83/100. Brevo costs less on its entry and Standard plans at the list sizes we priced and includes unlimited automation workflows from $18 a month, while Mailchimp has the more polished editor, 300+ integrations and more seats on its entry plans.

Pick Mailchimp if…

  • You want the most familiar editor and 300+ integrations
  • You value polished, easy-to-read reports over the lowest price
  • Several people need access on a mid-priced plan (3 seats on Essentials, 5 on Standard)

Pick Brevo if…

  • You want the lower bill at the same list size ($9 vs $13 a month at 500 contacts)
  • You need multi-step automations and A/B testing for under $20 a month
  • You also send transactional email or SMS and want one platform

Where Mailchimp is stronger

  • Editor and reporting. Its drag-and-drop builder and reports are polished and quick to learn, which is why it rates 4.5/5 on ease of use in our ranking.
  • Integrations. More than 300 integrations connect it to most stores, CRMs and form tools.
  • Seats on entry plans. Essentials includes 3 seats and Standard 5, against 1 on Brevo Starter and 3 on Brevo Standard.

Where Brevo is stronger

  • Price at the same list size. At 1,500 contacts, Starter is $17 a month against $26.50 for Mailchimp Essentials, about $114 less a year.
  • Automation for less. Standard ($18 a month at the first tier) includes unlimited multi-step workflows and A/B testing; Mailchimp Essentials caps flows at 4 steps.
  • More than email. SMS and transactional email sit in the same platform, and the free plan keeps up to 100,000 contacts.

How to decide

  1. Count your contacts and how many emails you send each month; Brevo prices on sends, Mailchimp on contacts.
  2. Find the cheapest plan on each side that has the automation and seats you need, and compare that price.
  3. Check Mailchimp's rate after the first 12 months at your contact tier.
  4. Send one real campaign and one automation from each free plan or trial before you migrate your list.

Mailchimp vs Brevo FAQ

Is Brevo cheaper than Mailchimp?

Yes, at the list sizes we priced. At 500 contacts Brevo Starter is $9 a month against $13 for Mailchimp Essentials; at 1,500 contacts it is $17 against $26.50, on monthly billing.

Which has the better free plan?

Brevo's: it sends up to 300 emails a day and stores up to 100,000 contacts. Mailchimp's free plan covers 250 contacts and 500 sends a month, with no automation flows.

Which is better for automation?

Brevo for the money. Its Standard plan includes unlimited multi-step workflows from $18 a month. Mailchimp Essentials limits flows to 4 steps; Standard ($20 at 500 contacts) allows up to 200 flows.

Which is easier to use?

Mailchimp, slightly. It rates 4.5/5 on ease of use in our ranking against 4/5 for Brevo, and connects to 300+ other tools.
