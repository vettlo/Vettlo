Xero vs Zoho Books: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

Short answer

In our accounting ranking, Xero scores 86/100 and Zoho Books scores 86/100. They tie on our weighted criteria. Xero rates higher on bills and expenses and on ease of use; Zoho Books rates higher on price, because multi-currency, projects and inventory cost $40 a month billed yearly instead of $97.

Pick Xero if…

  • Many people need access: Xero charges no per-user license fees, while Zoho Books plans include 1 to 10 users
  • You enter and pay vendor bills but don't want to pay for multi-currency or projects: Growing ($59) includes unlimited bills, while Zoho Books needs Professional
  • You want a 60-day cash flow forecast and up to 3 budgets on a mid-tier plan

Pick Zoho Books if…

  • You're under $50K in annual revenue and want a free plan with 1 user plus your accountant
  • You need multi-currency, project profitability or stock tracking: they start on Professional at $40 a month billed yearly, against $97 for Xero Established
  • You already run other Zoho apps or want a 14-day trial before paying

Where Xero is stronger

  • Users cost nothing extra. A 12-person office pays $59 a month on Growing; on Zoho Books, 12 users need Premium with 10 included plus 2 extra users at $2.50, or $65 a month billed yearly.
  • Bills on the mid-tier plan. Growing includes unlimited bills and free standard ACH bill payments; Zoho Books lists bills only from Professional, which is why Xero rates 4.5/5 on bills and expenses in our ranking against 4/5.
  • Cash flow and budgets earlier. Growing has a 60-day cash flow forecast and 3 budgets; Zoho Books puts budgeting and its cash flow forecasting report on Premium.
  • Easier to learn. It rates 4/5 on ease of use in our ranking against 3.5/5 for Zoho Books, and it has 1,857 G2 reviews against 331, both rated 4.4/5.

Where Zoho Books is stronger

  • A real free plan. Businesses under $50K in revenue get 1 user plus an accountant, bank reconciliation, recurring invoices and 1099 tracking at $0; Xero has no free plan.
  • Growth features for less. Multi-currency, projects, stock tracking and approvals start on Professional at $40 a month billed yearly; Xero lists multi-currency and projects on Established at $97, so a 5-person team saves $684 a year at list prices.
  • Generous volume caps. Even Standard allows 5,000 invoices a year, more than 400 a month, while Xero Early stops at 20 a month.
  • Workflow automation. Professional includes 10 workflow rules and sales and purchase approvals, rising to 200 rules on Premium.

How to decide

  1. List who needs a login, including your accountant: at 10 or more users, Xero Growing ($59) costs less than Zoho Books Premium ($60 billed yearly).
  2. Count invoices and vendor bills per month: under 20 invoices and 5 bills, Xero Early or Zoho Books Free may be enough.
  3. Check whether you need multi-currency or project profitability now; that's where the gap is largest ($97 against $40 billed yearly).
  4. Connect your bank in a Zoho Books trial and during Xero's discounted first months, and reconcile one real month of transactions in each.

Xero vs Zoho Books FAQ

Is Zoho Books cheaper than Xero?

Usually, yes. At US list prices Zoho Books Standard is $15 a month billed yearly against $27 for Xero Early, and Professional is $40 against $97 for Xero Established. Xero can cost less for large teams because it has no per-user fees.

Does Xero have a free plan like Zoho Books?

No. Xero offers new US customers 80% off the base plan for 3 months. Zoho Books is free for businesses under $50K in annual revenue, with 1 user plus 1 accountant and 1,000 invoices a year.

Which plan do I need to enter vendor bills?

On Xero, any plan: Early allows 5 bills a month and Growing ($59) removes the cap. On Zoho Books, the bills module is listed from Professional ($40 a month billed yearly).

Which scores higher in Vettlo's ranking?

Neither: both score 86/100. Xero rates higher on bills and expenses and on ease of use; Zoho Books rates higher on price. Both rate 4.5/5 on core bookkeeping and on invoicing.
