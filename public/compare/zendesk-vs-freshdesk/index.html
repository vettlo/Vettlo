Zendesk vs Freshdesk: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Oct 26, 2026 · Prices verified Oct 26, 2026

We may earn a commission when you click some links on this page. It never changes which tools we include or how we rank them. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our help desk ranking, Zendesk scores 87/100 and Freshdesk scores 83/100. Both start at $19 per agent, but Freshdesk includes a knowledge base and an AI agent allowance on that entry plan, while Zendesk puts them on Suite Team at $55.

Pick Zendesk if…

  • You need every channel, including voice, in one platform
  • You want a large app marketplace and room to grow
  • Budget allows for Suite plans

Pick Freshdesk if…

  • You want a knowledge base and AI agent on the entry plan
  • You need SLAs and routing without Zendesk Suite prices
  • You're a growing team watching cost per agent

Where Zendesk is stronger

  • Channel coverage. Suite plans bring email, messaging, live chat and telephony into one platform with omnichannel routing.
  • Ecosystem. A large marketplace of apps and integrations.
  • Scale. Skills-based routing and IVR on Suite Professional suit larger support operations.

Where Freshdesk is stronger

  • More on the entry plan. Growth includes a knowledge base, customer portal and 500 AI agent sessions for $19 per agent.
  • Lower price for advanced features. Pro adds custom dashboards, intelligent routing and multiple SLA policies for $55, half of Zendesk Suite Professional.
  • Simpler to run. Smaller teams usually need less setup time.

How to decide

  1. List the channels you support today and in a year.
  2. Price the plan on each side that covers them, then add your expected AI resolution volume.
  3. Forward a week of real tickets into both trials and time how fast agents resolve them.

Zendesk vs Freshdesk FAQ

Is Freshdesk cheaper than Zendesk?

At the entry level both are $19 per agent billed annually, but Freshdesk includes a knowledge base and AI agent allowance that need Zendesk Suite Team ($55).

Which has better AI?

Both include AI agents. Zendesk includes 5 to 10 automated resolutions per agent per month on Suite plans; Freshdesk Growth includes 500 AI agent sessions and sells Copilot as a $29 add-on.

Which is better for large teams?

Zendesk's Suite plans offer broader channel coverage and routing for large, multichannel operations.

Do both have free trials?

Yes, both offer 14-day trials.
