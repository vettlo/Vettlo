Mixpanel vs PostHog: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 9, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 9, 2026

We may earn money when you click links on this page. Partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our product analytics tools ranking, Mixpanel scores 89/100 and PostHog scores 88/100. Mixpanel rates higher on analysis depth, ease of use and data governance, and includes SAML SSO on Growth. PostHog rates higher on price, its free plan and replay, flags and experiments, and at 10M events a month it costs less than 40% of Mixpanel's yearly-billed price.

Pick Mixpanel if…

  • Product managers and analysts, not only engineers, will build most reports
  • You need SAML SSO and SCIM without an enterprise contract (both on Growth)
  • You want 2 years of data on the free plan and unlimited seats

Pick PostHog if…

  • You want the lowest price at volume: about $324 a month for 10M analytics events against $1,176 on Mixpanel Growth billed monthly
  • You want analytics, session replay, feature flags and experiments in one platform, each with its own free allowance
  • Your team is engineering-led and comfortable with a more technical tool

Where Mixpanel is stronger

  • Deeper self-serve analysis. Funnels, retention, flows and cohorts are built for non-technical users, which is why it rates 5/5 on analysis depth and 4.5/5 on ease of use in our ranking, against 4/5 and 3.5/5 for PostHog.
  • SSO without an add-on. SAML SSO and SCIM are on Growth, which starts at $0 for the first 1M events; on PostHog, SAML is in the $750-a-month Scale add-on.
  • Longer free history. The free plan keeps 2 years of data, against 1 year on PostHog's free plan.
  • More replays included. Growth includes 20,000 session replays in the base price, and Free includes 10,000, against 5,000 free recordings a month on PostHog.

Where PostHog is stronger

  • Much lower price at volume. 10M analytics events cost about $324 a month, against $1,176 on Mixpanel Growth billed monthly or $840 billed yearly, which is why it rates 5/5 on price in our ranking against 4/5.
  • More products on one platform. Session replay, feature flags and experiments each have their own free allowance and usage price, and it rates 5/5 on replay, flags and experiments against 4/5 for Mixpanel.
  • Published prices at any volume. Per-event tiers are listed past 250M events a month; Mixpanel needs a quote above 20M.
  • No seat or report limits. There are no per-seat charges, and the free plan has no saved-report cap like Mixpanel's 5 per seat.

How to decide

  1. Count last month's events, and if you use replay, recordings; price both tools at that volume.
  2. Decide who will build reports: product managers usually get further faster in Mixpanel, engineers in PostHog.
  3. If your security team requires SAML SSO, compare Mixpanel Growth against PostHog plus its $750-a-month Scale add-on.
  4. Send the same events to both free plans for two weeks and rebuild three reports you use every week.

Mixpanel vs PostHog FAQ

Is PostHog cheaper than Mixpanel?

At list prices, yes. At 2M events a month PostHog's product analytics costs $50 against $259 on Mixpanel Growth billed monthly ($185 billed yearly), and at 10M about $324 against $1,176 ($840 billed yearly). Both are free up to 1M events a month.

Which has the better free plan?

It depends on what you need. Both include 1M events a month. Mixpanel adds unlimited seats, 10,000 session replays and 2 years of data but caps saved reports at 5 per seat. PostHog adds 5,000 recordings and 1M feature flag requests, with 1 project and 1 year of data.

Which one includes SAML SSO for less?

Mixpanel. SAML SSO and SCIM are on Growth, which costs $0 for the first 1M events and $438 a month at 5M billed yearly. PostHog lists SAML in its Scale add-on at $750 a month on top of usage.

Which scores higher in Vettlo's ranking?

Mixpanel, with 89/100 against 88/100 for PostHog. Mixpanel rates higher on analysis depth, ease of use and data governance; PostHog rates higher on price, its free plan and replay, flags and experiments.
