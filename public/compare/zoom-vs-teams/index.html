Zoom vs Microsoft Teams: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 1, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 1, 2026

We may earn money when you click links on this page. Partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our video conferencing ranking, Zoom Workplace scores 93/100 and Microsoft Teams scores 90/100. Zoom scores higher for recording and its included AI Companion; Teams is far cheaper, with 300-person, 30-hour meetings from $4 per user, and comes free with most Microsoft 365 business plans.

Pick Zoom if…

  • You meet mostly with clients and partners outside your company
  • You want AI meeting summaries included at no extra cost
  • You need cloud recording on a monthly plan without an annual commitment

Pick Microsoft Teams if…

  • Your company already runs on Microsoft 365
  • You need meetings of up to 300 people at the lowest price
  • You want chat, files and meetings in one place

Where Zoom is stronger

  • AI included. Paid plans include unlimited meeting summaries and in-meeting questions at no extra cost.
  • Monthly billing. Pro and Business are available month to month at $16.99 and $21.99 per user.
  • Cloud recording on the first paid plan. Pro includes 10 GB of cloud recording per license.

Where Microsoft Teams is stronger

  • Price. Teams Essentials is $4 per user paid yearly, about a quarter of Zoom Pro's annual price.
  • Bigger meetings for less. 300 participants from the entry plan; Zoom needs Business ($18.33) for 300.
  • Longer free meetings. Free group meetings run 60 minutes, versus 40 on Zoom Basic.
  • Microsoft 365 bundle. Business Basic ($7) adds business email and Office web apps to the same license.

How to decide

  1. Check whether your company already pays for Microsoft 365; if so, Teams costs nothing extra.
  2. Size your largest regular meeting: over 100 people needs Zoom Business but stays on Teams Essentials.
  3. Decide whether you need AI summaries included or will pay for Copilot.
  4. Run a week of real external calls on both free plans and see which your guests join faster.

Zoom vs Microsoft Teams FAQ

Is Zoom or Microsoft Teams cheaper?

Teams. Teams Essentials is $4 per user per month paid yearly, versus $14.16 for Zoom Pro billed annually, and it allows 300 participants instead of 100.

Which has the better free plan?

Teams' free plan allows 60-minute group meetings for 100 participants; Zoom's Basic plan allows 40 minutes for 100.

Does Zoom or Teams include AI meeting notes?

Zoom includes AI Companion summaries on paid plans at no extra cost. Microsoft lists Copilot as an add-on on Teams Essentials and Business Basic, and includes it in Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot ($23.50).

Can I pay monthly?

Zoom offers monthly billing ($16.99 for Pro). Microsoft's Teams plans on its business comparison page are annual subscriptions that auto-renew.
