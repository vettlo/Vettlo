Ramp vs Brex: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 5, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 5, 2026

We may earn money when you click links on this page. Partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our expense management ranking, Ramp scores 92/100 and Brex scores 94/100. Both start at $0 per user and are rated 4.8/5 on G2. Brex includes reimbursements and multi-level approvals on its free plan and charges $12 per user for Premium; Ramp's free plan does more automatic receipt work, but its pricing page lists reimbursement tools from Plus at $15 per user plus a platform fee.

Pick Ramp if…

  • Most spend runs on company cards and you want receipts collected and matched automatically
  • You want expenses completed by SMS or Slack
  • You want unlimited cards, approval workflows and bill pay at $0 and can live without reimbursement tools
  • You'd like a 30-day trial of the paid plan

Pick Brex if…

  • Employees also pay out of pocket and you want reimbursements on the free plan
  • You need multi-level approval chains without paying
  • You want multiple expense policies, ERP sync and multi-entity at a published $12 per user
  • You have entities outside the US

What it costs

25 users, from each vendor's list prices. Ramp's $15 is the monthly price (20% off with annual billing) and Plus adds a platform fee based on team size that Ramp doesn't publish. Card fees and travel excluded.

Where Ramp is stronger

  • More receipt automation for free. Auto-receipt collection and matching, with expenses completed by SMS or Slack, on the $0 plan.
  • Easier to start. Unlimited cards, approval workflows and QuickBooks Online or Xero sync on the free plan, and a 30-day trial of Plus.
  • More reviews behind its rating. Both are rated 4.8/5 on G2, but Ramp's score comes from 2,572 reviews against 1,657 for Brex.

Where Brex is stronger

  • Reimbursements for free. Brex lists reimbursements on Essentials; Ramp lists reimbursement features from Plus.
  • Multi-level approvals at $0. Multi-level approval chains are on Essentials, with dynamic spend limit chains on Premium.
  • Published paid price. Premium is $12 per user with no platform fee listed; Ramp Plus is $15 per user plus a fee based on team size.
  • International entities. Premium adds multi-entity support for US and international entities.

How to decide

  1. Count how much of your spend is out of pocket; if it's a real share, check Ramp Free's reimbursement limits or plan on Brex Essentials.
  2. Decide whether you need NetSuite or Sage Intacct sync: that means Ramp Plus or Brex Premium.
  3. Ask Ramp for its platform fee at your team size before comparing Plus with Brex Premium at $12 per user.
  4. Run last month's card transactions and receipts through both and compare how many are matched without edits.

Ramp vs Brex FAQ

Is Ramp or Brex cheaper?

Both have $0 plans. For paid plans, Brex Premium is $12 per user a month; Ramp Plus is $15 per user a month (20% less billed annually) plus a platform fee based on team size. For 25 users, Brex Premium is $3,600 a year and Ramp Plus $4,500 a year before the platform fee.

Does Ramp's free plan include reimbursements?

Ramp's pricing page lists reimbursement features, such as reimbursement limits, bulk review and weekly batched reimbursements, on Plus and Enterprise only. Brex lists reimbursements on its free Essentials plan.

Which integrates with NetSuite?

Ramp adds NetSuite and Sage Intacct on Plus. Brex includes accounting integrations on Essentials and customizable ERP and HRIS integrations on Premium.

Which scores higher in Vettlo's ranking?

Brex, with 94/100 against 92/100 for Ramp. Brex rates higher on approvals and price; Ramp rates higher on ease of use, and they tie on cards, receipts and integrations.
