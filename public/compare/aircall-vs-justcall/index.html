Aircall vs JustCall: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Oct 26, 2026 · Prices verified Oct 26, 2026

We may earn a commission when you click some links on this page. It never changes which tools we include or how we rank them. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our sales dialers ranking, Aircall scores 81/100 and JustCall scores 83/100. Their power dialer plans cost almost the same; Aircall is the simpler phone system, while JustCall offers more dialing modes and includes AI coaching on a plan instead of an add-on.

Pick Aircall if…

  • You need a reliable phone system for inbound and outbound calls first
  • Your reps call mostly within the US and Canada and want unlimited outbound minutes there
  • You want a simple rollout and a large integration marketplace

Pick JustCall if…

  • You want predictive dialing as your team grows
  • You want AI call scoring and real-time assist included in a plan
  • You have a 2-person team (Aircall's minimum is 3 licenses)

Where Aircall is stronger

  • Phone system first. Easy setup for inbound and outbound teams, with IVR, call queues and recording from the entry plan.
  • Unlimited calling. Essentials and Professional include unlimited US and Canada outbound calls.
  • Integrations. A large marketplace, with HubSpot listed from Essentials and Salesforce from Professional.

Where JustCall is stronger

  • More dialing modes. Power dialing on Pro and predictive dialing on up to 10 lines with SalesPro.
  • AI coaching in the plan. Pro Plus includes AI call scoring and real-time agent assist for $89, less than Aircall Professional plus AI Assist Pro.
  • Smaller teams. Plans start at 2 users, and the 14-day trial needs no card.

How to decide

  1. Decide whether you need a phone system, a power dialer or predictive dialing.
  2. Estimate outbound minutes per rep; JustCall lists 1,000 per user in the US and Canada.
  3. Price the plan on each side that includes your dialer and AI needs.
  4. Run a week of real calls in both trials and compare connect rates and CRM logging.

Aircall vs JustCall FAQ

Is JustCall cheaper than Aircall?

Slightly, at each comparable tier: $29 vs $30 for the entry plan and $49 vs $50 for the power dialer plan, billed annually. With AI coaching, JustCall Pro Plus ($89) costs less than Aircall Professional plus AI Assist Pro ($99).

Which has a power dialer?

Both, on their second plan: Aircall Professional and JustCall Pro. Neither includes it on the entry plan.

Does Aircall have a predictive dialer?

Aircall's pricing page lists a Power Dialer, not a predictive dialer. JustCall offers predictive dialing on its quote-based SalesPro plan.

Do both offer free trials?

JustCall offers a 14-day trial with no card needed. Aircall offers free access to test the platform; its pricing page doesn't state the length.
