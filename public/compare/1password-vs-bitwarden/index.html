1Password vs Bitwarden: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Oct 30, 2026 · Prices verified Oct 30, 2026

We may earn a commission when you click some links on this page. It never changes which tools we include or how we rank them. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our password managers ranking, 1Password scores 88/100 and Bitwarden scores 90/100. Bitwarden is cheaper at every tier that includes provisioning or SSO, while 1Password is easier to roll out and cheaper only for a team of up to 10 that doesn't need identity-provider integrations.

Pick 1Password if…

  • Your team is 10 people or fewer and only needs shared vaults ($299.40 a year on the Teams Starter Pack)
  • Non-technical staff need to adopt it quickly
  • You want SSO, provisioning, reports and family accounts on one self-serve plan

Pick Bitwarden if…

  • You want SSO and enterprise policies for less ($720 vs $1,078.80 a year for 10 users)
  • You want SCIM provisioning on the entry business plan
  • You need to self-host the server

Where 1Password is stronger

  • Small teams. The Teams Starter Pack covers up to 10 users for $299.40 a year, $180.60 less than Bitwarden Teams at 10 users.
  • Ease of use. It rates 5/5 on sharing and ease of use in our ranking against 4/5 for Bitwarden, which matters when the whole company has to adopt it.
  • One plan for everything. Business puts SSO unlock, automated provisioning, reports, an audit trail, Watchtower alerts and Families accounts on one plan.

Where Bitwarden is stronger

  • Lower price with SSO. Enterprise costs $720 a year for 10 users against $1,078.80 for 1Password Business, a $358.80 difference.
  • Provisioning on the entry plan. SCIM and Directory Connector are on the $4 Teams plan; 1Password puts provisioning on Business.
  • Self-hosting and a free tier. Enterprise can be self-hosted, and a free organization lets two people share.

How to decide

  1. Count users: at 10 or fewer without SSO, 1Password's Teams Starter Pack is the cheaper option.
  2. If your identity provider must create and remove accounts, compare Bitwarden Teams ($4) with 1Password Business ($8.99).
  3. If you need SSO, compare Bitwarden Enterprise ($6) with 1Password Business ($8.99).
  4. Run both 14-day trials with a few non-technical staff and see which app they keep using.

1Password vs Bitwarden FAQ

Is Bitwarden cheaper than 1Password?

Yes, for most teams. At 10 users billed annually, Bitwarden Teams costs $480 and Enterprise $720, against $1,078.80 for 1Password Business. Only 1Password's Teams Starter Pack ($299.40 a year for up to 10 users) is cheaper.

Which includes SSO?

Both, on different plans. 1Password includes unlock with SSO on Business ($8.99 a user); Bitwarden on Enterprise ($6 a user).

Do both give employees a free family plan?

Yes. 1Password Business includes a free Families membership for everyone in the company, and Bitwarden Enterprise a free Families plan for all users. Neither entry plan includes it.

Which scores higher in Vettlo's ranking?

Bitwarden, with 90/100 against 88/100 for 1Password. Bitwarden rates higher on price; 1Password rates higher on ease of use, SSO and extras for staff.
