Notion vs Slab: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 9, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 9, 2026

We may earn money when you click links on this page. Partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our knowledge base software ranking, Notion scores 87/100 and Slab scores 84/100. Notion rates higher on its editor and AI answers, while Slab rates higher on ease of use, upkeep and price, and its SSO plan costs $7.50 a user less.

Pick Notion if…

  • You want docs, wikis and databases in one workspace
  • You want AI search, meeting notes and agents in the same tool (Business, $20 a seat)
  • Your wiki also holds project plans, trackers and team pages

Pick Slab if…

  • You want a focused team wiki and nothing else
  • You need SAML SSO and SCIM for less ($12.50 a user on Business against $20 on Notion Business, which leaves SCIM to Enterprise)
  • You want content verification on the free plan for up to 10 users

Where Notion is stronger

  • More flexible pages. Docs, wikis and databases share one workspace, so a wiki page can sit next to a project tracker, which is why it rates 5/5 on editor and organization in our ranking against 4/5 for Slab.
  • Broader AI. Business adds Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes and Enterprise Search, and it rates 4.5/5 on search and AI answers against 4/5 for Slab.
  • Free for individuals. The Free plan has unlimited blocks for a single user, so people can start before the team joins.
  • Much larger review base. 4.6/5 across 13,972 G2 reviews, against 4.6/5 across 309 for Slab.

Where Slab is stronger

  • Lower price. Startup is $6.67 a user billed annually against $10 for Notion Plus, so 25 people cost $2,001 a year instead of $3,000.
  • Cheaper SSO and SCIM. Business ($12.50) includes SAML SSO and SCIM; on Notion, SAML SSO is on Business ($20) and SCIM needs Enterprise.
  • Verification on the free plan. Page verification is included from Free, while Notion lists it from Business, which is why Slab rates 4.5/5 on verification and upkeep against 4/5.
  • Easier to keep tidy. Posts and topics give every page a fixed place, and it rates 4.5/5 on ease of use in our ranking against 4/5 for Notion.

How to decide

  1. Decide whether you want only a wiki or a workspace that also tracks projects; that usually settles it.
  2. Price both at your real headcount, and at the plan with SSO if your IT team requires it.
  3. Count guests and occasional readers, since both tools bill per user or seat.
  4. Move 20 real pages into each free plan and ask three teammates to find answers in them.

Notion vs Slab FAQ

Is Slab cheaper than Notion?

Yes, at list prices. Slab Startup is $6.67 a user a month billed annually and Business $12.50, against $10 and $20 for Notion Plus and Business. For 25 people that's $2,001 against $3,000 a year on the entry plans, and $3,750 against $6,000 on the plans with SAML SSO.

Which has the better free plan for a team?

Slab's, for a team wiki: it's free for up to 10 users with verification and 90-day version history. Notion's Free plan has unlimited blocks for individuals but a block limit for workspaces with 2 or more members.

Do Notion and Slab have AI search?

Both do, on their second plan. Notion lists Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes and Enterprise Search on Business ($20 a seat); Slab lists AI Ask on Business ($12.50 a user billed annually) and AI Autofix from Startup.

Which scores higher in Vettlo's ranking?

Notion, with 87/100 against 84/100 for Slab. Notion rates higher on its editor and AI answers; Slab rates higher on ease of use, verification and price.
