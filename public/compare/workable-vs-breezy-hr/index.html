Workable vs Breezy HR: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 7, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 7, 2026

We may earn money when you click links on this page. Partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our applicant tracking ranking, Workable scores 88/100 and Breezy HR scores 79/100. Workable includes more on its entry plan, from e-signatures to a custom report builder, while Breezy HR costs about half as much for a small company and keeps a flat price with unlimited users.

Pick Workable if…

  • You want e-signatures, offer letter templates and a custom report builder on the entry plan
  • You post widely: Workable distributes to 200+ job boards against 50+ for Breezy HR
  • You'd like a basic HRIS in the same tool, included on every plan
  • You want AI credits included: 3,000 on every paid account

Pick Breezy HR if…

  • You want the lowest price for a small company: $157 a month billed annually on Startup
  • Your price shouldn't change as you grow: Breezy HR charges a flat fee with unlimited users and positions
  • You need EEOC/OFCCP reporting and interview self-scheduling on the entry plan
  • Video interviews should be included rather than a $109 a month add-on

Where Workable is stronger

  • More on the entry plan. Standard includes e-signatures, offer letter templates, a custom report builder and a basic HRIS, which Breezy HR spreads across Growth and Business or doesn't offer.
  • Wider job distribution. Workable posts to 200+ job boards against 50+ for Breezy HR.
  • AI credits included. Every paid account starts with 3,000 AI credits; Breezy HR sells AI as an add-on.

Where Breezy HR is stronger

  • Lower price for small companies. Startup costs $1,884 a year billed annually, against $3,588 for Workable Standard at 1 to 20 employees.
  • Flat pricing. Every plan has unlimited users and positions, and the price doesn't depend on company size.
  • Video interviews included. Integrated video and one-way candidate video are on Startup; Workable sells video interviews as a $109 a month add-on on Standard.

How to decide

  1. List what you need in year one: e-signatures, offers, assessments, texting or video interviews.
  2. Price the cheapest plan on each side that covers them at your company size; Workable's price rises above 20 employees.
  3. Add AI: Workable includes 3,000 credits, Breezy HR charges from $30 per 100,000.
  4. Run one live role through both trials with a hiring manager and compare how fast you schedule interviews.

Workable vs Breezy HR FAQ

Is Workable or Breezy HR cheaper?

Breezy HR, for small companies. Startup is $157 a month billed annually ($1,884 a year), and Workable Standard is $299 a month for companies with 1 to 20 employees ($3,588 a year).

Does either have a free plan?

No. Workable offers a 15-day trial and Breezy HR a 14-day trial, both without a credit card.

Which includes e-signatures?

Workable includes native e-signatures on every plan. Breezy HR lists electronic signatures from Growth, at $273 a month billed annually.

Which scores higher in Vettlo's ranking?

Workable, with 88/100 against 79/100 for Breezy HR. Workable rates higher on job distribution, AI and reporting; Breezy HR rates higher on price.
