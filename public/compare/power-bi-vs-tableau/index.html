Power BI vs Tableau: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 3, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 3, 2026

We may earn money when you click links on this page. Partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our business intelligence ranking, Microsoft Power BI scores 92/100 and Tableau scores 83/100. Power BI rates higher on data modeling, ease of use and price, and costs less at every team size we priced; Tableau rates higher on visualization and is nine points behind overall.

Pick Power BI if…

  • You want the lowest per-user price: Pro is $14 a user a month, paid yearly
  • Your company already pays for Microsoft 365 E5, which includes Power BI Pro
  • Analysts need deep data modeling, with up to 100 GB models on Premium Per User ($24)

Pick Tableau if…

  • A dedicated analyst team builds many custom visual dashboards
  • Most of your audience only views dashboards, at $15 a Viewer on Standard
  • Your team works on Macs or wants authoring in the browser with Tableau Desktop and Prep Builder included

Annual cost from each vendor's list prices, billed yearly. Power BI viewers need Pro to see shared content unless you buy F64 or larger Fabric capacity; Tableau needs at least one Creator. Taxes excluded.

Where Power BI is stronger

  • Lower cost at every size. 10 people cost $1,680 a year on Pro against $3,240 on Tableau Standard with 2 Creators, and 25 people $4,200 against $8,280.
  • Included in Microsoft 365 E5. Companies on E5 or Office 365 E5 already have Pro licenses.
  • Bigger models for $24. Premium Per User raises the model memory limit from 1 GB to 100 GB and refreshes from 8 to 48 a day.

Where Tableau is stronger

  • Visual analysis. Tableau scores 5/5 on visualization in our ranking, the highest in the category.
  • Cheaper viewers. A Tableau Viewer costs $15 a month, close to Power BI Pro, while authors pay more.
  • Larger review base. Rated 4.4/5 on G2 across 4,235 reviews, against 4.5/5 from 1,662 reviews for Power BI.
  • Authoring tools included. Creator includes Tableau Desktop, Prep Builder and Tableau Pulse.

How to decide

  1. Count how many people will build reports and how many will only view them.
  2. Price Power BI at $14 for every user and Tableau at $75 per Creator, $42 per Explorer and $15 per Viewer, all billed annually.
  3. Check whether your company already has Microsoft 365 E5, which includes Power BI Pro.
  4. Rebuild one real dashboard in both trials and see which one your analysts and viewers prefer.

Power BI vs Tableau FAQ

Is Power BI or Tableau cheaper?

Power BI, at every size we priced. For 10 people with 2 authors and 8 viewers, Power BI Pro costs $1,680 a year against $3,240 on Tableau Standard; for 25 people, $4,200 against $8,280.

Is Power BI free?

Power BI Desktop and a free account are free for personal analysis, but sharing reports and viewing shared content need Pro ($14 a user a month) or Premium Per User ($24), unless content sits on F64 or larger Fabric capacity.

Does Tableau have monthly billing?

No. Tableau's pricing page says all products require an annual contract, billed annually. Power BI Pro is shown at $14 a user a month paid yearly.

Which scores higher in Vettlo's ranking?

Power BI, with 92/100 against 83/100 for Tableau. Power BI rates higher on data modeling, ease of use and price; Tableau rates higher on visualization.
