FreshBooks vs QuickBooks: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 16, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 16, 2026

We may earn money when you click links on this page. Partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our accounting ranking, FreshBooks scores 79/100 and QuickBooks Online scores 87/100. QuickBooks rates higher on core bookkeeping, bills and reporting, and adds inventory and budgets on Plus. FreshBooks rates higher on invoicing, ease of use and price, and costs less for teams of 3 to 10, but its plans cap billable clients at 5 (Lite) and 50 (Plus).

Pick FreshBooks if…

  • You bill clients for time and projects and have no more than 50 of them: Plus ($43) adds proposals, retainers and e-signatures
  • Two to five people work in the books: 3 people cost $65 a month on FreshBooks Plus against $85 on QuickBooks Essentials
  • You want project profitability for less: it starts on Premium at $70, against $140 for QuickBooks Plus

Pick QuickBooks if…

  • You invoice more than 50 customers but work alone: Simple Start ($38) has no customer cap, while FreshBooks needs Premium ($70)
  • You sell products and need inventory tracking, budgets or classes, all listed on Plus
  • Your accountant works in QuickBooks: every plan includes 2 accountant users, while FreshBooks adds accountant access from Plus

Where FreshBooks is stronger

  • Cheaper once a second person joins. Owner plus 2 team members on Plus is $780 a year against $1,020 for QuickBooks Essentials, and the gap grows to $2,052 a year at 10 people.
  • Built around client billing. Proposals, retainers and e-signatures come with Plus, and time tracking is unlimited on every plan, which is why it rates 5/5 on invoicing and payments in our ranking against 4.5/5.
  • Project profitability at half the price. Premium lists it at $70 a month; QuickBooks puts it on Plus at $140.
  • Easier to pick up. It rates 5/5 on ease of use against 4/5, and 4.5/5 on G2 from 982 reviews against 4.0/5 from 3,909 for QuickBooks Online.

Where QuickBooks is stronger

  • Full bookkeeping from the first plan. It rates 5/5 on core bookkeeping and reconciliation in our ranking against 3.5/5 for FreshBooks, whose Lite plan has no double-entry reports or bank reconciliation.
  • No cap on customers. Plans are sized by users, and the pricing page lists no customer cap, so a one-person business invoicing 200 customers still pays $38 a month on Simple Start.
  • More room to grow. Plus adds inventory, budget planning and 40 classes and locations, and Advanced adds approval workflows, user permissions and free 1099 e-filing for up to 25 users; it rates 4.5/5 on reporting against 3/5.
  • Accountant seats included. Two accountant users come with every plan, and QuickBooks is the tool many US accountants already work in.

How to decide

  1. Count the clients you invoice in a year: above 50, FreshBooks needs Premium ($70) while QuickBooks Simple Start ($38) has no cap.
  2. Count the people who need a login: from 3 people up, FreshBooks plus $11 team members costs less than the QuickBooks plan that covers them.
  3. List what you sell: products and inventory point to QuickBooks Plus; time, retainers and proposals point to FreshBooks Plus.
  4. Ask your accountant which tool they use, then run one month of real invoices and bank transactions through both 30-day trials.

FreshBooks vs QuickBooks FAQ

Is FreshBooks cheaper than QuickBooks?

For most small teams, yes. At regular US prices FreshBooks Lite is $23 a month against $38 for QuickBooks Simple Start, and 3 people cost $65 on FreshBooks Plus against $85 on QuickBooks Essentials. A one-person business with more than 50 clients pays less on QuickBooks: $38 against $70 for FreshBooks Premium.

Does FreshBooks do real double-entry accounting?

Yes, from Plus ($43 a month), which adds double-entry accounting reports, bank reconciliation and accountant access. Lite ($23) covers invoicing, expenses, time tracking and tax-time reports.

Can my accountant log in to FreshBooks and QuickBooks?

QuickBooks Online includes 2 accountant users on every plan and 3 on Advanced. FreshBooks lists accountant access from Plus; it isn't on Lite.

Which scores higher in Vettlo's ranking?

QuickBooks Online, with 87/100 against 79/100 for FreshBooks. QuickBooks rates higher on core bookkeeping, bills and expenses, and reporting; FreshBooks rates higher on invoicing, ease of use and price.
