BambooHR vs Rippling: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 1, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 1, 2026

We may earn money when you click links on this page. Partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our HRIS ranking, BambooHR scores 85/100 and Rippling scores 80/100. BambooHR is the simpler HRIS with published prices, with time off and hiring on Core and reviews on Pro; Rippling starts lower per employee but quotes every module, and wins when payroll and IT need to live on the same employee record.

Pick BambooHR if…

  • You want published prices and month-to-month terms
  • Your HR team mainly needs records, time off, hiring and reviews
  • You want an HRIS a small HR team can run without IT help

Pick Rippling if…

  • You want payroll, devices and app access on the same employee record
  • You hire in several countries and expect global payroll
  • You'd rather pay per employee from day one than a $250 monthly minimum

Where BambooHR is stronger

  • Prices you can see. All three plans are published, from $250 a month for up to 25 employees, with month-to-month terms.
  • Hiring and reviews in the plans. Applicant tracking is on every plan, and 360° reviews and 1:1s come with Pro rather than as an add-on.
  • Fewer moving parts. Records, time off and hiring work on Core without extra modules, and it's rated 4.4/5 on G2 across more than 8,000 reviews.

Where Rippling is stronger

  • One record for HR, payroll and IT. Payroll, global payroll, devices and app access run on the same employee database as the HRIS.
  • Lower entry price per employee. The HRIS module starts at $8 per employee, against BambooHR's $250 flat minimum or $10 per employee above 25.
  • Automated offboarding. Offboarding workflows can recover devices and remove app access along with HR tasks.

How to decide

  1. List what you need in year one: records and time off only, reviews, payroll, or device and app management.
  2. Price BambooHR from its published plans at your headcount.
  3. Ask Rippling for a written quote covering the same modules, including any base or implementation fees.
  4. If payroll and IT should share the employee record, weigh that against BambooHR's simpler setup and published prices.

BambooHR vs Rippling FAQ

Is BambooHR or Rippling cheaper?

It depends on the modules. For records and time off at 20 employees, Rippling's HRIS starts from $1,920 a year against $3,000 for BambooHR Core. Once you add payroll, performance or IT, Rippling's total comes from a quote.

Does BambooHR include payroll?

No. Payroll is an add-on for US employees only, with a 15% discount when bundled with Benefits Administration; its price isn't published.

Which is better for performance reviews?

BambooHR includes 360° reviews and 1:1s on Pro ($425 a month up to 25 employees). Rippling sells performance as a separate module.

Which scores higher in Vettlo's ranking?

BambooHR, with 85/100 against 80/100 for Rippling. BambooHR rates higher on price transparency and ease of use; Rippling rates higher on payroll and integrations.
