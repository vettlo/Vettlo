Docusign vs PandaDoc: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 4, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 4, 2026

We may earn money when you click links on this page. Partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our e-signature ranking, Docusign scores 88/100 and PandaDoc scores 85/100. Docusign leads on signing options, integrations and admin controls; PandaDoc is cheaper on its entry plan, builds the document as well as signing it, and has no yearly envelope cap per user.

Pick Docusign if…

  • You mainly send finished contracts, forms and agreements for signature
  • You need signer attachments, identity verification or 1,000+ integrations
  • You want web forms, payments and bulk send in the core plan (Business Pro)
  • Each user sends fewer than about 100 envelopes a year

Pick PandaDoc if…

  • Your team writes proposals, quotes and sales agreements from templates
  • You want unlimited documents and e-signatures without a per-user envelope cap
  • You want pricing tables, a product catalog and CRM integrations
  • You'd like a free plan to start

Where Docusign is stronger

  • More signing options. Delegated and scheduled sending and in-person signing from Standard, plus signer attachments, drop-down fields and PowerForms on Business Pro.
  • Integrations. 1,000+ integrations and an established API.
  • Signer verification. Identity verification and SMS delivery are available, with a one-time bonus of 5 each on Standard and Business Pro.

Where PandaDoc is stronger

  • Cheaper entry. Starter is $19 a seat billed annually, against $30 for Docusign Standard.
  • No yearly envelope cap per user. Starter and Business include unlimited documents and e-signatures sent from the app.
  • Builds the document too. A drag-and-drop editor from Starter, and pricing tables, a product catalog and payments on Business.

How to decide

  1. Count how many agreements each person sends a year; over about 100, Docusign Standard's cap matters.
  2. Decide whether you sign finished PDFs (Docusign) or build proposals and quotes (PandaDoc).
  3. List the integrations you need on day one and check which plan includes them.
  4. Send three real contracts from each tool to colleagues before you buy.

Docusign vs PandaDoc FAQ

Is Docusign or PandaDoc cheaper?

PandaDoc Starter is cheaper at $19 per seat a month billed annually, against $30 for Docusign Standard. At the next tier the gap closes: Docusign Business Pro is $45 and PandaDoc Business $49.

Does PandaDoc have a free plan?

Yes. PandaDoc's free plan sends 5 documents a month with up to 5 templates. Docusign lists no free plan; its cheapest plan, Personal, is $11 a month billed annually.

How many documents can I send with Docusign?

Personal allows 5 envelopes a month. Standard and Business Pro allow 100 envelopes per user per year on annual plans, or 10 per user per month on monthly plans.

Which is better for sales teams?

PandaDoc, if reps build proposals and quotes: Business adds pricing tables, a product catalog, CRM integrations and payments. Docusign suits sales teams that send finished contracts and need its wider integrations.
