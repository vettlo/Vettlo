Clockify vs Toggl: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 3, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 3, 2026

We may earn money when you click links on this page. Partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our time tracking ranking, Clockify scores 89/100 and Toggl Track scores 87/100. Clockify rates higher on approvals, billing and price, and costs less at every tier for 10 users; Toggl rates higher on time capture, reporting and ease of use, and is two points behind overall.

Pick Clockify if…

  • You want timesheet approvals and invoicing for $5.49 a seat billed annually
  • Up to 5 people need a free plan
  • You need SSO, SCIM and an audit log without a custom quote ($11.99 on Enterprise)

Pick Toggl if…

  • Ease of use matters most and you want people to track time without reminders
  • You want a 30-day trial before you pay
  • You need labor costs, profitability reports and fixed-fee projects in one plan (Premium)

Where Clockify is stronger

  • Lower cost at every tier. 10 users with approvals cost $659 a year on Standard against $1,920 on Toggl Premium.
  • A bigger free plan. Clockify Free covers up to 5 users; Toggl Free covers up to 3.
  • More operations features. Pro adds GPS, scheduling, expenses and labor cost; Enterprise adds SCIM and an audit log.

Where Toggl is stronger

  • Easier to adopt. Toggl scores 5/5 on ease of use and time capture, with timers and calendar integrations on every plan.
  • More reviews behind its rating. Both are rated 4.5/5 on G2, but Toggl's score comes from 1,684 reviews against 205 for Clockify.
  • Longer trial. A 30-day trial of paid plans, against Clockify's 7 days.
  • Profitability in one plan. Premium combines labor costs, profitability reports, fixed-fee projects, approvals and SSO.

How to decide

  1. Count everyone who will track time, including part-timers and contractors.
  2. Price the plan where you first get approvals: Clockify Standard at $5.49 or Toggl Premium at $16 a user billed annually.
  3. List the extras you need, such as invoicing, GPS, time off or Jira and Asana integrations, and find the plan that includes them.
  4. Run a two-week trial with your team and compare how many people track time every day.

Clockify vs Toggl FAQ

Is Clockify or Toggl cheaper?

Clockify, at every tier. For 10 users billed annually, Clockify Basic is $479 a year against $1,080 for Toggl Starter, and Clockify Standard with approvals is $659 against $1,920 for Toggl Premium.

Which has the better free plan?

Clockify's free plan covers up to 5 users with unlimited tracking and projects. Toggl's covers up to 3 users, with timers, calendar integrations and personal reports.

Do both have timesheet approvals?

Yes. Clockify includes approvals on Standard ($5.49 a seat billed annually) and Toggl on Premium ($16 a user billed annually).

Which scores higher in Vettlo's ranking?

Clockify, with 89/100 against 87/100 for Toggl Track. Clockify rates higher on approvals, billing and price; Toggl rates higher on time capture, reporting and ease of use.
