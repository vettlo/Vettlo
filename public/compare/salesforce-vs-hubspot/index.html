Salesforce vs HubSpot: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Oct 26, 2026 · Prices verified Oct 26, 2026

We may earn a commission when you click some links on this page. It never changes which tools we include or how we rank them. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our CRM software ranking, Salesforce Sales Cloud scores 91/100 and HubSpot Sales Hub scores 88/100. Salesforce is the deeper, more configurable platform for complex sales orgs; HubSpot is cheaper to start, easier for reps and strongest when marketing shares the same database.

Pick Salesforce if…

  • You have several sales motions, territories or complex approval rules
  • You have, or will hire, a Salesforce admin
  • You need the largest ecosystem of apps and implementation partners

Pick HubSpot if…

  • Your marketing already runs on HubSpot or you want sales and marketing on one database
  • You want reps productive quickly without an admin
  • You're a small or mid-sized team watching total cost

Where Salesforce is stronger

  • Configurability. Objects, rules and permissions can be shaped around complex sales processes, territories and approval chains.
  • Ecosystem. The largest marketplace of apps and certified implementation partners.
  • Scale. Core, Advanced and Max add Slack, Tableau, stronger security and more AI for very large organizations.

Where HubSpot is stronger

  • Lower cost to start. Starter is $7 per seat billed annually against $25 for Salesforce Starter Suite, and the free plan covers 2 users.
  • One database with marketing. Sales and marketing share the same records, so there's no sync for RevOps to maintain.
  • Rep adoption. It is easier for reps to pick up without an admin, which is why it scores higher than Salesforce on usability in our ranking.

How to decide

  1. Map your sales process: number of teams, territories and approval steps.
  2. Decide who will administer the CRM; Salesforce usually needs an admin or partner.
  3. Price the plan you'll need in a year, including HubSpot onboarding fees and Salesforce add-ons.
  4. Run your real pipeline in Salesforce's 30-day trial and HubSpot's free plan before committing.

Salesforce vs HubSpot FAQ

Is HubSpot cheaper than Salesforce?

At the entry level, yes: HubSpot Starter is $7 per seat billed annually against $25 for Salesforce Starter Suite. With forecasting, a 10-seat team pays $12,300 in year one on HubSpot Professional (including onboarding) against $12,000 on Salesforce Pro Suite.

Which is better for enterprise teams?

Salesforce, for complex processes that need custom objects, territories and permissions, and a large partner ecosystem. It scores 91/100 in our CRM ranking against 88/100 for HubSpot.

Which is easier to use?

HubSpot. Reps usually adopt it faster, while Salesforce needs more configuration and admin time.

Do they offer free versions?

HubSpot's free plan covers up to 2 users. Salesforce lists a Free Suite and offers a 30-day free trial of its editions with no credit card.
