Concord vs ContractSafe: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 5, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 5, 2026

We may earn money when you click links on this page. Partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our contract management ranking, Concord scores 79/100 and ContractSafe scores 76/100. Concord prices per platform with 5 users included and puts unlimited documents, e-signatures and negotiation on its $499 entry plan. ContractSafe includes unlimited users on every plan and prices by how many contracts you store, from $450 a month, but its entry plan is a repository: redlining, approvals and e-signature start on Finalize.

Pick Concord if…

  • A few people draft and negotiate contracts and you want that on the entry plan
  • You want unlimited documents and e-signatures at a fixed platform price
  • You want every plan's price published before you talk to sales
  • Outside parties and occasional viewers can use free viewer and guest access

Pick ContractSafe if…

  • Many people across the company need access; every plan has unlimited users
  • Your main job is getting existing signed contracts searchable, with dates extracted
  • You want to pay month to month
  • You don't need redlining or approvals yet, or can start on Finalize later

What it costs

10 users and a few hundred contracts, billed annually. Concord prices are its published platform prices plus 5 extra users; ContractSafe publishes only its starting price, and each tier's price depends on contract volume.

Where Concord is stronger

  • Drafting on the entry plan. Essentials includes unlimited documents and e-signatures, collaboration and negotiation, and amendment management.
  • Published prices for every plan. $499, $899 and $1,299 a month paid annually, each with 5 users.
  • Free outside access. Unlimited free viewers and guests on every plan.

Where ContractSafe is stronger

  • Unlimited users. No per-user fees on any plan, against $49 to $89 a month for each Concord user beyond 5.
  • Lower starting price. From $450 a month billed annually, and month-to-month billing at $540.
  • Repository first. AI data extraction, AI contract chat, custom fields and dashboards on the entry plan.

How to decide

  1. Count how many people need to log in; past about 10 regular users, Concord's per-user add-ons start to outweigh ContractSafe's volume pricing.
  2. Count the contracts you'd store, including legacy ones; ContractSafe's tiers run from 100 or fewer to over 10,000.
  3. Decide whether you need drafting and approvals now; on ContractSafe that means Finalize, on Concord approvals mean Business.
  4. Get a written ContractSafe quote for your contract count and compare it with Concord's published price.

Concord vs ContractSafe FAQ

Is Concord or ContractSafe cheaper?

ContractSafe starts lower, at $450 a month billed annually ($5,400 a year), against $499 a month for Concord Essentials ($5,988). With 10 users, Concord Essentials is $8,928 a year, while ContractSafe's price depends on how many contracts you store.

Does ContractSafe charge per user?

No. ContractSafe includes unlimited users on all plans and prices by contract volume. Concord includes 5 users and charges $49, $69 or $89 a month for each extra user, depending on plan.

Which includes e-signature?

Concord includes unlimited e-signatures from Essentials. ContractSafe lists e-signature and a Docusign integration from Finalize.

Which scores higher in Vettlo's ranking?

Concord, with 79/100 against 76/100 for ContractSafe. Concord rates higher on drafting, workflows and integrations; ContractSafe rates higher on its repository, ease of setup and price.
