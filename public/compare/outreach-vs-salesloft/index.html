Outreach vs Salesloft: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

Short answer

In our sales engagement ranking, Outreach scores 80/100 and Salesloft scores 80/100. They score the same in our ranking and neither publishes prices. Outreach packages its platform into four Amplify tiers with set AI credits; Salesloft sells a product line that runs from Cadence to Clari Forecast.

Pick Outreach if…

  • You want clearly defined tiers with set AI credit allowances
  • You need territory management and multi-currency forecasting on the same platform
  • You run multi-channel sequences and want the Outreach Voice dialer

Pick Salesloft if…

  • You want sequencing, call intelligence and Clari Forecast from one vendor
  • Your leaders run pipeline inspection and forecast calls weekly
  • You weigh user ratings heavily (4.5/5 on G2 across nearly 10,000 reviews)

Where Outreach is stronger

  • Clear packaging. Each Amplify tier lists its AI credits, API calls and custom objects, which makes like-for-like quotes easier.
  • Forecasting and territories in one tier. Amplify Pro adds forecast projection, scenario planning, territory management and multi-currency.
  • No platform fee. Its pricing page says pricing is per user with no platform fees and support included.

Where Salesloft is stronger

  • Forecasting depth. Clari Forecast and Inspect sit in the same product line as sequencing and call intelligence.
  • Rep workflow. Rhythm helps each seller focus on the next action that matters.
  • User ratings. Rated 4.5/5 on G2 across nearly 10,000 reviews, against 4.3/5 for Outreach.

How to decide

  1. List what you need on day one: sequences, dialer, call recording, forecasting.
  2. Ask both vendors to quote that exact scope for the same seat count and term.
  3. Ask Outreach how many AI credits your use will consume, and Salesloft how AI features are licensed.
  4. Run a guided pilot with one team and compare CRM sync and rep adoption.

Outreach vs Salesloft FAQ

Is Outreach or Salesloft cheaper?

Neither publishes prices, so it depends on your quote. Outreach prices per user plus AI credits with no platform fee; ask both for the same scope, seat count and contract length.

Which is better for forecasting?

Both cover it. Salesloft sells Clari Forecast and Inspect alongside Cadence; Outreach puts forecast projection, scenario planning and territory management on Amplify Pro.

Do Outreach and Salesloft have free trials?

Neither lists a free trial on its pricing page. Both sell through a demo.

Do they include contact data?

No. Both need a separate data provider, such as those in our B2B data providers ranking.
