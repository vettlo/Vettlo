Jira vs Linear: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 2, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 2, 2026

We may earn money when you click links on this page. Partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our issue tracking ranking, Jira scores 87/100 and Linear scores 86/100. Jira rates higher on workflow depth, agile planning and reporting, and costs less at 10 and 25 users on annual plans; Linear rates higher on ease of use and is one point behind overall.

Pick Jira if…

  • Several teams need their own workflows, fields and permissions
  • You want a free plan for 10 users without an issue limit
  • You need cross-team planning, a sandbox or a 99.9% uptime SLA (Premium)

Pick Linear if…

  • You want a fast tracker that works well with little setup
  • Your team plans in 1- to 8-week cycles and wants unfinished work to roll over automatically
  • You'd rather not cap the number of members on the free plan

Where Jira is stronger

  • Lower cost on annual plans. Standard is $950 a year for 10 users and $2,450 for 25, against $1,200 and $3,000 on Linear Basic.
  • Depth for many teams. Advanced planning, a sandbox, a 99.9% uptime SLA and 24/7 support come with Premium.
  • A free plan without an issue cap. Jira Free covers 10 users with 2 GB of storage; Linear Free stops at 250 issues.

Where Linear is stronger

  • Easier to adopt. Cycles, triage and projects work with sensible defaults, so teams spend less time configuring.
  • Automatic sprint rollover. Unfinished issues move to the next cycle on their own, on every plan.
  • No member cap on any plan. Every plan, including Free, allows unlimited members; the free limit is 2 teams and 250 issues.
  • Higher G2 rating. 4.6/5 against 4.3/5 for Jira, though from far fewer reviews.

How to decide

  1. Count the teams and users who will file issues in year one, including non-engineers.
  2. Price Jira on its annual user tier and Linear at $10 or $16 per user billed yearly.
  3. Decide whether you need Jira Premium's advanced planning and SLA, or Linear Business's Insights and Asks.
  4. Run one real sprint in both free plans and see which your engineers keep using.

Jira vs Linear FAQ

Is Jira or Linear cheaper?

On annual plans Jira is cheaper at these sizes: $950 a year for 10 users on Standard against $1,200 on Linear Basic, and $2,450 against $3,000 at 25 users. At the higher tier for 25 users, Jira Premium is $4,900 and Linear Business $4,800.

Which has the better free plan?

Jira Free covers 10 users and 2 GB of storage with no issue limit. Linear Free has unlimited members but allows 2 teams and 250 issues.

Did Jira raise its prices?

Yes. From Oct 13, 2026, Jira Standard's monthly list price for 1 to 100 users went from $9.05 to $9.70 per user, and Premium from $18.30 to $19.60.

Which scores higher in Vettlo's ranking?

Jira, with 87/100 against 86/100 for Linear. Jira rates higher on workflows, agile planning and reporting; Linear rates higher on ease of use and value.
