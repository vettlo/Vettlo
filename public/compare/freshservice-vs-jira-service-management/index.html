Freshservice vs Jira Service Management: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 6, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 6, 2026

We may earn money when you click links on this page. Partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our ITSM ranking, Freshservice scores 83/100 and Jira Service Management scores 84/100. They're a point apart: Jira Service Management is free for 3 agents and costs less once you need change and problem management, while Freshservice has the higher user rating and puts SLAs, a service catalog and on-call management on its $49 Growth plan.

Pick Freshservice if…

  • You want SLAs, a service catalog and on-call management on a mid-tier plan ($49 Growth)
  • Your IT team doesn't use Jira and wants a ready-made ITIL service desk
  • User ratings matter to you: 4.6/5 on G2 across 1,387 reviews
  • You want release and major incident management alongside change management

Pick Jira Service Management if…

  • You have 3 or fewer agents and want to start free
  • You need change and problem management for less: Premium is $55.28 per agent against $99 for Freshservice Pro
  • You want asset and configuration management included, with 5,000 objects on Standard
  • Your developers already work in Jira and changes should link to their work

Where Freshservice is stronger

  • Higher user rating. Freshservice is rated 4.6/5 on G2 across 1,387 reviews, against 4.3/5 across 1,004 for Jira Service Management.
  • More ITIL on the mid-tier plan. Growth ($49) adds a service catalog, SLA management, on-call and cloud management; Pro adds release and major incident management next to change and problem.
  • Built for IT out of the box. It's a dedicated IT service desk, so teams that don't use Jira don't need to set up projects and workflows first.

Where Jira Service Management is stronger

  • Free for small teams. The Free plan covers up to 3 agents with queues, workflows, alerts and on-call schedules.
  • Cheaper change and problem management. Premium at $55.28 per agent costs $6,634 a year for 10 agents, against $11,880 for Freshservice Pro.
  • Assets included. Standard includes asset and configuration management with 5,000 objects; extra objects cost $0.02 each per month.
  • Linked to development. Changes and incidents sit next to the Jira work your engineers already track.

How to decide

  1. List the ITIL processes you need in year one: incidents only, or problem and change management too.
  2. Find the cheapest plan on each side that covers them: Freshservice Pro or Jira Service Management Premium for change and problem management.
  3. Count your assets; Jira Service Management includes 5,000 objects on Standard, and Freshservice sells asset units in packs of 500.
  4. Run one real request, one incident and one change through both trials with your own IT staff.

Freshservice vs Jira Service Management FAQ

Is Freshservice or Jira Service Management cheaper?

At the entry level they're close: Freshservice Starter is $19 per agent billed annually and Jira Service Management Standard is $21.50. For change and problem management, Jira Service Management Premium ($55.28) costs about half as much as Freshservice Pro ($99).

Does either have a free plan?

Jira Service Management is free for up to 3 agents. Freshservice has no free plan but offers a 14-day trial with all features.

Which is better for teams that don't use Jira?

Freshservice, which is built as a standalone IT service desk and puts a service catalog, SLAs and on-call management on its $49 Growth plan.

Which includes AI?

Freshservice sells Freddy AI Copilot as a $29 per agent add-on and includes Freddy AI Agent on Enterprise. Jira Service Management includes Rovo agents from Standard and a virtual service agent on Premium, with 1,000 assisted conversations a month.
