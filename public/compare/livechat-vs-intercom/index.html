LiveChat vs Intercom: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Nov 4, 2026 · Prices verified Nov 4, 2026

We may earn money when you click links on this page. Partner compensation may affect which products we include and where they appear. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our live chat ranking, LiveChat scores 85/100 and Intercom scores 84/100. LiveChat rates higher on chat and routing, reporting, ease of use and price; Intercom rates higher on AI and channels and is one point behind overall.

Pick LiveChat if…

  • Your team staffs website chat all day and needs supervision, takeover and per-agent reports
  • You want AI resolutions included in the seat price: 200 a month on Growth
  • You want the same $19 Essential price after the first year, not an introductory rate

Pick Intercom if…

  • You support customers inside a web or mobile app with the Messenger
  • AI should resolve most questions, and $0.99 per Fin outcome fits your volume
  • You need SLAs, SSO or HIPAA support on a published plan (Expert, $132)

Where LiveChat is stronger

  • AI included in the seat price. Growth includes 200 AI resolutions a month; the same volume on Intercom's Fin costs $2,376 a year.
  • Lower cost with routing. 5 agents on Growth cost $4,740 a year against $5,100 on Intercom Advanced.
  • No introductory pricing. LiveChat lists Essential at $19 a user billed annually with no new-customer condition; Intercom's regular annual price is $29.

Where Intercom is stronger

  • Stronger AI. Intercom scores 5/5 on AI in our ranking, with Fin available on every plan.
  • In-app Messenger. Messenger, ticketing and a help center come together on Essential.
  • More reviews behind its rating. Both are rated 4.5/5 on G2, but Intercom's score comes from 3,949 reviews against 821 for LiveChat.
  • Published SLA and SSO plan. Expert ($132) adds SLAs, SSO and HIPAA support; LiveChat's SSO is on Enterprise, by quote.

How to decide

  1. Count the agents who answer chats and the people who only need to view or comment.
  2. Estimate how many chats AI should resolve each month and price it: included on LiveChat up to the plan's allowance, $0.99 an outcome on Intercom.
  3. Pick the plan where you first get routing: LiveChat Growth at $79 or Intercom Advanced at $85 a seat billed annually.
  4. Run both 14-day trials on a live page and compare how many chats each AI resolves without a handoff.

LiveChat vs Intercom FAQ

Is LiveChat or Intercom cheaper?

It depends on the plan. Both Essential plans are $19 a seat billed annually, but Intercom's is an introductory price ($29 regular). With routing, 5 agents cost $4,740 a year on LiveChat Growth against $5,100 on Intercom Advanced, before Fin outcomes at $0.99 each.

Do LiveChat and Intercom have free plans?

No. Both offer a 14-day free trial with no credit card needed.

How is AI priced on LiveChat and Intercom?

LiveChat includes 10 AI resolutions a month on Essential and 200 on Growth, then sells packs of 50 for $49.50. Intercom charges $0.99 per Fin outcome on every plan.

Which scores higher in Vettlo's ranking?

LiveChat, with 85/100 against 84/100 for Intercom. LiveChat rates higher on chat and routing, reporting, ease of use and price; Intercom rates higher on AI and channels.
