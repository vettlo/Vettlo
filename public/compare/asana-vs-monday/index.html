Asana vs monday.com: Which Should You Choose in 2026?

By Vettlo Editorial TeamUpdated Oct 26, 2026 · Prices verified Oct 26, 2026

We may earn a commission when you click some links on this page. It never changes which tools we include or how we rank them. See how we make money.

Short answer

In our project management ranking, Asana scores 87/100 and monday.com scores 85/100. The two are close: Asana gives more automation and structure for the money, while monday.com is easier to roll out across a whole company.

Pick Asana if…

  • You want unlimited automations on the first paid plan
  • You run many cross-functional projects with goals and portfolios
  • Your team likes structured lists and clear owners

Pick monday.com if…

  • Many non-technical people need to adopt the tool quickly
  • You prefer visual, colorful boards
  • Lots of stakeholders only need to view work, not edit it

Where Asana is stronger

  • Automation for the price. Starter includes unlimited automations; monday.com caps automation actions per month by plan.
  • Structure at scale. Goals, portfolios, approvals and resource management on Advanced suit organizations running many projects.
  • Timeline on the first paid plan. Timeline and Gantt views start on Starter.

Where monday.com is stronger

  • Adoption. Its visual boards and 200+ templates make it the easier tool to roll out to non-technical teams.
  • Cheaper entry seat. Basic is $9 per seat billed annually versus $10.99 for Asana Starter.
  • Time tracking sooner. Time tracking is on Pro ($19) rather than Asana Advanced ($24.99).

How to decide

  1. List the views and automations you need on day one.
  2. Find the cheapest plan on each side that includes all of them; compare that price, not the entry price.
  3. Count who needs a paid seat versus who only views work.
  4. Run one real project in both for two weeks with the people who'll use it daily.

Asana vs monday.com FAQ

Is Asana or monday.com cheaper?

monday.com's Basic seat is cheaper ($9 vs $10.99 billed annually), but Asana Starter includes timeline views and unlimited automations that need monday.com Standard ($12) or Pro ($19).

Which has the better free plan?

Both free plans cover 2 users. Asana's includes unlimited tasks and projects; monday.com's is limited to 3 boards.

Which is easier to use?

monday.com is generally faster for non-technical teams to adopt; Asana rewards teams that want more structure.

Which is better for large organizations?

Asana Advanced adds portfolios, goals and resource management at a published price; monday.com puts portfolio and resource management on Enterprise, priced by quote.
