Privacy Policy

Last updated Nov 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Vettlo handles when you visit vettlo.com, contact us or use the vendor portal. Contact: vendors@vettlo.com or our contact page.

Visiting the site

Vettlo does not use advertising trackers or marketing cookies. We measure visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without building a profile of you. Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, processes standard technical data such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver and protect the site.

Contact, suggestion and newsletter forms

When you send a form, your name, email address and message are emailed to us so we can reply. To stop abuse, we keep a one-way hashed version of your IP address for up to 24 hours.

Vendor portal

  • Account details: your email address, company name and website, and the name and website of the products you add.
  • Billing details: if you enter them in the portal, the name, phone number, tax or ID number and address you give us for invoicing.
  • Sign-in cookie: when you sign in, a strictly necessary cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It isn't used for advertising or tracking.
  • Visit reports: we count clicks on product links using a hashed version of the visitor's IP address to tell repeat clicks apart. We don't store the IP address itself.
  • Order records: your orders, invoices and when you accepted our terms.

Payments

When you pay for a position, you pay by card or bank transfer through a payment request we send you. Card details are handled by the payment provider and never reach Vettlo. We keep a record of each payment (amount, date and reference) for our accounts.

Providers we use

  • Cloudflare: hosting, security, database and email routing.
  • Brevo: sending sign-in links and service emails.
  • Payment providers: processing the payments vendors make to us.

Some of these providers process data outside your country.

Links to other sites

Reviews link to software vendors and review platforms. Some links are affiliate links. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, through our contact page.

Changes

We'll post any changes on this page and update the date above.
