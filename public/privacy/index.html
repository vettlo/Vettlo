This policy explains what information Vettlo handles when you visit vettlo.com.

What we collect

Vettlo does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard technical data such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver and protect the site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Forms and newsletter

Our newsletter and contact forms do not yet send or store any information. When they go live, we'll update this policy to say which provider handles your details and how to unsubscribe or request deletion.

Links to other sites

Reviews link to software vendors and review platforms. Some links are affiliate links. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, through our contact page.

Changes
